## 40:16 three baskets of white bread | three open-weave baskets
kind: two senses
call: close
why: The word after "baskets" stands only here in Scripture. It is close to the word for white cloth (Esther 1:6; 8:15; Isaiah 19:9, "weavers of white cloth"), which gives baskets of something white, and what a baker carries is bread. Elsewhere a basket named this way is named by what is in it: "the basket of unleavened bread" (Exodus 29:23; Leviticus 8:2; Numbers 6:15). The dream then goes on to the top basket as the one that held more: "some of every food of Pharaoh, the work of a baker" (40:17).
other: The word is just as close to the word for a hole (1 Samuel 14:11, "the holes where they hid themselves"; 2 Kings 12:9, "bored a hole in its lid"), which gives baskets full of holes, woven open. Read this way the dream says what was in a basket only at 40:17, and Joseph's interpretation uses only the number of the baskets, the head and the birds (40:18-19).
review: open

## 39:17 The Hebrew slave whom you brought to us came to me to toy with me | The Hebrew slave came to me, the one you brought to us to toy with me
kind: grammar
call: close
why: In the Hebrew the words "to toy with me" stand last, after "whom you brought to us", and the way the Hebrew text marks the pauses of the verse sets them apart from those words, so that they go with "came to me". It is the build of what she told the men of her house: "He came to me to lie with me" (39:14). What she goes on to say, and what her husband hears, is about what the slave did: "he left his garment beside me and fled outside" (39:18); "This is the kind of thing your slave did to me" (39:19).
other: The words "to toy with me" come straight after "whom you brought to us", and to the men of her house she joined the same two things: "he has brought us a Hebrew man to toy with us" (39:14). Read this way she lays the blame on her husband to his face, as she did behind his back.

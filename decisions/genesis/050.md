## 50:5 that I dug out for myself | that I bought for myself
kind: two senses
call: clear
why: The same Hebrew words are said of the grave of King Asa: "they buried him in his graves, which he had dug out for himself in the city of David" (2 Chronicles 16:14). It is the verb Genesis has already used of a well: "there Isaac's servants dug out a well" (26:25). And Scripture speaks of a grave as a thing cut into the rock: "you have cut out a grave for yourself here" (Isaiah 22:16). Jacob had himself buried Leah in that cave (49:31).
other: A second Hebrew verb, written the same way, means "buy": "water too you are to buy from them with silver" (Deuteronomy 2:6), and "I bought her for myself for fifteen pieces of silver" (Hosea 3:2). The grave Jacob named was a bought one: "the field that Abraham bought from Ephron the Hittite as a holding for a grave" (49:30; 50:13). But it was Abraham who bought it, not Jacob, and the word for "bought" there is the ordinary one.
review: open

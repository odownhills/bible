## 17:36 each of those will be asked about | each of those will itself be asked about it
kind: grammar
call: close
why: Two verses earlier the same word is said of a thing a person must answer for: "The covenant will be asked about" (17:34), and so again, "God's covenant will be asked about" (33:15). Read the same way here, the hearing, the sight and the heart are what a person answers for. They are what he was given to know with, which is the matter of this command: "God brought you out of your mothers' bellies knowing nothing, and he gave you hearing, sight and hearts, so that you may give thanks" (16:78; so 23:78; 67:23). And people are asked about what they were given: "Then on that day you will certainly be asked about the delight" (102:8).
other: The Arabic can also be put together so that each of the three is the one who is questioned, about the thing that was followed without knowledge. Wherever else the Quran says that someone is "asked about" a thing, the one who is asked is the subject of the verb: "He is not asked about what he does, but they are asked" (21:23); "you will certainly be asked about what you used to do" (16:93). The word for "those" in this verse is the one normally used of persons, while two verses later the whole list of deeds is summed up with the word used of things, "all that" (17:38). And the Quran says elsewhere that hearing and sight will speak on that day: "their hearing, their sight and their skins bear witness against them about what they used to do" (41:20; so 41:22, and of tongues, hands and feet 24:24; 36:65).
review: open

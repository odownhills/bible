## 17:71 with their leader | with their record
kind: two senses
call: close
why: The Arabic word is the one for whoever stands at the head and is followed, and the Quran uses it most often of persons: "I am making you a leader for the people" (2:124); "we made them leaders guiding by our command" (21:73; so 32:24); of Pharaoh and his forces, "we made them leaders who call to the Fire" (28:41); "the leaders of disbelief" (9:12). The Quran says that peoples come on that day with the ones they followed: Pharaoh "will go before his people on the Day of Resurrection and lead them down to the Fire" (11:98); "Our Lord, we obeyed our masters and our great ones, and they led us astray from the way" (33:67; so 7:38; 14:21). And every community is brought with its own messenger or witness: "How will it be when we bring a witness from every community" (4:41; so 16:84, 89); "Every community has a messenger. So when their messenger comes, it is decided between them fairly, and they are not wronged" (10:47), which ends as this verse ends. For what each person is handed, this verse then uses another word, "book".
other: The same word is also used of a writing that is followed. The Quran says of the record of what people did, "everything, we have counted it in a clear record" (36:12), and it twice calls the Book of Moses by this word (11:17; 46:12). The verse nearest to this one in wording has "book" where this one has this word: "Every community is called to its book" (45:28). This verse itself goes straight on to the book each one is given, and the surah has already said, "on the Day of Resurrection we will bring out for him a book that he will meet spread open" (17:13); so also "the book is set down" (18:49; 39:69). Read that way, each group of people is called with the record of what it did.
review: open

## 17:85 is from the command of my Lord | is part of my Lord's matter
kind: two senses
call: clear
why: The Quran joins the spirit to God's command in these same words where it tells how revelation comes: "He sends down the angels with the spirit, from his command, on whomever he wills among his servants" (16:2); "he casts the spirit, from his command, on whomever he wills among his servants" (40:15); "And that is how we revealed to you a spirit from our command" (42:52). In each of them the spirit comes because he commands it. So also "the angels and the spirit come down in it by the permission of their Lord" (97:4), and those who come down say, "We come down only by the command of your Lord" (19:64). The next verse here is about the same thing: "what we have revealed to you" (17:86).
other: The Arabic word for "command" is also the word for a matter or an affair, and "from" can be "part of". The Quran speaks in this way of what is in God's hands and no one else's: "You have no part in the matter" (3:128); "The matter belongs wholly to God" (3:154; so 13:31; 30:4). Where people ask about a thing that God alone knows, the answer hands it to him: "They ask you about the Hour ... Say, 'The knowledge of it is only with my Lord'" (7:187; so 33:63; 79:42-44). And this verse goes on, "you have been given only a little knowledge". Read that way, the answer is that the spirit is one of the things that are his to know.
review: open

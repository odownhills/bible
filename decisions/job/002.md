## 2:9 Curse God and die | Bless God and die
kind: two senses
call: clear
why: The adversary has just said, with this same word, that Job "will certainly curse you to your face" (2:5), and her words push Job to do it. Job answers that she speaks "as one of the foolish women speaks", and the writer adds that Job "did not sin with his lips" (2:10), so what she urged was a sin of the lips. The same word stands in for "curse" at 1:5 and 1:11 and in the charge against Naboth (1 Kings 21:10, 13).
other: The Hebrew word is the ordinary word "bless", which is what it means everywhere outside these few places, and it is the word Job himself used: "may the name of Yahweh be blessed" (1:21). Her first words repeat Yahweh's own praise of Job (2:3), so she may be throwing Job's word back at him in bitterness: keep blessing God, and die.
review: open

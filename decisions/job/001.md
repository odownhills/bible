## 1:6 the adversary | Satan
kind: two senses
call: ask
why: In Hebrew this is an ordinary word, "adversary," and all fourteen times in Job 1 and 2 it has "the" in front of it, which a personal name does not take; so here it is a title for what he does. The same word, as plain "adversary," is used of the angel of Yahweh in Balaam's way (Numbers 22:22, 32), of David (1 Samuel 29:4) and of Solomon's enemies (1 Kings 11:14, 23, 25), and with "the" again in Zechariah 3:1-2, where he stands at the right hand to accuse.
other: Without "the," 1 Chronicles 21:1 uses the word as a name, and the New Testament names the one who does this very thing: "the one called the devil and Satan ... the accuser of our brothers, who accuses them before our God day and night" (Revelation 12:9-10; see also Luke 22:31).

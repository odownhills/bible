## 1:2 Lord of the worlds | Lord of all peoples
kind: two senses
call: ask
why: The word is the plural of "world". This surah does not explain it, so the choice rests on how the Quran explains the title elsewhere, and there it answers with everything there is. Pharaoh says, "And what is the Lord of the worlds?" and Moses answers, "The Lord of the heavens and the earth and what is between them" (26:23-24), then "Your Lord and the Lord of your forefathers" (26:26), then "The Lord of the east and the west and what is between them" (26:28). The title stands in a row with "Lord of the heavens and Lord of the earth" (45:36), it is given to the one who created the heavens and the earth in six days (7:54) and the earth in two (41:9), and the one who bears it is "the Lord of everything" (6:162-164). Peoples are among the worlds, so the wider wording does not lose what the narrower one says.
other: Everywhere the word stands without "Lord of", 31 times, it is used of people: "the women of all peoples" (3:42), "no one among all peoples" (7:80), "what is in the breasts of all peoples" (29:10), the Children of Israel set above them (2:47), "a reminder to all peoples, to whoever among you wills to go straight" (81:27-28), with the title following at once (81:29). The word has the plural ending that Arabic gives to persons. It is people who stand before the bearer of this title on the great day for which they are raised (83:4-6), Moses' second answer to Pharaoh names people, "Your Lord and the Lord of your forefathers" (26:26), God is "the Lord of mankind" (114:1), and what this surah goes on to say is about people: those who worship, those favored, those who have incurred anger, those who have gone astray (1:5-7).
review: open

## 1:4 Master of the Day of Judgment | King of the Day of Judgment
kind: readings differ
call: clear
why: The main text always follows the Hafs reading, and it reads the first word with a long vowel: the one who owns a thing and has it in his power, "master". The Quran describes this Day with the verb of that word: "And what will make you know what the Day of Judgment is? ... A day when no soul will have power to do anything for another soul, and the command that day belongs to God" (82:17-19); and of the day he gathers them all (34:40), "So today none of you has power to benefit or harm another" (34:42). God is called by the same word at 3:26: "Master of the kingdom, you give the kingdom to whom you will."
other: The other established reading of the same letters has a short vowel: "king". God is "King of mankind" (114:2) and "the King" (20:114; 23:116; 59:23; 62:1). And the Quran says of that day, "Whose is the kingdom today? God's, the One" (40:16); "The true kingdom that day belongs to the Most Merciful" (25:26); "The kingdom that day belongs to God; he will judge between them" (22:56); "his is the kingdom on the day the horn is blown" (6:73).
review: open

Job 5

Listen only

The fool and his home

1"Call now; is there anyone who will answer you? And to which of the holy ones will you turn? 2For resentment kills the fool, and jealousy puts the simpleton to death.

3"I myself have seen a fool taking root, and suddenly I put a curse on his home. 4His sons are far from safety, and they are crushed in the gate, and there is no one to rescue them. 5The hungry eat his harvest and take it even from among thorns, and the snare pants for their wealth [alt: and the thirsty pant for their wealth].

6"For harm does not come out of the dust, and misery does not sprout from the ground; 7but a human being is born to misery [alt: begets misery], as sparks fly upward. [In Hebrew "human being" sounds like the word for "ground" in verse 6.]

I would seek God

8"But I myself would seek God, and I would put my cause to God, 9who does great things that cannot be searched out, wonders beyond number, 10who gives rain on the face of the earth and sends water on the face of the open country, 11to set the lowly on high, and those who mourn are raised to safety.

12"He frustrates the plans of the shrewd, and their hands achieve no success. 13He catches the wise in their shrewdness, and the counsel of the devious is rushed headlong. 14By day they meet darkness, and at noon they grope as in the night. 15And he saves the needy from the sword, from their mouth, and from the hand of the strong. 16So the poor have hope, and injustice shuts its mouth.

Happy is the one God corrects

17"Look, happy is the mortal whom God corrects; so do not reject the discipline of the Almighty [alt: Shaddai]. [In Hebrew "discipline" is from the same word as "instructed" in 4:3.] 18For he himself causes pain and binds up; he wounds, and his hands heal.

19"In six troubles he will rescue you, and in seven no evil will touch you. 20In famine he will ransom you from death, and in war from the power of the sword. 21In the lashing of the tongue you will be hidden, and you will not be afraid of destruction when it comes. 22At destruction and starvation you will laugh, and do not be afraid of the wild animals of the earth. 23For your covenant is with the stones of the field, and the wild animals of the field have been brought to peace with you.

24"And you will know that your tent is at peace, and you will inspect your home and find nothing missing [alt: and you will not sin]. 25And you will know that your offspring are many, and your descendants like the plants of the earth. 26You will come to the grave in full vigor [alt: in ripe old age], as a stack of grain goes up in its season.

27"Look, this—we have searched it out; so it is. Hear it, and you, know it for yourself."

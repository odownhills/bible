Job 11

Watch Listen only

Zophar answers Job

1And Zophar the Naamathite answered and said:

2"Is a multitude of words to go unanswered, or is a man full of talk to be righteous? 3Are men to be silent at your idle talk? You mock, and there is no one to put you to shame; 4and you say, 'My teaching is pure, and I am clean in your eyes.'

5"But if only God would speak and open his lips with you, 6and tell you the secrets of wisdom! For sound wisdom has two sides. And know that God lets some of your guilt be forgotten for you.

The limit of the Almighty

7"Can you find what is to be searched out in God, or find your way to the limit of the Almighty? 8It is as high as the heavens—what can you do? It is deeper than Sheol—what can you know? 9Its measure is longer than the earth and broader than the sea.

10"If he sweeps on and shuts someone in and calls an assembly, who can turn him back? 11For he himself knows empty men; he sees harm, and will he not look closely at it? [alt: he sees harm without having to look closely.] 12And a hollow man will get sense when a wild donkey's colt is born a human being [alt: Yet a hollow man can get sense, though a human being is born a wild donkey's colt]. [In Hebrew "get sense" comes from the word for "heart" and sounds like "hollow."]

If you set your heart right

13"If you yourself set your heart right and spread out your palm to him— 14if harm is in your hand, put it far away, and do not let injustice live in your tents— 15surely then you will lift your face without blemish; you will be solid and will not be afraid.

Brighter than noon

16"For you yourself will forget misery; you will remember it like water that has passed away. 17And life will rise brighter than noon; though it grows black, it will be like the morning. 18And you will trust, because there is hope; you will look around and lie down in security. 19You will bed down, and there will be no one to make you tremble, and many will seek your favor.

20"But the eyes of the wicked will fail; refuge is lost to them, and their hope is the breathing out of life."

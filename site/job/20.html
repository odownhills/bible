Zophar answers Job again

1And Zophar the Naamathite answered and said:

2"Therefore my anxious thoughts reply to me, and this is because of my haste within me. 3I hear discipline that puts me to shame, and a spirit from my understanding answers me.

The joy of the godless is for a moment

4"Do you know this—from of old, from when humankind was set on earth— 5that the cry of joy of the wicked is short, and the joy of the godless man is for a moment? 6Though his height goes up to the heavens and his head reaches the clouds, 7he will perish forever like his dung; those who saw him will say, 'Where is he?' 8Like a dream he will fly away and not be found, and he will be chased away like a vision of the night. 9An eye has glimpsed him and will not do so again, and his place will no longer catch sight of him.

10"His sons will appease the poor [alt: The poor will crush his sons], and his hands will give back his riches. 11His bones are full of his youth, and it will lie down with him on the dust.

Sweet in his mouth

12"Though evil is sweet in his mouth, though he conceals it under his tongue, 13spares it and does not abandon it, and keeps it back within his palate, 14his bread turns in his stomach; the gall of cobras is within him. 15He has swallowed wealth, and he will vomit it up; God will turn it out of his belly. 16He will suck the venom of cobras; a viper's tongue will kill him.

He will not swallow it

17"Let him not look on the streams, the rivers, the torrents of honey and curds. 18He gives back what he toiled for and will not swallow it, as much as the wealth he got in exchange, and he will have no enjoyment. 19For he has crushed, he has abandoned the poor; he has seized a house, and he will not build it up [alt: he has seized a house that he did not build]. 20For he has known no ease in his belly; he will not escape with what he desires [alt: he lets nothing that he desires escape]. 21There is no survivor from his eating; that is why his good will not endure. 22When his plenty is full, he will be in distress; the hand of everyone in misery will come upon him [alt: the whole power of misery will come upon him].

The day of his anger

23"When his belly is to be filled, he will send the burning of his anger on him and rain it on him as his food [alt: and rain it on him, into his flesh]. 24He will flee from an iron weapon; a bronze bow will pierce him. 25He draws it out, and it comes out of his back, and the flashing point comes from his gall; terrors are upon him. 26All darkness is hidden for what he has hidden away; a fire that no one blew on will devour him; it will go ill with the survivor in his tent. 27The heavens will uncover his guilt, and the earth will rise up against him. 28The produce of his house will go into exile, poured away in the day of his anger. [In Hebrew "go into exile" is the same verb as "uncover" in verse 27.]

29"This is the portion of a wicked human being from God, and the inheritance decreed for him by God."

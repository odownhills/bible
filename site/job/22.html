Job 22

▶ Listen from here

Eliphaz answers Job a third time

1And Eliphaz the Temanite answered and said:

2"Is it to God that a man can be of use? Rather, one who has insight is of use to himself. 3Does the Almighty care that you are righteous, or is it profit to him that you make your ways blameless? 4Is it for your fear of God that he corrects you [alt: Is it out of fear of you that he corrects you], that he comes into judgment with you? 5Is not your evil great, and your guilty deeds without end?

You sent widows away empty-handed

6"For you have taken pledges from your brothers for nothing, and stripped the naked of their garments. 7You have given the exhausted no water to drink, and from the hungry you have kept back bread. 8But the man with a strong arm—the land was his, and the favored man lived in it. 9You have sent widows away empty-handed, and the arms of orphans have been crushed.

10"That is why traps are all around you, and dread suddenly dismays you, 11or darkness, so that you cannot see, and an abundance of water covers you.

What does God know?

12"Is not God as high as the heavens? And see the highest stars, how high they are! 13And you say, 'What does God know? Can he judge through thick darkness? 14Clouds are a hiding place for him, and he does not see, and he walks about on the circle of the heavens.'

15"Will you keep to the path of old that men of harm have trodden, 16who were seized before their time, on whose foundation a river was poured out? 17They said to God, 'Depart from us,' and asked what the Almighty could do for them [alt: do to them]. 18And he himself filled their houses with good; and the counsel of the wicked is far from me.

19"The righteous see it and rejoice, and the innocent man mocks at them: 20'Surely those who rose against us have been wiped out, and what they had left, fire has devoured.'

Return to the Almighty

21"Get to know him now, and be at peace; by these things good will come to you. [In Hebrew "get to know" sounds like "be of use" in verse 2.] 22Take teaching from his mouth now, and put his words in your heart.

23"If you return to the Almighty, you will be built up—if you put injustice far away from your tent. 24And lay gold ore on the dust, and the gold of Ophir among the rocks of the torrents, 25and the Almighty will be your gold ore, and heaps of silver for you. [In Hebrew "gold ore" sounds like "among the rocks", and "dust" sounds like "Ophir".]

26"For then you will take delight in the Almighty, and lift your face to God. 27You will plead with him, and he will hear you, and you will pay your vows. 28You will decree a thing, and it will stand for you, and light will shine on your ways.

29"When people are brought low and you say, 'Rise up,' he will save the one with downcast eyes [alt: For they are brought low, and you will say, 'It was pride'; but he saves the one with downcast eyes]. 30He will deliver one who is not innocent [alt: the innocent man, as the old Greek and Latin versions read], and he will be delivered by the cleanness of your palms."

Job 12

Watch Listen only

Job answers Zophar

1And Job answered and said:

2"Truly you are the people, and with you wisdom will die. 3I too have sense, as you do; I do not fall short of you; and who does not have such things as these?

A laughingstock to his friend

4"I am one who is a laughingstock to his friend, one who called to God, and he answered him—a laughingstock, a righteous, blameless man! 5In the thought of one who is untroubled there is contempt for misfortune; it is ready for those whose foot slips [alt: A despised torch, in the thought of one who is untroubled, is the man whose foot is ready to slip]. 6The tents of destroyers are at ease, and there is security for those who provoke God, for the one who brings a god in his hand [alt: for the one into whose hand God brings it].

Ask the beasts

7"But ask the beasts now, and they will teach you; and the birds of the sky, and they will tell you. 8Or speak to the earth, and it will teach you, and the fish of the sea will recount it to you. 9Who among all these does not know that the hand of Yahweh has done this? 10In his hand is the life of every living thing and the spirit of all human flesh.

With him are wisdom and might

11"Does not the ear test words, as the palate tastes food for itself? 12Among the aged is wisdom, and in length of days understanding. [alt: Is wisdom among the aged, and understanding in length of days?] 13With him are wisdom and might; counsel and understanding are his.

14"Look, he tears down, and it is not rebuilt; he shuts a man in, and it is not opened. 15Look, he holds back the waters, and they dry up; and he sends them out, and they overturn the earth. 16With him are power and sound wisdom; the one who goes astray and the one who leads astray are his.

Counselors, kings and priests

17"He leads counselors away stripped and drives judges mad. 18He undoes the discipline of kings [alt: the bonds of kings] and binds a loincloth around their waist. 19He leads priests away stripped and overthrows the long-established. 20He removes the speech of the trusted and takes away the discernment of the elders. [In Hebrew "discernment" is the word for "taste," as in verse 11.] 21He pours out contempt on nobles and loosens the belt of the strong.

He makes nations grow and destroys them

22"He uncovers deep things out of darkness and brings the shadow of death out to the light. 23He makes nations grow and destroys them; he spreads nations out and leads them away [alt: and guides them]. 24He removes the sense of the chiefs of the people of the earth and makes them wander in a waste with no way. 25They grope in darkness and without light, and he makes them wander like a drunk man.

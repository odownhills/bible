Job 16

TV mode Listen only

Job answers Eliphaz again

1And Job answered and said:

2"I have heard many things like these; you are comforters who bring misery, all of you. 3Is there an end to words of wind? Or what pains you, that you answer? 4I myself also could speak as you do. If your soul were in the place of my soul, I could string words together against you and shake my head at you. 5I could make you firm with my mouth, and the movement of my lips would restrain your pain.

His anger has torn me

6"If I speak, my pain is not restrained; and if I cease, what of it goes from me? 7Surely now he has wearied me; you have devastated all my company. 8And you have shriveled me up [alt: you have seized me]; it has become a witness, and my leanness has risen against me [alt: the lie told about me has risen against me]; it testifies to my face.

9"His anger has torn me, and he has borne a grudge against me; he has ground his teeth at me; my foe sharpens his eyes against me. 10They have gaped at me with their mouth; with insult they have struck my cheeks; together they mass against me. 11God hands me over to the unjust [alt: to a child], and into the hands of the wicked he hurls me.

He set me up as a target

12"I was at ease, and he broke me to pieces; he seized me by the back of the neck and dashed me to pieces, and he set me up as a target for himself. 13His archers surround me; he splits open my kidneys and does not spare; he pours out my gall on the ground. 14He breaches me, breach upon breach; he runs at me like a mighty man.

My witness is in the heavens

15"I have sewn sackcloth over my skin, and I have thrust my horn into the dust. 16My face is red from weeping, and on my eyelids is the shadow of death, 17though there is no violence in my palms, and my prayer is pure.

18"Earth, do not cover my blood, and let there be no resting place for my cry. 19Even now, look, my witness is in the heavens, and the one who testifies for me is on high. 20My friends scoff at me [alt: My spokesman is my friend]; to God my eye sheds tears, 21that he would argue for a man with God, as a human being does for his friend [alt: and decide between a human being and his friend]. 22For a few years will come, and I will go the path from which I will not return.

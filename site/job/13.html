Job 13

Listen only

I would speak to the Almighty

1"Look, my eye has seen it all; my ear has heard and discerned it. 2What you know, I myself also know; I do not fall short of you.

3"But I myself would speak to the Almighty, and I wish to argue with God. 4But you yourselves plaster with lies; you are worthless healers, all of you. 5If only you would actually be silent, and that would be wisdom for you!

Will you speak deceit for God?

6"Hear now my argument, and pay attention to the contentions of my lips. 7Is it for God that you speak injustice, and for him that you speak deceit? 8Is it to him that you show favoritism, or for God that you contend?

9"Will it be good when he searches you out? Or will you fool him as one fools a mortal? 10He will certainly correct you if in secret you show favoritism. 11Will his majesty not terrify you, and will the dread of him not fall on you? [In Hebrew "majesty" is from the verb "lift," and "show favoritism" in verses 8 and 10 is "lift the face."] 12Your reminders are proverbs of ashes; your defenses are defenses of clay.

I will argue my ways to his face

13"Be silent and let me be, and I myself will speak, and let come over me what may. 14Why do I carry my flesh in my teeth and put my life in my hand? 15Look, he will kill me; I will not wait [alt: I will wait for him, as the Hebrew is traditionally read aloud]; but I will argue my ways to his face. 16This too will be my salvation [alt: He too will be my salvation], for a godless man does not come before him.

17"Hear my word carefully, and let my declaration be in your ears. 18Look now, I have set out my case; I know that I myself will be found righteous. 19Who is it that will contend with me? For then I would be silent and breathe my last.

Call, and I will answer

20"Only do not do two things to me; then I will not hide from your face. 21Put your palm far away from me, and let the terror of you not terrify me. 22Then call, and I myself will answer; or let me speak, and you reply to me.

Why do you hide your face?

23"How many are my guilty deeds and my sins? Let me know my rebellion and my sin. 24Why do you hide your face and count me as your enemy? [In Hebrew "enemy" sounds like the name "Job."] 25Will you terrorize a driven leaf, and pursue dry stubble? 26For you write bitter things against me and make me inherit the guilty deeds of my youth, 27and you put my feet in the stocks and keep watch on all my paths; you draw a line around the soles of my feet [alt: you cut a mark into the soles of my feet]. 28And he wears out like something rotten, like a garment that a moth has eaten.

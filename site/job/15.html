Job 15

TV mode Listen only

Eliphaz answers Job again

1And Eliphaz the Temanite answered and said:

2"Does a wise man answer with windy knowledge and fill his belly with the east wind, 3arguing with talk that is of no use, and with words by which he gains nothing? 4You yourself even do away with fear of God, and you take away meditation before God. 5For your guilt teaches your mouth [alt: For your mouth makes your guilt known], and you choose the tongue of the shrewd. 6Your mouth condemns you, and not I, and your lips testify against you.

Were you born the first human being?

7"Were you born the first human being, and were you brought to birth before the hills? 8Do you listen in the council of God and take wisdom away for yourself? 9What do you know that we do not know? What do you discern that is not with us? 10Both the gray-haired and the aged are among us, greater in days than your father.

11"Are the comforts of God too little for you, and a word that is gentle with you? 12Why does your heart carry you away, and why do your eyes flash, 13that you turn your spirit against God and bring out words from your mouth?

Not pure in his eyes

14"What is a mortal, that he should be pure, and that one born of a woman should be righteous? 15Look, in his holy ones he puts no trust, and the heavens are not pure in his eyes; 16how much less one who is detestable and corrupt, a man who drinks injustice like water!

What the wise have told

17"I will declare it to you; hear me; and what I have gazed on I will recount, 18what the wise tell from their fathers and have not concealed— 19to them alone the land was given, and no stranger passed among them.

The wicked man writhes all his days

20"All his days the wicked man writhes, through the number of years hidden away for the ruthless. 21Sounds of dread are in his ears; in a time of peace the destroyer comes upon him. 22He does not believe that he will return from darkness, and he is watched for by the sword. 23He wanders about for bread: 'Where is it?' [alt: He wanders about as food for the vulture, as the old Greek version takes it.] He knows that a day of darkness is ready at his hand. 24Distress and anguish terrify him; they overpower him like a king ready for the attack.

25"For he has stretched out his hand against God, and against the Almighty he acts the mighty man. 26He runs at him with a stiff neck, with the thick bosses of his shields. 27For he has covered his face with his fat and put on fat over his flanks, 28and he has lived in cities that were wiped out, in houses that no one lives in, which were ready to become stone heaps.

A flame will dry up his shoots

29"He will not be rich, and his wealth will not last, and their gain will not stretch out over the land [alt: and he will not stretch out a shadow over the land, as the old Greek version has it]. 30He will not depart from darkness; a flame will dry up his shoots, and he will depart by the wind of his mouth. 31Let him not put trust in emptiness, led astray as he is, for emptiness will be what he gets in exchange. 32Before his time it will be paid in full, and his frond will not be green. 33Like a vine he will tear off his unripe grapes, and like an olive tree he will throw off his blossom.

34"For the company of the godless is barren, and fire devours the tents of bribery. 35They conceive misery and give birth to harm, and their womb prepares deceit." [In Hebrew "womb" is the same word as "belly" in verse 2.]

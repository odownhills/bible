Job 17

Listen only

Be my guarantor

1"My spirit is ruined; my days are snuffed out; graves are mine. 2Surely mockers are with me, and my eye dwells on their provocation.

3"Lay down a pledge now; be my guarantor with yourself. Who is it that will clasp my hand in pledge? 4For you have hidden their heart away from insight; that is why you will not raise them high. 5One who informs on friends for a share—the eyes of his sons will fail [alt: A man invites friends to a share, while the eyes of his sons fail].

A byword among the peoples

6"And he has set me as a byword among the peoples, and I am one whose face is spat on [alt: and formerly I was like a drum]. 7And my eye has grown dim from grief, and all my limbs are like a shadow.

8"The upright are appalled at this, and the innocent man rouses himself against the godless man. 9And the righteous man holds to his way, and the one with clean hands grows firmer.

Where then is my hope?

10"But all of you, come again now, and I will not find a wise man among you. 11My days have passed; my purposes are broken off, the desires of my heart. 12They turn night into day: 'Light is near,' in the face of darkness.

13"If I hope for Sheol as my house, if I have spread my bed in the darkness, 14if I have called to the pit, 'You are my father,' and to the maggot, 'My mother and my sister,' 15where then is my hope? And my hope—who will catch sight of it? 16It will go down to the bars of Sheol, when there is rest together on the dust [alt: Will it go down to the bars of Sheol? Shall we go down together to the dust?]."

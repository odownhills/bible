Job of Uz

1There was a man in the land of Uz; his name was Job. And that man was blameless and upright, and he feared God and turned away from evil. 2And seven sons and three daughters were born to him. 3And his herds were seven thousand sheep and three thousand camels and five hundred pairs of oxen and five hundred female donkeys, and very many servants. And that man was greater than all the people of the east.

4And his sons would go and hold a feast in the house of each one on his day, and they would send and invite their three sisters to eat and to drink with them. 5And when the days of the feast had run their course, Job would send and make them holy, and he would rise early in the morning and offer up burnt offerings according to the number of them all; for Job said, "Perhaps my sons have sinned and cursed God in their heart." [In Hebrew the word here and in verse 11 is "bless," standing in place of "curse"; it is the same word as "blessed" in verses 10 and 21.] This is what Job would always do.

The adversary before Yahweh

6And one day the sons of God came to present themselves before Yahweh, and the adversary [alt: Satan] also came among them. 7And Yahweh said to the adversary, "Where do you come from?" And the adversary answered Yahweh and said, "From roaming the earth and from walking about on it." 8And Yahweh said to the adversary, "Have you considered my servant Job? For there is no one like him on the earth, a blameless and upright man, who fears God and turns away from evil."

9And the adversary answered Yahweh and said, "Is it for nothing that Job fears God? 10Have you yourself not put a hedge around him and around his house and around all that he has, on every side? The work of his hands you have blessed, and his herds have spread out in the land. 11But reach out your hand now and touch all that he has; he will certainly curse you to your face." 12And Yahweh said to the adversary, "Look, all that he has is in your hand; only against him do not reach out your hand." And the adversary went out from the presence of Yahweh.

Four messengers

13And one day, when his sons and his daughters were eating and drinking wine in the house of their firstborn brother, 14a messenger came to Job and said, "The oxen were plowing and the female donkeys were grazing beside them, 15and Sheba fell on them and took them, and they struck down the young men with the edge of the sword, and I escaped, only I alone, to tell you."

16While this one was still speaking, another came and said, "The fire of God fell from the sky and burned among the sheep and the young men and devoured them, and I escaped, only I alone, to tell you."

17While this one was still speaking, another came and said, "Chaldeans formed three bands and made a raid on the camels and took them, and they struck down the young men with the edge of the sword, and I escaped, only I alone, to tell you."

18While this one was speaking, another came and said, "Your sons and your daughters were eating and drinking wine in the house of their firstborn brother, 19and look, a great wind came from across the wilderness and hit the four corners of the house, and it fell on the young people and they died, and I escaped, only I alone, to tell you." [In Hebrew "hit" is the same verb as "touch" in verse 11, and "the young people" is the same word as "the young men" in verses 15 to 17.]

Job bows down

20And Job rose and tore his robe and shaved his head, and he fell to the ground and bowed down. 21And he said, "Naked I came out of my mother's womb, and naked I will return there. Yahweh gave, and Yahweh has taken; may the name of Yahweh be blessed." 22In all this Job did not sin, and he did not charge God with wrongdoing.

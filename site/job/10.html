Job 10

Watch Listen only

I will say to God

1"My soul loathes my life; I will abandon myself to my complaint; I will speak in the bitterness of my soul. 2I will say to God, 'Do not condemn me; let me know why you contend against me.

3"'Is it good for you that you oppress, that you reject the toil of your hands, and shine on the counsel of the wicked? 4Do you have eyes of flesh, or do you see as a mortal sees? 5Are your days like the days of a mortal, or your years like the days of a man, 6that you look for my guilt and seek out my sin, 7though you know that I am not wicked, and there is no one to rescue from your hand?

Your hands shaped me

8"'Your hands shaped me and made me—altogether, on every side—and you have swallowed me up. 9Remember now that you made me like clay, and you will return me to dust. 10Did you not pour me out like milk and curdle me like cheese? 11With skin and flesh you clothed me, and with bones and sinews you wove me together [alt: you hedged me in]. 12You granted me life and faithful love, and your care kept watch over my spirit.

Hidden in your heart

13"'Yet these things you hid away in your heart; I know that this is in your mind: 14if I sin, you keep watch on me, and you will not hold me innocent of my guilt. 15If I am wicked, woe to me! And if I am righteous, I cannot lift my head, full of disgrace and seeing my affliction [alt: and see my affliction!]. 16And if my head rises, you hunt me like a fierce lion, and again you work wonders against me. 17You renew your witnesses against me and increase your resentment toward me; relays of troops are against me [alt: turn after turn of a soldier's service is mine].

Why did you bring me out of the womb?

18"'And why did you bring me out of the womb? I would have breathed my last, and no eye would have seen me. 19I would be as though I had not been; I would have been carried from the womb to the grave.'

20"Are my days not few? Let him cease; let him withdraw from me [alt: Cease, and withdraw from me, as the Hebrew is traditionally read aloud], so that I may be cheerful a little, 21before I go, and do not return, to a land of darkness and the shadow of death, 22a land of blackness like deep darkness, of the shadow of death and without order, and it shines like deep darkness."

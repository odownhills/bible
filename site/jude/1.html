Jude 1

TV mode Listen only

Greeting

1Jude, a slave of Jesus Christ and brother of James, to those who are called, who have been loved [alt: made holy, as other manuscripts read] in God the Father and kept for Jesus Christ [alt: kept by Jesus Christ]: 2May mercy and peace and love be multiplied to you.

Struggle for the faith

3Beloved, while I was making every effort to write to you concerning our shared salvation, I had to write to you, urging you to struggle for the faith that was handed over to the holy ones once for all. 4For certain people have sneaked in, those who long ago were written down beforehand for this judgment, godless people, who change the grace of our God into debauchery and deny our only Master and Lord, Jesus Christ [alt: the only Master, and our Lord Jesus Christ; or the only Master, God, and our Lord Jesus Christ, as other manuscripts read].

Reminders from the past

5And I wish to remind you, though you know all things once for all, that Jesus [alt: the Lord, as other manuscripts read], having saved a people from the land of Egypt, the second time [alt: afterward] destroyed those who did not believe, 6and the angels who did not keep their own domain [alt: their own beginning] but abandoned their own dwelling he has kept in everlasting bonds under gloom for the judgment of the great day, 7as Sodom and Gomorrah and the cities around them, which, in a way like these, committed sexual immorality and went away after different flesh, are set out as an example, undergoing the penalty of eternal fire.

Dreamers and slanderers

8Yet likewise these also, as they dream, defile flesh, reject lordship and slander glories [alt: blaspheme glorious ones]. 9But Michael the archangel, when, disputing with the devil, he was arguing concerning the body of Moses, did not dare to bring a judgment of slander against him, but said, "May the Lord rebuke you." 10But these slander whatever they do not know; and whatever they understand by nature, as the unreasoning animals do—in these things they are being ruined.

11Woe to them, because they have gone the way of Cain, and have poured themselves out into the error of Balaam [alt: the leading astray of Balaam] for wages, and have perished in the rebellion of Korah.

Waterless clouds

12These are the ones who are reefs [alt: stains] at your love feasts, feasting together without fear, shepherding themselves [alt: feasting together, shepherding themselves without fear]; waterless clouds carried along by winds; late-autumn trees, fruitless, having died twice, uprooted; 13wild waves of the sea, foaming up their own shameful deeds; wandering stars, for which the gloom of darkness has been kept forever. [In Greek "wandering" shares a root with "error" in verse 11.]

Enoch's prophecy

14And Enoch, the seventh from Adam, prophesied about these also [alt: to these also], saying, "Look, the Lord came with his holy myriads 15to do judgment against all and to expose every soul [alt: to convict every soul; or to expose all the godless, as other manuscripts read] concerning all their works of godlessness that they did godlessly, and concerning all the hard things that godless sinners spoke against him."

16These are grumblers, complaining about their lot, going on their way according to their own desires—and their mouth speaks inflated things—admiring faces [alt: showing favoritism to people] for the sake of benefit.

The words of the apostles

17But you yourselves, beloved, remember the words said beforehand by the apostles of our Lord Jesus Christ, 18that they were saying to you, "In the last time there will be mockers, going on their way according to their own desires of godlessness." 19These are the ones who make divisions, natural people, not having spirit [alt: not having the spirit]. [In Greek "natural" is formed from the word for "soul."]

Keep yourselves in the love of God

20But you yourselves, beloved, building yourselves up on your most holy faith, praying in holy spirit [alt: in the holy spirit], 21keep yourselves in the love of God, looking forward to the mercy of our Lord Jesus Christ into eternal life.

22And have mercy on some who are doubting [alt: who are disputing]; 23and save others, seizing them from the fire; and on others have mercy in fear [alt: have mercy on some, making a distinction, and save others in fear, seizing them from the fire, as other manuscripts read from verse 22 to here], hating even the tunic stained by the flesh.

To the only God

24And to the one who can guard you [alt: guard them, as other manuscripts read] from stumbling and make you stand in the presence of his glory without blemish, in exultation, 25to the only God, our savior, through Jesus Christ our Lord, be glory, greatness, might and authority before all time and now and for all time. Amen.

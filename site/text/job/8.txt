Job, chapter 8. And Bildad the Shuhite answered and said: "How long will you utter these things, and the sayings of your mouth be a mighty wind? Does God pervert justice, or does the Almighty pervert righteousness? "If your sons sinned against him, he sent them away into the power of their rebellion. If you yourself search for God and plead with the Almighty, if you are pure and upright, surely now he will rouse himself for you and restore your righteous home. And your beginning will be a small thing, and your latter days will grow very great. "For ask now of an earlier generation, and set your mind on what their fathers searched out. For we are of yesterday and do not know, for our days on earth are a shadow. Will they themselves not teach you, tell you, and bring out words from their heart? "Does papyrus grow tall where there is no marsh? Does reed grass grow without water? While it is still green and not picked, it withers sooner than any grass. So are the paths of all who forget God, and the hope of the godless man perishes, whose confidence breaks, and whose trust is a spider's house. He leans on his house, and it does not stand; he holds fast to it, and it does not stay up. "He is full of sap before the sun, and his shoots go out over his garden. His roots are entwined over a stone heap; he gazes on a house of stones. If he is swallowed up from his place, it denies him: 'I have not seen you.' Look, that is the gladness of his way, and from the dust others sprout. "Look, God will not reject a blameless man, and he will not hold the hand of evildoers, until he fills your mouth with laughter, and your lips with shouting. Those who hate you will be clothed with shame, and the tent of the wicked will be no more."

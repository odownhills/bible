Job, chapter 12. And Job answered and said: "Truly you are the people, and with you wisdom will die. I too have sense, as you do; I do not fall short of you; and who does not have such things as these? "I am one who is a laughingstock to his friend, one who called to God, and he answered him—a laughingstock, a righteous, blameless man! In the thought of one who is untroubled there is contempt for misfortune; it is ready for those whose foot slips. The tents of destroyers are at ease, and there is security for those who provoke God, for the one who brings a god in his hand. "But ask the beasts now, and they will teach you; and the birds of the sky, and they will tell you. Or speak to the earth, and it will teach you, and the fish of the sea will recount it to you. Who among all these does not know that the hand of Yahweh has done this? In his hand is the life of every living thing and the spirit of all human flesh. "Does not the ear test words, as the palate tastes food for itself? Among the aged is wisdom, and in length of days understanding. With him are wisdom and might; counsel and understanding are his. "Look, he tears down, and it is not rebuilt; he shuts a man in, and it is not opened. Look, he holds back the waters, and they dry up; and he sends them out, and they overturn the earth. With him are power and sound wisdom; the one who goes astray and the one who leads astray are his. "He leads counselors away stripped and drives judges mad. He undoes the discipline of kings and binds a loincloth around their waist. He leads priests away stripped and overthrows the long-established. He removes the speech of the trusted and takes away the discernment of the elders. He pours out contempt on nobles and loosens the belt of the strong. "He uncovers deep things out of darkness and brings the shadow of death out to the light. He makes nations grow and destroys them; he spreads nations out and leads them away. He removes the sense of the chiefs of the people of the earth and makes them wander in a waste with no way. They grope in darkness and without light, and he makes them wander like a drunk man.

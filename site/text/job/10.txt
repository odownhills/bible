Job, chapter 10. "My soul loathes my life; I will abandon myself to my complaint; I will speak in the bitterness of my soul. I will say to God, 'Do not condemn me; let me know why you contend against me. "'Is it good for you that you oppress, that you reject the toil of your hands, and shine on the counsel of the wicked? Do you have eyes of flesh, or do you see as a mortal sees? Are your days like the days of a mortal, or your years like the days of a man, that you look for my guilt and seek out my sin, though you know that I am not wicked, and there is no one to rescue from your hand? "'Your hands shaped me and made me—altogether, on every side—and you have swallowed me up. Remember now that you made me like clay, and you will return me to dust. Did you not pour me out like milk and curdle me like cheese? With skin and flesh you clothed me, and with bones and sinews you wove me together. You granted me life and faithful love, and your care kept watch over my spirit. "'Yet these things you hid away in your heart; I know that this is in your mind: if I sin, you keep watch on me, and you will not hold me innocent of my guilt. If I am wicked, woe to me! And if I am righteous, I cannot lift my head, full of disgrace and seeing my affliction. And if my head rises, you hunt me like a fierce lion, and again you work wonders against me. You renew your witnesses against me and increase your resentment toward me; relays of troops are against me. "'And why did you bring me out of the womb? I would have breathed my last, and no eye would have seen me. I would be as though I had not been; I would have been carried from the womb to the grave.' "Are my days not few? Let him cease; let him withdraw from me, so that I may be cheerful a little, before I go, and do not return, to a land of darkness and the shadow of death, a land of blackness like deep darkness, of the shadow of death and without order, and it shines like deep darkness."

Job, chapter 4. And Eliphaz the Temanite answered and said: "If someone ventures a word with you, will you be weary? But who can hold back words? Look, you have instructed many, and you have strengthened weak hands. Your words have raised up the one who was stumbling, and you have made buckling knees firm. But now it comes to you, and you are weary; it touches you, and you are dismayed. Is not your fear of God your confidence, and your hope the blamelessness of your ways? "Remember now: who, being innocent, has perished? And where have the upright been wiped out? As I have seen, those who plow harm and sow misery harvest it. By the breath of God they perish, and by the wind of his anger they come to an end. The roar of the lion and the voice of the fierce lion, and the teeth of the young lions, are broken. The strong lion perishes for lack of prey, and the cubs of the lioness are scattered. "Now to me a word was brought by stealth, and my ear took in a whisper of it. In anxious thoughts from visions of the night, when deep sleep falls on people, dread came over me, and trembling, and it filled all my bones with dread. And a spirit passed before my face; the hair of my flesh bristled. It stood still, and I did not recognize its appearance. A form was before my eyes; I heard a hush, and a voice: "'Can a mortal be righteous before God, or a man be clean before his maker? Look, in his servants he puts no trust, and his angels he charges with error; how much more those who live in houses of clay, whose foundation is in the dust, who are crushed sooner than a moth! From morning to evening they are shattered; without anyone considering it, they perish forever. Is not their tent cord pulled up within them? They die, and without wisdom.'

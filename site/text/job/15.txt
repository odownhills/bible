Job, chapter 15. And Eliphaz the Temanite answered and said: "Does a wise man answer with windy knowledge and fill his belly with the east wind, arguing with talk that is of no use, and with words by which he gains nothing? You yourself even do away with fear of God, and you take away meditation before God. For your guilt teaches your mouth, and you choose the tongue of the shrewd. Your mouth condemns you, and not I, and your lips testify against you. "Were you born the first human being, and were you brought to birth before the hills? Do you listen in the council of God and take wisdom away for yourself? What do you know that we do not know? What do you discern that is not with us? Both the gray-haired and the aged are among us, greater in days than your father. "Are the comforts of God too little for you, and a word that is gentle with you? Why does your heart carry you away, and why do your eyes flash, that you turn your spirit against God and bring out words from your mouth? "What is a mortal, that he should be pure, and that one born of a woman should be righteous? Look, in his holy ones he puts no trust, and the heavens are not pure in his eyes; how much less one who is detestable and corrupt, a man who drinks injustice like water! "I will declare it to you; hear me; and what I have gazed on I will recount, what the wise tell from their fathers and have not concealed— to them alone the land was given, and no stranger passed among them. "All his days the wicked man writhes, through the number of years hidden away for the ruthless. Sounds of dread are in his ears; in a time of peace the destroyer comes upon him. He does not believe that he will return from darkness, and he is watched for by the sword. He wanders about for bread: 'Where is it?' He knows that a day of darkness is ready at his hand. Distress and anguish terrify him; they overpower him like a king ready for the attack. "For he has stretched out his hand against God, and against the Almighty he acts the mighty man. He runs at him with a stiff neck, with the thick bosses of his shields. For he has covered his face with his fat and put on fat over his flanks, and he has lived in cities that were wiped out, in houses that no one lives in, which were ready to become stone heaps. "He will not be rich, and his wealth will not last, and their gain will not stretch out over the land. He will not depart from darkness; a flame will dry up his shoots, and he will depart by the wind of his mouth. Let him not put trust in emptiness, led astray as he is, for emptiness will be what he gets in exchange. Before his time it will be paid in full, and his frond will not be green. Like a vine he will tear off his unripe grapes, and like an olive tree he will throw off his blossom. "For the company of the godless is barren, and fire devours the tents of bribery. They conceive misery and give birth to harm, and their womb prepares deceit."

Job, chapter 19. And Job answered and said: "How long will you torment my soul and crush me with words? Ten times now you have put me to shame; you are not ashamed to deal harshly with me. And even if I have truly gone astray, my error stays with me. If you truly make yourselves great against me and argue the insult I bear against me, know then that God has wronged me and has closed his hunting net around me. "Look, I cry out, 'Violence!' and I am not answered; I cry for help, and there is no justice. He has walled up my path, and I cannot pass; and on my pathways he sets darkness. He has stripped my glory from me and removed the crown from my head. He breaks me down on every side, and I am gone; and he pulls up my hope like a tree. He makes his anger burn against me and counts me as one of his foes. His troops come together; they build up their way against me and camp on every side of my tent. "He has put my brothers far away from me, and those who know me have become nothing but strangers to me. Those near to me have fallen away, and those I know well have forgotten me. Those who stay in my house and my female servants count me as a stranger; I have become a foreigner in their eyes. I call to my servant, and he does not answer; with my mouth I plead with him. My breath is strange to my wife, and I am repulsive to the sons of my mother's womb. Even young children reject me; I rise, and they speak against me. All the men of my inner circle detest me, and those I love have turned against me. "My bone clings to my skin and to my flesh, and I have escaped with the skin of my teeth. Have pity on me, have pity on me, you my friends, for the hand of God has touched me. Why do you pursue me as God does, and do not have your fill of my flesh? "If only, then, my words were written! If only they were inscribed in a book, cut into the rock forever with an iron pen and lead! And I myself know that my redeemer lives, and at the last he will rise on the dust. And after this skin of mine has been struck off, from my flesh I will gaze on God, whom I myself will gaze on for myself, and my eyes will see, and not a stranger. My kidneys fail within me. "If you say, 'How will we pursue him?' and that the root of the matter is found in me, be afraid of the sword yourselves, for wrath brings the punishments of the sword, so that you may know that there is a judgment."

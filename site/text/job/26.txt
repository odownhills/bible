Job, chapter 26. And Job answered and said: "How you have helped the one without strength! How you have saved the arm without power! How you have counseled the one without wisdom, and made sound wisdom known in abundance! To whom have you told words, and whose breath has come out of you? "The dead writhe beneath the waters and those who live in them. Sheol is naked before him, and there is no covering for Abaddon. He stretches out the north over the waste; he hangs the earth on nothing. He wraps up water in his thick clouds, and the cloud does not burst under it. He closes off the face of the throne, spreading his cloud over it. He has drawn a circle as a boundary on the face of the waters, as far as the limit of light with darkness. "The pillars of the heavens quake and are astonished at his rebuke. By his strength he stirred up the sea, and by his understanding he wounded Rahab. By his wind the heavens are fair; his hand pierced the fleeing snake. Look, these are the edges of his way, and what a whisper of a word is heard of him! But the thunder of his might—who can understand it?"

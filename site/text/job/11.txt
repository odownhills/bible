Job, chapter 11. And Zophar the Naamathite answered and said: "Is a multitude of words to go unanswered, or is a man full of talk to be righteous? Are men to be silent at your idle talk? You mock, and there is no one to put you to shame; and you say, 'My teaching is pure, and I am clean in your eyes.' "But if only God would speak and open his lips with you, and tell you the secrets of wisdom! For sound wisdom has two sides. And know that God lets some of your guilt be forgotten for you. "Can you find what is to be searched out in God, or find your way to the limit of the Almighty? It is as high as the heavens—what can you do? It is deeper than Sheol—what can you know? Its measure is longer than the earth and broader than the sea. "If he sweeps on and shuts someone in and calls an assembly, who can turn him back? For he himself knows empty men; he sees harm, and will he not look closely at it? And a hollow man will get sense when a wild donkey's colt is born a human being. "If you yourself set your heart right and spread out your palm to him— if harm is in your hand, put it far away, and do not let injustice live in your tents— surely then you will lift your face without blemish; you will be solid and will not be afraid. "For you yourself will forget misery; you will remember it like water that has passed away. And life will rise brighter than noon; though it grows black, it will be like the morning. And you will trust, because there is hope; you will look around and lie down in security. You will bed down, and there will be no one to make you tremble, and many will seek your favor. "But the eyes of the wicked will fail; refuge is lost to them, and their hope is the breathing out of life."

Job, chapter 23. And Job answered and said: "Even today my complaint is defiance; my hand is heavy on my sighing. If only I knew where to find him, that I might come right to his seat! I would set out my case before him, and fill my mouth with arguments. I would know the words with which he would answer me, and understand what he would say to me. Is it with great strength that he would contend with me? No; surely he himself would consider me. There an upright man would be arguing with him, and I would go free from my judge forever. "Look, I go east, and he is not there; and west, and I do not discern him; north, when he is at work, and I do not gaze on him; he wraps himself in the south, and I do not see him. "For he knows the way I take; when he has tested me, I will come out like gold. My foot has held to his footsteps; I have kept to his way and have not turned aside. The commandment of his lips—I have not departed from it; I have hidden away the words of his mouth more than what is allotted to me. "But he is one, and who can turn him back? What his soul desires, he does. For he will carry out what is allotted to me, and many such things are in his mind. "That is why I am dismayed before him; I look closely, and I am in dread of him. God has made my heart faint, and the Almighty has dismayed me. For I was not brought to an end before the darkness, and he did not cover the deep darkness from my face.

Job, chapter 25. And Bildad the Shuhite answered and said: "Dominion and dread are with him; he makes peace in his heights. Is there any number to his troops? And on whom does his light not rise? "And how can a mortal be righteous with God, and how can one born of a woman be pure? Look, even the moon does not shine, and the stars are not pure in his eyes; how much less a mortal, who is a maggot, and a human being, who is a worm!"

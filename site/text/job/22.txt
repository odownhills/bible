Job, chapter 22. And Eliphaz the Temanite answered and said: "Is it to God that a man can be of use? Rather, one who has insight is of use to himself. Does the Almighty care that you are righteous, or is it profit to him that you make your ways blameless? Is it for your fear of God that he corrects you, that he comes into judgment with you? Is not your evil great, and your guilty deeds without end? "For you have taken pledges from your brothers for nothing, and stripped the naked of their garments. You have given the exhausted no water to drink, and from the hungry you have kept back bread. But the man with a strong arm—the land was his, and the favored man lived in it. You have sent widows away empty-handed, and the arms of orphans have been crushed. "That is why traps are all around you, and dread suddenly dismays you, or darkness, so that you cannot see, and an abundance of water covers you. "Is not God as high as the heavens? And see the highest stars, how high they are! And you say, 'What does God know? Can he judge through thick darkness? Clouds are a hiding place for him, and he does not see, and he walks about on the circle of the heavens.' "Will you keep to the path of old that men of harm have trodden, who were seized before their time, on whose foundation a river was poured out? They said to God, 'Depart from us,' and asked what the Almighty could do for them. And he himself filled their houses with good; and the counsel of the wicked is far from me. "The righteous see it and rejoice, and the innocent man mocks at them: 'Surely those who rose against us have been wiped out, and what they had left, fire has devoured.' "Get to know him now, and be at peace; by these things good will come to you. Take teaching from his mouth now, and put his words in your heart. "If you return to the Almighty, you will be built up—if you put injustice far away from your tent. And lay gold ore on the dust, and the gold of Ophir among the rocks of the torrents, and the Almighty will be your gold ore, and heaps of silver for you. "For then you will take delight in the Almighty, and lift your face to God. You will plead with him, and he will hear you, and you will pay your vows. You will decree a thing, and it will stand for you, and light will shine on your ways. "When people are brought low and you say, 'Rise up,' he will save the one with downcast eyes. He will deliver one who is not innocent, and he will be delivered by the cleanness of your palms."

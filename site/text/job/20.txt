Job, chapter 20. And Zophar the Naamathite answered and said: "Therefore my anxious thoughts reply to me, and this is because of my haste within me. I hear discipline that puts me to shame, and a spirit from my understanding answers me. "Do you know this—from of old, from when humankind was set on earth— that the cry of joy of the wicked is short, and the joy of the godless man is for a moment? Though his height goes up to the heavens and his head reaches the clouds, he will perish forever like his dung; those who saw him will say, 'Where is he?' Like a dream he will fly away and not be found, and he will be chased away like a vision of the night. An eye has glimpsed him and will not do so again, and his place will no longer catch sight of him. "His sons will appease the poor, and his hands will give back his riches. His bones are full of his youth, and it will lie down with him on the dust. "Though evil is sweet in his mouth, though he conceals it under his tongue, spares it and does not abandon it, and keeps it back within his palate, his bread turns in his stomach; the gall of cobras is within him. He has swallowed wealth, and he will vomit it up; God will turn it out of his belly. He will suck the venom of cobras; a viper's tongue will kill him. "Let him not look on the streams, the rivers, the torrents of honey and curds. He gives back what he toiled for and will not swallow it, as much as the wealth he got in exchange, and he will have no enjoyment. For he has crushed, he has abandoned the poor; he has seized a house, and he will not build it up. For he has known no ease in his belly; he will not escape with what he desires. There is no survivor from his eating; that is why his good will not endure. When his plenty is full, he will be in distress; the hand of everyone in misery will come upon him. "When his belly is to be filled, he will send the burning of his anger on him and rain it on him as his food. He will flee from an iron weapon; a bronze bow will pierce him. He draws it out, and it comes out of his back, and the flashing point comes from his gall; terrors are upon him. All darkness is hidden for what he has hidden away; a fire that no one blew on will devour him; it will go ill with the survivor in his tent. The heavens will uncover his guilt, and the earth will rise up against him. The produce of his house will go into exile, poured away in the day of his anger. "This is the portion of a wicked human being from God, and the inheritance decreed for him by God."

Genesis, chapter 49. And Jacob called his sons and said, "Gather together, and let me tell you what will happen to you in the latter days. Come together and listen, sons of Jacob, and listen to Israel your father. "Reuben, you are my firstborn, my strength and the beginning of my vigor, excelling in dignity and excelling in power. Reckless as water, you will not excel, for you went up to your father's bed; then you desecrated it. He went up to my couch! "Simeon and Levi are brothers; weapons of violence are their swords. Into their council may my soul not come; with their assembly may my glory not be united. For in their anger they killed a man, and at their pleasure they hamstrung an ox. Cursed is their anger, for it is fierce, and their fury, for it is harsh. I will divide them in Jacob and scatter them in Israel. "Judah, it is you your brothers will praise. Your hand will be on the back of your enemies' necks; your father's sons will bow down to you. Judah is a lion's cub. From the prey, my son, you have gone up. He crouches, he lies down like a lion, and like a lioness; who will rouse him? The scepter will not depart from Judah, nor the ruler's staff from between his feet, until Shiloh comes, and the obedience of peoples will be his. Tying his colt to the vine, and his female donkey's foal to the choice vine, he washes his clothing in wine, and his robe in the blood of grapes. His eyes are dark from wine, and his teeth white from milk. "Zebulun will live by the shore of the seas, and he will be by the shore of ships, and his far side will be by Sidon. "Issachar is a strong-boned donkey, lying down between the sheepfolds. And he saw that rest was good, and that the land was pleasant, and he bent his shoulder to bear a burden, and he became a forced laborer. "Dan will judge his people as one of the tribes of Israel. May Dan be a snake by the road, an adder by the path, that bites a horse's heels, so that its rider falls backward. For your salvation I hope, Yahweh. As for Gad, raiders will raid him, but he will raid at their heels. "From Asher, his bread is rich, and he will give a king's delicacies. "Naphtali is a doe let loose; he gives beautiful words. "Joseph is a fruitful bough, a fruitful bough by a spring; its branches climb over a wall. And the archers dealt bitterly with him and shot at him and bore a grudge against him. But his bow stayed firm, and the arms of his hands were nimble, by the hands of the Mighty One of Jacob, from there, the Shepherd, the Stone of Israel, from the God of your father, and may he help you, and with the Almighty, and may he bless you with blessings of the sky above, blessings of the deep that lies below, blessings of breasts and womb. The blessings of your father have prevailed over the blessings of my parents, as far as the delight of the everlasting hills. May they be on the head of Joseph, and on the crown of the head of the one set apart from his brothers. "Benjamin is a wolf that tears. In the morning he eats prey, and at evening he divides spoil." All these are the twelve tribes of Israel, and this is what their father spoke to them. And he blessed them; each according to his own blessing he blessed them. And he commanded them and said to them, "I am going to be gathered to my people. Bury me with my fathers, in the cave that is in the field of Ephron the Hittite, in the cave that is in the field of Machpelah, which is facing Mamre, in the land of Canaan, the field that Abraham bought from Ephron the Hittite as a holding for a grave. There they buried Abraham and Sarah his wife; there they buried Isaac and Rebekah his wife; and there I buried Leah. The purchase of the field and of the cave that is in it was from the sons of Heth." And Jacob finished commanding his sons, and he gathered his feet up onto the bed, and he breathed his last and was gathered to his people.

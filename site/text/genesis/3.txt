Genesis, chapter 3. Now the snake was more shrewd than any wild animal of the field that Yahweh God had made. And he said to the woman, "Did God really say, 'You must not eat from any tree of the garden'?" And the woman said to the snake, "From the fruit of the trees of the garden we may eat, but from the fruit of the tree that is in the middle of the garden, God said, 'You must not eat from it, and you must not touch it, so that you do not die.'" And the snake said to the woman, "You will certainly not die, for God knows that on the day you eat from it your eyes will be opened, and you will be like God, knowing good and bad." And the woman saw that the tree was good for food, and that it was a delight to the eyes, and that the tree was desirable for gaining insight. And she took some of its fruit and ate, and she also gave some to her husband, who was with her, and he ate. And the eyes of the two of them were opened, and they knew that they were naked. And they sewed fig leaves together and made loincloths for themselves. And they heard the sound of Yahweh God walking about in the garden in the breeze of the day, and the human and his wife hid from the presence of Yahweh God among the trees of the garden. And Yahweh God called to the human and said to him, "Where are you?" And he said, "I heard the sound of you in the garden, and I was afraid because I was naked, and I hid." And he said, "Who told you that you are naked? Have you eaten from the tree that I commanded you not to eat from?" And the human said, "The woman you gave to be with me—she gave me some from the tree, and I ate." And Yahweh God said to the woman, "What is this you have done?" And the woman said, "The snake deceived me, and I ate." And Yahweh God said to the snake, "Because you have done this, cursed are you more than all the livestock and more than any wild animal of the field. On your belly you will go, and dust you will eat all the days of your life. And I will put hostility between you and the woman, and between your offspring and her offspring. He will strike your head, and you will strike his heel." To the woman he said, "I will greatly multiply your painful toil and your pregnancies; in pain you will bear children. And your longing will be for your husband, and he will govern you." And to Adam he said, "Because you listened to the voice of your wife and ate from the tree about which I commanded you, saying, 'You must not eat from it,' cursed is the ground because of you. In painful toil you will eat from it all the days of your life. And thorns and thistles it will sprout for you, and you will eat the plants of the field. By the sweat of your face you will eat bread until you return to the ground, because from it you were taken; for you are dust, and to dust you will return." And the human named his wife Eve, because she was the mother of all the living. And Yahweh God made tunics of skin for Adam and for his wife, and he clothed them. And Yahweh God said, "Look, the human has become like one of us, knowing good and bad. And now, so that he does not reach out his hand and take also from the tree of life, and eat, and live forever—" So Yahweh God sent him out of the garden of Eden to work the ground from which he had been taken. And he drove the human out, and east of the garden of Eden he stationed the cherubim and the flame of the whirling sword, to guard the way to the tree of life.

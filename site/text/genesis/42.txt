Genesis, chapter 42. And Jacob saw that there was grain for sale in Egypt, and Jacob said to his sons, "Why do you look at one another?" And he said, "Look, I have heard that there is grain for sale in Egypt. Go down there and buy grain for us from there, so that we may live and not die." And ten of Joseph's brothers went down to buy grain from Egypt. But Benjamin, Joseph's brother, Jacob did not send with his brothers, for he said, "Harm might come to him." And the sons of Israel came to buy grain among those who came, for the famine was in the land of Canaan. Now Joseph was the one who held power over the land; he was the one who sold grain to all the people of the land. And Joseph's brothers came and bowed down to him with their faces to the ground. And Joseph saw his brothers and recognized them, but he acted as a stranger toward them and spoke harshly with them. And he said to them, "From where have you come?" And they said, "From the land of Canaan, to buy food." And Joseph recognized his brothers, but they did not recognize him. And Joseph remembered the dreams that he had dreamed about them, and he said to them, "You are spies; to see the nakedness of the land you have come." And they said to him, "No, my lord, but your servants have come to buy food. All of us are sons of one man. We are honest; your servants have not been spies." And he said to them, "No, but it is the nakedness of the land that you have come to see." And they said, "We, your servants, are twelve brothers, the sons of one man in the land of Canaan, and look, the youngest is with our father today, and one is gone." And Joseph said to them, "It is what I spoke to you, saying, 'You are spies.' By this you will be tested: by the life of Pharaoh, you will not go out from here unless your youngest brother comes here. Send one of you, and let him get your brother, and as for you, you are to be confined, and let your words be tested, whether there is truth with you. And if not, by the life of Pharaoh, you are surely spies." And he gathered them into custody for three days. And Joseph said to them on the third day, "Do this and live; it is God I fear. If you are honest, let one of your brothers be confined in the house where you are in custody, and as for you, go, bring grain for the hunger of your households. And your youngest brother you are to bring to me, so that your words may be confirmed, and you will not die." And they did so. And they said to one another, "Indeed, we are guilty over our brother, whose distress of soul we saw when he pleaded with us, and we did not listen. That is why this distress has come to us." And Reuben answered them, saying, "Did I not say to you, saying, 'Do not sin against the child'? But you did not listen. And his blood too, look, is being demanded." And they did not know that Joseph understood, for the interpreter was between them. And he turned away from them and wept. And he returned to them and spoke to them, and he took Simeon from them and bound him before their eyes. And Joseph commanded that their containers be filled with grain, and that their silver be returned, each man's to his sack, and that provisions be given to them for the way. And so it was done for them. And they loaded the grain they had bought on their donkeys and went from there. And one of them opened his sack to give fodder to his donkey at the lodging place, and he saw his silver, and look, it was in the mouth of his bag. And he said to his brothers, "My silver has been returned, and yes, look, it is in my bag!" And their hearts sank, and they turned to one another trembling, saying, "What is this that God has done to us?" And they came to Jacob their father, to the land of Canaan, and they told him all that had happened to them, saying, "The man, the lord of the land, spoke harshly with us and treated us as men spying on the land. And we said to him, 'We are honest; we have not been spies. We are twelve brothers, the sons of our father. One is gone, and the youngest is with our father today in the land of Canaan.' And the man, the lord of the land, said to us, 'By this I will know that you are honest: leave one of your brothers with me, and take grain for the hunger of your households, and go. And bring your youngest brother to me, so that I may know that you are not spies, that you are honest. Your brother I will give to you, and you may trade in the land.'" And as they were emptying their sacks, look, each man's bundle of silver was in his sack. And they saw their bundles of silver, they and their father, and they were afraid. And Jacob their father said to them, "It is me you have bereaved. Joseph is gone, and Simeon is gone, and Benjamin you would take. It is on me that all these things have come." And Reuben said to his father, saying, "My two sons you may put to death if I do not bring him to you. Put him in my care, and I myself will bring him back to you." And he said, "My son will not go down with you, for his brother is dead, and he alone is left. And if harm comes to him on the way that you go, you will bring down my gray hair in sorrow to Sheol."

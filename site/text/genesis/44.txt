Genesis, chapter 44. And he commanded the one who was over his house, saying, "Fill the men's bags with food, as much as they are able to carry, and put each man's silver in the mouth of his bag. And my goblet, the silver goblet, you are to put in the mouth of the bag of the youngest, and the silver for his grain." And he did according to the word that Joseph had spoken. The morning was light, and the men were sent on their way, they and their donkeys. They had gone out of the city; they had not gone far when Joseph said to the one who was over his house, "Get up, pursue the men, and when you overtake them, say to them, 'Why have you repaid evil for good? Is this not what my master drinks from, and with it he certainly learns by divination? You have done evil in what you have done.'" And he overtook them and spoke these words to them. And they said to him, "Why does my lord speak words like these? Far be it from your servants to do such a thing! Look, the silver that we found in the mouth of our bags we brought back to you from the land of Canaan. And how could we steal silver or gold from your master's house? The one of your servants with whom it is found will die, and we too will become slaves to my lord." And he said, "Now too, as your words are, so let it be: the one with whom it is found will be my slave, and as for you, you will be innocent." And they hurried, and each man let down his bag to the ground, and each man opened his bag. And he searched: with the eldest he began, and with the youngest he finished. And the goblet was found in Benjamin's bag. And they tore their clothes, and each man loaded his donkey, and they returned to the city. And Judah and his brothers came to Joseph's house, and he was still there, and they fell before him to the ground. And Joseph said to them, "What is this deed that you have done? Did you not know that a man like me certainly learns by divination?" And Judah said, "What can we say to my lord? What can we speak? And how can we show ourselves righteous? God has found the guilt of your servants. Look, we are slaves to my lord, both we and the one in whose hand the goblet was found." And he said, "Far be it from me to do this! The man in whose hand the goblet was found, he will be my slave, and as for you, go up in peace to your father." And Judah approached him and said, "Pardon, my lord. Please let your servant speak a word in the ears of my lord, and do not let your anger burn against your servant, for you are like Pharaoh. My lord asked his servants, saying, 'Do you have a father or a brother?' And we said to my lord, 'We have an old father, and a child of his old age, a young one, and his brother is dead, and he alone is left of his mother, and his father loves him.' And you said to your servants, 'Bring him down to me, so that I may set my eye on him.' And we said to my lord, 'The boy cannot leave his father; if he left his father, he would die.' And you said to your servants, 'If your youngest brother does not come down with you, you will not see my face again.' And when we went up to your servant my father, we told him the words of my lord. And our father said, 'Return, buy us a little food.' And we said, 'We cannot go down. If our youngest brother is with us, then we will go down, for we cannot see the man's face when our youngest brother is not with us.' And your servant my father said to us, 'You yourselves know that my wife bore me two sons. And the one went out from me, and I said, "Surely he has certainly been torn to pieces," and I have not seen him since. And if you take this one also from my presence and harm comes to him, you will bring down my gray hair in misery to Sheol.' And now, when I come to your servant my father and the boy is not with us—and his soul is bound up with the boy's soul— then when he sees that the boy is not there, he will die, and your servants will bring down the gray hair of your servant our father in sorrow to Sheol. For your servant became guarantor for the boy to my father, saying, 'If I do not bring him to you, then I will have sinned against my father for all time.' And now, please let your servant stay in place of the boy as a slave to my lord, and let the boy go up with his brothers. For how can I go up to my father when the boy is not with me? Let me not look on the evil that would find my father."

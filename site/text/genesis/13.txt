Genesis, chapter 13. And Abram went up from Egypt, he and his wife and all that he had, and Lot with him, to the Negev. Now Abram was very rich in herds, in silver and in gold. And he went by his stages from the Negev as far as Bethel, as far as the place where his tent had been at the beginning, between Bethel and Ai, to the place of the altar that he had made there at the first, and there Abram called on the name of Yahweh. And Lot too, who went with Abram, had flocks and cattle and tents. And the land did not support them living together, for their possessions were many, and they were not able to live together. And there was a quarrel between the shepherds of Abram's herds and the shepherds of Lot's herds. Now the Canaanites and the Perizzites were then living in the land. And Abram said to Lot, "Please, let there be no quarreling between me and you, or between my shepherds and your shepherds, for we are men who are brothers. Is not the whole land before you? Please separate from me. If to the left, I will go to the right, and if to the right, I will go to the left." And Lot lifted his eyes and saw the whole plain of the Jordan, that all of it was well watered (before Yahweh ruined Sodom and Gomorrah), like the garden of Yahweh, like the land of Egypt, as you go toward Zoar. And Lot chose for himself the whole plain of the Jordan, and Lot traveled eastward, and they separated, each from his brother. Abram lived in the land of Canaan, and Lot lived in the cities of the plain and moved his tent as far as Sodom. Now the men of Sodom were evil and sinners against Yahweh, very much so. And Yahweh said to Abram, after Lot had separated from him, "Lift your eyes now and look from the place where you are, northward and southward and eastward and westward, for the whole land that you see, to you I will give it, and to your offspring, forever. And I will make your offspring like the dust of the earth, so that if anyone is able to count the dust of the earth, your offspring too will be counted. Rise, walk about in the land, through its length and its width, for to you I will give it." And Abram moved his tent and came and lived among the oaks of Mamre, which are in Hebron, and he built an altar there to Yahweh.

Genesis, chapter 9. And God blessed Noah and his sons, and he said to them, "Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the earth. And the fear of you and the terror of you will be on every wild animal of the earth and on every bird of the sky; with everything that creeps on the ground and with all the fish of the sea, into your hand they are given. Every moving thing that is alive will be yours for food; like the green plants, I give you everything. "Only flesh with its life, its blood, you must not eat. And surely the blood of your lives I will demand; from the hand of every living thing I will demand it, and from the hand of humankind, from the hand of each one's brother, I will demand the life of humankind. Whoever sheds the blood of humankind, by humankind his blood will be shed, for in the image of God he made humankind. And as for you, be fruitful and multiply; swarm on the earth and multiply on it." And God said to Noah and to his sons with him, saying, "And as for me, look, I am about to establish my covenant with you and with your offspring after you, and with every living creature that is with you—the birds, the livestock, and every wild animal of the earth with you—of all that went out of the ark, to every living thing of the earth. And I will establish my covenant with you, and never again will all flesh be cut off by the waters of the flood, and never again will there be a flood to ruin the earth." And God said, "This is the sign of the covenant that I am setting between me and you and every living creature that is with you, for everlasting generations: my bow I have set in the cloud, and it will serve as a sign of a covenant between me and the earth. And when I bring cloud over the earth and the bow is seen in the cloud, I will remember my covenant that is between me and you and every living creature among all flesh, and never again will the waters become a flood to ruin all flesh. And the bow will be in the cloud, and I will see it, to remember an everlasting covenant between God and every living creature among all flesh that is on the earth." And God said to Noah, "This is the sign of the covenant that I have established between me and all flesh that is on the earth." And the sons of Noah who went out of the ark were Shem and Ham and Japheth. Now Ham was the father of Canaan. These three were the sons of Noah, and from these the people of the whole earth scattered. And Noah, the man of the ground, made a beginning and planted a vineyard. And he drank some of the wine and became drunk, and he uncovered himself inside his tent. And Ham, the father of Canaan, saw the nakedness of his father, and he told his two brothers outside. And Shem and Japheth took the cloak and put it on the shoulders of the two of them, and they walked backward and covered the nakedness of their father, and their faces were turned backward, and the nakedness of their father they did not see. And Noah awoke from his wine, and he knew what his youngest son had done to him. And he said, "Cursed is Canaan. A slave of slaves he will be to his brothers." And he said, "Blessed is Yahweh, the God of Shem, and may Canaan be a slave to him. May God enlarge Japheth, and may he live in the tents of Shem, and may Canaan be a slave to him." And Noah lived three hundred and fifty years after the flood. And all the days of Noah were nine hundred and fifty years, and he died.

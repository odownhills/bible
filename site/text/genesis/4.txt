Genesis, chapter 4. Now the human knew his wife Eve, and she conceived and gave birth to Cain. And she said, "I have acquired a man with Yahweh." And she gave birth again, to his brother Abel. And Abel became a shepherd of flocks, and Cain became a worker of the ground. And after some time, Cain brought some of the fruit of the ground as an offering to Yahweh. And Abel too brought some of the firstborn of his flock and some of their fat. And Yahweh looked at Abel and at his offering, but at Cain and at his offering he did not look. And Cain was very angry, and his face fell. And Yahweh said to Cain, "Why are you angry, and why has your face fallen? If you do good, will there not be a lifting up? But if you do not do good, sin is crouching at the entrance, and its longing is for you, but you must govern it." And Cain spoke to his brother Abel. And when they were in the field, Cain rose up against his brother Abel and killed him. And Yahweh said to Cain, "Where is your brother Abel?" And he said, "I do not know. Am I my brother's guardian?" And he said, "What have you done? The voice of your brother's blood is crying out to me from the ground. And now, cursed are you from the ground, which has opened its mouth to take your brother's blood from your hand. When you work the ground, it will no longer give you its strength. A drifter and a wanderer you will be on the earth." And Cain said to Yahweh, "My punishment is too great to bear. Look, you have driven me out today from the face of the ground, and from your face I will be hidden. And I will be a drifter and a wanderer on the earth, and anyone who finds me will kill me." And Yahweh said to him, "Therefore, if anyone kills Cain, vengeance will be taken seven times over." And Yahweh set a sign for Cain, so that anyone who found him would not strike him down. And Cain went out from the presence of Yahweh and lived in the land of Nod, east of Eden. And Cain knew his wife, and she conceived and gave birth to Enoch. And he became the builder of a city, and he named the city after his son, Enoch. And Irad was born to Enoch, and Irad fathered Mehujael, and Mehujael fathered Methushael, and Methushael fathered Lamech. And Lamech took two wives for himself. The name of the first was Adah, and the name of the second was Zillah. And Adah gave birth to Jabal. He was the father of those who live in tents and among herds. And his brother's name was Jubal. He was the father of all who handle the lyre and the pipe. And Zillah, she too gave birth to Tubal-cain, who forged every cutting tool of bronze and iron. And Tubal-cain's sister was Naamah. And Lamech said to his wives, "Adah and Zillah, hear my voice; wives of Lamech, listen closely to what I say. For I have killed a man for my wound, and a boy for my bruise. If Cain is avenged seven times over, then Lamech seventy-seven times." And Adam knew his wife again, and she gave birth to a son and named him Seth, "because God has set another offspring for me in place of Abel, since Cain killed him." And to Seth also a son was born, and he named him Enosh. At that time, calling on the name of Yahweh began.

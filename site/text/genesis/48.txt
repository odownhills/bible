Genesis, chapter 48. And after these things it was said to Joseph, "Look, your father is sick." And he took his two sons with him, Manasseh and Ephraim. And it was told to Jacob, saying, "Look, your son Joseph has come to you." And Israel gathered his strength and sat up on the bed. And Jacob said to Joseph, "God Almighty appeared to me at Luz in the land of Canaan, and he blessed me. And he said to me, 'Look, I am about to make you fruitful, and I will multiply you, and I will make you into an assembly of peoples, and I will give this land to your offspring after you as an everlasting holding.' And now, your two sons who were born to you in the land of Egypt before I came to you in Egypt, they are mine. Ephraim and Manasseh will be mine, like Reuben and Simeon. And those born to you, whom you have fathered after them, will be yours. They will be called by the name of their brothers in their inheritance. And as for me, when I came from Paddan, Rachel died on me in the land of Canaan, on the way, when there was still a stretch of land to go to Ephrath, and I buried her there on the way to Ephrath (that is, Bethlehem)." And Israel saw Joseph's sons, and he said, "Who are these?" And Joseph said to his father, "They are my sons, whom God has given me here." And he said, "Please bring them to me, and let me bless them." Now Israel's eyes were heavy from old age; he could not see. And he brought them close to him, and he kissed them and embraced them. And Israel said to Joseph, "I did not think I would see your face, and look, God has let me see your offspring as well." And Joseph brought them out from beside his knees, and he bowed down with his face to the ground. And Joseph took the two of them, Ephraim in his right hand, on Israel's left, and Manasseh in his left hand, on Israel's right, and he brought them close to him. And Israel reached out his right hand and laid it on the head of Ephraim, who was the younger, and his left hand on the head of Manasseh; he guided his hands with insight, for Manasseh was the firstborn. And he blessed Joseph and said, "The God before whom my fathers Abraham and Isaac walked, the God who has shepherded me all my life until this day, the angel who has redeemed me from all evil, may he bless the boys. And may my name be called in them, and the name of my fathers Abraham and Isaac, and may they teem into a multitude in the midst of the land." And Joseph saw that his father was laying his right hand on the head of Ephraim, and it was bad in his eyes, and he grasped his father's hand to remove it from the head of Ephraim to the head of Manasseh. And Joseph said to his father, "Not so, my father, for this one is the firstborn. Put your right hand on his head." And his father refused and said, "I know, my son, I know. He too will become a people, and he too will be great. But his younger brother will be greater than he, and his offspring will be the fullness of the nations." And he blessed them on that day, saying, "By you Israel will bless, saying, 'May God make you like Ephraim and like Manasseh.'" And he put Ephraim before Manasseh. And Israel said to Joseph, "Look, I am about to die, and God will be with you and will bring you back to the land of your fathers. And as for me, I give you one shoulder of land more than your brothers, which I took from the hand of the Amorites with my sword and with my bow."

Genesis, chapter 46. And Israel set out with all that he had, and he came to Beersheba, and he offered sacrifices to the God of his father Isaac. And God said to Israel in visions of the night, and he said, "Jacob, Jacob!" And he said, "Here I am." And he said, "I am God, the God of your father. Do not be afraid to go down to Egypt, for I will make you into a great nation there. I myself will go down with you to Egypt, and I myself will certainly bring you up as well, and Joseph will lay his hand on your eyes." And Jacob rose from Beersheba, and the sons of Israel carried Jacob their father and their little ones and their wives in the wagons that Pharaoh had sent to carry him. And they took their herds and their possessions that they had amassed in the land of Canaan, and they came to Egypt, Jacob and all his offspring with him. His sons and his sons' sons with him, his daughters and his sons' daughters, and all his offspring he brought with him to Egypt. And these are the names of the sons of Israel who came to Egypt, Jacob and his sons: Jacob's firstborn, Reuben. And the sons of Reuben were Enoch and Pallu and Hezron and Carmi. And the sons of Simeon were Jemuel and Jamin and Ohad and Jachin and Zohar, and Shaul the son of the Canaanite woman. And the sons of Levi were Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. And the sons of Judah were Er and Onan and Shelah and Perez and Zerah. And Er and Onan died in the land of Canaan. And the sons of Perez were Hezron and Hamul. And the sons of Issachar were Tola and Puvah and Iob and Shimron. And the sons of Zebulun were Sered and Elon and Jahleel. These are the sons of Leah, whom she bore to Jacob in Paddan-Aram, and Dinah his daughter. All his sons and his daughters were thirty-three people. And the sons of Gad were Ziphion and Haggi, Shuni and Ezbon, Eri and Arodi and Areli. And the sons of Asher were Imnah and Ishvah and Ishvi and Beriah, and Serah their sister. And the sons of Beriah were Heber and Malchiel. These are the sons of Zilpah, whom Laban gave to Leah his daughter, and she bore these to Jacob: sixteen people. The sons of Rachel, Jacob's wife, were Joseph and Benjamin. And to Joseph in the land of Egypt were born Manasseh and Ephraim, whom Asenath the daughter of Potiphera, priest of On, bore to him. And the sons of Benjamin were Bela and Becher and Ashbel, Gera and Naaman, Ehi and Rosh, Muppim and Huppim and Ard. These are the sons of Rachel who were born to Jacob: fourteen people in all. And the sons of Dan: Hushim. And the sons of Naphtali were Jahzeel and Guni and Jezer and Shillem. These are the sons of Bilhah, whom Laban gave to Rachel his daughter, and she bore these to Jacob: seven people in all. All the people belonging to Jacob who came to Egypt, who came out of his loins, besides the wives of Jacob's sons, were sixty-six people in all. And the sons of Joseph who were born to him in Egypt were two people. All the people of the house of Jacob who came to Egypt were seventy. And Judah he sent ahead of him to Joseph, to show the way ahead of him to Goshen, and they came to the land of Goshen. And Joseph harnessed his chariot and went up to meet Israel his father, to Goshen, and he appeared to him and fell on his neck and wept on his neck for a long time. And Israel said to Joseph, "Now let me die, after I have seen your face, for you are still alive." And Joseph said to his brothers and to his father's house, "I will go up and tell Pharaoh, and I will say to him, 'My brothers and my father's house, who were in the land of Canaan, have come to me. And the men are shepherds of flocks, for they have been herdsmen, and their flocks and their cattle and all that they have they have brought.' And when Pharaoh calls you and says, 'What is your work?' you are to say, 'Your servants have been herdsmen from our youth until now, both we and our fathers,' so that you may live in the land of Goshen, for every shepherd of flocks is a detestable thing to Egypt."

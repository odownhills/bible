Genesis, chapter 6. And when humankind began to multiply on the face of the ground and daughters were born to them, the sons of God saw that the daughters of humankind were beautiful, and they took wives for themselves, whichever they chose. And Yahweh said, "My spirit will not remain in humankind forever, because they too are flesh, and their days will be a hundred and twenty years." The Nephilim were on the earth in those days, and also afterward, when the sons of God would sleep with the daughters of humankind and they would bear children to them. These were the mighty men who were of old, the men of renown. And Yahweh saw that the evil of humankind was great on the earth, and that everything formed in the thoughts of their heart was only evil all day long. And Yahweh regretted that he had made humankind on the earth, and he was pained to his heart. And Yahweh said, "I will wipe humankind, whom I created, off the face of the ground, from humankind to beasts, to creeping things, and to the birds of the sky, for I regret that I made them." But Noah found favor in the eyes of Yahweh. These are the generations of Noah. Noah was a righteous man, blameless in his generations. With God Noah walked. And Noah fathered three sons: Shem, Ham, and Japheth. And the earth was ruined before God, and the earth was filled with violence. And God saw the earth, and look, it was ruined, for all flesh had ruined its way on the earth. And God said to Noah, "The end of all flesh has come before me, for the earth is full of violence because of them. And look, I am about to ruin them along with the earth. Make for yourself an ark of gopher wood. You are to make the ark with compartments, and cover it inside and outside with pitch. And this is how you are to make it: the length of the ark three hundred cubits, its width fifty cubits, and its height thirty cubits. You are to make an opening for light for the ark, and finish it to a cubit at the top, and you are to put the entrance of the ark in its side. You are to make it with lower, second, and third decks. "And as for me, look, I am about to bring the flood of water on the earth, to ruin all flesh in which there is the spirit of life, from under the sky. Everything that is on the earth will breathe its last. But I will establish my covenant with you, and you will go into the ark, you and your sons and your wife and your sons' wives with you. And of every living thing, of all flesh, you are to bring two of each into the ark, to keep them alive with you. They are to be male and female. Of the birds according to their kinds, and of the beasts according to their kinds, of every creeping thing of the ground according to its kind, two of each will come to you, to be kept alive. And as for you, take for yourself some of every food that is eaten, and gather it to yourself, and it will be yours and theirs for food." And Noah did it; according to all that God had commanded him, so he did.

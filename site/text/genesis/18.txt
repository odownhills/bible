Genesis, chapter 18. And Yahweh appeared to him among the oaks of Mamre, as he was sitting at the entrance of the tent in the heat of the day. And he lifted his eyes and saw, and look, three men were standing near him. And he saw, and he ran from the entrance of the tent to meet them and bowed down to the ground. And he said, "Lord, if I have found favor in your eyes, please do not pass your servant by. Let a little water be fetched, please, and wash your feet, and recline under the tree. And let me fetch a piece of bread, and strengthen yourselves; afterward you may pass on, since you have passed your servant's way." And they said, "Do so, as you have spoken." And Abraham hurried into the tent to Sarah and said, "Hurry! Three seahs of flour, fine flour! Knead it and make cakes." And to the cattle Abraham ran, and he took a calf, tender and good, and gave it to the young man, and he hurried to prepare it. And he took curds and milk and the calf that he had prepared and set them before them, and he was standing by them under the tree, and they ate. And they said to him, "Where is Sarah your wife?" And he said, "There, in the tent." And he said, "I will certainly return to you about this time next year, and look, Sarah your wife will have a son." Now Sarah was listening at the entrance of the tent, and it was behind him. Now Abraham and Sarah were old, advanced in years; the way of women had stopped for Sarah. And Sarah laughed within herself, saying, "After I am worn out, am I to have pleasure, when my lord is old?" And Yahweh said to Abraham, "Why is it that Sarah laughed, saying, 'Will I really and truly give birth, when I have grown old?' Is anything too hard for Yahweh? At the set time I will return to you, about this time next year, and Sarah will have a son." And Sarah denied it, saying, "I did not laugh," for she was afraid. And he said, "No, you did laugh." And the men rose from there and looked down toward Sodom, and Abraham was walking with them to send them on their way. Now Yahweh said, "Am I to hide from Abraham what I am going to do, when Abraham will certainly become a great and powerful nation, and all the nations of the earth will be blessed in him? For I have known him, so that he may command his sons and his household after him, and they will keep the way of Yahweh by doing righteousness and justice, so that Yahweh may bring on Abraham what he has spoken concerning him." And Yahweh said, "The outcry of Sodom and Gomorrah is great indeed, and their sin is very heavy indeed. I will go down now and see whether they have done altogether according to its outcry that has come to me; and if not, I will know." And the men turned from there and went toward Sodom, and Abraham was still standing before Yahweh. And Abraham approached and said, "Will you really sweep away the righteous with the wicked? Perhaps there are fifty righteous people inside the city. Will you really sweep it away and not forgive the place for the sake of the fifty righteous who are within it? Far be it from you to do such a thing, to put the righteous to death with the wicked, so that the righteous is like the wicked! Far be it from you! Will the judge of the whole earth not do justice?" And Yahweh said, "If I find in Sodom fifty righteous people inside the city, I will forgive the whole place because of them." And Abraham answered and said, "Look now, I have taken it on myself to speak to the Lord, though I am dust and ashes. Perhaps the fifty righteous will lack five. Will you ruin the whole city over the five?" And he said, "I will not ruin it if I find forty-five there." And he spoke to him yet again and said, "Perhaps forty will be found there." And he said, "I will not do it, because of the forty." And he said, "Please let the Lord not be angry, and let me speak. Perhaps thirty will be found there." And he said, "I will not do it if I find thirty there." And he said, "Look now, I have taken it on myself to speak to the Lord. Perhaps twenty will be found there." And he said, "I will not ruin it, because of the twenty." And he said, "Please let the Lord not be angry, and let me speak only this once. Perhaps ten will be found there." And he said, "I will not ruin it, because of the ten." And Yahweh went away when he had finished speaking to Abraham, and Abraham returned to his place.

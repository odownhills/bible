Genesis, chapter 30. And Rachel saw that she had borne Jacob no children, and Rachel envied her sister, and she said to Jacob, "Give me sons, and if not, I am dead." And Jacob's anger burned against Rachel, and he said, "Am I in the place of God, who has kept back from you the fruit of the womb?" And she said, "Look, here is my female servant Bilhah. Sleep with her, so that she may give birth on my knees, and I too may be built up from her." And she gave him Bilhah her female slave as his wife, and Jacob slept with her. And Bilhah conceived and bore Jacob a son. And Rachel said, "God has judged in my favor, and he has also listened to my voice and given me a son." That is why she named him Dan. And Bilhah, Rachel's female slave, conceived again and bore Jacob a second son. And Rachel said, "With wrestlings of God I have wrestled with my sister, and I have prevailed too." And she named him Naphtali. And Leah saw that she had stopped bearing children, and she took Zilpah her female slave and gave her to Jacob as his wife. And Zilpah, Leah's female slave, bore Jacob a son. And Leah said, "What good fortune!" And she named him Gad. And Zilpah, Leah's female slave, bore Jacob a second son. And Leah said, "How happy I am! For women have called me happy." And she named him Asher. And Reuben went in the days of the wheat harvest and found mandrakes in the field, and he brought them to Leah his mother. And Rachel said to Leah, "Please give me some of your son's mandrakes." And she said to her, "Is your taking my husband a small thing, that you would take my son's mandrakes too?" And Rachel said, "Therefore he will lie with you tonight in exchange for your son's mandrakes." And Jacob came in from the field in the evening, and Leah went out to meet him and said, "It is with me you are to sleep, for I have certainly hired you with my son's mandrakes." And he lay with her that night. And God listened to Leah, and she conceived and bore Jacob a fifth son. And Leah said, "God has given my pay, because I gave my female slave to my husband." And she named him Issachar. And Leah conceived again and bore Jacob a sixth son. And Leah said, "God has endowed me with a good gift. This time my husband will make his home with me, because I have borne him six sons." And she named him Zebulun. And afterward she gave birth to a daughter, and she named her Dinah. And God remembered Rachel, and God listened to her and opened her womb. And she conceived and gave birth to a son, and she said, "God has taken away my disgrace." And she named him Joseph, saying, "May Yahweh add another son to me." And when Rachel had given birth to Joseph, Jacob said to Laban, "Send me on my way, so that I may go to my place and to my land. Give me my wives and my children, for whom I have served you, and let me go, for you yourself know my service that I have done for you." And Laban said to him, "Please, if I have found favor in your eyes—I have learned by divination that Yahweh has blessed me on account of you." And he said, "Name your pay to me, and I will give it." And he said to him, "You yourself know how I have served you and what your herds have become with me. For it was little that you had before I came, and it has spread out into a multitude, and Yahweh has blessed you wherever I set foot. And now, when will I too do something for my household?" And he said, "What should I give you?" And Jacob said, "You will not give me anything. If you will do this thing for me, I will again graze your flock and watch over it. I will pass through all your flock today. Let every speckled and spotted animal be removed from there, and every dark animal among the sheep, and the spotted and speckled among the goats, and that will be my pay. And my righteousness will testify for me on a day to come, when it comes before you concerning my pay. Any that is not speckled or spotted among the goats, or dark among the sheep, is stolen if it is with me." And Laban said, "Look, may it be according to your word." And on that day he removed the male goats that were striped and spotted, and all the female goats that were speckled and spotted, every one that had white on it, and every dark one among the sheep, and he gave them into the hand of his sons. And he put a journey of three days between himself and Jacob, and Jacob was grazing the rest of Laban's flock. And Jacob took for himself sticks of fresh poplar and of almond and plane tree, and he peeled white strips in them, laying bare the white that was on the sticks. And he set the sticks that he had peeled in the channels, in the water troughs where the flock came to drink, in front of the flock, and they bred when they came to drink. And the flock bred at the sticks, and the flock gave birth to young that were striped, speckled, and spotted. And the sheep Jacob separated, and he set the faces of the flock toward the striped and every dark one in Laban's flock, and he set flocks for himself alone and did not set them with Laban's flock. And whenever the sturdy ones of the flock bred, Jacob would put the sticks before the eyes of the flock in the channels, so that they would breed among the sticks. But when the flock was feeble, he would not put them there, and the feeble ones became Laban's and the sturdy ones Jacob's. And the man spread out very greatly, and he came to have many flocks and female slaves and male slaves and camels and donkeys.

Genesis, chapter 43. Now the famine was severe in the land. And when they had finished eating the grain that they had brought from Egypt, their father said to them, "Return, buy us a little food." And Judah said to him, saying, "The man solemnly warned us, saying, 'You will not see my face unless your brother is with you.' If you are going to send our brother with us, we will go down and buy food for you. But if you are not going to send him, we will not go down, for the man said to us, 'You will not see my face unless your brother is with you.'" And Israel said, "Why did you do me harm by telling the man that you had another brother?" And they said, "The man asked closely about us and about our relatives, saying, 'Is your father still alive? Do you have a brother?' And we told him according to these words. Could we really have known that he would say, 'Bring your brother down'?" And Judah said to Israel his father, "Send the boy with me, and let us get up and go, so that we may live and not die, both we and you and also our little ones. I myself will be guarantor for him; from my hand you will require him. If I do not bring him to you and set him before you, then I will have sinned against you for all time. For if we had not lingered, surely by now we would have returned twice." And Israel their father said to them, "If it is so, then do this: take some of the prized produce of the land in your containers, and bring down a present to the man, a little balm and a little honey, gum and resin, pistachios and almonds. And take double the silver in your hand, and the silver that was returned in the mouth of your bags you are to bring back in your hand. Perhaps it was a mistake. And take your brother, and get up, return to the man. And may God Almighty give you compassion before the man, and may he release to you your other brother and Benjamin. And as for me, if I am bereaved, I am bereaved." And the men took this present, and double the silver they took in their hand, and Benjamin, and they rose and went down to Egypt and stood before Joseph. And Joseph saw Benjamin with them, and he said to the one who was over his house, "Bring the men into the house, and slaughter an animal and make ready, for it is with me that the men will eat at noon." And the man did as Joseph had said, and the man brought the men to Joseph's house. And the men were afraid because they had been brought to Joseph's house, and they said, "It is over the matter of the silver that came back in our bags the first time that we are being brought in, so that they may rush on us and fall on us and take us as slaves, and our donkeys." And they approached the man who was over Joseph's house and spoke to him at the entrance of the house. And they said, "Pardon, my lord. We did come down the first time to buy food. And when we came to the lodging place and opened our bags, look, each man's silver was in the mouth of his bag, our silver in its full weight, and we have brought it back in our hand. And other silver we have brought down in our hand to buy food. We do not know who put our silver in our bags." And he said, "It is well with you; do not be afraid. Your God and the God of your father has given you hidden treasure in your bags. Your silver came to me." And he brought Simeon out to them. And the man brought the men to Joseph's house, and he gave water, and they washed their feet, and he gave fodder to their donkeys. And they made the present ready for Joseph's coming at noon, for they had heard that they would eat bread there. And Joseph came into the house, and they brought him the present that was in their hand, into the house, and they bowed down to him to the ground. And he asked them whether it was well with them, and he said, "Is it well with your old father, of whom you spoke? Is he still alive?" And they said, "It is well with your servant our father; he is still alive." And they bowed their heads and bowed down. And he lifted his eyes and saw Benjamin his brother, his mother's son, and he said, "Is this your youngest brother, of whom you spoke to me?" And he said, "May God favor you, my son." And Joseph hurried, for his compassion was stirred toward his brother, and he wanted to weep, and he went into the inner room and wept there. And he washed his face and came out, and he controlled himself and said, "Set out the bread." And they set it out for him by himself, and for them by themselves, and for the Egyptians who were eating with him by themselves, for the Egyptians could not eat bread with the Hebrews, for that was a detestable thing to Egypt. And they sat before him, the firstborn according to his birthright and the youngest according to his youth, and the men turned to one another in astonishment. And portions were carried to them from before him, and Benjamin's portion was larger than the portions of all of them, five times over. And they drank and became drunk with him.

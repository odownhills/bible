Deuteronomy, chapter 5. And Moses called to all Israel and said to them, "Hear, Israel, the statutes and the judgments that I am speaking in your ears today, and learn them and be careful to do them. "Yahweh our God made a covenant with us at Horeb. It was not with our fathers that Yahweh made this covenant, but with us, we ourselves, these here today, all of us alive. Face to face Yahweh spoke with you at the mountain, from the midst of the fire (I was standing between Yahweh and you at that time, to tell you the word of Yahweh, for you were afraid of the fire and did not go up on the mountain), saying: "'I am Yahweh your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slaves. You must have no other gods before my face. You must not make for yourself a carved image, any form that is in the heavens above, or that is on the earth below, or that is in the waters under the earth. You must not bow down to them, and you must not serve them, for I, Yahweh your God, am a jealous God, punishing the children for the guilt of the fathers, and the third and the fourth generation of those who hate me, but showing faithful love to thousands of those who love me and keep his commandments. "'You must not take up the name of Yahweh your God in vain, for Yahweh will not hold innocent the one who takes up his name in vain. "'Keep the Sabbath day, to make it holy, as Yahweh your God has commanded you. Six days you will labor and do all your work, but the seventh day is a Sabbath to Yahweh your God. You must not do any work, you or your son or your daughter, or your male slave or your female slave, or your ox or your donkey or any of your livestock, or your resident foreigner who is within your gates, so that your male slave and your female slave may rest as you do. And remember that you were a slave in the land of Egypt, and Yahweh your God brought you out from there with a strong hand and with an outstretched arm. That is why Yahweh your God has commanded you to observe the Sabbath day. "'Honor your father and your mother, as Yahweh your God has commanded you, so that your days may be long, and so that it may go well with you, on the ground which Yahweh your God is giving you. "'You must not murder. "'And you must not commit adultery. "'And you must not steal. "'And you must not testify against your neighbor as a false witness. "'And you must not desire your neighbor's wife. And you must not crave your neighbor's house, his field, or his male slave or his female slave, his ox or his donkey, or anything that is your neighbor's.' "These words Yahweh spoke to your whole assembly at the mountain, from the midst of the fire, the cloud and the thick darkness, with a great voice, and he added no more. And he wrote them on two tablets of stone and gave them to me. "And when you heard the voice from the midst of the darkness, while the mountain was burning with fire, you came near to me, all the heads of your tribes and your elders, and you said, 'Look, Yahweh our God has shown us his glory and his greatness, and his voice we have heard from the midst of the fire. This day we have seen that God speaks with a human being, and he lives. And now, why should we die? For this great fire will devour us. If we go on hearing the voice of Yahweh our God any longer, we will die. For who is there of all flesh that has heard the voice of the living God speaking from the midst of the fire, as we have, and lived? You yourself go near and hear all that Yahweh our God says, and you yourself will speak to us all that Yahweh our God speaks to you, and we will hear it and do it.' "And Yahweh heard the sound of your words when you spoke to me, and Yahweh said to me, 'I have heard the sound of the words of this people, which they have spoken to you. They have done well in all that they have spoken. If only this heart of theirs were theirs, to fear me and to keep all my commandments always, so that it might go well with them and with their children forever! Go, say to them, "Return to your tents." But as for you, stand here with me, and I will speak to you the whole commandment and the statutes and the judgments that you are to teach them, and they are to do them in the land that I am giving them to take possession of.' "And you must be careful to do as Yahweh your God has commanded you. You must not turn aside to the right or to the left. You must walk in all the way that Yahweh your God has commanded you, so that you may live, and it may be good for you, and you may lengthen your days in the land that you will take possession of.

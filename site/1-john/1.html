The word of life

1What was from the beginning, what we have heard, what we have seen with our eyes, what we gazed at and our hands felt, concerning the word of life [alt: the Word of life]— 2and the life was revealed, and we have seen and testify and announce to you the eternal life, which was with the Father and was revealed to us— 3what we have seen and heard we announce to you also, so that you also may have fellowship with us. And indeed our fellowship is with the Father and with his Son, Jesus Christ. 4And we ourselves are writing these things [alt: we are writing these things to you, as other manuscripts read] so that our joy [alt: your joy, as other manuscripts read] may be made full.

God is light

5And this is the message that we have heard from him and declare to you, that God is light, and in him there is no darkness at all. 6If we say that we have fellowship with him and walk in the darkness, we lie and do not do the truth. 7But if we walk in the light as he himself is in the light, we have fellowship with one another, and the blood of Jesus his Son cleanses us from all sin.

8If we say that we have no sin, we lead ourselves astray, and the truth is not in us. 9If we confess our sins, he is faithful and righteous [alt: just], so that he forgives us our sins and cleanses us from all unrighteousness. 10If we say that we have not sinned, we make him a liar, and his word is not in us.

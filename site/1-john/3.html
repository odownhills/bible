1 John 3

▶ Listen from here

Children of God

1See what kind of love the Father has given us, so that we should be called children of God, and we are. For this reason the world does not know us, because it did not know him. 2Beloved, now we are children of God, and what we will be has not yet been revealed. We know that when he is revealed [alt: when it is revealed, or if he is revealed] we will be like him, because we will see him just as he is. 3And everyone who has this hope set on him purifies himself, just as that one is pure.

Sin is lawlessness

4Everyone who does sin also does lawlessness, and sin is lawlessness. 5And you know that that one was revealed so that he might take away sins, and in him there is no sin. 6Everyone who remains in him does not sin; everyone who sins has not seen him, nor has he come to know him.

7Little children, let no one lead you astray. The one who does righteousness is righteous [alt: just], just as that one is righteous; 8the one who does sin is from the devil, because the devil has been sinning from the beginning. For this purpose the Son of God was revealed: so that he might undo [alt: destroy] the works of the devil. 9Everyone who has been born from God does not do sin, because his seed remains in him [alt: because his offspring remains in him], and he cannot sin, because he has been born from God.

The children of God and the children of the devil

10By this the children of God and the children of the devil are revealed: everyone who does not do righteousness is not from God, and neither is the one who does not love his brother, 11because this is the message that you heard from the beginning, that we should love one another, 12not as Cain was from the evil one and slaughtered his brother. And for what reason did he slaughter him? Because his works were evil, but his brother's were righteous.

From death into life

13Do not be surprised, brothers, if the world hates you. 14We ourselves know that we have crossed over from death into life, because we love the brothers. The one who does not love remains in death. 15Everyone who hates his brother is a murderer, and you know that no murderer has eternal life remaining in him.

Love in deed and truth

16By this we have come to know love: that that one laid down his life [alt: his soul] for us. And we ourselves ought to lay down our lives for the brothers. 17But whoever has the world's possessions and sees his brother in need and shuts off his compassion from him, how does the love of God remain in him? 18Little children, let us not love in word or with the tongue, but in deed and truth.

Boldness toward God

19By this we will know that we are from the truth, and before him we will reassure our heart 20in whatever our heart condemns us, because God is greater than our heart and knows all things [alt: and before him we will persuade our heart that, if our heart condemns us, God is greater than our heart and knows all things]. [In Greek "condemns" is the word "knows" with a prefix meaning "against."] 21Beloved, if our heart does not condemn us, we have boldness toward God, 22and whatever we ask we receive from him, because we keep his commandments and do the things that are pleasing in his sight.

23And this is his commandment: that we should believe the name [alt: believe in the name] of his Son, Jesus Christ, and love one another, just as he gave us a commandment. 24And the one who keeps his commandments remains in him, and he himself in him. And by this we know that he remains in us: from the spirit that he gave us.

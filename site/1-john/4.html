1 John 4

▶ Listen from here

Test the spirits

1Beloved, do not believe every spirit, but test the spirits, whether they are from God, because many false prophets have gone out into the world. 2By this you know the spirit of God: every spirit that confesses Jesus Christ as having come in the flesh [alt: that confesses Jesus as the Christ who has come in the flesh] is from God, 3and every spirit that does not confess Jesus is not from God. And this is the spirit of the antichrist, which you have heard is coming, and now it is already in the world.

4You yourselves are from God, little children, and you have conquered them, because the one in you is greater than the one in the world. 5They themselves are from the world; for this reason they speak from the world, and the world hears them [alt: listens to them]. 6We ourselves are from God. The one who knows God hears us; whoever is not from God does not hear us. From this we know the spirit of truth and the spirit of error [alt: the spirit of leading astray].

God is love

7Beloved, let us love one another, because love is from God, and everyone who loves has been born from God and knows God. 8The one who does not love did not know God, because God is love. 9By this the love of God was revealed in us [alt: among us]: that God has sent his only Son [alt: his only-born Son] into the world so that we might live through him. 10In this is love: not that we ourselves have loved God, but that he himself loved us and sent his Son as the atonement [alt: the appeasement] for our sins.

11Beloved, if God loved us in this way [alt: loved us so much], we ourselves also ought to love one another. 12No one has ever gazed at God. If we love one another, God remains in us, and his love has been made complete in us.

God remains in us

13By this we know that we remain in him and he himself in us: that he has given us from his spirit. 14And we ourselves have gazed and testify that the Father has sent the Son as savior of the world. 15Whoever confesses that Jesus is the Son of God, God remains in him and he himself in God. 16And we ourselves have come to know and have believed the love that God has in us [alt: for us]. God is love, and the one who remains in love remains in God, and God remains in him.

Love made complete

17By this, love has been made complete with us, so that we may have boldness on the day of judgment, because just as that one is, so we ourselves are in this world. 18There is no fear in love, but complete love throws fear out, because fear has punishment, and the one who fears has not been made complete in love.

19We ourselves love [alt: let us love; or we love him, as other manuscripts read], because he himself first loved us. 20If anyone says, "I love God," and hates his brother, he is a liar; for the one who does not love his brother, whom he has seen, cannot love God, whom he has not seen. 21And we have this commandment from him: that the one who loves God should love his brother also.

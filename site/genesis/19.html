Genesis 19

The two angels in Sodom

1And the two angels came to Sodom in the evening, and Lot was sitting in the gate of Sodom. And Lot saw, and he rose to meet them and bowed down with his face to the ground. 2And he said, "Look now, my lords, please turn aside to your servant's house and spend the night, and wash your feet, and you may rise early and go on your way." And they said, "No, we will spend the night in the square." 3And he pressed them hard, and they turned aside to him and came into his house. And he held a feast for them and baked unleavened bread, and they ate. 4They had not yet lain down when the men of the city, the men of Sodom, surrounded the house, from young to old, all the people, to the last one. 5And they called to Lot and said to him, "Where are the men who came to you tonight? Bring them out to us, so that we may be intimate with them." 6And Lot went out to them, to the entrance, and shut the door behind him. 7And he said, "Please, my brothers, do not do evil. 8Look now, I have two daughters who have not been intimate with a man. Please let me bring them out to you, and do to them as is good in your eyes. Only do nothing to these men, since they have come under the shadow of my roof." 9And they said, "Stand back!" And they said, "This one came to stay as a foreigner, and he is actually judging! Now we will do worse evil to you than to them." And they pressed hard against the man, against Lot, and approached to break the door. 10And the men reached out their hands and brought Lot in to them, into the house, and they shut the door. 11And the men who were at the entrance of the house they struck with blindness, from the smallest to the greatest, and they wearied themselves trying to find the entrance.

Lot is brought out

12And the men said to Lot, "Who else do you have here? A son-in-law, and your sons and your daughters, and all that you have in the city—bring them out of the place. 13For we are going to ruin this place, for their outcry has become great before Yahweh, and Yahweh has sent us to ruin it." 14And Lot went out and spoke to his sons-in-law, who were to marry his daughters [alt: who had married his daughters], and said, "Get up, go out of this place, for Yahweh is going to ruin the city." But in the eyes of his sons-in-law he was like one who was joking. 15And as dawn came up, the angels urged Lot, saying, "Get up, take your wife and your two daughters who are here, so that you are not swept away in the punishment of the city." 16And he lingered. And the men took hold of his hand and his wife's hand and the hands of his two daughters, because Yahweh was sparing him, and they brought him out and set him down outside the city. 17And when they had brought them outside, he said, "Escape for your life! Do not look behind you, and do not stop anywhere in the plain. Escape to the hill country, so that you are not swept away." 18And Lot said to them, "Please, no, Lord [alt: my lords]! 19Look now, your servant has found favor in your eyes, and you have made great your faithful love that you have shown me in keeping me alive. But I cannot escape to the hill country, or the disaster will overtake me and I will die. 20Look now, this city is near to flee to, and it is a small thing. Please let me escape there—is it not a small thing?—and I may live." 21And he said to him, "Look, I grant you this thing also: I will not overturn the city of which you have spoken. 22Hurry, escape there, for I cannot do anything until you come there." That is why the city was named Zoar. [In Hebrew the name "Zoar" sounds like the word for "a small thing."]

Sodom and Gomorrah overturned

23The sun had risen over the earth when Lot came to Zoar. 24And Yahweh rained sulfur and fire on Sodom and on Gomorrah, from Yahweh, from the sky. 25And he overturned those cities and all the plain, and all those who lived in the cities, and what sprouted from the ground. 26And his wife looked back from behind him, and she became a pillar of salt. 27And Abraham rose early in the morning and went to the place where he had stood before Yahweh. 28And he looked down toward Sodom and Gomorrah and toward all the land of the plain, and he saw, and look, the smoke of the land had gone up like the smoke of a furnace. 29And when God ruined the cities of the plain, God remembered Abraham, and he sent Lot out from the midst of the overturning, when he overturned the cities in which Lot had lived.

Lot and his daughters

30And Lot went up from Zoar and lived in the hill country, and his two daughters with him, for he was afraid to live in Zoar. And he lived in a cave, he and his two daughters. 31And the firstborn said to the younger, "Our father is old, and there is no man in the land [alt: on the earth] to sleep with us, as is the way of all the earth. 32Come, let us make our father drink wine, and let us lie with him, and let us keep offspring alive from our father." 33And they made their father drink wine that night, and the firstborn went in and lay with her father, and he did not know when she lay down or when she rose. 34And on the next day the firstborn said to the younger, "Look, last night I lay with my father. Let us make him drink wine tonight also, and go in and lie with him, and let us keep offspring alive from our father." 35And they made their father drink wine that night also, and the younger rose and lay with him, and he did not know when she lay down or when she rose. 36And the two daughters of Lot conceived by their father. 37And the firstborn gave birth to a son and named him Moab. [In Hebrew the name "Moab" sounds like the words for "from father."] He is the father of Moab to this day. 38And the younger also gave birth to a son, and she named him Ben-Ammi. [In Hebrew the name means "son of my people," and the name "Ammon" sounds like it.] He is the father of the sons of Ammon to this day.

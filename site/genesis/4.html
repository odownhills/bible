Cain and Abel

1Now the human knew [alt: was intimate with] his wife Eve, and she conceived and gave birth to Cain. And she said, "I have acquired a man with Yahweh." [alt: I have brought a man into being with Yahweh] [In Hebrew the name "Cain" sounds like the word for "acquired."] 2And she gave birth again, to his brother Abel. [In Hebrew the name "Abel" is the word for "breath."] And Abel became a shepherd of flocks, and Cain became a worker of the ground.

3And after some time, Cain brought some of the fruit of the ground as an offering to Yahweh. 4And Abel too brought some of the firstborn of his flock and some of their fat [alt: of the choicest of them]. And Yahweh looked at [alt: looked with favor on] Abel and at his offering, 5but at Cain and at his offering he did not look. And Cain was very angry, and his face fell.

6And Yahweh said to Cain, "Why are you angry, and why has your face fallen? 7If you do good, will there not be a lifting up? [alt: will you not be accepted, or will there not be forgiveness] But if you do not do good, sin is crouching at the entrance, and its longing is for you, but you must govern it." [alt: and you will govern it]

8And Cain spoke to his brother Abel. [alt: And Cain said to his brother Abel, "Let us go out to the field," as other ancient versions read] And when they were in the field, Cain rose up against his brother Abel and killed him.

Cain driven from the ground

9And Yahweh said to Cain, "Where is your brother Abel?" And he said, "I do not know. Am I my brother's guardian?" 10And he said, "What have you done? The voice of your brother's blood is crying out to me from the ground. [alt: Listen! Your brother's blood is crying out to me from the ground.] 11And now, cursed are you from the ground [alt: more than the ground], which has opened its mouth to take your brother's blood from your hand. 12When you work the ground, it will no longer give you its strength. A drifter and a wanderer you will be on the earth." [In Hebrew "drifter" and "wanderer" sound alike.]

13And Cain said to Yahweh, "My punishment is too great to bear. [alt: My guilt is too great to be forgiven.] 14Look, you have driven me out today from the face of the ground, and from your face I will be hidden. And I will be a drifter and a wanderer on the earth, and anyone who finds me will kill me."

15And Yahweh said to him, "Therefore, if anyone kills Cain, vengeance will be taken seven times over." And Yahweh set a sign for Cain [alt: put a mark on Cain], so that anyone who found him would not strike him down. 16And Cain went out from the presence of Yahweh and lived in the land of Nod, east of Eden. [In Hebrew the name "Nod" sounds like the word for "wanderer."]

The line of Cain

17And Cain knew his wife, and she conceived and gave birth to Enoch. And he became the builder of a city, and he named the city after his son, Enoch. 18And Irad was born to Enoch, and Irad fathered Mehujael, and Mehujael [alt: Mehijael] fathered Methushael, and Methushael fathered Lamech.

19And Lamech took two wives for himself. The name of the first was Adah, and the name of the second was Zillah. 20And Adah gave birth to Jabal. He was the father of those who live in tents and among herds. 21And his brother's name was Jubal. He was the father of all who handle the lyre and the pipe. 22And Zillah, she too gave birth to Tubal-cain, who forged every cutting tool of bronze and iron [alt: who sharpened every craftsman in bronze and iron]. And Tubal-cain's sister was Naamah.

23And Lamech said to his wives, "Adah and Zillah, hear my voice; wives of Lamech, listen closely to what I say. For I have killed a man for my wound, and a boy [alt: a young man] for my bruise. 24If Cain is avenged seven times over, then Lamech seventy-seven times."

Seth and Enosh

25And Adam knew his wife again, and she gave birth to a son and named him Seth, "because God has set another offspring [alt: another seed] for me in place of Abel, since Cain killed him." [In Hebrew the name "Seth" sounds like the word for "set."] 26And to Seth also a son was born, and he named him Enosh. At that time, calling on the name of Yahweh began.

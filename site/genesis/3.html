Genesis 3

Listen only

The snake and the tree

1Now the snake was more shrewd than any wild animal of the field that Yahweh God had made. [In Hebrew "shrewd" sounds like the word for "naked" in 2:25.] And he said to the woman, "Did God really say, 'You must not eat from any tree of the garden'?" 2And the woman said to the snake, "From the fruit of the trees of the garden we may eat, 3but from the fruit of the tree that is in the middle of the garden, God said, 'You must not eat from it, and you must not touch it, so that you do not die.'" 4And the snake said to the woman, "You will certainly not die, 5for God knows that on the day you eat from it your eyes will be opened, and you will be like God [alt: like gods], knowing good and bad [alt: good and evil]."

6And the woman saw that the tree was good for food, and that it was a delight to the eyes, and that the tree was desirable for gaining insight [alt: desirable to contemplate]. And she took some of its fruit and ate, and she also gave some to her husband, who was with her, and he ate. [In Hebrew "husband" is the same word as "man."] 7And the eyes of the two of them were opened, and they knew that they were naked. And they sewed fig leaves together and made loincloths for themselves.

Hiding from Yahweh God

8And they heard the sound of Yahweh God walking about in the garden in the breeze of the day, and the human and his wife hid from the presence of Yahweh God among the trees of the garden. 9And Yahweh God called to the human and said to him, "Where are you?" 10And he said, "I heard the sound of you in the garden, and I was afraid because I was naked, and I hid." 11And he said, "Who told you that you are naked? Have you eaten from the tree that I commanded you not to eat from?" 12And the human said, "The woman you gave to be with me—she gave me some from the tree, and I ate." 13And Yahweh God said to the woman, "What is this you have done?" And the woman said, "The snake deceived me, and I ate."

The consequences

14And Yahweh God said to the snake, "Because you have done this, cursed are you more than all the livestock and more than any wild animal of the field. [alt: cursed are you, cut off from all the livestock and from every wild animal of the field] [In Hebrew "cursed" sounds like "shrewd" in verse 1.] On your belly you will go, and dust you will eat all the days of your life. 15And I will put hostility between you and the woman, and between your offspring [alt: your seed] and her offspring. He will strike [alt: crush, or bruise] your head, and you will strike his heel." [alt: They will strike your head, and you will strike their heel]

16To the woman he said, "I will greatly multiply your painful toil and your pregnancies; [alt: your pain in pregnancy] in pain you will bear children. And your longing will be for your husband, and he will govern you."

17And to Adam [alt: to the human] he said, "Because you listened to the voice of your wife and ate from the tree about which I commanded you, saying, 'You must not eat from it,' cursed is the ground because of you. In painful toil you will eat from it all the days of your life. 18And thorns and thistles it will sprout for you, and you will eat the plants of the field. 19By the sweat of your face you will eat bread until you return to the ground, because from it you were taken; for you are dust, and to dust you will return."

Sent out of the garden

20And the human named his wife Eve, because she was the mother of all the living. [In Hebrew the name "Eve" sounds like the word for "living."] 21And Yahweh God made tunics of skin for Adam [alt: for the human] and for his wife, and he clothed them.

22And Yahweh God said, "Look, the human has become like one of us, knowing good and bad. And now, so that he does not reach out his hand and take also from the tree of life, and eat, and live forever—" 23So Yahweh God sent him out of the garden of Eden to work the ground from which he had been taken. [In Hebrew "ground" sounds like the word for "human."] 24And he drove the human out, and east of the garden of Eden he stationed the cherubim and the flame of the whirling sword, to guard the way to the tree of life. [In Hebrew "guard" is the same word as "watch over" in 2:15.]

Genesis 12

Watch Listen only

Yahweh tells Abram to go

1And Yahweh said to Abram, "Go from your land and from your relatives [alt: your birthplace] and from your father's house to the land that I will show you. 2And I will make you into a great nation, and I will bless you and make your name great, and be a blessing [alt: so that you will be a blessing]. 3And I will bless those who bless you, and the one who calls down a curse on you I will curse, and all the families of the ground will be blessed in you [alt: will bless themselves by you]."

Abram in the land of Canaan

4And Abram went, as Yahweh had spoken to him, and Lot went with him. Now Abram was seventy-five years old when he went out from Haran. 5And Abram took Sarai his wife, and Lot his brother's son, and all their possessions that they had amassed, and the people they had gained in Haran, and they went out to go to the land of Canaan, and they came to the land of Canaan. 6And Abram passed through the land as far as the place of Shechem, as far as the oak of Moreh. Now the Canaanites were then in the land. 7And Yahweh appeared to Abram and said, "To your offspring I will give this land." And he built an altar there to Yahweh, who had appeared to him. 8And he moved on from there to the hill country east of Bethel and pitched his tent, with Bethel on the west and Ai on the east, and he built an altar there to Yahweh and called on the name of Yahweh. 9And Abram traveled steadily on toward the Negev.

Abram and Sarai in Egypt

10And there was a famine in the land, and Abram went down to Egypt to stay there, for the famine was severe in the land. 11And when he came near to entering Egypt, he said to Sarai his wife, "Look now, I know that you are a woman beautiful in appearance. 12And when the Egyptians see you, they will say, 'This is his wife,' and they will kill me, but you they will let live. 13Please say that you are my sister, so that it may go well with me because of you, and I may live on account of you." 14And when Abram entered Egypt, the Egyptians saw that the woman was very beautiful. 15And Pharaoh's officials saw her and praised her to Pharaoh, and the woman was taken into Pharaoh's house. 16And he treated Abram well because of her, and he came to have flocks and cattle and donkeys and male slaves and female slaves and female donkeys and camels. 17And Yahweh struck Pharaoh and his household with great plagues over the matter of Sarai, Abram's wife. 18And Pharaoh called Abram and said, "What is this you have done to me? Why did you not tell me that she was your wife? 19Why did you say, 'She is my sister,' so that I took her as my wife? And now, look, here is your wife. Take her and go." 20And Pharaoh commanded men concerning him, and they sent him out, with his wife and all that he had.

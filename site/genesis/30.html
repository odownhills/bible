Genesis 30

Watch Listen only

Bilhah's sons

1And Rachel saw that she had borne Jacob no children, and Rachel envied her sister, and she said to Jacob, "Give me sons, and if not, I am dead." 2And Jacob's anger burned against Rachel, and he said, "Am I in the place of God, who has kept back from you the fruit of the womb?" 3And she said, "Look, here is my female servant Bilhah. Sleep with her, so that she may give birth on my knees, and I too may be built up from her." 4And she gave him Bilhah her female slave as his wife, and Jacob slept with her. 5And Bilhah conceived and bore Jacob a son. 6And Rachel said, "God has judged in my favor, and he has also listened to my voice and given me a son." That is why she named him Dan. [In Hebrew the name "Dan" sounds like the word for "judged."] 7And Bilhah, Rachel's female slave, conceived again and bore Jacob a second son. 8And Rachel said, "With wrestlings of God [alt: mighty wrestlings] I have wrestled with my sister, and I have prevailed too." And she named him Naphtali. [In Hebrew the name "Naphtali" sounds like the word for "wrestled."]

Zilpah's sons

9And Leah saw that she had stopped bearing children, and she took Zilpah her female slave and gave her to Jacob as his wife. 10And Zilpah, Leah's female slave, bore Jacob a son. 11And Leah said, "What good fortune!" And she named him Gad. [In Hebrew the name "Gad" is the word for "good fortune."] 12And Zilpah, Leah's female slave, bore Jacob a second son. 13And Leah said, "How happy I am! For women have called me happy." And she named him Asher. [In Hebrew the name "Asher" sounds like the word for "happy."]

The mandrakes

14And Reuben went in the days of the wheat harvest and found mandrakes in the field, and he brought them to Leah his mother. And Rachel said to Leah, "Please give me some of your son's mandrakes." 15And she said to her, "Is your taking my husband a small thing, that you would take my son's mandrakes too?" And Rachel said, "Therefore he will lie with you tonight in exchange for your son's mandrakes." 16And Jacob came in from the field in the evening, and Leah went out to meet him and said, "It is with me you are to sleep, for I have certainly hired you with my son's mandrakes." And he lay with her that night. 17And God listened to Leah, and she conceived and bore Jacob a fifth son. 18And Leah said, "God has given my pay, because I gave my female slave to my husband." And she named him Issachar. [In Hebrew the name "Issachar" sounds like the words for "hired" and "pay."] 19And Leah conceived again and bore Jacob a sixth son. 20And Leah said, "God has endowed me with a good gift. This time my husband will make his home with me [alt: will honor me], because I have borne him six sons." And she named him Zebulun. [In Hebrew the name "Zebulun" sounds like the words for "endowed" and "make his home."] 21And afterward she gave birth to a daughter, and she named her Dinah.

Rachel's son

22And God remembered Rachel, and God listened to her and opened her womb. 23And she conceived and gave birth to a son, and she said, "God has taken away my disgrace." 24And she named him Joseph, saying, "May Yahweh add another son to me." [In Hebrew the name "Joseph" is the word for "may he add," and it sounds like the word for "taken away."]

Jacob's pay

25And when Rachel had given birth to Joseph, Jacob said to Laban, "Send me on my way, so that I may go to my place and to my land. 26Give me my wives and my children, for whom I have served you, and let me go, for you yourself know my service that I have done for you." 27And Laban said to him, "Please, if I have found favor in your eyes—I have learned by divination that Yahweh has blessed me on account of you." 28And he said, "Name your pay to me, and I will give it." 29And he said to him, "You yourself know how I have served you and what your herds have become with me. 30For it was little that you had before I came, and it has spread out into a multitude, and Yahweh has blessed you wherever I set foot. And now, when will I too do something for my household?" 31And he said, "What should I give you?" And Jacob said, "You will not give me anything. If you will do this thing for me, I will again graze your flock and watch over it. 32I will pass through all your flock today. Let every speckled and spotted animal be removed from there, and every dark animal among the sheep, and the spotted and speckled among the goats, and that will be my pay. 33And my righteousness will testify for me on a day to come, when it comes before you concerning my pay. Any that is not speckled or spotted among the goats, or dark among the sheep, is stolen if it is with me." 34And Laban said, "Look, may it be according to your word." 35And on that day he removed the male goats that were striped and spotted, and all the female goats that were speckled and spotted, every one that had white on it, and every dark one among the sheep, and he gave them into the hand of his sons. 36And he put a journey of three days between himself and Jacob, and Jacob was grazing the rest of Laban's flock.

The peeled sticks

37And Jacob took for himself sticks of fresh poplar and of almond and plane tree, and he peeled white strips in them, laying bare the white that was on the sticks. [In Hebrew "white" is the name "Laban," and the word for "poplar" sounds like it.] 38And he set the sticks that he had peeled in the channels, in the water troughs where the flock came to drink, in front of the flock, and they bred when they came to drink. 39And the flock bred at the sticks, and the flock gave birth to young that were striped, speckled, and spotted. 40And the sheep Jacob separated, and he set the faces of the flock toward the striped and every dark one in Laban's flock, and he set flocks for himself alone and did not set them with Laban's flock. 41And whenever the sturdy ones of the flock bred, Jacob would put the sticks before the eyes of the flock in the channels, so that they would breed among the sticks. 42But when the flock was feeble, he would not put them there, and the feeble ones became Laban's and the sturdy ones Jacob's. 43And the man spread out very greatly, and he came to have many flocks and female slaves and male slaves and camels and donkeys.

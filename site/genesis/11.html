Genesis 11

Watch Listen only

The city and the tower

1And the whole earth had one speech and the same words [alt: and few words]. 2And as they traveled eastward [alt: from the east], they found a plain in the land of Shinar, and they lived there. 3And they said to one another, "Come, let us make bricks and bake them thoroughly." And brick served them as stone, and tar served them as mortar. 4And they said, "Come, let us build ourselves a city, and a tower with its top in the sky, and let us make a name for ourselves, so that we are not scattered over the face of the whole earth."

Yahweh scatters them

5And Yahweh came down to see the city and the tower that the sons of humankind had built. 6And Yahweh said, "Look, they are one people, and they all have one speech, and this is what they have begun to do. And now nothing that they intend to do will be out of their reach. 7Come, let us go down and confuse their speech there, so that they will not understand one another's speech." 8And Yahweh scattered them from there over the face of the whole earth, and they stopped building the city. 9That is why it was named Babylon, because there Yahweh confused the speech of the whole earth, and from there Yahweh scattered them over the face of the whole earth. [In Hebrew the name "Babylon" sounds like the word for "confused."]

From Shem to Terah

10These are the generations of Shem. Shem was a hundred years old, and he fathered Arpachshad two years after the flood. 11And Shem lived five hundred years after he fathered Arpachshad, and he fathered sons and daughters.

12And Arpachshad lived thirty-five years, and he fathered Shelah. 13And Arpachshad lived four hundred and three years after he fathered Shelah, and he fathered sons and daughters.

14And Shelah lived thirty years, and he fathered Eber. 15And Shelah lived four hundred and three years after he fathered Eber, and he fathered sons and daughters.

16And Eber lived thirty-four years, and he fathered Peleg. 17And Eber lived four hundred and thirty years after he fathered Peleg, and he fathered sons and daughters.

18And Peleg lived thirty years, and he fathered Reu. 19And Peleg lived two hundred and nine years after he fathered Reu, and he fathered sons and daughters.

20And Reu lived thirty-two years, and he fathered Serug. 21And Reu lived two hundred and seven years after he fathered Serug, and he fathered sons and daughters.

22And Serug lived thirty years, and he fathered Nahor. 23And Serug lived two hundred years after he fathered Nahor, and he fathered sons and daughters.

24And Nahor lived twenty-nine years, and he fathered Terah. 25And Nahor lived a hundred and nineteen years after he fathered Terah, and he fathered sons and daughters.

26And Terah lived seventy years, and he fathered Abram, Nahor, and Haran.

The family of Terah

27And these are the generations of Terah. Terah fathered Abram, Nahor, and Haran, and Haran fathered Lot. 28And Haran died in the presence of Terah his father, in the land of his birth, in Ur of the Chaldeans. 29And Abram and Nahor took wives for themselves. The name of Abram's wife was Sarai, and the name of Nahor's wife was Milcah, the daughter of Haran, the father of Milcah and the father of Iscah. 30And Sarai was barren; she had no child.

31And Terah took Abram his son, and Lot the son of Haran, his son's son, and Sarai his daughter-in-law, the wife of Abram his son, and they went out with them from Ur of the Chaldeans to go to the land of Canaan. And they came as far as Haran, and they lived there. [In Hebrew the name of this place is not the name of Terah's son Haran; the two begin with different letters.] 32And the days of Terah were two hundred and five years [alt: a hundred and forty-five years, as the Samaritan copy of Genesis reads], and Terah died in Haran.

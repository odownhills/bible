Verse 55 is verse 1 of chapter 32 in the Hebrew text, so every verse of chapter 32 is numbered one higher there.

Jacob is told to return

1And he heard the words of Laban's sons, saying, "Jacob has taken all that was our father's, and from what was our father's he has gained all this wealth." 2And Jacob saw Laban's face, and look, he was not with him as before. 3And Yahweh said to Jacob, "Return to the land of your fathers and to your relatives, and I will be with you." 4And Jacob sent and called Rachel and Leah to the field, to his flock. 5And he said to them, "I see your father's face, that he is not toward me as before, but the God of my father has been with me. 6And you yourselves know that with all my strength I have served your father. 7And your father has fooled me and changed my wages ten times, but God has not let him do me harm. 8If he said this: 'The speckled will be your pay,' then all the flock gave birth to speckled young. And if he said this: 'The striped will be your pay,' then all the flock gave birth to striped young. 9And God has taken away your father's herds and given them to me. 10And at the time when the flock bred, I lifted my eyes and saw in a dream, and look, the he-goats that were mounting the flock were striped, speckled, and dappled. 11And the angel of God said to me in the dream, 'Jacob!' And I said, 'Here I am.' 12And he said, 'Lift your eyes now and see: all the he-goats that are mounting the flock are striped, speckled, and dappled, for I have seen all that Laban is doing to you. 13I am the God of Bethel, where you anointed a pillar, where you made a vow to me. Now get up, go out of this land and return to the land of your birth.'" 14And Rachel and Leah answered and said to him, "Do we still have a share and an inheritance in our father's house? 15Are we not counted by him as foreigners? For he has sold us, and he has actually eaten up our silver too. 16For all the riches that God has taken away from our father are ours and our children's. And now, all that God has said to you, do."

Jacob flees

17And Jacob rose and lifted his children and his wives onto the camels. 18And he drove all his herds and all his possessions that he had amassed, the herds he had acquired, which he had amassed in Paddan-Aram, to go to Isaac his father, to the land of Canaan. 19Now Laban had gone to shear his flock, and Rachel stole the teraphim that were her father's. 20And Jacob deceived Laban the Aramean by not telling him that he was fleeing. [In Hebrew "deceived" is "stole the heart of," with the same verb as "stole" in the verse before; the verb returns in this sense in verses 26 and 27.] 21And he fled, he and all that he had, and he rose and crossed the River and set his face toward the hill country of Gilead.

Laban overtakes Jacob

22And it was told to Laban on the third day that Jacob had fled. 23And he took his brothers with him and pursued him a journey of seven days, and he caught up with him in the hill country of Gilead. 24And God came to Laban the Aramean in a dream by night and said to him, "Be careful that you do not speak with Jacob either good or bad." 25And Laban overtook Jacob. Now Jacob had pitched his tent in the hill country, and Laban pitched with his brothers in the hill country of Gilead. 26And Laban said to Jacob, "What have you done, deceiving me and driving off my daughters like captives of the sword? 27Why did you flee in secret and deceive me, and not tell me? I would have sent you on your way with joy and with songs, with drum and with lyre. 28And you did not let me kiss my sons and my daughters. Now you have done a foolish thing. 29It is in the power of my hand to do you harm, but the God of your father said to me last night, saying, 'Be careful not to speak with Jacob either good or bad.' 30And now, you have certainly gone because you yearned greatly for your father's house. Why did you steal my gods?" 31And Jacob answered and said to Laban, "Because I was afraid, for I said, 'You might seize your daughters from me.' 32The one with whom you find your gods will not live. Before our brothers, recognize for yourself whatever is with me, and take it." Now Jacob did not know that Rachel had stolen them.

The search for the teraphim

33And Laban went into Jacob's tent and into Leah's tent and into the tent of the two female servants, and he did not find them. And he went out of Leah's tent and went into Rachel's tent. 34Now Rachel had taken the teraphim and put them in the camel's saddle and sat on them. And Laban groped through the whole tent and did not find them. 35And she said to her father, "Let there be no anger in the eyes of my lord that I am not able to rise before you, for the way of women is on me." And he searched and did not find the teraphim.

Jacob's anger

36And Jacob was angry and quarreled with Laban, and Jacob answered and said to Laban, "What is my offense? What is my sin, that you have chased hotly after me? 37For you have groped through all my things. What have you found of all the things of your house? Put it here before my brothers and your brothers, and let them decide between the two of us. 38These twenty years I have been with you. Your ewes and your female goats have not miscarried, and the rams of your flock I have not eaten. 39A torn animal I did not bring to you; I myself would bear the loss of it. From my hand you would require it, whether stolen by day or stolen by night. 40There I was: by day the heat consumed me, and the frost by night, and my sleep wandered from my eyes. 41This makes twenty years for me in your house: I served you fourteen years for your two daughters and six years for your flock, and you have changed my wages ten times. 42If the God of my father, the God of Abraham and the Dread of Isaac, had not been on my side, surely by now you would have sent me away empty-handed. My affliction and the toil of my hands God has seen, and he gave his decision last night."

The covenant at the stone heap

43And Laban answered and said to Jacob, "The daughters are my daughters, and the sons are my sons, and the flock is my flock, and all that you see is mine. And for my daughters, what can I do for these today [alt: do to these today], or for their sons whom they have borne? 44And now, come, let us make a covenant, I and you, and let it serve as a witness between me and you." 45And Jacob took a stone and raised it as a pillar. 46And Jacob said to his brothers, "Gather stones." And they took stones and made a stone heap, and they ate there on the stone heap. 47And Laban called it Jegar-Sahadutha, and Jacob called it Galeed. [Both names mean "stone heap of witness," the first in Aramaic and the second in Hebrew, and "Galeed" sounds like "Gilead."] 48And Laban said, "This stone heap is a witness between me and you today." That is why it was named Galeed, 49and Mizpah, because he said, "May Yahweh keep watch between me and you when we are hidden from one another. [In Hebrew the name "Mizpah" sounds like the word for "keep watch."] 50If you afflict my daughters, and if you take wives in addition to my daughters—no man is with us; see, God is witness between me and you." 51And Laban said to Jacob, "Look, this stone heap, and look, the pillar that I have set up between me and you. 52This stone heap is a witness, and the pillar is a witness, that I will not cross over this stone heap to you, and that you will not cross over this stone heap and this pillar to me, for harm. 53May the God of Abraham and the god of Nahor judge between us, the gods of their father [alt: the God of Abraham and the God of Nahor, the God of their father]." And Jacob swore by the Dread of his father Isaac. 54And Jacob offered a sacrifice in the hill country and invited his brothers to eat bread, and they ate bread and spent the night in the hill country. 55And Laban rose early in the morning and kissed his sons and his daughters and blessed them, and Laban went and returned to his place.

Genesis 49

▶ Listen from here

Jacob calls his sons together

1And Jacob called his sons and said, "Gather together, and let me tell you what will happen to you in the latter days. 2Come together and listen, sons of Jacob, and listen to Israel your father.

Reuben, Simeon and Levi

3"Reuben, you are my firstborn, my strength and the beginning of my vigor, excelling in dignity and excelling in power. 4Reckless as water, you will not excel, for you went up to your father's bed; then you desecrated it. He went up to my couch!

5"Simeon and Levi are brothers; weapons of violence are their swords [alt: their dealings, or their kinship]. 6Into their council may my soul not come; with their assembly may my glory not be united. For in their anger they killed a man, and at their pleasure they hamstrung an ox [alt: they tore down a wall, as old Aramaic and Latin versions read]. 7Cursed is their anger, for it is fierce, and their fury, for it is harsh. I will divide them in Jacob and scatter them in Israel.

Judah

8"Judah, it is you your brothers will praise. Your hand will be on the back of your enemies' necks; your father's sons will bow down to you. [In Hebrew the name "Judah" sounds like the words for "will praise you" and "your hand."] 9Judah is a lion's cub. From the prey, my son, you have gone up. He crouches, he lies down like a lion, and like a lioness; who will rouse him? 10The scepter will not depart from Judah, nor the ruler's staff from between his feet, until Shiloh comes [alt: until he comes to Shiloh; or, as old versions read it, until he comes to whom it belongs], and the obedience of peoples will be his. 11Tying his colt to the vine, and his female donkey's foal to the choice vine, he washes his clothing in wine, and his robe in the blood of grapes. 12His eyes are dark from wine, and his teeth white from milk. [alt: His eyes are darker than wine, and his teeth whiter than milk.]

Zebulun, Issachar, Dan, Gad, Asher and Naphtali

13"Zebulun will live by the shore of the seas, and he will be by the shore of ships, and his far side will be by Sidon.

14"Issachar is a strong-boned donkey, lying down between the sheepfolds. 15And he saw that rest was good, and that the land was pleasant, and he bent his shoulder to bear a burden, and he became a forced laborer.

16"Dan will judge his people as one of the tribes of Israel. [In Hebrew the name "Dan" sounds like the word for "will judge."] 17May Dan be a snake by the road, an adder by the path, that bites a horse's heels, so that its rider falls backward. 18For your salvation I hope, Yahweh. 19As for Gad, raiders will raid him, but he will raid at their heels. [In Hebrew "Gad," "raiders" and "raid" sound alike.]

20"From Asher, his bread is rich, and he will give a king's delicacies.

21"Naphtali is a doe let loose; he gives beautiful words [alt: a doe let loose that bears beautiful fawns; or a spreading tree that puts out beautiful branches].

Joseph and Benjamin

22"Joseph is a fruitful bough, a fruitful bough by a spring; its branches climb over a wall. [alt: Joseph is a wild donkey's colt, a wild donkey's colt by a spring; its young stride along by Shur.] 23And the archers dealt bitterly with him and shot at him and bore a grudge against him. 24But his bow stayed firm, and the arms of his hands were nimble, by the hands of the Mighty One of Jacob, from there, the Shepherd, the Stone of Israel, 25from the God of your father, and may he help you, and with the Almighty, and may he bless you with blessings of the sky above, blessings of the deep that lies below, blessings of breasts and womb. [In Hebrew "breasts" sounds like "the Almighty."] 26The blessings of your father have prevailed over the blessings of my parents, as far as the delight of the everlasting hills [alt: over the blessings of the enduring mountains, the delight of the everlasting hills, with "mountains" as in the old Greek]. May they be on the head of Joseph, and on the crown of the head of the one set apart from his brothers.

27"Benjamin is a wolf that tears. In the morning he eats prey, and at evening he divides spoil."

28All these are the twelve tribes of Israel, and this is what their father spoke to them. And he blessed them; each according to his own blessing he blessed them.

Jacob's command about his burial, and his death

29And he commanded them and said to them, "I am going to be gathered to my people. Bury me with my fathers, in the cave that is in the field of Ephron the Hittite, 30in the cave that is in the field of Machpelah, which is facing Mamre, in the land of Canaan, the field that Abraham bought from Ephron the Hittite as a holding for a grave. 31There they buried Abraham and Sarah his wife; there they buried Isaac and Rebekah his wife; and there I buried Leah. 32The purchase of the field and of the cave that is in it was from the sons of Heth." 33And Jacob finished commanding his sons, and he gathered his feet up onto the bed, and he breathed his last and was gathered to his people.

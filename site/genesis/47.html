Genesis 47

Joseph's brothers and his father before Pharaoh

1And Joseph came and told Pharaoh, and he said, "My father and my brothers, and their flocks and their cattle and all that they have, have come from the land of Canaan, and look, they are in the land of Goshen." 2And from among his brothers he took five men and set them before Pharaoh. 3And Pharaoh said to his brothers, "What is your work?" And they said to Pharaoh, "Your servants are shepherds of flocks, both we and our fathers." 4And they said to Pharaoh, "To stay in the land we have come, for there is no pasture for the flocks that your servants have, for the famine is severe in the land of Canaan. And now, please let your servants live in the land of Goshen." 5And Pharaoh said to Joseph, saying, "Your father and your brothers have come to you. 6The land of Egypt is before you. In the best of the land settle your father and your brothers; let them live in the land of Goshen. And if you know that there are capable men among them, make them chiefs of herds over what is mine."

7And Joseph brought Jacob his father and stood him before Pharaoh, and Jacob blessed Pharaoh. 8And Pharaoh said to Jacob, "How many are the days of the years of your life?" 9And Jacob said to Pharaoh, "The days of the years of my staying as a foreigner are a hundred and thirty years. Few and bad have been the days of the years of my life, and they have not reached the days of the years of the life of my fathers in the days of their staying as foreigners." 10And Jacob blessed Pharaoh and went out from the presence of Pharaoh.

11And Joseph settled his father and his brothers and gave them a holding in the land of Egypt, in the best of the land, in the land of Rameses, as Pharaoh had commanded. 12And Joseph provided for his father and his brothers and all his father's house with bread, according to the little ones.

Joseph buys the ground of Egypt for Pharaoh

13Now there was no bread in all the land, for the famine was very severe, and the land of Egypt and the land of Canaan grew faint because of the famine. 14And Joseph gathered all the silver that was found in the land of Egypt and in the land of Canaan, for the grain that they were buying, and Joseph brought the silver into Pharaoh's house. 15And the silver ran out from the land of Egypt and from the land of Canaan, and all Egypt came to Joseph, saying, "Give us bread. And why should we die in front of you? For the silver is gone." 16And Joseph said, "Give your herds, and I will give to you for your herds, if the silver is gone." 17And they brought their herds to Joseph, and Joseph gave them bread for the horses and for the herds of sheep and for the herds of cattle and for the donkeys, and he led them through that year with bread, for all their herds.

18And that year came to an end, and they came to him in the second year and said to him, "We will not conceal from my lord that the silver has run out, and the herds of livestock have gone to my lord. Nothing is left before my lord except our bodies and our ground. 19Why should we die before your eyes, both we and our ground? Buy us and our ground for bread, and we and our ground will be slaves to Pharaoh. And give seed, so that we may live and not die, and the ground will not be desolate." 20And Joseph bought all the ground of Egypt for Pharaoh, for Egypt sold, each man his field, for the famine was strong over them, and the land became Pharaoh's. 21And as for the people, he moved them to the cities [alt: he made them slaves, as the Samaritan copy and the old Greek have it], from one end of the border of Egypt to its other end. 22Only the ground of the priests he did not buy, for the priests had an allowance from Pharaoh, and they ate their allowance that Pharaoh gave them. That is why they did not sell their ground.

23And Joseph said to the people, "Look, I have bought you today, and your ground, for Pharaoh. Here is seed for you, and you are to sow the ground. 24And when the crops come in, you are to give a fifth to Pharaoh, and four parts will be yours, as seed for the field and as your food and for those who are in your houses and as food for your little ones." 25And they said, "You have kept us alive. Let us find favor in the eyes of my lord, and we will be slaves to Pharaoh." 26And Joseph made it a statute, to this day, over the ground of Egypt: the fifth is Pharaoh's. Only the ground of the priests alone did not become Pharaoh's.

Israel asks to be buried with his fathers

27And Israel lived in the land of Egypt, in the land of Goshen, and they took holdings in it and were fruitful and multiplied greatly. 28And Jacob lived in the land of Egypt seventeen years, and the days of Jacob, the years of his life, were a hundred and forty-seven years. 29And the days came near for Israel to die, and he called his son Joseph and said to him, "If I have found favor in your eyes, please put your hand under my thigh, and show loyalty and truth to me: please do not bury me in Egypt. 30And I will lie with my fathers, and you are to carry me out of Egypt and bury me in their grave." And he said, "I myself will do according to your word." 31And he said, "Swear to me." And he swore to him. And Israel bowed down at the head of the bed [alt: on the top of his staff, as the old Greek has it].

Genesis 38

Judah's sons

1And at that time Judah went down from his brothers and turned aside to an Adullamite man, and his name was Hirah. 2And Judah saw there the daughter of a Canaanite man, and his name was Shua, and he took her and slept with her. 3And she conceived and gave birth to a son, and he named him Er. 4And she conceived again and gave birth to a son, and she named him Onan. 5And yet again she gave birth to a son, and she named him Shelah. And he was at Chezib when she gave birth to him [alt: And she was at Chezib when she gave birth to them, as the old Greek has it].

Tamar is left a widow

6And Judah took a wife for Er his firstborn, and her name was Tamar. 7And Er, Judah's firstborn, was evil in the eyes of Yahweh, and Yahweh put him to death. [In Hebrew "evil" is the name "Er" written backward.] 8And Judah said to Onan, "Sleep with your brother's wife and do the duty of a husband's brother to her, and raise up offspring for your brother." 9And Onan knew that the offspring would not be his, and whenever he slept with his brother's wife he would waste his seed on the ground, so as not to give offspring to his brother. 10And what he did was evil in the eyes of Yahweh, and he put him to death also. 11And Judah said to Tamar his daughter-in-law, "Live as a widow in your father's house until Shelah my son grows up"—for he said, "He too might die, like his brothers." And Tamar went and lived in her father's house.

Tamar by the road to Timnah

12And many days passed, and the daughter of Shua, Judah's wife, died. And Judah was comforted, and he went up to the shearers of his flock, he and Hirah his friend the Adullamite [alt: Hirah his shepherd the Adullamite, as the old Greek has it], to Timnah. 13And it was told to Tamar, saying, "Look, your father-in-law is going up to Timnah to shear his flock." 14And she took off her widow's garments and covered herself with a veil and wrapped herself up, and she sat at the entrance of Enaim, which is on the way to Timnah, for she saw that Shelah had grown up, and she had not been given to him as a wife. 15And Judah saw her and took her for a prostitute, for she had covered her face. 16And he turned aside to her by the road and said, "Come, please, let me sleep with you"—for he did not know that she was his daughter-in-law. And she said, "What will you give me, that you may sleep with me?" 17And he said, "I myself will send a goat kid from the flock." And she said, "If you give a pledge until you send it." 18And he said, "What is the pledge that I should give you?" And she said, "Your seal and your cord and your staff that is in your hand." And he gave them to her and slept with her, and she conceived by him. 19And she rose and went, and she took off her veil and put on her widow's garments.

The pledge is not recovered

20And Judah sent the goat kid by the hand of his friend the Adullamite, to take the pledge from the woman's hand, and he did not find her. 21And he asked the men of her place, saying, "Where is the shrine prostitute, the one who was at Enaim by the road?" [The Hebrew word here is not the one for "prostitute" in 38:15; it comes from the root of "holy."] And they said, "There has been no shrine prostitute here." 22And he returned to Judah and said, "I did not find her, and also the men of the place said, 'There has been no shrine prostitute here.'" 23And Judah said, "Let her keep it for herself, so that we are not held in contempt. Look, I sent this kid, and you did not find her."

Judah recognizes the pledge

24And about three months later it was told to Judah, saying, "Tamar your daughter-in-law has prostituted herself, and also, look, she is pregnant by prostitution." And Judah said, "Bring her out, and let her be burned." 25She was being brought out when she sent to her father-in-law, saying, "By the man to whom these belong I am pregnant." And she said, "Recognize, please, whose these are, the seal and the cords and the staff." 26And Judah recognized them and said, "She is more righteous than I [alt: She is in the right; it is by me], since I did not give her to Shelah my son." And he was not intimate with her again.

Perez and Zerah are born

27And at the time of her giving birth, look, there were twins in her womb. 28And as she was giving birth, one put out a hand, and the midwife took a scarlet thread and tied it on his hand, saying, "This one came out first." 29And as he drew back his hand, look, his brother came out, and she said, "What a breach you have made for yourself!" And he was named Perez. [In Hebrew the name "Perez" is the word for "breach."] 30And afterward his brother came out, on whose hand was the scarlet thread, and he was named Zerah.

Genesis 41

Watch Listen only

Pharaoh's two dreams

1And at the end of two full years Pharaoh was dreaming, and look, he was standing by the Nile. 2And look, coming up from the Nile were seven cows, beautiful in appearance and fat in flesh, and they grazed in the reed grass. 3And look, seven other cows were coming up after them from the Nile, bad in appearance and thin in flesh, and they stood beside the cows on the bank of the Nile. 4And the cows that were bad in appearance and thin in flesh ate the seven cows that were beautiful in appearance and fat. And Pharaoh awoke. 5And he slept and dreamed a second time, and look, seven ears of grain were coming up on one stalk, fat and good. 6And look, seven ears, thin and scorched by the east wind, were sprouting after them. 7And the thin ears swallowed the seven fat and full ears. And Pharaoh awoke, and look, it was a dream. 8And in the morning his spirit was disturbed, and he sent and called all the magicians of Egypt and all its wise men, and Pharaoh recounted his dream to them, and there was no one to interpret them for Pharaoh.

The chief cupbearer speaks

9And the chief cupbearer spoke with Pharaoh, saying, "My sins I am mentioning today. 10Pharaoh was furious with his servants, and he put me in custody in the house of the commander of the guard, me and the chief baker. 11And we dreamed a dream in one night, I and he; we dreamed, each dream with its own interpretation. 12And there with us was a young Hebrew man, a slave of the commander of the guard, and we recounted them to him, and he interpreted our dreams for us; for each according to his dream he interpreted. 13And as he interpreted for us, so it was: me he restored to my post, and him he hanged."

Joseph before Pharaoh

14And Pharaoh sent and called Joseph, and they rushed him out of the pit, and he shaved and changed his clothes and came to Pharaoh. 15And Pharaoh said to Joseph, "I have dreamed a dream, and there is no one to interpret it. And I have heard it said of you that you can hear a dream and interpret it." 16And Joseph answered Pharaoh, saying, "Not I; God will give an answer for Pharaoh's welfare." 17And Pharaoh spoke to Joseph, "In my dream, look, I was standing on the bank of the Nile. 18And look, coming up from the Nile were seven cows, fat in flesh and beautiful in form, and they grazed in the reed grass. 19And look, seven other cows were coming up after them, poor and very bad in form and lean in flesh; I have not seen any like them in all the land of Egypt for badness. 20And the lean and bad cows ate the first seven cows, the fat ones. 21And they went inside them, but it could not be known that they had gone inside them, and their appearance was bad, as at the beginning. And I awoke. 22And I saw in my dream, and look, seven ears were coming up on one stalk, full and good. 23And look, seven ears, shriveled, thin, scorched by the east wind, were sprouting after them. 24And the thin ears swallowed the seven good ears. And I said it to the magicians, and there was no one to tell me."

Joseph interprets the dreams

25And Joseph said to Pharaoh, "Pharaoh's dream is one. What God is going to do he has told Pharaoh. 26The seven good cows are seven years, and the seven good ears are seven years; it is one dream. 27And the seven lean and bad cows that came up after them are seven years, and the seven empty ears, scorched by the east wind, will be seven years of famine. 28That is the word that I have spoken to Pharaoh: what God is going to do he has shown Pharaoh. 29Look, seven years are coming of great plenty in all the land of Egypt. 30And seven years of famine will arise after them, and all the plenty will be forgotten in the land of Egypt, and the famine will make an end of the land. 31And the plenty will not be known in the land because of that famine afterward, for it will be very severe. 32And as for the dream being repeated to Pharaoh twice, it is because the matter is established by God, and God is hurrying to do it. 33And now let Pharaoh look for a discerning and wise man and set him over the land of Egypt. 34Let Pharaoh act and put overseers in charge of the land, and let him take a fifth from the land of Egypt [alt: and let him make the land of Egypt ready] in the seven years of plenty. 35And let them gather all the food of these good years that are coming, and let them heap up grain under Pharaoh's hand as food in the cities and keep it. 36And the food will be a reserve for the land for the seven years of famine that will be in the land of Egypt, and the land will not be cut off by the famine."

Joseph set over Egypt

37And the matter was good in the eyes of Pharaoh and in the eyes of all his servants. 38And Pharaoh said to his servants, "Can we find anyone like this, a man in whom is the spirit of God [alt: a spirit of gods]?" 39And Pharaoh said to Joseph, "Since God has made all this known to you, there is no one as discerning and wise as you. 40You will be over my house, and all my people will submit to your word [alt: will arm themselves at your word]; only as to the throne will I be greater than you." 41And Pharaoh said to Joseph, "See, I have set you over all the land of Egypt." 42And Pharaoh removed his signet ring from his hand and put it on Joseph's hand, and he clothed him in garments of fine linen and put the gold chain on his neck. 43And he had him ride in his second chariot, and they called out before him, "Kneel!" [alt: an Egyptian word whose meaning is not given] And so he set him over all the land of Egypt. 44And Pharaoh said to Joseph, "I am Pharaoh, and without you no man will raise his hand or his foot in all the land of Egypt." 45And Pharaoh named Joseph Zaphenath-Paneah, and he gave him Asenath the daughter of Potiphera, priest of On, as his wife. And Joseph went out over the land of Egypt. 46Now Joseph was thirty years old when he stood before Pharaoh king of Egypt. And Joseph went out from the presence of Pharaoh and passed through all the land of Egypt.

The seven years of plenty

47And in the seven years of plenty the land produced by the handful. 48And he gathered all the food of the seven years that were in the land of Egypt and put food in the cities; the food of the fields around each city he put inside it. 49And Joseph heaped up grain like the sand of the sea, very much, until he stopped counting, for it was beyond number. 50And to Joseph two sons were born before the year of famine came, whom Asenath the daughter of Potiphera, priest of On, bore to him. 51And Joseph named the firstborn Manasseh, "because God has made me forget all my misery and all my father's house." [In Hebrew the name "Manasseh" sounds like the word for "made me forget."] 52And the second he named Ephraim, "because God has made me fruitful in the land of my affliction." [In Hebrew the name "Ephraim" sounds like the word for "made me fruitful."]

The famine begins

53And the seven years of the plenty that was in the land of Egypt came to an end. 54And the seven years of famine began to come, as Joseph had said. And there was famine in all the lands, but in all the land of Egypt there was bread. 55And all the land of Egypt was hungry, and the people cried out to Pharaoh for bread. And Pharaoh said to all Egypt, "Go to Joseph; what he says to you, you are to do." 56Now the famine was over all the face of the earth. And Joseph opened all that had grain in them and sold grain to Egypt, and the famine grew strong in the land of Egypt. 57And the people of the whole earth came to Egypt, to Joseph, to buy grain, for the famine was strong in the whole earth.

Genesis 35

Watch Listen only

Jacob goes up to Bethel

1And God said to Jacob, "Get up, go up to Bethel and live there, and make an altar there to the God who appeared to you when you fled from Esau your brother." 2And Jacob said to his household and to all who were with him, "Remove the foreign gods that are among you, and make yourselves clean, and change your clothes. 3And let us get up and go up to Bethel, and I will make an altar there to the God who answered me on the day of my distress and has been with me on the way that I have gone." 4And they gave Jacob all the foreign gods that were in their hands and the rings that were in their ears, and Jacob hid them under the terebinth that was by Shechem. 5And they set out, and the terror of God was on the cities that were around them, and they did not pursue the sons of Jacob. 6And Jacob came to Luz, which is in the land of Canaan (that is, Bethel), he and all the people who were with him. 7And he built an altar there, and he called the place "God of Bethel," for there God had revealed himself to him [alt: there the divine beings had revealed themselves to him] when he fled from his brother.

8And Deborah, Rebekah's nurse, died, and she was buried below Bethel, under the oak, and it was named Allon-Bacuth. [In Hebrew the name "Allon-Bacuth" means "oak of weeping."]

God appears to Jacob at Bethel

9And God appeared to Jacob again when he came from Paddan-Aram, and he blessed him. 10And God said to him, "Your name is Jacob. Your name will no longer be called Jacob, but Israel will be your name." And he named him Israel. 11And God said to him, "I am God Almighty. Be fruitful and multiply. A nation and an assembly of nations will be from you, and kings will come out of your loins. 12And the land that I gave to Abraham and to Isaac, to you I will give it, and to your offspring after you I will give the land." 13And God went up from him at the place where he had spoken with him. 14And Jacob set up a pillar at the place where he had spoken with him, a pillar of stone, and he poured out a drink offering on it and poured oil on it. 15And Jacob named the place where God had spoken with him Bethel.

Rachel dies giving birth to Benjamin

16And they traveled from Bethel, and when there was still a stretch of land to go to Ephrath, Rachel gave birth, and she had hard labor. 17And when she was in hard labor, the midwife said to her, "Do not be afraid, for this one too is a son for you." 18And as her life was going out, for she was dying, she named him Ben-Oni, but his father called him Benjamin. [In Hebrew the name "Ben-Oni" means "son of my sorrow" (it can also be heard as "son of my vigor"), and the name "Benjamin" means "son of the right hand."] 19And Rachel died, and she was buried on the way to Ephrath (that is, Bethlehem). 20And Jacob set up a pillar over her grave. It is the pillar of Rachel's grave to this day.

Reuben and Bilhah; the twelve sons of Jacob

21And Israel traveled on and pitched his tent beyond Migdal-Eder. 22And while Israel was living in that land, Reuben went and lay with Bilhah, his father's concubine, and Israel heard.And the sons of Jacob were twelve. 23The sons of Leah were Jacob's firstborn, Reuben, and Simeon and Levi and Judah and Issachar and Zebulun. 24The sons of Rachel were Joseph and Benjamin. 25And the sons of Bilhah, Rachel's female slave, were Dan and Naphtali. 26And the sons of Zilpah, Leah's female slave, were Gad and Asher. These are the sons of Jacob who were born to him in Paddan-Aram.

The death of Isaac

27And Jacob came to Isaac his father at Mamre, at Kiriath-Arba (that is, Hebron), where Abraham and Isaac had stayed. 28And the days of Isaac were a hundred and eighty years. 29And Isaac breathed his last and died, and he was gathered to his people, old and full of days, and Esau and Jacob, his sons, buried him.

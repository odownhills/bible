Genesis 28

Watch Listen only

Isaac sends Jacob to Paddan-Aram

1And Isaac called Jacob and blessed him, and he commanded him and said to him, "You must not take a wife from the daughters of Canaan. 2Get up, go to Paddan-Aram, to the house of Bethuel, your mother's father, and take a wife for yourself from there, from the daughters of Laban, your mother's brother. 3And may God Almighty bless you and make you fruitful and multiply you, and you will become an assembly of peoples. 4And may he give you the blessing of Abraham, to you and to your offspring with you, so that you take possession of the land where you are staying, which God gave to Abraham." 5And Isaac sent Jacob, and he went to Paddan-Aram, to Laban the son of Bethuel the Aramean, the brother of Rebekah, the mother of Jacob and Esau.

Esau takes another wife

6And Esau saw that Isaac had blessed Jacob and sent him away to Paddan-Aram to take a wife for himself from there, and that when he blessed him he commanded him, saying, "You must not take a wife from the daughters of Canaan," 7and that Jacob listened to his father and to his mother and went to Paddan-Aram. 8And Esau saw that the daughters of Canaan were bad in the eyes of Isaac his father, 9and Esau went to Ishmael and took Mahalath the daughter of Ishmael the son of Abraham, the sister of Nebaioth, as his wife, in addition to his wives.

Jacob's dream

10And Jacob went out from Beersheba and went toward Haran. 11And he came upon the place and spent the night there, because the sun had set. And he took one of the stones of the place and put it at his head, and he lay down in that place. 12And he dreamed, and look, a stairway [alt: a ladder] was set up on the earth, and its top reached to the heavens, and look, angels of God were going up and coming down on it. 13And look, Yahweh was standing beside him [alt: above it], and he said, "I am Yahweh, the God of Abraham your father and the God of Isaac. The land on which you are lying, to you I will give it, and to your offspring. 14And your offspring will be like the dust of the earth, and you will spread out westward and eastward and northward and southward, and all the families of the ground will be blessed in you and in your offspring. 15And look, I am with you, and I will watch over you wherever you go, and I will bring you back to this ground, for I will not abandon you until I have done what I have spoken to you." 16And Jacob awoke from his sleep and said, "Surely Yahweh is in this place, and I did not know it." 17And he was afraid and said, "How fearsome this place is! This is none other than the house of God, and this is the gate of the heavens."

Jacob's vow at Bethel

18And Jacob rose early in the morning and took the stone that he had put at his head, and he set it up as a pillar and poured oil on its top. [In Hebrew "pillar" is from the same root as "set up" (28:12) and "standing" (28:13).] 19And he named that place Bethel, but Luz was the name of the city at the first. [In Hebrew the name "Bethel" means "house of God."] 20And Jacob made a vow, saying, "If God will be with me and watch over me on this way that I am going and give me bread to eat and clothing to wear, 21and I return in peace to my father's house, then Yahweh will be God to me, 22and this stone that I have set up as a pillar will be the house of God [alt: and Yahweh will be God to me, then this stone that I have set up as a pillar will be the house of God], and of all that you give me I will certainly give a tenth to you."

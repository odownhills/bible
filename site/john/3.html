John 3

Listen only

Nicodemus comes at night

1Now there was a man from the Pharisees; his name was Nicodemus, a ruler of the Jews [alt: the Judeans]. 2This one came to him at night and said to him, "Rabbi, we know that you have come from God as a teacher; for no one can do these signs that you yourself are doing unless God is with him."

3Jesus answered and said to him, "Amen, amen [alt: Truly, truly], I say to you, unless someone is born from above [alt: born again], he cannot see the kingdom of God." 4Nicodemus says to him, "How can a human being be born when he is old? He cannot enter his mother's womb a second time and be born, can he?"

5Jesus answered, "Amen, amen, I say to you, unless someone is born from water and spirit, he cannot enter the kingdom of God. 6What has been born from the flesh is flesh, and what has been born from the spirit is spirit. 7Do not be surprised that I said to you, 'You all must be born from above.' 8The wind [alt: The spirit] blows where it wants, and you hear its sound [alt: its voice], but you do not know where it comes from and where it goes; it is this way with everyone who has been born from the spirit." [In Greek "wind" and "spirit" are the same word.]

9Nicodemus answered and said to him, "How can these things come to be?" 10Jesus answered and said to him, "You are the teacher of Israel, and you do not know these things? 11Amen, amen, I say to you, we speak what we know and we testify to what we have seen, and you all do not receive our testimony. 12If I have said the earthly things to you all and you do not believe, how will you believe if I say the heavenly things to you? 13And no one has gone up into heaven except the one who came down from heaven, the Son of Man [alt: the Son of Man, who is in heaven, as other manuscripts read]. 14And just as Moses lifted up the snake in the wilderness, in this way the Son of Man must be lifted up, 15so that everyone who believes may have eternal life in him [alt: everyone who believes in him may have eternal life].

God loved the world

16"For God loved the world in this way [alt: so much], that he gave the only Son [alt: the only-born Son], so that everyone who believes in him might not perish but have eternal life. 17For God did not send the Son into the world so that he might judge the world, but so that the world might be saved through him. 18The one who believes in him is not judged; but the one who does not believe has already been judged, because he has not believed in the name of the only Son of God.

19"And this is the judgment: that the light has come into the world, and human beings loved the darkness rather than the light [alt: more than the light], for their works were evil. 20For everyone who practices worthless things hates the light and does not come to the light, so that his works may not be exposed; 21but the one who does the truth comes to the light, so that his works may be revealed, that they have been worked in God [alt: because they have been worked in God]."

John and the bridegroom

22After these things Jesus and his disciples came into the Judean land, and there he was spending time with them and baptizing [alt: immersing]. 23And John also was baptizing in Aenon near Salim, because there was much water there, and people were arriving and being baptized; 24for John had not yet been thrown into prison.

25So a debate arose from John's disciples with a Jew [alt: with Jews, as other manuscripts read] concerning cleansing. 26And they came to John and said to him, "Rabbi, the one who was with you across the Jordan, to whom you yourself have testified—look, this one is baptizing, and everyone is coming to him."

27John answered and said, "A human being cannot receive even one thing unless it has been given to him from heaven. 28You yourselves testify for me that I said, 'I myself am not the Christ,' but, 'I have been sent ahead of that one.' 29The one who has the bride is the bridegroom; but the friend of the bridegroom, the one who stands and hears him [alt: listens to him], rejoices with joy because of the bridegroom's voice. So this joy of mine has been made full. 30That one must grow, but I must become less.

The one who comes from above

31"The one who comes from above is above all; the one who is from the earth is from the earth and speaks from the earth. The one who comes from heaven is above all; 32what he has seen and heard, to this he testifies, and no one receives his testimony. 33The one who received his testimony set his seal to this: that God is true. 34For the one whom God sent speaks the words of God, for he does not give the spirit by measure. 35The Father loves the Son and has given all things into his hand. 36The one who believes in the Son has eternal life; but the one who disobeys the Son [alt: refuses to believe the Son] will not see life, but the anger of God remains on him."

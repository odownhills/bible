The wedding in Cana

1And on the third day a wedding took place in Cana of Galilee, and the mother of Jesus was there. 2And Jesus and his disciples were also invited [alt: called] to the wedding.

3And when wine ran short, the mother of Jesus says to him, "They have no wine." 4And Jesus says to her, "What do I have to do with you, woman? [alt: What is that to me and to you, woman?] My hour has not yet come." 5His mother says to the servants, "Do whatever he says to you."

6Now there were six stone water jars set there according to the cleansing [alt: for the cleansing] of the Jews [alt: the Judeans], each holding two or three measures. 7Jesus says to them, "Fill the water jars with water." And they filled them up to the top. 8And he says to them, "Now draw some out and carry it to the master of the banquet [alt: the head steward]." And they carried it.

9And when the master of the banquet tasted the water that had become wine, and did not know where it was from (but the servants knew, the ones who had drawn the water), the master of the banquet calls the bridegroom 10and says to him, "Every human being sets out the fine wine first, and when people have become drunk, the lesser; you yourself have kept the fine wine until now."

11Jesus did this as the beginning of the signs, in Cana of Galilee, and he revealed his glory, and his disciples believed in him.

12After this he went down to Capernaum, he and his mother and his brothers and his disciples, and they stayed [alt: remained] there not many days.

Jesus in the temple

13And the Passover of the Jews was near, and Jesus went up to Jerusalem. 14And in the temple he found those who were selling cattle and sheep and doves, and the coin changers sitting, 15and he made a whip out of cords [alt: something like a whip out of cords, as other manuscripts read] and drove them all out of the temple, both the sheep and the cattle [alt: along with the sheep and the cattle], and he poured out the coins of the money changers and overturned the tables, 16and to those who were selling the doves he said, "Take these things away from here; do not make my Father's house a house of trade." 17His disciples remembered that it is written, "Zeal for your house will devour me."

18So the Jews answered and said to him, "What sign do you show us, since you are doing these things?" 19Jesus answered and said to them, "Destroy this sanctuary [alt: temple], and in three days I will raise it." 20So the Jews said, "This sanctuary was built in forty-six years [alt: has been under construction for forty-six years], and you yourself will raise it in three days?" 21But that one was speaking concerning the sanctuary of his body. 22So when he was raised from the dead [alt: rose from the dead], his disciples remembered that he had said this, and they believed the scripture and the word that Jesus had said.

At the Passover in Jerusalem

23And when he was in Jerusalem at the Passover, at the festival, many believed in his name, seeing his signs that he was doing. 24But Jesus himself did not entrust himself to them, because he knew everyone 25and because he had no need for anyone to testify concerning a human being; for he himself knew what was in a human being. [In Greek "entrust" in verse 24 is the same verb as "believe" in verse 23.]

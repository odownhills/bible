The Word

1In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God. 2This one was in the beginning with God. 3All things came to be through him, and apart from him not even one thing came to be. What has come to be 4in him was life [alt: and apart from him not even one thing came to be that has come to be. In him was life], and the life was the light of human beings. 5And the light shines in the darkness, and the darkness did not overcome it [alt: did not grasp it].

A man sent from God

6There arose a man, sent from God; his name was John. 7This one came for testimony, so that he might testify concerning the light, so that all might believe through him. 8That one was not the light, but came so that he might testify concerning the light.

The true light

9The true light, which gives light to every human being, was coming into the world [alt: There was the true light, which gives light to every human being who comes into the world]. 10He was in the world, and the world came to be through him, and the world did not know him. 11He came to what was his own, and those who were his own did not take him in. 12But as many as received him, to them he gave authority [alt: the right] to become children of God, to those who believe in his name, 13who were born not from blood, nor from the will of flesh, nor from the will of a man [alt: of a husband], but from God.

The Word became flesh

14And the Word became flesh and pitched his tent among us [alt: lived among us], and we gazed at his glory, glory as of the only Son from the Father [alt: the only-born Son; or as of an only son from a father], full of grace and truth— 15John testifies concerning him and has cried out, saying, "This was the one of whom I said, 'The one who comes after me has come to be ahead of me, because he was before me'"— 16because from his fullness we all received, and grace in place of grace [alt: grace upon grace], 17because the law was given through Moses; grace and truth came to be through Jesus Christ. 18No one has ever seen God; the only Son, God [alt: the only-born God; or the only Son, as other manuscripts read], the one who is in the Father's lap [alt: at the Father's side], that one made him known.

The testimony of John

19And this is the testimony of John, when the Jews [alt: the Judeans] sent priests and Levites to him from Jerusalem so that they might question him, "Who are you?" 20And he confessed and did not deny, and he confessed, "I myself am not the Christ." 21And they questioned him, "What then? Are you Elijah?" And he says, "I am not." "Are you the prophet?" And he answered, "No." 22So they said to him, "Who are you?—so that we may give an answer to those who sent us. What do you say concerning yourself?" 23He said, "I am a voice of one shouting in the wilderness, 'Make straight the way of the Lord,' just as Isaiah the prophet said."

24And they had been sent from the Pharisees [alt: And some of the Pharisees had been sent]. 25And they questioned him and said to him, "Why then do you baptize [alt: immerse], if you are not the Christ, nor Elijah, nor the prophet?" 26John answered them, saying, "I myself baptize in water [alt: with water]; among you stands one whom you yourselves do not know, 27the one who comes after me, the strap of whose sandal I am not worthy to undo." 28These things took place in Bethany [alt: Bethabara, as other manuscripts read] across the Jordan, where John was baptizing.

The lamb of God

29The next day he sees Jesus coming toward him and says, "Look, the lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world. 30This is the one about whom I myself said, 'After me comes a man who has come to be ahead of me, because he was before me.' 31And I myself did not know him, but so that he might be revealed to Israel, for this reason I myself came baptizing in water."

32And John testified, saying, "I have gazed at the spirit coming down as a dove from heaven, and it remained on him. 33And I myself did not know him, but the one who sent me to baptize in water, that one said to me, 'Whoever you see the spirit coming down and remaining on, this is the one who baptizes in holy spirit [alt: in the holy spirit].' 34And I myself have seen and have testified that this is the Son of God [alt: the chosen one of God, as other manuscripts read]."

The first disciples

35The next day John was standing again, and two of his disciples, 36and looking at Jesus as he walked, he says, "Look, the lamb of God." 37And the two disciples heard him speaking, and they followed Jesus. 38And Jesus turned, and gazing at them as they followed, he says to them, "What are you seeking?" And they said to him, "Rabbi" (which, when translated, means "Teacher"), "where are you staying [alt: remaining]?" 39He says to them, "Come, and you will see." So they came and saw where he was staying, and they stayed with him that day. It was about the tenth hour.

40Andrew, the brother of Simon Peter, was one of the two who heard from John and followed him. 41This one first finds his own brother Simon and says to him, "We have found the Messiah" (which is, when translated, "Christ"). [Both words mean "anointed one."] 42He brought him to Jesus. Looking at him, Jesus said, "You are Simon the son of John [alt: of Jonah, as other manuscripts read]; you will be called Cephas" (which is interpreted "Peter"). [Both names mean "rock."]

Philip and Nathanael

43The next day he wanted to go out into Galilee, and he finds Philip. And Jesus says to him, "Follow me." 44Now Philip was from Bethsaida, from the city of Andrew and Peter. 45Philip finds Nathanael and says to him, "The one of whom Moses wrote in the law, and the prophets, we have found: Jesus, son of Joseph, the one from Nazareth." 46And Nathanael said to him, "Can anything good be from Nazareth?" Philip says to him, "Come and see."

47Jesus saw Nathanael coming toward him and says concerning him, "Look, truly an Israelite, in whom there is no deceit." 48Nathanael says to him, "Where do you know me from?" Jesus answered and said to him, "Before Philip called you, while you were under the fig tree, I saw you." 49Nathanael answered him, "Rabbi, you are the Son of God; you are king of Israel." 50Jesus answered and said to him, "Because I said to you that I saw you underneath the fig tree, do you believe [alt: you believe]? You will see greater things than these." 51And he says to him, "Amen, amen [alt: Truly, truly], I say to you all, you will see heaven opened and the angels of God going up and coming down on the Son of Man."

John 6

Listen only

Loaves for five thousand

1After these things Jesus went away across the Sea of Galilee, of Tiberias. 2And a large crowd was following him, because they were seeing the signs that he was doing on those who were sick. 3And Jesus went up onto the mountain, and there he was sitting with his disciples. 4Now the Passover, the festival of the Jews [alt: the Judeans], was near.

5So Jesus, lifting his eyes and gazing at a large crowd coming toward him, says to Philip, "Where are we to buy loaves from, so that these may eat?" 6But he was saying this, putting him to the test; for he himself knew what he was about to do. 7Philip answered him, "Loaves worth two hundred denarii are not enough for them, so that each one may receive a little." 8One of his disciples, Andrew, the brother of Simon Peter, says to him, 9"There is a little boy here who has five barley loaves and two fish [alt: two small fish]; but what are these for so many?"

10Jesus said, "Make the people recline." Now there was much grass in the place. So the men reclined, in number about five thousand. 11So Jesus took the loaves, and having given thanks, he distributed them to those who were reclining; likewise also from the fish, as much as they wanted. 12And when they had had their fill, he says to his disciples, "Gather the broken pieces that are left over, so that nothing may perish [alt: may be lost]." 13So they gathered them and filled twelve baskets with broken pieces from the five barley loaves, pieces that were left over by those who had eaten.

14So the people, seeing the sign that he did, were saying, "This is truly the prophet who is coming into the world." 15So Jesus, knowing that they were about to come and seize him so that they might make him king, withdrew again onto the mountain, he himself alone.

Jesus walks on the sea

16And when it became evening, his disciples went down to the sea, 17and stepping into a boat, they were coming across the sea to Capernaum. And darkness had already come to be, and Jesus had not yet come to them, 18and the sea was being roused, as a great wind was blowing.

19So, having rowed about twenty-five or thirty stadia, they see Jesus walking on the sea [alt: by the sea] and coming to be near the boat, and they feared. 20But he says to them, "It is I [alt: I am]; do not fear." 21So they wanted to take him into the boat, and immediately the boat came to be at the land to which they were going.

The crowd seeks Jesus

22The next day the crowd that was standing across the sea saw that there had been no other small boat there except one, and that Jesus had not entered the boat with his disciples, but his disciples had gone away alone. 23Other small boats came from Tiberias near the place where they had eaten the bread after the Lord had given thanks. 24So when the crowd saw that Jesus was not there, nor his disciples, they themselves stepped into the small boats and came to Capernaum, seeking Jesus.

Bread from heaven

25And having found him across the sea, they said to him, "Rabbi, when did you come to be here?" 26Jesus answered them and said, "Amen, amen [alt: Truly, truly], I say to you, you seek me not because you saw signs, but because you ate from the loaves and were satisfied. 27Do not work for the food that perishes, but for the food that remains into eternal life, which the Son of Man will give you; for on this one the Father, God, has set his seal."

28So they said to him, "What are we to do, so that we may work the works of God?" 29Jesus answered and said to them, "This is the work of God: that you should believe in the one whom that one sent."

30So they said to him, "What sign then do you yourself do, so that we may see and believe you? What do you work? 31Our fathers ate the manna in the wilderness, just as it is written, 'He gave them bread from heaven to eat.'"

32So Jesus said to them, "Amen, amen, I say to you, it is not Moses who has given you the bread from heaven, but my Father gives you the true bread from heaven; 33for the bread of God is the one that comes down from heaven [alt: the one who comes down from heaven] and gives life to the world."

34So they said to him, "Sir [alt: Lord], always give us this bread."

The bread of life

35Jesus said to them, "I am the bread of life; the one who comes to me will certainly not be hungry, and the one who believes in me will certainly never be thirsty. 36But I said to you that you have both seen me and do not believe.

37"Everything that the Father gives me will come to me, and the one who comes to me I will certainly not drive out, 38because I have come down from heaven not so that I may do my will, but the will of the one who sent me. 39And this is the will of the one who sent me: that everything that he has given me—I should lose nothing of it, but should raise it up on the last day. 40For this is the will of my Father: that everyone who sees the Son and believes in him should have eternal life, and I myself will raise him up on the last day." [In Greek "lose" in verse 39 is the same verb as "perish" in verses 12 and 27.]

The Jews grumble

41So the Jews were grumbling concerning him, because he said, "I am the bread that came down from heaven," 42and they were saying, "Is this not Jesus, the son of Joseph, whose father and mother we ourselves know? How does he now say, 'I have come down from heaven'?"

43Jesus answered and said to them, "Do not grumble with one another. 44No one can come to me unless the Father who sent me draws him, and I myself will raise him up on the last day. 45It is written in the prophets, 'And they will all be taught by God.' Everyone who has heard from the Father and learned comes to me. 46Not that anyone has seen the Father, except the one who is from God; this one has seen the Father.

47"Amen, amen, I say to you, the one who believes has eternal life. 48I am the bread of life. 49Your fathers ate the manna in the wilderness, and they died; 50this is the bread that comes down from heaven, so that anyone may eat from it and not die. 51I am the living bread that came down from heaven; if anyone eats from this bread, he will live forever; and indeed the bread that I myself will give is my flesh, for the life of the world."

Flesh and blood

52So the Jews were fighting with one another, saying, "How can this one give us his flesh to eat?" 53So Jesus said to them, "Amen, amen, I say to you, unless you eat the flesh of the Son of Man and drink his blood, you do not have life in yourselves. 54The one who feeds on my flesh [alt: chews my flesh] and drinks my blood has eternal life, and I myself will raise him up on the last day. 55For my flesh is true food, and my blood is true drink.

56"The one who feeds on my flesh and drinks my blood remains in me, and I myself in him. 57Just as the living Father sent me and I myself live because of the Father, so the one who feeds on me, that one also will live because of me. 58This is the bread that came down from heaven, not as the fathers ate and died; the one who feeds on this bread will live forever."

59He said these things in a synagogue, teaching in Capernaum.

Many disciples go back

60So many of his disciples, having heard, said, "This word is hard; who can hear it [alt: listen to it, or listen to him]?" 61But Jesus, knowing in himself that his disciples were grumbling concerning this, said to them, "Does this cause you to stumble? 62If then you see the Son of Man going up where he was before—? 63The spirit is the one that makes alive; the flesh is of no benefit at all; the words that I myself have spoken to you are spirit and are life. 64But there are some of you who do not believe." For Jesus knew from the beginning who those were who did not believe, and who the one was who would hand him over [alt: betray him]. 65And he was saying, "For this reason I have said to you that no one can come to me unless it has been given to him from the Father."

66From this [alt: From this time] many of his disciples went back and were no longer walking with him.

The twelve

67So Jesus said to the twelve, "You yourselves do not also want to go, do you?" 68Simon Peter answered him, "Lord, to whom will we go away? You have words of eternal life, 69and we ourselves have believed and have come to know that you are the Holy One of God [alt: the Christ, the Son of the living God, as other manuscripts read]." 70Jesus answered them, "Did I myself not choose you, the twelve? And one of you is a devil." 71Now he was speaking of Judas, son of Simon Iscariot; for this one was about to hand him over—one of the twelve.

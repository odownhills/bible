# Notes on Job 23, for the translator of Job 24

Text followed: the Masoretic Text; the chapter has no Ketiv and Qere and no Hebrew paragraph break. Job's eighth speech is 23:2-24:25: chapter 23 opens with a quotation mark and ends with none. God is "he" from 23:3 and is not named until 23:16; nothing is added.

Brackets: five, each with an entry in the decision log: 23:2 "defiance" [alt: bitter, the old Syriac and Latin] (close); 23:2 "my hand" [alt: his hand, the old Greek and Syriac] (close); 23:12 "more than what is allotted to me" [alt: within me, the old Greek and Latin] (close); 23:13 "he is one" [alt: he is of one mind] (close); 23:17, the whole verse (ask).

Places where the old copies differ, not bracketed:
- 23:7 the old Greek and Latin versions have "my case" for "from my judge" (the same letters): "I would carry my case through forever". Either way he is cleared for good.
- 23:9 the old Syriac and Latin have "I turn" for "he wraps himself", and the Syriac "I seek him" for "when he is at work". Either way Job does not see him there.

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 23:3 the Hebrew is "If only I knew and could find him"; "where" is how English says it.
- 23:6b "consider" is the short form of "set the heart" (4:20; 1:8), as the Job 4 line said. Taking "strength" from the first half as its object ("he would put strength in me") is not noted: this writer's short form is "consider" (4:20; 34:23).
- 23:7 "would be arguing": the Hebrew has no tense (a participle); it is set by "I would" in 23:4-6.
- 23:8-9 the four words are "front", "behind", "left" and "right", which are also Hebrew's east, west, north and south (Genesis 14:15, "north of"). With "I go" the compass is used; either way he looks in every direction.
- 23:9 "wraps himself": the verb of Psalm 65:13 and 73:6. A sense "turns aside" stands nowhere else in Scripture.
- 23:10 "For": the Hebrew word can also be "But". "The way I take" is "the way with me". "When he has tested me": the Hebrew has no "when" or "if" ("he has tested me; I will come out like gold"); either way he says he will pass the test.
- 23:12 "what is allotted to me" is the word that is "limit" at 14:5, 13 and "allowance" in Genesis 47:22; the Job 14 line thought of "portion" or "decree". One English wording is used for 23:12 and 23:14, as the Hebrew has one word.
- 23:13 "What his soul desires, he does": the Hebrew is "and his soul desires, and he does".
- 23:14 "in his mind" is "with him", as at 10:13.
- 23:16 the Hebrew sets "God" and "the Almighty" first in their clauses; "It is God who ..." would say more than the main text of 23:17 needs.
- 23:15, 17 "before him", "before the darkness" and "from my face" are one Hebrew word ("face") three times; "in the face of darkness" (17:12) is the same Hebrew as "before the darkness".

For Job 24: the speech goes on with no break, so the chapter opens with a quotation mark and closes the speech at 24:25. 24:1 takes up 23:12 and 23:8-9 in the same words: "hidden away" (the verb of 23:12) and "gaze on" (23:9), so keep both; 24:1 has a Ketiv and Qere. Keep "the Almighty", "God" for all three names, "darkness" and "deep darkness" (23:17; 24:15-17 has "twilight", "darkness", "the shadow of death" and "terrors", as listed), "pathway" and "way" (24:13 beside 23:10-11), "take pledges from" (24:3, 9), "seize" for the verb of 20:19 (24:2, 9, 19), "cry for help" (24:12), and at 24:12 the short form of "consider" again beside the word that is "wrongdoing" at 1:22. 24:25 "If not, then, who" is the wording of 9:24.

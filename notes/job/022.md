# Notes on Job 22, for the translator of Job 23

Text followed: the Masoretic Text; the chapter has no Ketiv and Qere, and its only Hebrew paragraph break is at the end. Eliphaz's third speech is 22:2-30, all in this chapter. Words quoted inside it take single marks: Job's supposed words (22:13-14), the men of old (22:17), the righteous (22:20), Job's word (22:29).

Brackets: four, each with an entry in the decision log: 22:4 "for your fear of God" [alt: out of fear of you] (clear); 22:17 "do for them" [alt: do to them] (close); 22:29 "you say, 'Rise up,'" [alt: you will say, 'It was pride'] (ask); 22:30 "one who is not innocent" [alt: the innocent man, as the old Greek and Latin read] (close). Two notes on sound: 22:21 ("get to know" and "be of use", 22:2) and 22:25 ("gold ore" and "among the rocks"; "dust" and "Ophir").

Places where the old copies differ, not bracketed:
- 22:6 some Hebrew copies have "your brother" for "your brothers".
- 22:9 the old versions have "you have crushed" for "have been crushed"; in a list of what Job did, the sense is the same.
- 22:11 the old Greek has "the light has turned to darkness for you".
- 22:17 the old Greek has "us" for "them"; the Hebrew turns from their words to a report of them, and "asked" carries that.
- 22:18 the old Greek has "far from him" for "far from me"; the Hebrew repeats 21:16 word for word.
- 22:20 the old Greek has "their substance" for the word put as "those who rose against us" (only here).
- 22:23 the old Greek has "and humble yourself" for "you will be built up".

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 22:2b can also be put together as "that one who has insight should be of use to him"; either way a man is of no use to God.
- 22:6-9 tenses: what Job is said to have done again and again is in the English perfect, as at 4:3-4. 22:8 has no verb in its first half; "was" and "lived" are set by the lines around it. Who the strong and favored man is (Job, or those he sided with) the Hebrew does not say.
- 22:10 "traps" and "dread" begin alike in Hebrew; not noted.
- 22:11 can also be read as a question ("Or do you not see the darkness, and the abundance of water that covers you?"); either way darkness and water are on him. 22:11b returns word for word at 38:34.
- 22:12 "as high as the heavens": or "in the height of the heavens"; 11:8 is followed.
- 22:15 "keep to": the verb is "keep" or "watch"; with a path it is to follow it.
- 22:16 "seized": the verb of 16:8 ("shriveled me up" [alt: seized me]); the Job 16 log quoted this verse with "seized". "On whose foundation a river was poured out": or "whose foundation was poured out as a river"; one picture.
- 22:19-20: the imperfects are put in the English present, as at 15:20-24. 22:20b keeps the wording quoted in the Job 4 log; the word can also be "their abundance" (the alt at 4:21).
- 22:21 "good will come to you": the Hebrew form is irregular and can also be read as the noun "your yield will be good". "Be at peace": the verb of 9:4 ("come out whole").
- 22:23b is read as a second "if"; 22:24 is a command in the Hebrew ("And lay"), with 22:25 as what follows from it. Some read 22:24 as a promise (gold as common as dust); the vowels make it a command.
- 22:25 "your gold ore": the same letters and vowels can be "against your foes"; after 22:24 the sentence does not allow it.
- 22:29 "people": the Hebrew verb is active with no subject and no object named ("they have brought low"), put in the passive, as at 4:19.
- 22:30 "he will be delivered": the one who is not innocent; "deliver" and "be delivered" are one Hebrew verb in two forms.

For Job 23: Job's eighth speech is 23:2-24:25, so chapter 23 opens with a quotation mark and ends with none. Job answers this chapter in its own words, and the English should let that be heard: "Get to know him" and "return to" (22:21, 23) against "If only I knew where to find him" (23:3); "come into judgment with" and "corrects" (22:4) beside "set out my case", "argument", "contend with" (23:4-7, as the Job 13 line planned); "keep to the path" (22:15) and "his way I have kept" (23:11, the same verb: "keep to"); "teaching from his mouth ... his words" (22:22) and "the words of his mouth" (23:12); "gold ore" and "gold of Ophir" (22:24) beside "gold" (23:10); "dread ... dismays you" and "darkness ... covers you" (22:10-11) return at 23:15-17 ("dismayed", "dread", "darkness", "cover"); pledges, the naked, the hungry, widows and orphans (22:6-9) return at 24:3-10 ("take pledges from", 24:3, 9). "The Almighty", "God" for all three names, and "And Job answered and said:" as before.

# Notes on Job 24, for the translator of Job 25

Text followed: the Masoretic Text, read by its Ketiv; one Hebrew paragraph break, at the end. Two Qere change only the spelling, so nothing is noted: 24:1 (the written word is "those who know him" spelled short, as at 15:15) and 24:6 (two forms of "harvest"). Job's eighth speech (23:2-24:25) ends here: the chapter opens with a quotation mark and closes the speech. God is named at 24:1 and 24:12 and is "he" with no name in 24:23, as the Hebrew has it.

Brackets: four, each with an entry in the decision log: 24:1 "when times are not hidden away from the Almighty" [alt: Why are times not stored up by the Almighty] (ask); 24:9 "take pledges from the afflicted" [alt: take the infant of the afflicted as a pledge] (close); 24:12 "makes no charge of wrongdoing" [alt: does not consider the prayer, the old Syriac] (close); 24:22 "drags away the mighty" [alt: prolongs the life of the mighty] (ask). One note on sound, 24:18 ("swift" and "is cursed").

Places where the old copies differ, not bracketed:
- 24:2 the old Greek has "a flock with its shepherd" for "a flock and pasture it"; it also names "the wicked" as the subject.
- 24:6 the old Greek and Latin have "a field that is not theirs" for "his fodder" (the same letters divided in two); either way they reap on another man's land.
- 24:12 the old Syriac has "the dying" for "men" (other vowels); either way the city groans, and the next words name the wounded.
- 24:21 the old Greek has "he did no good to the barren woman" for "feeds on"; either way he wrongs her.
- 24:24 the old Greek has a plant ("like mallow in the heat") for "like all others".

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 24:2-12 "they" is first the oppressors (24:2-4, 9) and then the afflicted (24:5-8, 10-12); the Hebrew names neither. "Some" (24:2) is the unnamed plural subject; the paragraphs mark the changes, and nothing else is added.
- 24:5 the Hebrew has no "like": the afflicted are called wild donkeys. "Food" is the word that is "prey" at 4:11 (as in Proverbs 31:15). "Each one's" is the Hebrew "to him", one of them at a time; "his fodder" (24:6) is left as open as the Hebrew (the wicked man's, or each one's own).
- 24:11 "walls": the word stands only here, and "rows" (of trees) is also possible. "Press oil" stands only here; the same letters can come from the word for noon ("they spend the noon between their walls", as the old Latin version has it). The winepresses beside it decide for oil. Obay may want this one bracketed.
- 24:12 "makes no charge of wrongdoing": the verb can also be the short form of "consider" (23:6), "does not consider it wrongdoing"; either way God lets the wrong pass. No object is supplied.
- 24:13 "These": the Hebrew pronoun is "they", set first; it can point back to the men of 24:2-12 or on to the murderer, the adulterer and the thief.
- 24:14 "At first light": the Hebrew is "at the light".
- 24:16 "they seal themselves in": "houses that they marked for themselves by day" is not noted; in Scripture the verb is always to seal or shut up (9:7; 14:17; 37:7).
- 24:17 can also be put together as "deep darkness is morning to them"; either way day and night have changed places for them. 24:17b can also be "when anyone recognizes them, there are the terrors of the shadow of death".
- 24:18-24 are statements in the Hebrew (its "not" at 24:18, 20 and 22 is the one for statements, not for wishes), and nothing marks them as the friends' words, so they stand as Job's own, with no quotation marks. They say that the wicked die as all do (24:24 "like all others"), as in 21:23-26, 32-33. "He" and "they" change as the Hebrew has them.
- 24:20 "the maggot is sweet to him": the wording of 21:33. "The maggot finds him sweet" would need a sense the verb has nowhere else in Scripture.
- 24:22 "he rises, and is not sure of life": the same man rises and is unsure, as in Deuteronomy 28:66. It can also be put together as "he rises, and no one is sure of life". 24:23 "He" is God; "his eyes are on their ways" is left as open as the Hebrew (to guard them, or to watch them).
- 24:24 "wither": the verb of 14:2 and 18:16; "are cut off" is the other verb with these letters. "Shut away": the verb is "shut" (5:16), only here in this form.

For Job 25: Bildad's third speech is 25:2-6, all in the chapter. 25:4 repeats 9:2 and 15:14 ("be righteous with God", "be pure", "one born of a woman"; 4:17 "a mortal"), and 25:5 repeats 15:15 ("pure in his eyes"). Keep "dread" (25:2), "on high" (16:19), "troops" (19:12), "light" (25:3, after 24:13-16), "a mortal", "a human being" for the Hebrew "son of a human being" (16:21), "maggot" (25:6, as at 24:20) and "worm" for the second word, as the Job 7 line planned.

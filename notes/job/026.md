# Notes on Job 26, for the translator of Job 27

Text followed: the Masoretic Text, read by its Ketiv; one Hebrew paragraph break, at the end. Three Qere, none noted: 26:12 (two spellings of "his understanding"); 26:14 "his way" (the Qere "his ways") and "his might" (the Qere "his mighty deeds") change only the number, as at 14:5. Job's ninth speech here is 26:2-14: the chapter opens with a quotation mark and closes with one at 26:14, because 27:1 gives Job a new heading ("And Job again took up his discourse and said"), so 27:2 opens a new quotation. "You" in 26:2-4 is singular (Bildad). God is "him" and "he" all through and is never named, as in Bildad's 25:2-3.

Brackets: six, each with an entry in the decision log: 26:4 "To whom have you told words" [alt: With whose help] (close); 26:5 "The dead" [alt: The Rephaim, the giant people of old] (clear); 26:9 "the face of the throne" [alt: the face of the full moon] (close); 26:12 "he stirred up the sea" [alt: he stilled the sea] (ask); 26:13 "By his wind" [alt: By his spirit] (close); 26:13 "pierced" [alt: brought forth] (close).

Places where the old copies differ, not bracketed:
- 26:5 the old Greek makes the line a question and has "its neighbors" for "those who live in them".
- 26:13 the old Greek has "the bars of heaven fear him" for the first half (the word for "fleeing" is close to the word for "bar"); its "he put to death" for the second half is cited in the decision log.
- 26:14 the old Greek has "a drop of a word" for "a whisper of a word".

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 26:2-3 can also be put as questions ("How have you helped ...?"); either way Job says that Bildad has been no help. "How" is repeated in 26:2b, as "How often" is at 21:17-18. "The one without strength" and "the one without wisdom" are left as open as the Hebrew (Job, as Bildad sees him). "Strength" and "power" are kept apart, as the Job 12 line planned.
- 26:4 "breath" is the word of "the breath of life", not the word that is "wind" in 26:13.
- 26:5 "writhe": the form is passive ("are made to writhe"); the verb is the one of 15:20, and the same form is "brought to birth" at 15:7, which does not fit here. The Hebrew accents divide the line after "writhe". "And those who live in them" can go with "beneath" (the dead are below the sea and its creatures) or be a second subject (the creatures of the waters writhe too); the English can be heard both ways.
- 26:6 "Abaddon" is from the verb "perish" (4:7; 28:22 will need it as a name again). "There is no covering" is the wording of 24:7, where "naked" stands too.
- 26:7 "the north": the northern sky, or the mountain in the far north of Isaiah 14:13; the English is as open. "The waste" is the word of 6:18 and 12:24 and of Genesis 1:2 ("desolate"); here it stands beside "nothing". "On nothing": the Hebrew is "on without-anything".
- 26:9 "closes off": the verb is "seize", "hold" (16:12; 17:9), only here in this form; it is "bar" of doors in Nehemiah 7:3. "Spreading" stands only here.
- 26:10 "boundary" is the word that is "limit" at 14:5 and 14:13; "limit" is kept here for the word of 11:7, since the two stand in one line. "As far as the limit of light with darkness" is where light borders on darkness; taken of time ("until light and darkness end") it is not noted, since the word is a limit in space at 11:7 and 28:3. The perfect is "has drawn"; those of 26:12-13 are past, like "bowed low" at 9:13.
- 26:11 "quake" stands only here; 9:6 has "shudder" beside the pillars of the earth, another verb.
- 26:12 "wounded" is the verb of 5:18 ("he wounds, and his hands heal"); with an enemy as its object it is a shattering blow (Psalm 68:21; 110:5-6). Obay may want a stronger word. "Rahab" has no bracket (the Job 9 line).
- 26:13 "are fair": the Hebrew has a noun, "the heavens are fairness", only here; no verb is supplied. "Snake" is the word of Genesis 3:1; "fleeing" is from the verb "flee" (9:25; 14:2), and Isaiah 27:1 calls Leviathan by the same two words.
- 26:14 "edges": the ends of a thing (28:24 "the ends of the earth"). "Is heard of him": it can also be "we hear", and "of him" can be "in it" (his way). "Understand" is the verb that is "look closely" at 11:11 and 23:15; here it answers "his understanding" (26:12), which is from the same root.
- 26:5-14 answers Bildad with his own subjects: "his heights" and the moon (25:2, 5) against Sheol, the throne and the cloud; nothing in the Hebrew marks these lines as anyone's but Job's.

For Job 27: 27:1 is a new heading, not the usual formula: "And Job again took up his discourse and said" (the same at 29:1), so 27:2 opens a new quotation; the speech runs through chapter 27 (and chapter 28 has no heading). Keep "breath" for the word of 26:4 and "spirit" beside it at 27:3 (the Job 12 line), "hold fast to" and "let go of" (27:6; 2:3, 9; 7:19), "blamelessness" (27:5; 2:3), "insult" for the verb of 27:6 (16:10), "the godless man" and "cut off" (27:8; 8:13; 6:9), "take delight in" (27:10; 22:26), "in vain" (27:12; 9:29), "portion" and "inheritance" (27:13 repeats 20:29 almost word for word), "survivor" (27:15), "terrors", "steal away" and "whirlwind" (27:20; 18:11; 21:18), "does not spare" (27:22; 6:10; 16:13), and "the Almighty" with no bracket.

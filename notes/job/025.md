# Notes on Job 25, for the translator of Job 26

Text followed: the Masoretic Text; the chapter has no Ketiv and Qere, and one Hebrew paragraph break, at the end. Bildad's third speech is 25:2-6, all in the chapter: it opens with a quotation mark and closes the speech. It is the shortest speech in the book, and Zophar does not speak a third time; nothing is said about that in the chapter. God is "him" and "his" from 25:2 and is named only at 25:4, as the Hebrew has it.

Brackets: one, with an entry in the decision log: 25:3 "on whom does his light not rise" [alt: against whom does his ambush not rise up, the old Greek] (close).

Places where the old copies differ, not bracketed:
- 25:2 the old Greek opens with a question ("What is the beginning, if not dread from him?"); the Hebrew has two nouns and no question.
- 25:5 the old Greek has "he gives the moon an order, and it does not shine"; either way the moon does not shine. A few Hebrew copies spell the verb in its usual way.

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 25:2 "Dominion" is a verb form used as a noun ("ruling"), only here; "govern" is the verb's English elsewhere (Genesis 1:18). "Dread" can be the dread he causes or the dread owed to him; the English is as open as the Hebrew. "He makes" is a participle ("the maker of peace"), made a verb as at 5:12.
- 25:2 "in his heights" is the word that is "on high" at 5:11 and 16:19, here in the plural with "his"; "those who are high" (21:22) is another form of the same root, which Bildad may be answering.
- 25:3 "on whom does his light not rise": the small word can also be "over" ("whose light does his not outshine"); "on" leaves that open. The verb is "rise", the one of 11:17, not the usual verb for the sun rising (9:7).
- 25:4 is 9:2 word for word with "And" before it, and its second half takes "be pure" and "one born of a woman" from 15:14. "With God" is the wording of 9:2; "before God" (4:17) is another small word.
- 25:5 "even the moon": the Hebrew is "as far as the moon". "Does not shine": the verb is written with one letter more than usual, which makes it look like the verb "pitch a tent" (Genesis 13:12); Isaiah 13:20 has that verb written the short way, and Isaiah 13:10 and Job 31:26 have "shine" beside the moon. "The moon pitches no tent" is not a possible sentence here, so nothing is noted. Obay may want to see this one.
- 25:5 "in his eyes" stands last in the Hebrew, as in the English, and can go with both halves.
- 25:5-6 is one sentence, as 15:15-16 is, with the same "how much less".
- 25:6 the Hebrew has no "who is": "a mortal, a maggot, and a human being, a worm". The words are supplied so that the four nouns are not heard as a list. "A human being" is the Hebrew "son of a human being", as at 16:21.

For Job 26: Job's ninth speech begins (26:1 "And Job answered and said:") and runs on past the chapter (27:1 has a new heading formula, "And Job again took up his discourse"), so Job 26 has to decide where its closing quotation mark goes. "You" in 26:2-4 is singular (Bildad). Keep "strength" and "power" apart (26:2, as the Job 12 line planned), "sound wisdom" (26:3), "breath" (26:4), "Sheol" (26:6), "thick clouds" or "cloud" for the two cloud words (26:8-9, the Job 20 line), "circle" and "limit" (26:10; 22:14; 11:7), "pillars" (26:11; 9:6, where the verb beside it is "shudder", another verb than the one here), "Rahab" with no bracket (26:12, the Job 9 line), "break out" for the verb of the sea at 7:5 (26:12), "whisper" (26:14; 4:12), and "peace in his heights" and "dominion" (25:2) if 26:5-14 answers them.

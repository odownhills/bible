# Notes on Genesis 11, for the translator of Genesis 12

Text followed: the Masoretic Text; the chapter has no Qere.

Places where the old copies differ:
- 11:32 is bracketed: Terah's "two hundred and five years" [alt: a hundred and forty-five, the Samaritan copy], because Acts 7:4 and Genesis 12:4 bear on it (decision log, call: clear).
- Not noted: the years of 11:10-25, which the Samaritan copy and the old Greek give differently (most of the fathers' ages a hundred years higher), and the Samaritan copy's added totals ("and all the days of ... were ..., and he died").
- Not noted: Kenan between Arpachshad and Shelah in the old Greek at 11:12-13. It is the alternate already bracketed at 10:24, and a bracket is not repeated.
- Not noted: 11:8, where the Samaritan copy and the old Greek add "and the tower"; 11:31, where they read "and he brought them out" for "and they went out with them" (the family leaves Ur under Terah either way).

Brackets: 11:1 "the same words" [alt: few words] (clear); 11:2 "eastward" [alt: from the east] (close); 11:32 (above). No "ask" in this chapter.

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 11:1 "words" as "matters" or "plans" (one purpose): the story goes on about speech.
- 11:3 the Hebrew plays on sounds ("make bricks", "brick" and "stone"; "tar" and "mortar"); "tar" and "mortar" carry some of it, and no note is written.
- 11:4 "sky" and not "heavens": one Hebrew word; "sky" is the listed word for what is seen overhead.
- 11:4 "so that we are not scattered" and "or else we will be scattered": one meaning.
- 11:5 "had built": the Hebrew verb is a finished one, though 11:8 says the building stopped.
- 11:6 "this is what they have begun to do" and "this is only the beginning of what they will do": the Hebrew is "this is their beginning to do", and the next sentence says the second.
- 11:7 "understand": the Hebrew verb is "hear" (as at 42:23); translated by its meaning.
- 11:9 "it was named": the Hebrew is "he called its name" with no one named, the usual way of saying "people called it" (16:14; 19:22); Yahweh as the one who named it is not noted.
- 11:12, 14: these two verses begin with the name and a different form of "lived"; the English is the same as in the other verses.
- 11:26: three sons at one age, as at 5:32; the English leaves open, as the Hebrew does, which was born that year (see the 11:32 entry).
- 11:28 "in the presence of Terah his father": the Hebrew is "on the face of"; "in the lifetime of" (Numbers 3:4) is implied by it and is not noted.
- 11:28 "the land of his birth": "the land of his relatives" would be the same place.
- 11:31 "they went out with them": the Hebrew does not say who "they" and "them" are, and the English does not either.
- 11:31 the note on the two names "Haran" is written once and is not to be repeated.
- "Babylon" (11:9) follows 10:10; "Babel" is the same name, and Obay can switch both.

Quoted ahead of time in the Genesis 11 decision log: 12:4 "was seventy-five years old when he went out from Haran"; 12:8 "the hill country east of Bethel", "Bethel on the west and Ai on the east"; 13:11 "and Lot traveled eastward"; 13:14 "northward and southward and eastward and westward"; 20:1 "And Abraham traveled from there to the land of the Negev".

For Genesis 12: keep "Abram", "Sarai", "Lot", "Haran" (the place) and "to go to the land of Canaan" (12:5 has the words of 11:31); "the land of his birth" (11:28) is the word that stands alone in 12:1 beside "your land" and "your father's house"; "make your name great" (12:2) answers "let us make a name for ourselves" (11:4), so keep "name"; "travel" for the verb of 11:2 (12:9); 12:3 has the two verbs for cursing in one sentence (see the Genesis 8 line of the glossary); Sarai "was barren; she had no child" (11:30) stands behind "a great nation" (12:2).

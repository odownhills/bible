# Notes on Genesis 44, for the translator of Genesis 45

Text followed: the Masoretic Text; the chapter has no Qere. The Hebrew has one paragraph break, after 44:17, where a section heading stands. Numbering is the same in Hebrew and English.

Brackets: one, with an entry in the decision log: 44:5 "and with it he certainly learns by divination" [alt: and he would certainly learn about it by divination] (call: clear).

Places where the old copies differ, not bracketed:
- 44:4-5 the old Greek adds, after "evil for good", "Why have you stolen my silver cup?" In the Hebrew the man says "this" without having named the goblet; translated as it stands ("Is this not what my master drinks from").
- 44:31 the Samaritan copy and the old Greek have "that the boy is not with us". 44:24, 27 the old Greek has "our father" for "my father".

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 44:5, 8 "my master", "your master's house": the word is the one for "my lord" (44:7, 9, 16, 18-24, 33); "master" is kept where a servant speaks of the one he serves (24:9; 39:8).
- 44:9, 10, 16, 17, 33 "slave", "slaves" and 44:7, 9, 16, 18-32 "your servant", "your servants" are one Hebrew word (the Genesis 43 notes); 44:9 has both in one sentence.
- 44:9 "will die": it can also be put "let him die". Jacob said the same of his gods (31:32).
- 44:10 "Now too, as your words are, so let it be": the Hebrew has no verb ("so it"). The man agrees and then names lighter terms than theirs; the English leaves that as it stands. "Innocent": free of the charge.
- 44:15 "Did you not know": it can also be put "Do you not know". "This deed": the noun of "done".
- 44:16 "God has found the guilt of your servants": the Hebrew does not say which guilt (the goblet, or what they did to Joseph, 42:21-22). "Found" is the verb of 44:8, 9, 10, 12, 16, 17 and 34, kept each time.
- 44:18 "for you are like Pharaoh": the Hebrew is "for like you, like Pharaoh".
- 44:19-23 Judah tells things that chapters 42 and 43 did not tell in these words (the question about a father and a brother is at 43:7; "set my eye on him" and 44:22 are new). Translated as it stands.
- 44:20 "he alone is left of his mother": of his mother's sons; the Hebrew is "left to his mother". "Loves him": a perfect, of what is still so.
- 44:21 "set my eye on him": the picture is kept, and it is as open as the Hebrew (to see him, 42:15-20, or to look after him, Jeremiah 39:12; 40:4).
- 44:22 "he would die": the Hebrew does not say who, the boy (42:4, 38) or the father (44:31, the same form); the English "he" is as open.
- 44:26 "If our youngest brother is with us, then we will go down": 43:4 has other words.
- 44:27 "two sons": "sons" is supplied.
- 44:28 "Surely he has certainly been torn to pieces": 37:33 with "Surely" before it. "Since": the Hebrew is "until now".
- 44:29 "in misery": not the word of 44:31 and 42:38 ("in sorrow"); it is a form of the word for "evil" (44:4, 34), and "in evil" is not English.
- 44:30 "his soul is bound up with the boy's soul": the Hebrew is "his soul is bound up with his soul"; the father is taken as the first, since the next words are about him. "Soul" as at 34:3, where "life" could also be said.
- 44:32 "to my father": the Hebrew is "from my father", from whom he got the boy on that pledge. Judah says "against my father" inside his own quoted words, where 43:9 has "against you".
- 44:34 "Let me not look on": the Hebrew is the word for "so that ... not" (3:3) after the question; 21:16 has the same English for another construction. "Would find": or "will find".

For Genesis 45: "control himself" (45:1; 43:31); "wept" (45:2, 14, 15; 43:30); "Is my father still alive?" (45:3; 43:7, 27, 28); "anger in the eyes of" (45:5; 31:35) and "be pained" (45:5; 6:6); "go up to my father" (45:9; 44:17, 34) and "come down", "bring down" (45:9, 13; 44:21, 23, 26); 45:8, 9 call Joseph "lord" of Pharaoh's house and of all Egypt, the word that is "my lord" all through chapter 44 and "master" at 44:5, 8 (42:30 has "the lord of the land"); "over his house" (44:1; 41:40) is not that word; "fall on the neck of" (45:14; 33:4); "clothes" (45:22; 44:13); "good things" (45:18, 20, 23; 24:10); "provisions for the way" (45:21; 42:25); "load" at 45:17 is a third verb (44:13; 42:26); "grain" (45:23; 41:35); "govern" (45:8, 26; 24:2).

# Notes on Genesis 20, for the translator of Genesis 21

Text followed: the Masoretic Text; the chapter has no Ketiv and Qere. The main text keeps to the Masoretic vowels and accents: 20:4 the word is written as at 18:3, "Lord", and it is said to God; 20:16 is divided where the accents divide it, after "all who are with you", and its last word is read as written.

Places where the old copies differ, none bracketed:
- 20:13 the Samaritan copy has the verb with "God" in the singular (it is in the decision log, as support for the main text).
- 20:16 the old Greek reads the last words as a command to Sarah, "and be truthful in everything", and has "the honor of your face" for "a covering of the eyes"; 20:4 it has "a nation that did not know, and righteous".

Brackets: 20:13 "God made me wander" [alt: the gods made me wander] (clear); 20:16 "it is" [alt: he is] (clear); 20:16 "you are cleared" [alt: you are rebuked] (ask). One note on a sound echo: 20:1 "stayed" and "Gerar".

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 20:2 "said of Sarah": the Hebrew has "to", used as "about", as "say of me" at 20:13.
- 20:3 "you are about to die": the same words can be heard as "you are a dead man". "For she is a married woman": the Hebrew is "and she is", and the phrase is "owned by a husband".
- 20:4 "Lord": no bracket here; the speaker is answering God in a dream. "A nation, even a righteous one": the Hebrew says "nation", though one man is in danger (20:7 "you and all that you have"; 20:9 "my kingdom"); the "even" can also be heard as "as well", as if after Sodom (18:23-25).
- 20:5 "palms": the glossary's word (Job 9:30; 16:17), kept apart from "hand". God's answer (20:6) takes up the heart and leaves out the palms; the English does the same.
- 20:6 "I too" twice, as the Hebrew has it. "Restrained" is the verb of Job 7:11, not "held back" (20:18). "Sinning against me": against God, not against Abraham.
- 20:7 "and he will pray for you, and you will live": the Hebrew can also be heard as "so that he may pray for you; and live!" "All that you have": as open as the Hebrew (people or goods), as at 19:12.
- 20:9 "how have I sinned against you": the Hebrew is "what". "Deeds that are not done": deeds that ought not to be done.
- 20:10 "What did you see": kept word for word; it can be heard as "what did you have in view".
- 20:11 "Because I said": he had said it aloud to Sarai (12:11-12), so it is not put as "I thought". "Surely": the word is "only".
- 20:12 no other verse says whose daughter Sarah was (11:29-31 names her only as Terah's daughter-in-law); nothing is added.
- 20:13 "made me wander": the glossary's wording (Job 12:24-25); the same verb elsewhere is "lead astray". "Loyalty": the word behind "faithful love" (19:19), between husband and wife.
- 20:16 "a thousand pieces of silver": "pieces" is supplied. "Your brother": Abimelech's word, kept. "A covering of the eyes": the picture is kept and not explained; whose eyes are covered is as open as in the Hebrew. "With everyone" can also be joined to what comes before it ("for all who are with you and with everyone").
- 20:17 "they bore children": the verb is the plural that takes in Abimelech too. "God" in 20:17, "Yahweh" in 20:18, kept as written.
- 20:18 "completely held back every womb": the verb of 16:2, doubled; the Hebrew is "held back around every womb", which is "shut every womb".

The decision log quotes 21:25 ahead of time: "And Abraham rebuked Abimelech over the well of water."

For Genesis 21: the chapter opens on Sarah's own womb just after 20:17-18 ("bore children", "held back"); 21:2 "the set time" (17:21; 18:14); 21:6, 9 "laugh" and the form listed as "joke" (19:14); 21:10, 12, 13 "female servant" is the word of 20:17, not "female slave" (16:1; 20:14); 21:22-32 Abimelech again, the same spelling; 21:23 "the loyalty that I have shown you" (20:13) and "stayed" (20:1; 21:34); 21:25 "rebuked" is the verb of the alternate at 20:16; 21:26 "this thing" (20:10); 21:27 "flocks and cattle" (20:14); 21:23 "God" in Abimelech's mouth, and "fear of God" (20:11) returns at 22:12.

# Notes on Genesis 13, for the translator of Genesis 14

Text followed: the Masoretic Text. 13:3 has the chapter's one Ketiv and Qere: "his tent" is written with the older spelling of "his" (as at 9:21 and 12:8) and read with the usual one; the meaning is the same, and nothing is noted.

Places where the old copies differ, none bracketed:
- 13:10 the old Syriac has "Zoan", the city of Egypt (Numbers 13:22), for "Zoar". The Hebrew copies and the old Greek have Zoar, which is in the plain (19:22-23; Deuteronomy 34:3).
- 13:18 the old Greek has "the oak" of Mamre, one tree; the Hebrew has the plural.

Bracket: 13:4 "and there Abram called on the name of Yahweh" [alt: where Abram had called on the name of Yahweh] (clear). No "ask" in this chapter.

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 13:1 "to the Negev": the same Hebrew form is "toward the Negev" at 12:9; here he arrives (13:3 "from the Negev").
- 13:2 "very rich" is the word "heavy" (12:10 "severe"); the echo is not shown.
- 13:3 "by his stages": from one camp to the next; "his" may mean the same stops as on the way down, and the English is as open as the Hebrew.
- 13:6 "support" is the verb "lift" or "carry" (13:10, 14 "lifted his eyes"); the echo is not shown.
- 13:7 "shepherds of ... herds": "shepherd" is kept for the word of 4:2, though the herds are more than sheep.
- 13:7 "were then living in the land": as open as the Hebrew, as at 12:6.
- 13:8 "men who are brothers": the Hebrew is "men, brothers". Lot is Abram's brother's son (12:5); the word "brother" covers him, and it is kept (13:11; 14:14, 16).
- 13:9 "the left" and "the right": for one facing east these are north and south, but the words are "left" and "right".
- 13:10 "well watered": the Hebrew is a noun, "a watered place", from the verb "water" of 2:6 and 2:10.
- 13:10 "the garden of Yahweh": the words are kept; chapter 2 calls it "the garden" and "the garden of Eden" (Isaiah 51:3; Ezekiel 28:13).
- 13:10 "as you go toward Zoar" can go with the plain or with "the land of Egypt"; the English keeps the Hebrew order and is as open. The words about Sodom and Gomorrah stand in the middle of the sentence in the Hebrew, and are set in parentheses.
- 13:10 "ruined": the verb of the flood (6:13, 17; 9:11, 15); it returns at 19:13, 14, 29.
- 13:11 "eastward": the words bracketed at 11:2; the bracket is not repeated.
- 13:12, 18 "moved his tent": one Hebrew verb, "to tent"; it is not the "pitched his tent" of 12:8.
- 13:13 "against Yahweh": the Hebrew is "to Yahweh", as in "sin against" (20:6; 39:9); "in Yahweh's sight" was weighed, and it says nearly the same. "Very much so" stands last, as in the Hebrew, and covers both words.
- 13:14 "now": the Hebrew particle of 13:8-9 ("please"); from Yahweh it is "now".
- 13:15 "offspring": one word, of one or of many, in English as in Hebrew (Galatians 3:16); 3:15 has the bracket, and it is not repeated.
- 13:16 "the dust of the earth": the word is "the land" everywhere else in the chapter. "Will be counted": "can be counted" is the same meaning.
- 13:18 "Mamre" is kept as a name; 14:13 has it as a man's name. "In Hebron": the Hebrew is "in".

For Genesis 14: keep "the oaks of Mamre" (14:13 "among the oaks of Mamre the Amorite", and "living" is another verb than 13:18, the "live" of 9:27); "possessions" (14:11, 12, 16, 21); Lot "his brother's son" (14:12, as at 12:5) and "his brother" (14:14, 16), with "brother" kept; "he was living in Sodom" (14:12); Sodom, Gomorrah, Admah, Zeboiim (10:19) and Zoar (14:2, 8); "valley" for the word of 14:3, 8, 10, 17, which is not "plain" (13:10; 11:2); "tar" (14:10; the Genesis 11 line); "to the hill country" (14:10; 12:8); "as far as" (14:6, 14, 15); "the Hebrew" (14:13) may want the note the Genesis 10 line speaks of; 14:19, 22 has the verb of 4:1 ("acquire"), to be weighed there.

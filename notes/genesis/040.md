# Notes on Genesis 40, for the translator of Genesis 41

Text followed: the Masoretic Text; the chapter has no marginal reading. The Hebrew has one paragraph break, after 40:23. Numbering is the same in Hebrew and English.

Brackets: one alternate, in the decision log, `close`, and one note.
- 40:16 "three baskets of white bread" [alt: three open-weave baskets]. The word stands only here.
- 40:14 note: "keep me in your memory" and "mention me" are two forms of the verb "remember" (40:23).

Places where the old copies differ, not bracketed:
- 40:19 the Latin version does not have the first "from off you"; the Hebrew and the old Greek have it. 40:16 the old Greek takes the word after "baskets" as a kind of bread.

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 40:1 "the cupbearer", "the baker": plain titles here and at 40:5; "the chief cupbearer", "the chief baker" from 40:2 on, as the Hebrew has them. "Their master" is the word of 39:2-20.
- 40:3 "in custody in the house of the commander of the guard, into the prison": the prison is in the house of Joseph's master (39:1, 20; 40:7). 40:4 "assigned Joseph to them": the Hebrew is "appointed Joseph with them", not "over them", and he attends them.
- 40:4 "for some time": the Hebrew is "days" (4:3).
- 40:5 "each dream with its own interpretation": the Hebrew is "each according to the interpretation of his dream"; 41:11 has the same words.
- 40:6 "troubled": a word of storm and rage elsewhere (Jonah 1:15; Proverbs 19:3, 12); of faces at Daniel 1:10. 40:7 "sad" is "bad" (Nehemiah 2:2).
- 40:8 "recount it": the Hebrew has no "it".
- 40:10 "as it budded": the small word before "budding" can also be "as if" ("it was as if it were budding"); the vine does the same thing either way. The last two clauses have no "and" between them.
- 40:11, 21 "on Pharaoh's palm"; 40:13 "into his hand": two Hebrew words.
- 40:13, 19, 20 "lift your head", "lifted the head": one phrase three times, kept as the picture it is. By itself it is said of a prisoner set free and honored (2 Kings 25:27) and of counting people (Exodus 30:12); 40:19 adds "from off you".
- 40:13 "your post" is "your base", a man's place. "The former practice": the word is "justice" elsewhere, here the way a thing is done.
- 40:14 "Only keep me in your memory": the Hebrew is "remember me with you" (compare Proverbs 2:1, "store up with you"). The sentence opens with two small words that can also make it "But if you remember me ..., then please show"; it is a request either way.
- 40:14 "loyalty": the word of 39:21 ("faithful love", of Yahweh); between people it is "loyalty" (20:13; 21:23; 24:49).
- 40:15 "stolen": the verb of 31:19, 30, said of a person as at Exodus 21:16; it bears on who "they" are at 37:28. "The pit" is the word of 37:24, here of the prison.
- 40:16 "saw that the interpretation was good": the Hebrew is "that he had interpreted good", which can be heard as "favorably" or as "well"; "good" leaves it as open.
- 40:17 "some of every food of Pharaoh": as 6:21. "Eating them": the Hebrew has "them" too.
- 40:19 "on a tree": the word is "tree" and also "wood", a post (Deuteronomy 21:22-23; Esther 2:23). The Hebrew does not say how he dies before he is hung up.
- 40:21 "his cupbearing": the Hebrew word is the same as "cupbearer" (or "his drink"); "he put the cup": the cupbearer, with no subject named.

For Genesis 41: "the chief cupbearer" (41:9) and "the chief baker" (41:10); "was furious with" and "in custody in the house of the commander of the guard" (41:10) as at 40:2-3; "each dream with its own interpretation" (41:11) as at 40:5; "recounted", "interpreted" (41:8, 12, 13); "restored to my post" and "hanged" (41:13) as at 40:13, 22; "the pit" (41:14) as at 40:15; "I have dreamed a dream, and there is no one to interpret it" (41:15) word for word as 40:8, and Joseph's answer (41:16) stands beside "Do interpretations not belong to God?"; 41:9 "mention" is the verb of 40:14 and "my sins" the root of "sinned" (40:1); 41:12 "slave" as at 39:17; 41:8 "was troubled" is not the word of 40:6 and needs another English word; 41:1 "at the end of" as at 8:6.

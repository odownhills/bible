# Notes on Genesis 18, for the translator of Genesis 19

Text followed: the Masoretic Text; the chapter has no Ketiv and Qere. The dots that the Hebrew text sets over "to him" (18:9) change nothing. The main text keeps to the Masoretic vowels and accents: 18:3 the word is written as "Lord" (bracketed, see below); 18:21 the accents join "they have done" and "altogether".

Places where the old copies differ, none bracketed on that ground:
- 18:10 the Samaritan copy and the old Greek have "and she was behind him" for "and it was behind him"; either way Sarah is at the entrance behind the speaker.
- 18:19 the old Greek and Latin have "For I know that he will command"; the Hebrew has "I have known him, so that".
- 18:21 the old Greek has "their outcry" and a verb ("whether they are carrying out") for the word behind "altogether".
- 18:22 a scribes' note handed down with the Hebrew text says the words once ran "Yahweh was still standing before Abraham". No old copy has that, so nothing is noted.

Brackets: 18:3 "Lord" [alt: My lord] (ask); 18:21 "whether they have done altogether according to its outcry that has come to me" [alt: ...; if so, it is the end] (clear). No notes on wordplay: "laugh" and "Isaac" were noted at 17:19.

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 18:1 "to him": the Hebrew does not name Abraham until 18:6; the chapter runs on from 17:27.
- 18:3-5 Abraham speaks to one in 18:3 and to all three in 18:4-5; English "you" cannot show it (the decision log says so). 18:9 "they said", 18:10 "he said", 18:13 "Yahweh said": kept as the Hebrew has them.
- 18:3, 5 one verb, "pass", three times: "pass your servant by", "pass on", "passed your servant's way".
- 18:5 "strengthen yourselves": the Hebrew is "sustain your heart" (Judges 19:5; Psalm 104:15).
- 18:6 "seahs": a measure of grain, not converted. "Cakes": round flat loaves, not sweet ones.
- 18:7-8 "he hurried to prepare it", "that he had prepared": the "he" is as open as the Hebrew (the young man, or Abraham).
- 18:10, 14 "about this time next year": the Hebrew is "about the time, living", the wording of 2 Kings 4:16-17; it has also been taken as "at the time of a birth". 17:21 says "next year" in other words.
- 18:11 "the way of women": her monthly bleeding; the Hebrew is as veiled as the English (31:35 has "the way of women is on me").
- 18:12 "pleasure": the word stands only here and is from the root of "Eden"; not noted. "Am I to have": the Hebrew is "has there been for me". Sarah says "my lord is old"; Yahweh reports her as saying "I have grown old" (18:13).
- 18:14 "too hard": the verb is that of "wonders" (Job 5:9); "anything" is the word for "word" or "thing".
- 18:17 "Now Yahweh said": the Hebrew breaks the chain here; it can also be heard as "had said", or as what he said to himself.
- 18:19 "I have known him": as at Amos 3:2 ("You only have I known"); "chosen" would say what it means. Yahweh speaks of himself by name twice in the verse.
- 18:20 "The outcry of Sodom and Gomorrah": as open as the Hebrew (the cry raised against them, or the cry that goes up from them); 19:13 has "their outcry". "Great indeed", "heavy indeed": the Hebrew word can also be "because", with 18:21 as the main clause.
- 18:21 "its outcry": the Hebrew turns from "their sin" to "her", the city or the land.
- 18:23, 25 "the righteous with the wicked": one of each in the Hebrew. 18:24, 26 "people" is supplied after "fifty righteous"; 18:24 "sweep it away": "it" is supplied.
- 18:24, 26 "forgive": the Hebrew is "lift" (the guilt) "for the place"; Job 7:21 has "carry away rebellion" for the fuller phrase.
- 18:25 "the judge ... do justice": one root in Hebrew, and nearly one in English. "Judge" is left in small letters.
- 18:27, 31 "taken it on myself": the verb is "be willing" at Job 6:9.
- 18:28 "over the five": the five that are lacking.

For Genesis 19: "the men" (18:16, 22) are two at 19:1, where the Hebrew calls them "the two angels" (the word of 16:7); 19:2 "my lords" (plural, and written differently from 18:3), with "please turn aside" and "your servant" as at 18:3; 19:18 has the word of 18:3 again and needs weighing; "wash your feet" (18:4); "the men of Sodom" (13:13); "outcry" (19:13, as at 18:20-21); "ruin" (19:13, 14, 29, as at 13:10 and 18:28-32); "sweep away" (19:15, 17); "the plain" (13:10-12); 19:28 "looked down toward" as at 18:16; "find favor in the eyes of" (19:19); 19:5 and 19:8 have the verb "know" (Obay's line on 4:1), and 19:31-35 "lie with" and "keep offspring alive" (7:3); Zoar (13:10; 14:2); 19:27 "the place where he had stood before Yahweh" (18:22).

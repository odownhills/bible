# Notes on Genesis 22, for the translator of Genesis 23

Text followed: the Masoretic Text; the chapter has no Ketiv and Qere. The main text keeps to the Masoretic vowels and accents: 22:13 the word after "a ram" is "behind", set apart by the accents, with "him" supplied; 22:14 the saying is read as pointed, "On the mountain of Yahweh", with a passive verb.

Places where the old copies differ. One is bracketed: 22:13 "one ram" for "a ram behind him" (other Hebrew copies, the Samaritan copy, the old Greek; clear). Not bracketed:
- 22:2 "Moriah": the old Greek has "the high land", and other old versions have other words; the name stands as in 2 Chronicles 3:1.
- 22:2, 12, 16 "your only one": the old Greek has "your beloved". 22:1 it has "Abraham, Abraham".
- 22:14 the old Greek has "On the mountain the Lord was seen" (it is in the decision log, as support for the alternate).
- 22:16 the Samaritan copy adds "from me", as at 22:12, and the old Greek "for my sake".

Brackets: 22:13 (clear); 22:14 "it will be seen to" [alt: he will be seen] (ask); 22:17 "their enemies" [alt: his enemies] (clear); 22:18 "will be blessed in your offspring" [alt: will bless themselves by your offspring] (close). One note: 22:14, that "see to" is the word "see".

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 22:1 "tested": one meaning; "tempted" is another English word for it.
- 22:2 "now": the word of request, as at 13:14. "Whom you love": the Hebrew verb is a perfect. "Go" as at 12:1.
- 22:3 "his two young men": the Hebrew has the two as known. "Told him of" (22:2, 3, 9) is the verb "say".
- 22:5 "bow down": the act is named, as at Job 1:20; it is worship here. "We will ... return": the plural is the Hebrew's.
- 22:7 "said ..., and he said": both verbs are in the Hebrew. "Sheep": the word is one animal of the flock, sheep or goat, of any age; "lamb" would shut out the ram of 22:13. Obay may prefer "lamb".
- 22:8 "see to": the verb is "see", used of picking out and providing (1 Samuel 16:1). "For himself" goes with the verb. "My son" can also be heard as naming the sheep; the English comma leaves it as open as the Hebrew.
- 22:9 "the altar" and 22:13 "the thicket": the Hebrew has the article. "Bound": a verb found only here.
- 22:12 "you fear God": the Hebrew is "you are one fearing God" (Job 1:1, 8). "Fear" sounds much like "see" in Hebrew; no note.
- 22:12, 16 "withheld": the verb of 20:6 ("restrained"), here with a thing as its object. It shares "withhold" with the verb planned for 23:6.
- 22:14 the name can also be heard as "Yahweh sees" (16:13); "to it" is supplied by the English idiom. A third way to take the saying, "one appears on the mountain of Yahweh" (the form of Deuteronomy 16:16), is not noted.
- 22:11, 15 "from the heavens" (21:17) and 22:17 "the stars of the sky" (15:5) are one Hebrew word.
- 22:16-17 one sentence across the verse break; the second "that" of the Hebrew is not repeated. "Declares Yahweh": the Hebrew is a noun, "the utterance of Yahweh".
- 22:16, 18 two Hebrew words for "because".
- 22:20 the speech is closed after 22:22; 22:23 ("Abraham's brother") is the writer's. "Sons": the eight are all sons.
- 22:21 Uz and Aram are not the men of 10:22-23; the names are the same.

For Genesis 23: 23:6 "withhold" is planned for another verb than the one at 22:12, 16 (the Genesis 8 line), and they will share it; "holding" (23:4, 9, 20) as at 17:8; "stay" and "resident foreigner" (23:4; 15:13; 21:23, 34); Hebron and Mamre as at 13:18; the Hittites, "the sons of Heth", as at 10:15 and 15:20; "bowed down" (23:7, 12) as at 18:2 and 22:5; "in the ears of" (23:10, 13, 16) as at 20:8; "silver" as at 20:16; "gate" as at 19:1 and 22:17; "cave" as at 19:30; Rebekah and Bethuel (22:22-23) return in chapter 24, and "take possession of the gate of" at 24:60 (the decision log of 22:17 quotes it ahead of time).

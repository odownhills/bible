# Notes on Genesis 14, for the translator of Genesis 15

Text followed: the Masoretic Text, read by its Ketiv. 14:2 and 14:8 each have a Ketiv and Qere at "Zeboiim": two spellings of the one name (10:19), and nothing is noted. The main text keeps to the Masoretic vowels: 14:5 "in Ham" is a place (the same letters with other vowels are "with them", as the old Greek and Latin have it).

Places where the old copies differ, none bracketed:
- 14:4 the Samaritan copy writes "and in the thirteenth year" in full; this is the sense of the main text, and the bracket there is about the Hebrew grammar.
- 14:6 the Samaritan copy and the old Greek have "in the mountains of Seir"; the Hebrew text has "in their hill country, Seir".
- 14:10 the Hebrew text has "the king of Sodom and Gomorrah" with a plural verb, put as "the kings of Sodom and Gomorrah"; the Samaritan copy and the old Greek write "and the king of Gomorrah".
- 14:14 for "led out" the old Greek has "counted", and the Samaritan copy another verb; the Hebrew verb is "emptied out", the word for drawing a sword (Exodus 15:9).
- 14:22 the old Greek and the old Syriac do not have "Yahweh", and the Samaritan copy has "God" in its place. The Hebrew text has the name, and it is kept; no other Scripture bears on it, so there is no bracket. Obay may want to know of it.

Brackets: 14:1 "king of Goiim" [alt: king of nations] (close); 14:4 "and in the thirteenth year they rebelled" [alt: and for thirteen years they had rebelled] (close); 14:19 "owner of heaven and earth" [alt: who brought heaven and earth into being] (ask). The bracket of 14:19 is not repeated at 14:22.

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 14:1-2 "in the days of" is read with all four kings (14:9 counts Amraphel among the four), not with Amraphel alone and the other three as the ones who made war.
- 14:3 "All these": the five kings just named, or all nine; as open as the Hebrew. "Joined together at": the Hebrew is "joined to".
- 14:3 "(that is, the Salt Sea)": the valley is named by what was there when the writer wrote. Its name has "the" in Hebrew ("the Siddim"); the old Greek has "the salt valley".
- 14:5 "Shaveh-Kiriathaim" can be "the plain of Kiriathaim", and 14:6 "El-Paran" "the great tree of Paran" (the word is close to "oak", 12:6); both are kept as names, and the places are the same either way.
- 14:7 "turned back": the verb is "return"; they had not been at Kadesh before. "Country" is the word "field". "En-Mishpat" means "spring of judgment".
- 14:10 "tar pits": the word is "wells" (21:25), said twice. "They fell there": the Hebrew does not say whether into the pits or in the fight, nor whether the kings themselves or their men (the king of Sodom is alive at 14:17); "those who were left" follows it. The English is as open.
- 14:13 "one who had escaped": the Hebrew has "the", as at Ezekiel 24:26. "Had a covenant with": the Hebrew is "owners of a covenant of Abram"; the word "covenant" is kept for chapter 15. The note on "Hebrew" gives both echoes (Eber, 10:21; "beyond the River", Joshua 24:2-3) and chooses neither.
- 14:14 "trained men": the word stands only here; its verb is "train" (Proverbs 22:6). "Dan" is the name in the text (Judges 18:29).
- 14:15 "divided his forces": the Hebrew is "he divided himself"; "forces" is supplied. "North of": the Hebrew is "on the left of" (Ezekiel 16:46). "Servants": the men born in his house (12:16 has "slaves" in a list of property).
- 14:17 "striking down": the verb of 14:5, 7, 15; it does not say that the kings were killed (Hebrews 7:1).
- 14:18 the note on "Melchizedek" and "Salem" follows Hebrews 7:2 (Psalm 76:2 has Salem beside Zion). "God Most High": the first word is "God" (Job 5:8), the second "Most High" (Psalm 78:35).
- 14:20 "And he gave him a tenth of everything": the Hebrew does not name who gave, and the English does not either (Hebrews 7:2, 4: Abraham gave). "Delivered" is a rare verb (Hosea 11:8) that sounds like "shield" (15:1); "tenth" and "made rich" (14:23) also sound alike.
- 14:22 "I have raised my hand": the oath gesture (Deuteronomy 32:40; Daniel 12:7); "I raise my hand", said in the doing, is the same oath.
- 14:23 the oath is in the Hebrew form "if ... and if ...", put as "I will not"; "so much as a thread or a sandal strap" is "from a thread to a sandal strap".
- 14:24 "Nothing for me": the Hebrew is one word, "apart from me" (41:16). "Share" is from the root of "divided" (14:15).

For Genesis 15: 15:1 "After these things" looks back to this chapter; "shield" (15:1) sounds like "delivered" (14:20), and "your reward" answers the possessions Abram refused (14:23), so "possessions" (15:14) is to stay the word of 14:11-21; "born in my house" (15:3 is "a son of my house", another phrase than 14:14 "born in his house"); Damascus (15:2), as at 14:15; the Amorites (15:16, 21), the Rephaim (15:20) and the peoples of 10:15-18 keep their spellings; "covenant" (15:18, "made a covenant", the Deuteronomy 5 line) beside 14:13; "Yahweh, God Most High" is not repeated in 15, where "Lord Yahweh" (15:2, 8) needs settling.

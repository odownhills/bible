# Notes on Genesis 30, for the translator of Genesis 31

Text followed: the Masoretic Text, read by its Ketiv; the chapter has no paragraph break. One Qere, at 30:11: the written text is one word, "With good fortune", put as "What good fortune!"; the text as read aloud is two words, "Good fortune has come". The sense is the same, so nothing is bracketed.

Places where the old copies differ, none bracketed:
- 30:11 the old Greek agrees with the written text ("in good fortune").
- 30:32 the old Greek has "let all your flock pass by today, and separate"; the Hebrew is "I will pass through".
- 30:36 the Samaritan copy adds here the angel's words that Jacob reports at 31:11-13.
- 30:36 the Samaritan copy and the old Greek have "between them" for "between himself".

Brackets: two. 30:8 "wrestlings of God" [alt: mighty wrestlings] (clear); 30:20 "will make his home with me" [alt: will honor me] (close). Seven notes: on the names Dan, Naphtali, Gad, Asher, Issachar, Zebulun and Joseph (30:6-24), and one at 30:37 on "white" and the name "Laban".

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 30:1 "sons": the word also means "children"; "sons" keeps it with "given me a son" (30:6). "I am dead" is the Hebrew's "dead am I"; it can also be "I am going to die".
- 30:6 "judged in my favor": the Hebrew is "judged me", as in "by your might judge me" (Psalm 54:1). The same verb is punishment at 15:14, but she names her son for it.
- 30:8 "wrestled": the verb stands in this sense only here; its root is "twist" ("the devious", Job 5:13). The verbs of 32:24-25 and 32:28 are different words.
- 30:11 "good fortune": the word is the name of the god Fortune at Isaiah 65:11. "A troop" (49:19 plays on that sound) is not a sense the word has anywhere else.
- 30:13 "have called me happy": the Hebrew verb is a past; it can also be heard as "will call me happy". Leah puts what she hopes for in the future form each time (29:32, 34; 30:20). "Women" is the Hebrew's "daughters", as at Song of Songs 6:9.
- 30:14 "mandrakes": the plant's usual English name; the word sounds like "love" (Song of Songs 7:12-13). The text does not say why Rachel wants them.
- 30:16 "It is with me you are to sleep": the Hebrew is "To me you will come", the phrase of 30:3-4.
- 30:18, 28, 32, 33 "pay": one word, the noun of "hired" (30:16); "wages" (29:15) is another word.
- 30:27 The sentence breaks off after "in your eyes", as in the Hebrew. "Learned by divination" is the verb of 44:5, 15.
- 30:30 "wherever I set foot": the Hebrew is "at my foot"; it can also be "since I came".
- 30:32 "Let ... be removed": the Hebrew form can be "removing" (Jacob does it) or "remove" (Laban does it); the passive leaves it open, and in 30:35 it is Laban who removes. "That will be my pay" is as open as the Hebrew: the animals removed, or all such animals from now on (30:33; 31:8).
- 30:33 "when it comes before you": the verb can also be "when you come"; "come before you", said of a thing and not a person, is the wording of Psalm 88:2 and Lamentations 1:22. "Testify for me": the phrase is "testify against" everywhere else. "On a day to come" is "on the day of tomorrow" (Exodus 13:14).
- 30:35 "he": the Hebrew does not name Laban.
- 30:37 "poplar", "almond", "plane tree": Scripture does not say which trees these are (Hosea 4:13; Ezekiel 31:8); the usual English names are kept.
- 30:38-41 "bred": the verb's root is "be hot"; it is "conceived" at Psalm 51:5.
- 30:40 is as hard in the Hebrew as in the English; nothing is smoothed. "Flocks" (Jacob's) and "flock" (Laban's) are two Hebrew words.
- 30:41-42 "sturdy", "feeble": the Hebrew is "bound" and "faint" (Lamentations 2:19); "early-born" and "late-born" has no support in Scripture.
- 30:43 "spread out": the verb of the promise at 28:14 and of 30:30.

For Genesis 31: "pay" (31:8) beside "wages" (31:7, 41, the word of 29:15); "speckled" and "striped" (31:8, 10, 12) as here, with a new fourth word beside them (31:10, 12), and the male goats of 31:10, 12 are another word than "male goats" here (30:35); "bred" (31:10); "flock" for the sheep and goats together, "herds" (31:9, 18) as at 30:29; "served", "service" (31:6, 41); "female servants" (31:33) is the word of 30:3, not "female slave"; "a journey of seven days" (31:23) like "a journey of three days" (30:36); "anger burned" (30:2) beside "be angry" (31:36); "gain" (31:1), "in a dream by night" (31:24), "make a vow", "pillar" and "the land of your birth" (31:13) as the glossary lists them; "sticks" (30:37) is the "staff" of 32:10. Names: Dan, Naphtali, Gad, Asher, Issachar, Zebulun, Dinah, Joseph.

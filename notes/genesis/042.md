# Notes on Genesis 42, for the translator of Genesis 43

Text followed: the Masoretic Text; the chapter has no marginal reading and no paragraph break inside it. Numbering is the same in Hebrew and English.

Brackets: no alternates, so there is no decision log. Two notes:
- 42:7 "recognized" and "acted as a stranger" are forms of one word.
- 42:23 "understood" is the word for "listen" in 42:21 and 42:22.

Places where the old copies differ, not bracketed:
- 42:1 "Why do you look at one another?", as the Hebrew has it; the old Greek has "Why are you idle?"
- 42:30 "treated us as men spying on the land"; the old Greek has "put us in custody as men spying on the land".
- 42:33 "take grain for the hunger of your households": the Hebrew is "take the hunger of your households", and "grain" is supplied from 42:19; the old Greek and other ancient versions have the word here too.

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 42:1, 2 "grain for sale", 42:26 "the grain they had bought", 42:19 "grain": one noun, made from the verb "buy grain", and not the "grain" of 42:3, 25 (41:35, 49). In 42:3 the Hebrew is "to buy grain" with that second noun added, and one "grain" serves.
- 42:4, 38 "harm": the word of Exodus 21:22-23, which does not say what kind; "might come to him" and "if harm comes to him" are one verb.
- 42:6 "the one who held power": not the "govern" of 24:2 and 45:8, 26.
- 42:7, 30 "spoke harshly": the Hebrew is "spoke hard things".
- 42:9 "dreamed about them": the Hebrew can also be heard as "dreamed for them". "The nakedness of the land": the picture is kept (9:22).
- 42:11, 31 "have not been spies": it can also be put "are not spies".
- 42:13, 32, 36 "is gone": the word of 37:30 and 5:24; it does not say whether he is dead, and 42:38 and 44:20 say "dead".
- 42:15, 16 "by the life of Pharaoh": an oath, as "As Yahweh lives" will be; the Hebrew spells the two differently.
- 42:16 "you are to be confined": an imperative. 42:24 "bound" is the same verb.
- 42:18 "it is God I fear": "God" is set first; the word has the article, as at 41:25, 28, 32.
- 42:21 "Indeed": the word of 17:19, where "But" is the alternate; here nothing stands before it to be set against, so there is no bracket. "Guilty": the word can also be said of one who is bearing the punishment (Psalm 34:21-22).
- 42:22 "is being demanded": 9:5, and 37:22, 26 for the blood.
- 42:23 "the interpreter", not the "spokesman" of the Job 16 line: he stands between two languages, and the Genesis 41 decision log had quoted the verse so.
- 42:25 "commanded that their containers be filled": the Hebrew is "commanded, and they filled ..., and to return ..., and to give". "It was done": the Hebrew is "he did", with no one named.
- 42:27, 28 "bag" and 42:25, 27, 35 "sack" are two words in the Hebrew for the same thing (42:27 has both); chapters 43 and 44 have only "bag".
- 42:28 "their hearts sank": the Hebrew is "their heart went out". "Turned to one another trembling": "trembled to one another".
- 42:35 "saw" and "were afraid" sound alike in the Hebrew; no note.
- 42:36 "It is on me that all these things have come": it can also be put "all these things are against me"; either way Jacob is the one who bears them.
- 42:37 "My two sons": 46:9 names four sons of Reuben. "Put him in my care": the Hebrew is "give him on my hand".
- 42:27-28 and 42:35: one finds his silver on the way, and all find it at home; 43:21 tells it as all at the lodging place. Each is translated as it stands.

For Genesis 43: "the famine was severe" (43:1; 12:10; 41:31); "the grain they had brought" (43:2) is the noun of 42:26, and "buy us a little food" the verb of 42:7, 10; "the man" (43:3-14) and "the lord of the land" (42:30, 33); "your youngest brother" and "is gone" as here; "relatives" (43:7; 12:1); "little ones" (43:8; 34:29); "require from my hand" (43:9; 31:39), not the "demand" of 42:22; "present", "balm", "gum", "resin" (43:11; 32:13; 37:25); "containers" (43:11; 42:25); "bag", "the mouth of your bags", "silver ... returned" (43:12, 18, 21-23; 42:27-28); "lodging place" (43:21; 42:27); "God Almighty" (43:14; 17:1); "bereaved" (43:14; 42:36; 27:45); "bowed down ... to the ground" (43:26, 28; 42:6); "Is it well with" (43:27; 29:6); Simeon was "bound before their eyes" (42:24) and is brought out at 43:23; Joseph "wept" (42:24; 43:30); "harm" and "bring down my gray hair in sorrow to Sheol" return at 44:29, 31; 43:28 has a marginal reading.

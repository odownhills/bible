# Notes on Genesis 43, for the translator of Genesis 44

Text followed: the Masoretic Text, read by its Ketiv; the chapter has no paragraph break inside it. 43:28: the Ketiv and the Qere are two spellings of "bowed down" (as at 27:29), so nothing is noted. Numbering is the same in Hebrew and English.

Brackets: none, so there is no decision log.

Places where the old copies differ, not bracketed:
- 43:28 the Samaritan copy and the old Greek add, after "he is still alive", "And he said, 'Blessed is that man by God.'"
- 43:16 the Samaritan copy and the old Greek have "saw them and Benjamin"; the Hebrew is "saw Benjamin with them".
- 43:23 "the God of your father"; the Samaritan copy and the old Greek have "of your fathers".
- 43:26 the old Greek adds "with their faces" before "to the ground" (42:6). 43:25 the old Greek has "that he would eat there".

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 43:2, 10, 13 "return": one verb all three times. 43:2 "the grain": the noun of 42:19, 26.
- 43:3 "solemnly warned", 43:7 "asked closely", 43:20 "We did come down": each is a verb with its own infinitive; 43:7 "really", in a question, as at 37:8.
- 43:3, 5 "see my face": the picture is kept (44:23, 26).
- 43:6 "that you had another brother": the Hebrew is "whether you still had a brother".
- 43:9 "I will have sinned against you for all time": it can also be put "I will bear the blame before you"; 44:32 quotes it with "my father" for "you".
- 43:11 "the prized produce of the land": one word, which elsewhere is "song" (Isaiah 51:3; Amos 5:23), and "strength" or "song" at Exodus 15:2; a verb with the same letters is "prune" (Leviticus 25:3). Whether it is what the land is praised for or what it yields, the list after it says what was taken.
- 43:12, 15 "double the silver": the word is also "second" (41:43). The English leaves open, as the Hebrew does, whether the double includes the silver that was returned; 43:22 calls the new silver "other silver".
- 43:12 "returned ... bring back" are one verb in the Hebrew; 43:18 "came back" is its simple form.
- 43:14 "if I am bereaved, I am bereaved": two perfects after "as", built like Esther 4:16 ("if I perish, I perish"). Heard as "bereaved as I am, I am bereaved", it would speak of Joseph and Simeon (42:36). "Give you compassion before the man": that the man may have compassion on them (1 Kings 8:50); 43:30 has the same word of Joseph.
- 43:14 "your other brother": the Hebrew is "your brother, another one".
- 43:16 "slaughter an animal": the Hebrew is "slaughter a slaughtering"; not the verb of 22:10 and 37:31.
- 43:18 "rush on us": the Hebrew is "roll himself on us" (the verb only here in this form); taken as "roll a charge on us" it would be "find a pretext against us". "They" is supplied: the Hebrew names no one.
- 43:20 "Pardon, my lord": the two words open a speech to a superior (44:18; Exodus 4:10; 1 Kings 3:17); what follows here is a statement, not a request.
- 43:21 tells the finding of the silver as all at the lodging place (42:27-28, 35); translated as it stands.
- 43:23 "It is well with you": the same two words as "It is well with your servant" (43:28); it can also be put "Peace to you" (Judges 6:23).
- 43:30 "wanted to weep": the Hebrew is "sought to weep". "Was stirred": the verb is "grow hot" (Lamentations 5:10).
- 43:32 "could not eat", "was a detestable thing": the narrator's own comment, put in the past like the story; "to Egypt" is the people (41:55).
- 43:33 "they sat": the Hebrew does not say that they were seated by someone. "Birthright": here the firstborn's place.
- 43:34 "portions were carried": the Hebrew is "he lifted portions", with no one named. "Larger than the portions of all of them, five times over": the Hebrew does not say "any" or "each". "Became drunk": the verb of 9:21, not softened.

For Genesis 44: "the one who was over his house" (44:1, 4; 43:16, 19); "bag", "the mouth of his bag", "silver" (44:1, 2, 8, 11, 12; 43:12, 21-23), and 44:8 takes up 43:21 ("the silver that we found in the mouth of our bags we brought back to you"); "learn by divination" (44:5, 15; 30:27); "slaves" (44:9, 10, 16, 17, 33; 43:18) beside "your servants" where the brothers speak of themselves (44:7, 16, 18; 43:28); "Pardon, my lord" (44:18; 43:20), with "please" kept for the request after it; "the boy" of Benjamin (44:22, 30-34; 43:8); "see my face", "see the man's face" (44:23, 26; 43:3, 5); "Return, buy us a little food" (44:25; 43:2) word for word; "has certainly been torn to pieces" (44:28; 37:33); "harm" and "bring down my gray hair in sorrow to Sheol" (44:29, 31; 42:38); "your servant became guarantor for the boy" and "I will have sinned against my father for all time" (44:32; 43:9); "control himself" returns at 45:1, and "wept" (43:30; 42:24) at 45:2, 14.

# Notes on Genesis 25, for the translator of Genesis 26

Text followed: the Masoretic Text, read by its Ketiv. 25:23: the Ketiv and the Qere are two spellings of "nations" (the same pair as at Psalm 79:10), so nothing is noted. The main text keeps to the Masoretic accents: 25:25 "red, all of him like a coat of hair".

Places where the old copies differ, none bracketed:
- 25:5 the old Greek has "to Isaac his son".
- 25:8 the Samaritan copy and the old Greek have "old and full of days" (35:29); the Hebrew text has no "of days", so "satisfied".
- 25:15 "Hadad": some Hebrew copies have "Hadar"; 1 Chronicles 1:30 has "Hadad".
- 25:22 the old Syriac version has "why am I alive", the main text's reading of the Hebrew.

Brackets: three. 25:21 "in front of his wife" [alt: on behalf of his wife] (close); 25:22 "why am I alive?" [alt: why is this happening to me?] (ask); 25:23 "the older will serve the younger" [alt: the greater will serve the lesser] (close).

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 25:1 "again took a wife": the Hebrew does not say when; 25:6 and 1 Chronicles 1:32 count Keturah among the concubines.
- 25:3 the three peoples of Dedan are names; the third is the word "peoples" (25:23) and the first is formed from "Asshur".
- 25:4 "Enoch": the Hebrew name is the one of 4:17 and 5:18, so it is spelled as there; many English Bibles write "Hanoch" here. Obay may prefer that.
- 25:6 "while he was still alive": Abraham; the English is as open as the Hebrew.
- 25:16 "tribes": a rare word, also at Numbers 25:15; "peoples" would also do.
- 25:18 "he settled": the verb is "fell". It is said of peoples lying in a valley (Judges 7:12), and also of raiders, "Sheba fell on them" (Job 1:15), which would go with the alternate at 16:12, "in defiance of all his brothers"; that bracket is not repeated. A third reading, "he died in the presence of all his brothers" (as at 11:28), was set aside: 25:17 has already told his death. "Facing Egypt" keeps the English of 23:19 and 25:9; the decision log of 16:12, which quotes this verse ahead of time, has "opposite Egypt".
- 25:20 "from Paddan-Aram" can go with Bethuel or with "took"; left open.
- 25:21 "pleaded ... granted his plea" is one Hebrew verb, active and then passive.
- 25:22 "crushed one another": the verb of Job 20:19, not softened. "The sons": the narrator's word, before she knows there are two.
- 25:23 the last line has no mark of which word is the subject. In the Hebrew order the first word is the one who serves, and 27:29, 37, 40 show it; "the younger will serve the older" was not bracketed. "From your body will separate": from birth on, or as two peoples that come out of her; left open.
- 25:26 "he was named": the verb is singular with no subject (25:25 has "they named"). "When they were born": the Hebrew is "at the bearing of them".
- 25:27 "a blameless man": the word said of Job (Job 1:1, 8). English Bibles often have "quiet" or "plain" here; no verse of Scripture uses the word that way, so it is not softened and not bracketed. Obay may want to see it.
- 25:28 "because game was in his mouth": Isaac's mouth, that is, he ate what Esau hunted (27:3-4); the picture is kept.
- 25:30 "the red, this red": the Hebrew says the word twice and names no dish; nothing is supplied.
- 25:31, 33 "first": the Hebrew is "as the day"; it is "first" at 1 Samuel 2:16 and can also be "today" or "at once".
- 25:32 "I am going to die": now, of hunger, or some day; the English is as open as the Hebrew.
- 25:34 "And Esau despised": plain "And", though it sums up.

For Genesis 26: "famine" and "go down to Egypt" (26:1-2) as at 12:10; "live" and "stay" are two verbs in 26:2-3 (16:12; 12:10); "tell you of", "establish" and "oath" (26:2-3) as at 22:2, 17:7 and 24:8; 26:4 "the stars of the sky" and "be blessed in" as at 22:17-18, with no new bracket; 26:5 "because" as at 22:18; 26:7 "beautiful in appearance" as at 24:16; 26:8 the verb of 21:9, which sounds like "Isaac"; Abimelech, Phicol, Gerar and Beersheba as in chapters 20 and 21; "quarrel" (26:20-22), "move on" and "make room for" (26:22) as the glossary plans; "blessed of Yahweh" (26:29) as at 24:31; Esau, Jacob, Rebekah and "the Hittite" (26:34) as here.

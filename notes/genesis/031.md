# Notes on Genesis 31, for the translator of Genesis 32

Text followed: the Masoretic Text; the chapter has no Qere and no paragraph break. English 31:55 is Hebrew 32:1, so all of chapter 32 is numbered one lower in English than in the Hebrew file (Hebrew 32:2 is English 32:1); the `numbering:` line of this chapter says so.

Places where the old copies differ, none bracketed by itself:
- 31:1 the old Greek names Jacob ("And Jacob heard"); the Hebrew has "he".
- 31:13 the old Greek has "I am the God who appeared to you in the place of God"; the Hebrew is "the God of Bethel".
- 31:29 the Samaritan copy and the old Greek have "your father" as said to one man; the Hebrew "your" is plural. English is the same.
- 31:49 the Samaritan copy has "and the pillar" where the Hebrew has "and Mizpah".
- 31:53 the old Greek and the Samaritan copy have "judge" in the singular, and the old Greek lacks "the God of their father": this is in the decision log under the bracket there.

Brackets: two. 31:43 "what can I do for these today" [alt: do to these today] (close); 31:53 "the god of Nahor ..., the gods of their father" [alt: the God of Nahor, the God of their father] (ask). Three notes: 31:20 on "deceived" ("stole the heart of"), 31:47 on the two names of the stone heap and "Gilead", 31:49 on "Mizpah".

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 31:1 "he": the Hebrew does not name Jacob. "Wealth" is the word for "glory".
- 31:2, 5 "as before" is the Hebrew's "as yesterday and the day before". In 31:2 Laban is not "with" Jacob; in 31:3 and 31:5 God is.
- 31:3 "relatives", as at 12:1 and 24:4 (the word can also be "birthplace"; the bracket stood once, at 12:1). It returns at 32:9.
- 31:9, 16 "taken away": the verb is "rescue" elsewhere (Job 5:19).
- 31:10, 12 "he-goats" is a second word for male goats (30:35 "male goats"); "dappled" is a new word beside "striped" and "speckled".
- 31:16, 17 "children" and 31:28, 43, 55 "sons" are one Hebrew word; it is "sons" where it stands beside "daughters".
- 31:19, 34, 35 "the teraphim": the name is kept, as "the Nephilim" is; Laban and Jacob call them "gods" (31:30, 32). "Household gods" would add a word; Obay can switch it.
- 31:20, 26, 27 "deceived": the idiom "stole the heart of" (31:27 "stole me") is put by its meaning, since "stole his heart" says something else in English; the note at 31:20 gives it.
- 31:21 "the River" is the Euphrates (15:18).
- 31:23, 25, 32, 37, 46, 54 "brothers": the men of each side's family, as at 13:8 and 29:15.
- 31:24, 29 "either good or bad" is the Hebrew's "from good to bad" (24:50 "bad or good").
- 31:36 "offense": the word is "rebellion" in Job; here it is a wrong one man charges another with, as at Exodus 22:9.
- 31:39 "bear the loss of" is a form of the verb "sin". "Whether stolen by day or stolen by night" is, word for word, "stolen of day and stolen of night".
- 31:42 "he gave his decision": the verb of 31:37 ("decide between the two of us"); it can also be "he rebuked you" (21:25). Both speak of the dream of 31:24.
- 31:42, 53 "the Dread of Isaac": a name for God, with the word "dread" of Job 3:25.
- 31:44 "let it serve as a witness": the Hebrew does not say what "it" is; its verb does not match "covenant".
- 31:46 "on the stone heap" can also be "by the stone heap".
- 31:49 "because he said" can also be "of which he said". "When we are hidden" can also be "for we will be hidden".
- 31:51 "that I have set up": Laban says "I", though Jacob raised the pillar (31:45).
- 31:54 "in the hill country" (twice) is the word of 31:25; it can also be "on the mountain".

For Genesis 32: 32:1 (Hebrew 32:2) follows straight on from 31:55, "And Laban ... returned to his place": "And Jacob went on his way"; "angels of God" as at 28:12 and "the angel of God" (31:11); "brothers" (32:3, 6, of Esau); "Return to your land and to your relatives" (32:9) repeats 31:3, with "relatives"; "the God of my father Abraham and the God of my father Isaac" (32:9) beside 31:42, 53; "staff" (32:10) is the "stick" of 30:37; "male goats" (32:14) is the word of 30:35, not the "he-goats" of 31:10; "ewes" and "rams" (32:14) as at 31:38; "female slaves" (32:5, 22) is the word of 30:4, not the "female servants" of 31:33; "the hill country of Gilead", "Mizpah" and "Galeed" are spelled as here; "prevailed" (32:28) as at 30:8.

# Notes on Genesis 47, for the translator of Genesis 48

Text followed: the Masoretic Text; the chapter has no Qere. The Hebrew has no paragraph break inside the chapter and one after 47:31; the three sections are editorial. Numbering is the same in Hebrew and English.

Brackets: two, both places where the old copies differ, each with an entry in the decision log (both call: clear): 47:21 "he moved them to the cities" [alt: he made them slaves]; 47:31 "at the head of the bed" [alt: on the top of his staff].

Places where the old copies differ, not bracketed:
- 47:5-6 the old Greek has these sentences in another order and tells twice that Jacob and his sons came to Joseph; nothing is said there that the Hebrew text does not say.
- 47:16 the Hebrew has no object after "I will give to you"; the Samaritan copy and the old Greek have "bread". Nothing is supplied.

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 47:2 "from among his brothers": the Hebrew is "from the whole of his brothers" (the word of 19:4); it does not say which five or why.
- 47:3 "shepherds of flocks" is singular in the Hebrew ("a shepherd of flocks your servants are"). 47:3 and 47:4 both begin "And they said to Pharaoh", as the Hebrew has it.
- 47:4 "To stay ... live": two verbs, the stay of a foreigner (12:10) and the ordinary word for living in a place.
- 47:7, 10 "blessed": the word is also the one for greeting a person and for taking leave (31:55); "blessed" is kept, as the Hebrew says it.
- 47:9 "staying as a foreigner": one Hebrew word, the noun of "stay" (17:8; 28:4; 37:1, "the land where ... stayed"); "as a foreigner" says what the word means and is not a second word. "Bad" is the word for "evil" and "bad" (41:19; 44:34).
- 47:12 "according to the little ones": the Hebrew is as short; it is the phrase for a share measured by something (by the number of the little ones, or by what they need).
- 47:13 "in all the land": or "in all the earth" (41:57). "Grew faint": the verb stands only here.
- 47:15, 20 "Egypt" for the people, as at 41:55.
- 47:17 "led them through": the verb for leading a flock gently (33:14).
- 47:18 "the second year": the year after "that year", not the second year of the famine (45:6). "Have gone to my lord": the Hebrew is "to my lord", with no verb; it could also be put "the silver and the herds of livestock have come to an end, to my lord".
- 47:18-26 "ground" for the fields the people own and sow, "land" for the country: two Hebrew words (both in 47:20). 47:19, 25 "slaves" and 47:3, 4 "servants" are one Hebrew word, as in 44:9 and 44:16.
- 47:21 "to the cities": it could also be put "city by city".
- 47:22 "allowance" and 47:26 "statute" are one Hebrew word.
- 47:24 "when the crops come in": the Hebrew is "at the crops"; "four parts" is "four hands", the word of 43:34.
- 47:25 "Let us find favor": as at 33:15; it could also be put "May we find favor".
- 47:26 "the fifth is Pharaoh's": the Hebrew is "for Pharaoh, for the fifth".
- 47:29 "show loyalty and truth to me": the wording of 24:49, between people; of God it is "faithful love and truth" (24:27; 32:10).
- 47:30 "And I will lie with my fathers, and you are to carry me": the Hebrew can be the wish (to lie with them and not in Egypt) or "When I lie with my fathers, you are to carry me", where to lie with them is to die; the English is left as open.
- 47:31 "bowed down": the Hebrew does not say to whom. "At the head of the bed": the preposition is "on".
- "Israel" and "Jacob" each as the Hebrew has it (47:7-10, 27-31).

For Genesis 48: "the bed" (48:2; 47:31), the same word; "Israel" and "Jacob" each as the Hebrew has it; "holding" (48:4; 47:11) and "be fruitful and multiply" (48:4; 47:27); "lay one's hand on" (48:14, 17; 46:4), not "put ... under" (47:29); "the land where ... stayed" and "staying as a foreigner" for the same noun (47:9); "grave" (47:30) and "bury" for Rachel (48:7; 35:19-20); "little ones" is not the word for "boys" (48:16); Jacob's fathers "Abraham and Isaac" (48:15-16) are the "fathers" of 47:9 and 47:30; "bowed down" (48:12; 47:31).

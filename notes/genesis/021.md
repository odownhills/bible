# Notes on Genesis 21, for the translator of Genesis 22

Text followed: the Masoretic Text; the chapter has no Ketiv and Qere. The main text keeps to the Masoretic vowels and accents: 21:14 "the child" is read as one more thing given to Hagar; 21:16 it is Hagar who weeps; 21:33 no subject is named ("he"), and the English names none.

Places where the old copies differ. One is bracketed: 21:16, where the old Greek has "and the child cried out and wept" (clear). Not bracketed:
- 21:9 the old Greek adds "with Isaac her son" (it is in the decision log, as support for the main text).
- 21:13 the Samaritan copy and the old Greek have "a great nation", as at 21:18.
- 21:14 the old Greek has "and he put the child on her shoulder" (it is in the decision log, as support for the alternate).
- 21:22, 32 the old Greek adds a third man, "Ahuzzath his friend" (26:26); 21:33 the Samaritan copy and the old Greek name Abraham.

Brackets: 21:6 "will laugh with me" [alt: will laugh at me] (close); 21:9 "laughing" [alt: mocking] (ask); 21:14 "and he gave her the child" [alt: and the child too, on her shoulder] (clear); 21:16 (clear). Three notes on sound echoes: 21:6 "laughter", "laugh" and "Isaac"; 21:17 "God has heard" and "Ishmael", who is never named in the chapter; 21:31 "Beersheba", "seven" and "swore".

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 21:1 "attended to": the verb is "inspect" in Job; of Yahweh turning to a person "visited" would be heard as paying a call. It returns at 50:24-25.
- 21:2 "that God had spoken of": the Hebrew word after "spoken" is "it"; the same letters can be heard as "with him" (17:22-23). The set time is the same.
- 21:6 "Laughter God has made for me": as open as the Hebrew (joy given to her, or laughter brought on her); the bracket stands on the second half only.
- 21:7 "Who would have said": a rare verb, "utter" at Job 8:2. "Sarah has nursed children": direct speech, the plural kept. "For I have borne a son in his old age": no "him" is added.
- 21:9 "laughing": the form listed as "joke" (19:14); here it is the verb of Isaac's name, and "laughing" lets it be heard with 21:6. "Playing" is the same reading in another word.
- 21:11 "his son": Ishmael, as 21:12 shows; the English is as open as the Hebrew.
- 21:12 "in Isaac offspring will be called yours": the Hebrew is "in Isaac offspring will be called for you" (it is quoted at Romans 9:7 and Hebrews 11:18).
- 21:14 "he gave her the child": the verb is supplied from "gave them to Hagar". "Sent her out" is the verb of 3:23, beside "drive out" (21:10; 3:24).
- 21:15 "threw": not softened. 21:16 "opposite him": "him" is supplied, both times. "About a bowshot away": the Hebrew is "like those who shoot a bow".
- 21:17 "from the heavens": 19:24 has "from the sky" of the fire. "Where he is": no more is said.
- 21:20 "an archer, a bowman": two words in the Hebrew; the first can also be heard as "growing up" ("as he grew he became a bowman").
- 21:22 a singular verb with two subjects; the speech is Abimelech's ("me", "my").
- 21:23 "here" can be heard of the place or as "now and here". "The same loyalty": the Hebrew is "according to the loyalty".
- 21:25 "rebuked": the verb form breaks the chain and can be heard as "kept rebuking". "Over the well" and "on account of his son" (21:11) are one Hebrew phrase.
- 21:26 "I do not know": the Hebrew can also be "I did not know".
- 21:30 "so that it may be a witness": the same form can be "so that you may be a witness"; the word for "witness" is the one used of things (31:52).
- 21:31 "was named": no subject is named. "Swore": no "an oath" is added.
- 21:33 "the Everlasting God": a title like "God Most High" (14:18) and "God Almighty" (17:1). "Called on the name of Yahweh" as at 12:8 and 13:4.

The decision log of 20:16 quoted 21:25 ahead of time, and the chapter keeps that wording.

For Genesis 22: 22:1 "After these things" (15:1); 22:2 "Go" (12:1) and "your son, your only one" after Ishmael has been sent out; 22:3 "rose early in the morning" (19:27; 20:8; 21:14); 22:5, 12 "the boy" is the word used of Ishmael (21:12, 17-20), and 22:3, 5, 19 the same word in the plural is "the young men" (18:7); 22:11, 15 "the angel of Yahweh called ... from the heavens" (21:17 has "the angel of God"); 22:12 "fear of God" (20:11); 22:13 "lifted his eyes and saw, and look" (18:2), where God "opened her eyes, and she saw" at 21:19; 22:17-18 "offspring" (21:12-13) and "be blessed in" (12:3; 18:18; 22:18 has another form of the verb); 22:19 Beersheba, the same spelling; 22:16 "swear by" (21:23).

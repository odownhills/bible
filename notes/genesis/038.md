# Notes on Genesis 38, for the translator of Genesis 39

Text followed: the Masoretic Text; the chapter has no marginal reading. The Hebrew has one paragraph break, after 38:30. Numbering is the same in Hebrew and English.

Brackets: three, each in the decision log, all `clear`.
- 38:5 "And he was at Chezib when she gave birth to him" [alt: And she was at Chezib when she gave birth to them, as the old Greek has it]. "He" is as open as the Hebrew, which names no one; the last man named is Judah.
- 38:12 "Hirah his friend the Adullamite" [alt: Hirah his shepherd the Adullamite, as the old Greek has it]. Not repeated at 38:20.
- 38:26 "She is more righteous than I" [alt: She is in the right; it is by me].

Bracketed notes: 38:7 ("evil" is the name "Er" written backward, as at 6:8); 38:21 (the word is not the one for "prostitute" in 38:15); 38:29 (the name "Perez").

Places where the old copies differ, not bracketed:
- 38:3 "he named him Er": the Hebrew text has "he" here and "she" at 38:4-5; the Samaritan copy and some Hebrew copies have "she" at 38:3 too.
- 38:15 the old Greek adds "and he did not recognize her". 38:21 "the men of her place": the Samaritan copy and the old Greek have "the men of the place", as 38:22 does. 38:29-30 "he was named": the Hebrew verb is "he named", with no subject (as at 25:26); other copies have "she named".

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 38:1 "turned aside to": the verb is the one for pitching a tent (12:8), with no "tent" here; 38:16 has it again of Judah and Tamar.
- 38:2 "a Canaanite man": the word is also "trader" in later books (Proverbs 31:24; Zechariah 14:21). Genesis uses it only of the people (24:3; 28:1), and 1 Chronicles 2:3 calls her "the Canaanite woman".
- 38:2, 12 "the daughter of Shua": Shua is her father's name (38:2); she is not named (1 Chronicles 2:3 writes the two words as if a name).
- 38:8-9 "offspring" three times is one Hebrew word, which is also "seed". At 38:9 the Hebrew has no object ("he would ruin to the ground"), and "his seed" is supplied from that word. "Waste" is the verb "ruin" of 6:11-13.
- 38:10 "he put him to death also": "he" is Yahweh; the Hebrew does not repeat the name.
- 38:11 "for he said": said to himself, as at 26:9 and 31:31.
- 38:14 "the entrance of Enaim": a place (38:21 "at Enaim"; Joshua 15:34-35 has an Enam in the lowland of Judah, in the same list as Adullam). The name is the word for "eyes" and "springs", and the two words together can be heard as "the opening of the eyes"; no note was written. "Covered herself": the Hebrew has "covered" with no object.
- 38:16 "that you may sleep with me": the small word can also be "when" or "if".
- 38:17 "If you give a pledge until you send it": the Hebrew leaves the sentence unfinished, and so does the English.
- 38:18 "your cord" is singular; 38:25 "the cords" is plural, and "seal" there is another form of the word. "That is in your hand" goes with the staff.
- 38:20 "he did not find her": the friend, as 38:22 shows.
- 38:21-22 "shrine prostitute": Hosea 4:14 sets these women beside prostitutes at the sacrifices, and Deuteronomy 23:17-18 forbids them in Israel. Judah saw "a prostitute" (38:15); his friend asks for the other word.
- 38:23 "Let her keep it": the Hebrew is "let her take for herself", with no object.
- 38:24 "prostituted herself": the verb covers any unfaithfulness of a woman who is bound to a man; she was bound to Shelah (38:11).
- 38:26 "since": the two small words of 18:5 and 19:8. "He was not intimate with her again": "not ... again" as at 8:12.
- 38:28 "one put out a hand": the Hebrew is "he gave a hand", with no subject.
- 38:29 "What a breach you have made for yourself!": the words can also be divided, "How you have broken through! The breach is on you" (as "The violence done to me is on you", 16:5). Either way the breach is his.
- 38:30 "Zerah": the name is from the verb "rise", said of the sun (32:31); the text draws nothing from it.

For Genesis 39: 39:1 takes up 37:36 ("Potiphar, a court official of Pharaoh, the commander of the guard", no bracket; "the Ishmaelites"; Joseph "was taken down", the verb of Judah's "went down", 38:1, and of 37:25); "sleep with" stays for the idiom of 38:2, 16 and "lie with" for the other verb (39:7, 10, 12, 14); "garment" (39:12-18) as at 38:14; "the Hebrew" (39:14, 17) and "joke" (39:14) as the glossary has them; "his master's anger burned" (39:19) as at 30:2; "evil" (39:9) as at 38:7.

# Notes on Genesis 48, for the translator of Genesis 49

Text followed: the Masoretic Text; the chapter has no Qere. The Hebrew has no paragraph break inside the chapter and one after 48:22; the three sections are editorial. Numbering is the same in Hebrew and English.

Brackets: three, each with an entry in the decision log: 48:12 "he bowed down with his face to the ground" [alt: they bowed down to him, as the Samaritan copy and the old Greek have it] (clear); 48:14 "he guided his hands with insight" [alt: he crossed his hands] (ask); 48:22 "one shoulder of land" [alt: Shechem, one portion] (clear), with a note that the word is also the name. One more note: 48:16, the verb "teem" is from the word for "fish".

Places where the old copies differ, not bracketed:
- 48:1 the old Greek has "it was reported to Joseph", the same thing in other words.
- 48:4 the old Greek has "to you and to your offspring after you".
- 48:7 the Samaritan copy and the old Greek have "Paddan-Aram" and the old Greek adds "your mother" after "Rachel".
- 48:15 the old Greek has "he blessed them" for "he blessed Joseph"; the blessing itself says "bless the boys", and 48:20 says "he blessed them".
- 48:20 the old Greek has "By you" in the plural and "Israel will be blessed"; the saying that follows shows the sense either way.

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 48:1, 2 the Hebrew has "he said", "he told" with no one named; the English passive is used, as at 22:20.
- 48:2 "has come to you": or "is coming to you". "Gathered his strength and sat up on the bed" is the wording the 47:31 decision log quoted ahead of time.
- 48:5 "before I came to you in Egypt": the Hebrew is "until my coming to you, to Egypt".
- 48:6 "whom you have fathered after them": the Hebrew verb is a past form; it can speak of children Joseph already has or of any he may yet have (Scripture names none). The English is left as the Hebrew has it.
- 48:7 "died on me": the Hebrew is "on me", as at 42:36 ("It is on me that all these things have come"); it could also be "beside me". Why Jacob speaks of Rachel here the text does not say, and nothing is added.
- 48:8, 10 Israel "saw" Joseph's sons and "could not see": both as the Hebrew has them. 48:10 "he brought them close": Joseph, but the Hebrew names no one.
- 48:11 "I did not think": the verb is elsewhere "judge" (1 Sam 2:25); only here of what one expected.
- 48:12 "from beside his knees": Israel's knees; the Hebrew "his" is as open.
- 48:14 "who was the younger": the Hebrew is "and he was the younger".
- 48:15-16 one sentence: "The God ..., the God ..., the angel ..." with one verb in the singular, "may he bless". "Has shepherded", "has redeemed": the Hebrew has "the one shepherding me", "the one redeeming me". "From all evil": or "from every harm".
- 48:16 "may my name be called in them": kept word for word, as at 21:12; it means that his name and his fathers' will live on in them. "In the midst of the land": or "of the earth".
- 48:19 "the fullness of the nations": word for word; what fills the nations, or a full number of nations. Kept apart from "a multitude of nations" (17:4).
- 48:20 "By you": singular in the Hebrew, though he blesses "them". "Put" (48:18, 20) and "make" (48:20) are one Hebrew verb.
- 48:21 "you" and "your fathers" are plural in the Hebrew (Joseph and his people); 48:22 "you" is Joseph alone.
- 48:22 "I give": or "I have given". "Which I took": Genesis tells of no war of Jacob's with the Amorites; at Shechem he bought a plot (33:19) and his sons used the sword (34:25-29). Nothing is added. "More than your brothers": the Hebrew is "above" or "over and above".
- "Israel" and "Jacob" each as the Hebrew has it (48:2 has both).

For Genesis 49: "be gathered to his people" (49:29, 33; 25:8) and "the bed" (49:33; 47:31; 48:2); "holding for a grave" and "facing Mamre" (49:30; 23:17-20); "firstborn" (49:3; 48:14, 18); Simeon and Levi and what they did at Shechem (49:5-7; 34:25-30; 48:22); "shoulder" (49:15) is the word of 48:22; "bless", "blessing" (49:25-28; 48:15-20) and "God Almighty" (49:25 has "the Almighty"; 48:3); "shepherd" of God (49:24; 48:15); "more than your brothers" is not the phrase of 49:26 ("set apart from his brothers"); "Israel" and "Jacob" each as the Hebrew has it; the sayings of chapter 49 are verse, each verse on one line.

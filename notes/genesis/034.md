# Notes on Genesis 34, for the translator of Genesis 35

Text followed: the Masoretic Text. No Qere changes the sense: in 34:3 (twice) and 34:12 "young woman" is written in its short, older spelling and read in the full one, as everywhere in Genesis (24:14). The Hebrew has one paragraph break, after 34:31. Numbering is the same in Hebrew and English.

Places where the old copies differ, not bracketed by themselves:
- 34:2 the old Greek has "the Horite" for "the Hivite".
- 34:7 the old Greek and Latin divide the verse as the alternate does; that is in the decision log.
- 34:14 the old Greek names the speakers, "Simeon and Levi, the brothers of Dinah".
- 34:27 the Samaritan copy and the old Greek begin "And the sons of Jacob"; the Hebrew has no "And".

Brackets: four. 34:7 "came in from the field when they heard, and the men were pained" [alt: came in from the field. When they heard, the men were pained] (close); 34:7 "in Israel" [alt: against Israel] (clear); 34:10 "trade in it" [alt: move about in it] (clear), not repeated at 34:21; 34:25 "came against the city in security" [alt: while it felt secure] (close). One note: 34:21 "at peace" is the word for "safely" (33:18).

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 34:1 "to see" is "to look at", "to look on" (21:16); the next verse answers it with "saw her".
- 34:2 "the chieftain of the land" can go with Hamor or with Shechem, in English as in Hebrew. "Lay with her": the Hebrew vowels make her the object of the verb, a rougher way of saying it (2 Samuel 13:14); the English is the same as for "lie with" (19:32).
- 34:2 "violated": the verb is "afflict" (15:13; 16:6; 31:50), "bring low". Of a man and a woman it names the wrong done to her and does not by itself say force (Deuteronomy 22:24 has it where she did not cry out; 22:29 and 2 Samuel 13:14 where she was seized). "Violated" is as wide as that; "raped" would say more than the word, "humbled" less than the sentence.
- 34:3 "spoke to the heart of": the Hebrew picture is kept (50:21; Ruth 2:13; Isaiah 40:2); it is speech that comforts and wins over.
- 34:4 "girl" is the feminine of "child" (21:8), not the "young woman" of 34:3, 12.
- 34:5 "he had defiled": Shechem; the Hebrew does not name him. "Defiled" is the verb of "unclean" (Job 14:4).
- 34:7 "outrage" is the noun of "foolish" (Job 2:10): what is done by one with no sense of what is right. "Israel" is the name of 32:28; the narrator still says "Jacob".
- 34:8, 9 "your daughter", "your daughters": "your" is plural, said to Jacob and his sons.
- 34:9, 16, 21 the daughters stand first or second as the Hebrew has them. Hamor says "you will give ... you will take" to Jacob's sons and "we will take ... we will give" to his own city.
- 34:10 "take holdings" is the verb of "holding" (17:8; 23:4).
- 34:12 "put on me": the Hebrew is "make very great on me".
- 34:13 "and spoke" stands by itself in the Hebrew. "Because he had defiled" can also be heard as "who had defiled" (of Shechem); so too 34:27, "the city that had defiled their sister". The fact is the same.
- 34:15, 22 "condition" is supplied ("only on this"). "Every male among you", "among us" as at 17:10.
- 34:17 "our daughter": so the brothers say.
- 34:19 "the most honored of all" is "honored more than all".
- 34:21 "broad on every side" is "broad of hands", put by its meaning.
- 34:23 "livestock" is the word of 1:24, not that of 33:14. "Will they not be ours?": the Hebrew has no verb.
- 34:24 "his city": so the Hebrew, both times, though 34:20 has "their city".
- 34:27 "they had defiled": the plural lays it on the whole city.
- 34:29 "in the houses": the Hebrew is "in the house", one for all.
- 34:30 "making me stink": the picture is kept (Exodus 5:21). "Few in number" is "men of number". "As for me" and "I and my household": Jacob speaks of his whole house as "I".
- 34:31 "Should he treat": the Hebrew names no one; it can also be heard as "Should our sister be treated". The chapter ends on the question.

For Genesis 35: "Shechem" (35:4) is the city of 33:18 and 34:20, and the tree there is a third word beside the two "oak" words (12:6; 35:8); "the terror of God" on the cities around them, who "did not pursue the sons of Jacob" (35:5), answers 34:30, and "terror" is of the root of the word at 9:2; "Bethel" and "when you fled from Esau your brother" (35:1, 7) as at 28:19 and 27:43; "God Almighty", "be fruitful and multiply" and "an assembly of nations" (35:11) beside 17:1 and 28:3, which has "peoples"; "Israel" (35:10) as at 32:28, where "said to be" is another verb than the "called" of 35:10; "pillar", "set up" and "poured oil" (35:14, 20) as at 28:18; "nurse" (35:8) as at 24:59; "lay with" (35:22) as at 34:2, 7, and "Israel heard" there beside "Jacob heard" (34:5); "Simeon and Levi" (35:23) as here.

# Notes on Genesis 23, for the translator of Genesis 24

Text followed: the Masoretic Text; the chapter has no Ketiv and Qere. The main text keeps to the Masoretic vowels and accents: 23:5 and 23:14 end with "saying to him", and the speech begins in the next verse.

Places where the old copies differ, none bracketed:
- 23:1 the old Greek does not have the last words, "the years of the life of Sarah".
- 23:2 the Samaritan copy and the old Greek add "in the valley" after Kiriath-Arba.
- 23:5-6 and 23:14-15: where the Hebrew has "saying to him", the old Greek has "No, my lord", as Ephron says at 23:11. The same letters with other vowels would be "if only", the word of 23:13. Either way they ask to be listened to, so nothing is noted.
- 23:13 the old Greek has "since you are on my side, listen to me".

Brackets: one. 23:6 "a chieftain of God" [alt: a mighty chieftain] (clear).

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 23:2 "came": the verb is also "went in" (to where she lay). Abraham was last said to live at Beersheba (22:19); the text does not say where he came from.
- 23:2 "lament": the verb is of wailing aloud for the dead; "mourn" is kept for another word.
- 23:3 "from beside his dead": the Hebrew is "from over the face of his dead". "His dead", "my dead", "your dead" are one dead person all through the chapter, in the Hebrew a masculine form.
- 23:4 "a resident foreigner and a settler": two nouns. "Among you" is "with you" both times. "Out of my sight" is "from before me" (23:4, 8).
- 23:6 "my lord" is singular, though the sons of Heth speak together. "Withhold" shares its English with the verb of 22:12, 16, as planned.
- 23:8 "If you are willing": the Hebrew is "if it is with your soul".
- 23:9 "Machpelah" has the article each time in the Hebrew, and the word is from a root that means "double"; no note. "Among you" can also be heard as "in your presence".
- 23:10 "was sitting among": the verb is also "lived among". He answers on the spot, in the gate (19:1), and "the Hittite" already says whose he is, so nothing is noted.
- 23:10 "of all who entered": the Hebrew sets these beside "the sons of Heth" as the same hearers. 23:18 "his city": the English is as open as the Hebrew (Ephron's, as at 23:10, or Abraham's).
- 23:11, 13 "I give": the Hebrew perfect, of a thing done in the saying of it (9:3; 15:18); "I have given" is the same form.
- 23:13 the Hebrew sentence breaks off after "if you" and starts again; the dash keeps that.
- 23:15 the Hebrew sets "your dead" first; English cannot without adding a word.
- 23:16 "listened to": the verb that is "listen to" six times in the chapter (23:6, 8, 11, 13, 15, 16). "He had spoken of": Ephron. "Current among the merchants": the Hebrew is "passing to the merchant".
- 23:17, 20 "was made over to": the verb is "rose", "stood", the verb of 23:3 and 23:7, here of a sale that stands. Leviticus 25:30 and 27:19 have it again and may need another wording. 23:17-18 is one sentence across the verse break.
- 23:17 "in front of" and 23:19 "facing" are two Hebrew phrases; both can mean "east of". In 23:17 both "that was" clauses can go with the field, in English as in Hebrew.
- 23:19 "(that is, Hebron)" stands after "Mamre"; 13:18 has "the oaks of Mamre, which are in Hebron".
- 23:20 "from the sons of Heth": the holding passed from them.

For Genesis 24: "advanced in years" (24:1) as at 18:11; 24:3 "among whom I am living" has the verb that is "sitting" at 23:10; "relatives" (24:4) as at 12:1 and "the land of my birth" (24:7) as at 11:28; "walk before" (24:40) as at 17:1; "bowed down" (24:26, 48, 52) as at 23:7, 12; "listen to" for heeding a person; "master" for the servant's word for Abraham (the Job 3 line), though it is the word that is "my lord" at 23:6, 11, 15; "shekel" where the Hebrew has it (24:22 has a weight of gold with no "shekels"); Rebekah, Bethuel, Milcah and Nahor as at 22:20-23; Beer-Lahai-Roi (24:62) as at 16:14; "take possession of the gate of" (24:60), which the decision log of 22:17 quotes ahead of time; "comfort" (24:67) as at 5:29, where Isaac is comforted after his mother, whose death this chapter tells; the cave and the field return at 25:9-10 ("facing Mamre", "from the sons of Heth") and at 49:29-32 and 50:13.

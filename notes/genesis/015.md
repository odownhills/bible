# Notes on Genesis 15, for the translator of Genesis 16

Text followed: the Masoretic Text; the chapter has no Ketiv and Qere. The main text keeps to the Masoretic vowels: 15:11 "drove them away" (the same letters with other vowels are "sat down with them", as the old Greek has it).

Places where the old copies differ, none bracketed on that ground:
- 15:1 the Samaritan copy has "I will multiply your reward greatly"; it is named in the decision log, on the side of the main text.
- 15:2 the old Greek takes the rare word as the name of a woman of the household ("the son of" her), and the old Latin has "the son of the steward of my house"; the Latin is named in the decision log.
- 15:21 the Samaritan copy and the old Greek add "the Hivites" (10:17) to the list; the Hebrew text has ten peoples.

Brackets: 15:1 "your reward will be very great" [alt: I am a shield to you, your very great reward] (close); 15:2 "the one who will get my house" [alt: the one in charge of my house] (close). No "ask" in this chapter. Three notes: 15:1 ("shield" and "delivered", 14:20), 15:2 (the rare word sounds like "Damascus"), 15:8 ("take possession of" and "be heir" are one verb).

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 15:2 "since I go childless": the verb is "walk" or "go"; it can be going on through life or going to one's death (25:32), and the English is as open.
- 15:2 "Eliezer of Damascus": the Hebrew is "he is Damascus Eliezer", with no "of"; the man is not named again in Scripture.
- 15:3 "one of my household": the Hebrew is "a son of my house" (Ecclesiastes 2:7), an idiom for one who belongs to the house; it is not the "born in his house" of 14:14. The word "son" in it, beside "no offspring", is not shown.
- 15:4 "came" is supplied (the Hebrew has "And look, the word of Yahweh to him"). "Out of your body": the word is the inward parts, said of a father at 2 Samuel 7:12.
- 15:5-7 "brought him outside" (15:5), "come out" (15:4, 14) and "brought you out" (15:7) are one verb.
- 15:6 "he put his trust in Yahweh": the glossary's wording for this verb with "in"; "believed in Yahweh" is the same meaning (Romans 4:3 and Galatians 3:6 quote it from the Greek as "believed God"). The verb form is not the usual one of the story line; the English is a plain past. Obay may want to choose between "put his trust in" and "believed".
- 15:6 "and he counted it to him as righteousness": the Hebrew does not name who counted or to whom, and the English does not either (Psalm 106:31 says nearly the same of Phinehas, in the passive: "it was counted to him as righteousness").
- 15:8 "how": the Hebrew is "by what".
- 15:9 "three-year-old": the word is "tripled", of an animal its age (1 Samuel 1:24 in the old Greek); "three heifers" would be said with the number. "Young bird": a nestling (Deuteronomy 32:11); the text does not say of what bird.
- 15:10 "the birds": one word in the singular for the two. "Half" (15:10) and "pieces" (15:17) are two Hebrew words (Jeremiah 34:18-19 has the first).
- 15:12 "a terror, a great darkness": two nouns side by side, as in the Hebrew.
- 15:13 "and they will serve them, and they will afflict them": the Hebrew changes the subject without saying so; 15:14 ("the nation that they serve") shows who serves. "Four hundred years" stands last, set apart by the accents, and covers the whole (Exodus 12:40 has four hundred and thirty).
- 15:16 "in the fourth generation": the Hebrew has no "in" ("a fourth generation, they will return"). "Not yet": the Hebrew is "until here", the word "here" of the first half. "Complete" sounds like "peace" (15:15) and "Salem" (14:18); not shown.
- 15:17 "dark": a rare word (Ezekiel 12:6-7), not the "darkness" of 15:12. "Oven": the word for a baking oven (Leviticus 2:4). The sentence has no main verb, as in the Hebrew.
- 15:18 "I give": the Hebrew perfect, a thing done in the saying (9:3); 12:7 and 13:15 have "I will give". The list of 15:19-21 stands in apposition to "this land".

For Genesis 16: "afflict" (15:13) is the verb of 16:6, and 16:9 and 16:11 are from the same root ("affliction", Job 10:15); "judge" at 16:5 is the other verb, not the one of 15:14; 16:10 "count" as at 15:5 and "offspring" as all through; 16:10 "greatly multiply" (3:16); 16:2, 4 the idiom of 6:4 ("sleep with"); "female slave" (12:16) for Hagar and "the Egyptian" beside 15:13-14 (the land not theirs is not named in 15); "the land of Canaan"; "angel" for a messenger from heaven (Job 1 line); Kadesh (16:14) as at 14:7.

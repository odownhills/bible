# Notes on Genesis 33, for the translator of Genesis 34

Text followed: the Masoretic Text. No Qere changes the sense (33:4 "his neck" is only written short). In 33:4 the Hebrew text has dots over the word "and kissed him"; the word is in the text and is translated. The Hebrew has paragraph breaks after 33:17 and after 33:20. Numbering is the same in Hebrew and English.

Places where the old copies differ, not bracketed by themselves:
- 33:13 the Samaritan copy and the old Greek have "if I drive them hard"; the Hebrew is "if they drive them hard", with no one named, put as "if they are driven hard".
- 33:18 the old Greek and Latin have "to Salem, a city of Shechem", and the Samaritan copy "in peace": both are in the decision log under the bracket there.
- 33:19 the old Greek has "a hundred lambs" for the hundred kesitahs. The unit is kept: at Job 42:11 it is given beside a gold ring, and Joshua 24:32 names the same price.
- 33:20 the old Greek has "he called on the God of Israel".

Brackets: two. 33:15 "Why is that? Let me find favor in the eyes of my lord" [alt: Why is it that I should find favor in the eyes of my lord?] (close); 33:18 "came safely to the city of Shechem" [alt: came to Salem, a city of Shechem] (clear). One note: 33:17 "Succoth" means "shelters".

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 33:2 "after them" twice: the Hebrew has the same word for Leah's place and for Rachel's ("behind", "last").
- 33:4 "fell on his neck": the gesture is kept (it returns at 45:14 and 46:29). "They wept" is plural.
- 33:5 "Who are these to you?" and 33:8 "What is all this camp of yours": the same small word, "to you", of what belongs to a man. In 33:8 the Hebrew asks "who", of a company.
- 33:5, 11 "favored": the verb of the noun "favor" (33:8, 10, 15); in Job 19:21 it is "have pity on".
- 33:9 "I have plenty" and 33:11 "I have everything" are two different words. In 33:9 "my brother" goes with the second half, as the Hebrew accents divide it.
- 33:10 "since I have seen your face as one sees the face of God": "since" as at 18:5 and 19:8. "The face of God" takes up Peniel (32:30); the word could also be heard as "a divine being", as at 32:28, where the bracket stood. "Accepted me" is not the verb of 32:20.
- 33:11 "my blessing": the Hebrew word is "blessing", used of a thing given (1 Samuel 25:27); it is kept, so that it is heard with 27:35-36. "He took it": "it" is supplied.
- 33:12 "in front of you": the word is "before", "in view of"; it can also be heard as "alongside you". Either way Esau offers to travel with him. "He said" is Esau; the Hebrew does not name him.
- 33:13 "nursing" is another word than at 32:15 (it stands with "lead" at Isaiah 40:11). "In my care" is "upon me". "Tender" as at 18:7.
- 33:14 "move along slowly" is "lead myself on at my gentleness"; "at the pace of" is "at the foot of"; "livestock" is the word "work" (2:2), used of animals as property (Exodus 22:8; 1 Samuel 15:9).
- 33:14, 16, 17 Jacob says "until I come to my lord at Seir", and the text says only that Esau went to Seir and Jacob to Succoth. Nothing is added; "And Jacob" is built like 32:1.
- 33:17 "was named": the Hebrew has "he named" or "one named", as at 19:22.
- 33:18 "the city of Shechem" is as open as the Hebrew: the city called Shechem, or the city of the man Shechem (33:19). "In front of the city" is "at the face of the city". "Camped" is the verb of the noun "camp" (33:8).
- 33:19 "from the hand of": kept, as at 9:5. "The father of Shechem" is the man of chapter 34.
- 33:20 "set up", the verb used of a pillar (35:14), not "built". The name can also be heard as a sentence, "God is the God of Israel". "Israel" here is Jacob himself (32:28).

For Genesis 34: "Shechem" the man is "the son of Hamor" (34:2), and "Hamor, the father of Shechem" as at 33:19; "the city" (34:20, 24, 25) is the city of 33:18; the decision log of 33:15 quotes 34:11 ahead of time ("Let me find favor in your eyes, and whatever you say to me I will give"), and that of 33:18 quotes 34:21 ("These men are at peace with us"), the same word as "safely" (33:18); "favor" (34:11) as at 33:8, 10, 15; "the field" (34:5, 7, 28), "herds" (34:5, 23) and "livestock" for another word (34:23) need keeping apart from 33:14; "the daughters of the land" (34:1) as at 27:46; "chieftain" (34:2) as at 17:20; "disgrace" (34:14) as the Genesis 30 line planned; Jacob is still "Jacob", and "Israel" (34:7) is the name of 32:28.

# Notes on Genesis 39, for the translator of Genesis 40

Text followed: the Masoretic Text, read by its Ketiv. 39:20 has the chapter's one marginal reading: the written word is "the bound ones of the king" and the read word "the prisoners of the king", two forms from one root, and both are "the king's prisoners", so nothing is noted. The Hebrew has one paragraph break, after 39:23. Numbering is the same in Hebrew and English.

Brackets: one, in the decision log, `close`.
- 39:17 "The Hebrew slave whom you brought to us came to me to toy with me" [alt: The Hebrew slave came to me, the one you brought to us to toy with me]. The main text follows the pauses of the Hebrew text.

Places where the old copies differ, not bracketed:
- 39:4 "all that he had": the Hebrew has no "that" here (39:5 and 39:8 have it); the Samaritan copy has it. 39:8 "in the house": the Samaritan copy and the old Greek have "in his house". 39:14 the old Greek has "a Hebrew slave".

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 39:1 "a court official": the bracket of 37:36 (eunuch) is not repeated. He has a wife (39:7). "The Ishmaelites": 37:36 has "the Medanites" sold him to Potiphar; each chapter keeps the name the Hebrew has.
- 39:2 "a successful man": the word is a form of "made succeed" (39:3, 23; 24:21).
- 39:4 "attended him": personal service, not the verb "serve"; 40:4 has it again of Joseph and the two officials.
- 39:6, 8 "with him there he gave no thought to anything": the Hebrew is "he did not know, with him, anything". "There" and "here" are supplied. It can also be heard as "he did not know of anything that was with him", in Joseph's keeping; either way the master kept no track of what he owned.
- 39:6 "the bread that he ate": left as it stands. 43:32 says Egyptians could not eat bread with Hebrews. Joseph says the same thing of his master with "except you" in the place of the bread (39:9), so "the bread" can be heard as a veiled word for the wife (compare Proverbs 30:20); the Hebrew says "bread".
- 39:6 "beautiful in form and beautiful in appearance": word for word what 29:17 says of Rachel, his mother, so the English is the same and not "handsome".
- 39:9 "He is not greater in this house than I am": the Hebrew word is "he is not", with the master as "he". "No one is greater in this house than I" would be worded differently in Hebrew.
- 39:10 "to lie beside her": not the "lie with" of 39:7, 12, 14. "To be with her" is as open as the Hebrew (her company, or more: 2 Samuel 13:20).
- 39:11 "on one such day": the Hebrew is "as this day"; one of the days of 39:10. "His work" is the word of 2:2-3.
- 39:14 "he has brought us": she does not name her husband. "Called the men of her house": 39:11 says none of them was in the house.
- 39:14, 17 "toy with": the verb of "laughing" (21:9; 26:8) and "joking" (19:14), here with a small word after it that it has nowhere else. It covers mocking and the play of 26:8, and "toy with" was chosen to carry both; "joke" did not fit.
- 39:14, 17 "came to me": the two Hebrew words are also the idiom "slept with" (38:16, 18). Here a purpose follows ("to lie with me"), and she says he fled (39:18), so it is "came to".
- 39:12-18: the narrator says the garment was left "in her hand" (39:12, 13); she says "beside me" both times (39:15, 18). She "raised" her voice (not the "lifted" of 27:38; another verb).
- 39:17, 19 "the Hebrew slave", "your slave": Joseph was bought (39:1). 39:17 "in words like these" and 39:19 "the kind of thing" are one Hebrew phrase ("according to these words", or "things"; 24:28 has "about these things").
- 39:19 "his anger burned": the Hebrew does not say against whom.
- 39:20 "the prison": the Hebrew is "the house of" a word found only in Genesis 39 and 40. "Confined": the verb is "bind"; Joseph works freely there (39:22), so "bound" was not written. "Joseph's master" is the plural form used of one master.
- 39:21 "gave him favor": the Hebrew is "gave his favor", favor toward Joseph, as at Exodus 3:21.
- 39:22 "he was the one who did it": as bare as the Hebrew, which does not say he only gave the orders.
- 39:23 "did not look into": the verb is "see". "What he did" has no "everything" and no "in his hand" here (39:3 has both).

For Genesis 40: "the commander of the guard" (40:3, 4) and "court officials" (40:2, 7) as at 39:1, with no bracket; "the prison" and "confined" (40:3, 5) as at 39:20, and 40:3 says the prison is in the house of the commander of the guard, Joseph's master (39:20; 40:7 "his master's house"); "attended them" (40:4) as at 39:4, where "put him in charge" is another form of the verb of 40:4; "After these things" (40:1) as at 39:7; "sinned against" (40:1) as at 39:9; "his master", "their master" for the same word; "pit" (40:15) as at 37:24; "the Hebrews" (40:15) as "Hebrew" at 39:14.

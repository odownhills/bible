# Notes on Genesis 26, for the translator of Genesis 27

Text followed: the Masoretic Text; the chapter has no Qere. The main text keeps to the Masoretic vowels: 26:12 "a hundredfold" (a hundred measures), 26:33 "Shibah", 26:35 "bitterness".

Places where the old copies differ, none bracketed:
- 26:12 the old Greek and the old Syriac read the same letters as "barley": "a hundred of barley".
- 26:18 the Samaritan copy, the old Greek and the Latin have "that the servants of Abraham his father had dug"; the Hebrew text has "that they had dug in the days of", put as "that had been dug".
- 26:32 the old Greek has "We have not found water": the word "to him" sounds the same as "not". The well is then named and the city is called after it, so the Hebrew text stands with nothing noted. Obay may want to see it.
- 26:33 the old Greek gives the name as "Oath".
- 26:34 the Samaritan copy and the old Greek have "Elon the Hivite".
- 26:35 the old Greek has "they were contending with Isaac and Rebekah", from another root.

Brackets: one. 26:8 "laughing with Rebekah his wife" [alt: caressing Rebekah his wife] (ask). Notes on names and sounds at 26:8, 20, 21, 22, 33; the note of 20:1 ("stayed" and "Gerar") is not repeated at 26:3.

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 26:2-3 "the land that I will tell you of" and "this land": the Hebrew does not say whether they are one land; nothing is added.
- 26:3 "establish the oath": the verb of 17:19, 21, kept so that the two are heard together; it means to make it stand.
- 26:4 "be blessed in your offspring": the form of 22:18; the bracket there ("bless themselves by") is not repeated.
- 26:5 "my laws": the word that will be "the law" in later books; "instructions" would also do. The four nouns have one "and", as in the Hebrew.
- 26:7 the Hebrew slides from "he was afraid to say" into Isaac's own words ("kill me"); a dash marks it and no "thinking" is supplied. "For she is beautiful in appearance" can be Isaac's thought or the writer's; it is left inside the quotation.
- 26:8 "the window": the Hebrew has the article; whose window is not said.
- 26:10 "one of the people": any one of them; "the first of the people", the king, was set aside (the same words are "one of" at 1 Samuel 26:15). "Could easily have": the Hebrew is "almost".
- 26:11 "touches": harm to him, and for her the word of 20:6; one English word, as in the Hebrew.
- 26:12 "got": the verb is "found", as at 26:19 and 26:32.
- 26:15 "stopped them up": the Hebrew does not say when; 26:18 says it was after Abraham's death. "Now" and a plain past leave it open.
- 26:16 "much more powerful than we are": also "much too powerful for us"; the same thing said two ways.
- 26:18 "dug again": the first verb is "returned"; with a second verb it is "again", so "Isaac went back and dug" was not bracketed.
- 26:19 "living water": spring water that flows; the words are kept, as at John 4:10.
- 26:20, 21, 22, 33 "he named", "he called": Isaac; the Hebrew names no subject.
- 26:22 "For now": the first word can also only open the speech ("Now Yahweh has made room").
- 26:25 "dug out": another verb than "dug" (26:15-22, 32); 26:32 seems to be the same well.
- 26:26 "Ahuzzath his friend": a name, as "Phicol" beside it is. The reading "a group of his friends" was set aside: no verse uses the word that way.
- 26:27, 29, 31 one verb, "sent away" and "sent on one's way"; "in peace" (26:29, 31) carries the echo.
- 26:28 "has been with you": also "is with you" (21:22). "An oath under a curse": the word is "curse" at 24:41, where the sentence makes it clear; here "a curse between us" would be heard as enmity. "We said": to one another, before coming.
- 26:29 "You are now blessed of Yahweh": a statement, as at 24:31; it can also be heard as a wish.
- 26:33 "Shibah" is the form of "seven" used with masculine nouns; the well is feminine.
- 26:34 "a wife", singular, though two are named, as in the Hebrew.
- 26:35 "a bitterness of spirit": the noun of Proverbs 14:10.

For Genesis 27: Esau's wives are "Judith" and "Basemath", "the Hittite", and 27:46 ("the daughters of Heth") looks back to "a bitterness of spirit" (26:35); the decision log of 25:22 quotes 27:46 ahead of time as "why do I have life?", and that of 25:23 quotes 27:1 ("the older son") and 27:40 ("you will serve your brother"); "game" (27:3) as at 25:28; "the elder" or "the older" and "the younger" of two sons (27:1, 15) as the glossary plans; "a curse" (27:12-13), "people" (27:29) and "bear a grudge against" (27:41) as the glossary plans; "birthright" (27:36) as at 25:31-34, with the name "Jacob" and "heel" (25:26) behind the wordplay there; "bless", "blessed" and "be with you" as in 26:3, 24, 29; "exhausted" (25:29-30) and "despised" (25:34) if they return.

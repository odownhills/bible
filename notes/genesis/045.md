# Notes on Genesis 45, for the translator of Genesis 46

Text followed: the Masoretic Text; the chapter has no Qere and no paragraph break in the Hebrew. Numbering is the same in Hebrew and English.

Brackets: two, each with an entry in the decision log: 45:7 "as a great company of survivors" [alt: by a great deliverance] (call: clear); 45:24 "Do not get angry on the way" [alt: Do not be afraid on the way] (call: ask).

Places where the old copies differ, not bracketed:
- 45:19 the Hebrew is "And as for you, you are commanded: 'Do this'", with "you" singular (Joseph) and "do" plural (the brothers). The old Greek and Latin have a command to Joseph, "And you, command this". Either way Pharaoh orders Joseph to tell his brothers; translated as it stands.
- 45:2 the old Greek has "all the Egyptians heard, and it was heard in Pharaoh's house". 45:7 it has "to nourish a great remnant of you".

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 45:1 "Have every man go out from me": the verb is "bring out" made into an order to others. "Before all who stood by him": his attendants.
- 45:2 "let out his voice in weeping": the Hebrew is "gave his voice in weeping". "Egypt heard": the people, as at 41:55.
- 45:3 "dismayed before him": the word of Job 4:5; it can also be put "terrified".
- 45:5 "let there be no anger in your eyes": anger at themselves or at one another; the Hebrew does not say (31:35 has the same words). "To preserve life": one noun in the Hebrew, from the verb "keep alive" (45:7).
- 45:6, 7 "in the midst of the land" and "on the earth" are one Hebrew word. In 45:6 it is where the famine is (43:1); in 45:7 "set a remnant for you on the earth" is taken as in 2 Samuel 14:7 ("neither name nor remnant on the face of the ground"). "A remnant in the land" is the other way to put it.
- 45:7 "to keep you alive": the Hebrew is "to keep alive for you", with the same "for you" as "set a remnant for you".
- 45:8 "a father to Pharaoh": kept as it stands. "Lord of all his house": "house", not "household", as at 41:40.
- 45:9 "do not delay": the Hebrew is "do not stand".
- 45:9-11 the message to Jacob stands in single marks and ends at 45:11; 45:12-13 are said to the brothers again. Set as at 32:4.
- 45:11 "come to poverty": the verb is "be dispossessed".
- 45:13 "honor": the word is "glory" (Deuteronomy 5:24) and "wealth" (31:1).
- 45:15 "wept on them": as "wept on his neck" (45:14). "Spoke with him": they could not at 45:3, and 37:4 says "they could not speak peaceably to him".
- 45:16 "the news": the word is "voice", the word of 45:2.
- 45:17 "go, enter the land of Canaan": two commands in the Hebrew. "Animals": a rare word, not the one for "livestock" or "donkeys".
- 45:18 "let me give you": it can also be put "I will give you". "Households": the word is "houses".
- 45:20 "do not look with regret on your things": the Hebrew is "let your eye not pity your things".
- 45:22 "changes of clothes" for each man is plural in the Hebrew, with no number.
- 45:23 "as follows": the Hebrew is "like this"; it could also be heard as "the same", what Benjamin got. "Food": a third word beside "grain" and "bread".
- 45:26 "and that he was the one governing": the Hebrew passes from their words to a report of them. "Went numb": the verb of Habakkuk 1:4.
- 45:27, 28 the father is "Jacob" in 45:25 and 45:27 and "Israel" in 45:28, as the Hebrew has it.
- 45:28 "Enough!": one word, "much". "Let me go": or "I will go".

For Genesis 46: "wagons" (46:5; 45:19, 21, 27) and "to carry him" (45:27); "little ones" and "wives" (46:5; 45:19); "the land of Goshen" (46:28, 34; 45:10); "fall on the neck of" and "wept on his neck" (46:29; 45:14); "Let me die now ... you are still alive" (46:30) answers "Joseph my son is still alive ... before I die" (45:28); "Israel" and "Jacob" each as the Hebrew has it (46:1, 2, 5); "the sons of Israel" (46:5, 8; 45:21); "go down to Egypt" and "bring up" (46:3, 4; 45:9, 13); "a great nation" (46:3; 12:2) is not the word of 45:7; "flocks and cattle and all that you have" (46:32; 45:10); "provide for" (47:12; 45:11); "a detestable thing to Egypt" (46:34; 43:32).

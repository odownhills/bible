# Notes on Genesis 27, for the translator of Genesis 28

Text followed: the Masoretic Text, read by its Ketiv. 27:3: the Ketiv and the Qere are two forms of one noun, "game", so nothing is noted. 27:29: the Ketiv and the Qere are two spellings of "bow down". The Hebrew text has no paragraph break inside the chapter.

Places where the old copies differ, none bracketed:
- 27:5 the old Greek has "to hunt game for his father", a word that looks much like "to bring".
- 27:24 the Hebrew has no question word ("You are really my son Esau?"); the Samaritan copy has the question word of 27:21.
- 27:27 the Samaritan copy and the old Greek have "a full field".
- 27:34 the Samaritan copy and the old Greek begin "And it came about, when Esau heard".
- 27:38 the old Greek adds a clause before "And Esau lifted his voice and wept": Isaac was struck silent. Obay may want to see it.
- 27:40 the old Greek has "when you pull down" (named in the decision log).

Brackets: two. 27:39 "away from the richness of the earth ... and away from the dew of the sky above" [alt: of ... of] (ask). 27:40 "when you grow restless" [alt: when you roam free] (close). One note on sounds, at 27:36; the note of 25:25 ("hair" and "Seir") is not repeated at 27:11.

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 27:4, 19, 25, 31 "my soul", "your soul": the Hebrew has it four times with "bless" and plain "I", "he" at 27:7, 10; it is kept, not made "I myself".
- 27:7 "before Yahweh before my death": two "before"s, as in the Hebrew.
- 27:12 "a mocker": the word is of those who scoff at the prophets (2 Chronicles 36:16); here the mocking is a trick played on a blind man, and "a deceiver" would say only half of it.
- 27:13 "On me be your curse": the curse that would fall on him; the Hebrew has no verb.
- 27:15 "the best garments": the word is "the desirable ones" (the root of 2:9; 3:6).
- 27:20 "made it happen before me": the wording of 24:12.
- 27:23 and 27:27 "blessed him" twice: the Hebrew has both, and nothing is smoothed.
- 27:28 "of the dew": "some of", the small word that 27:39 turns the other way. "God" has the article here.
- 27:29 "Be master": a command. "Cursed are", "blessed are": statements, as at 9:25-26; the Hebrew sets a plural beside a singular ("each of those who curse you").
- 27:33 "Yes, and blessed he will be": the first word is "also"; "he will indeed be blessed" is the same thing.
- 27:36 "Is it because he was named Jacob that": the shape of 29:15 ("Is it because you are my brother that"); "Was he not rightly named Jacob?" says the same. "Cheated": the verb made from "heel" (25:26), used of a brother at Jeremiah 9:4.
- 27:37 "servants", not "slaves" (9:25-27), beside "serve" (27:29, 40).
- 27:39 "the place where you live", as at 10:30. The decision log also names the reading with no small word, "the rich places of the earth will be the place where you live".
- 27:40 "by your sword": the Hebrew is "on your sword", as one lives on bread (Deuteronomy 8:3).
- 27:41 "the days of mourning for my father": when Isaac is dead. "Are coming near" can also be a wish, "let them come near".
- 27:42 "comforting himself about you by planning to kill you": "planning" is supplied; the Hebrew is "comforting himself about you, to kill you".
- 27:45 "of you both": Jacob and Esau. The Hebrew does not say how she would lose Esau too (9:6 may be behind it), and nothing is added.
- 27:46 "sick of my life": another verb than "loathe" (Job 10:1).
- The decision log of 10:21 quoted 27:1 ahead of time as "Esau his elder son"; the chapter has "his older son", with 25:23.

For Genesis 28: 28:1-9 goes straight on from 27:46: "the daughters of Heth" and "the daughters of the land" become "the daughters of Canaan"; "Laban", "Haran" (27:43), "Paddan-Aram", "Bethuel the Aramean" (25:20); "bless", "blessing" as here, and 28:4 "the blessing of Abraham" with "the land where you are staying" (17:8); "God Almighty" (28:3) and "be fruitful", "multiply" as the glossary plans; "his older son", "her younger son" of Esau and Jacob; "listened to" his father and mother (28:7) beside "listen to my voice" (27:8, 13, 43); "Judith" and "Basemath" (26:34) beside the new wife of 28:9, with "Nebaioth" (25:13); 28:14 "be blessed in", "the dust of the earth", "westward, eastward, northward, southward" as the glossary plans; "a few days" (27:44) returns at 29:20; "cheated" (27:36) returns at Jeremiah 9:4 and Hosea 12:3.

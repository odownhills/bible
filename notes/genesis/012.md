# Notes on Genesis 12, for the translator of Genesis 13

Text followed: the Masoretic Text; the chapter has no Qere. 12:8 "his tent" is written with the older spelling of "his" (as at 9:21); 13:3 has the same word with a Qere, and the meaning is the same.

Places where the old copies differ, none bracketed:
- 12:3 the Hebrew text has "the one who calls down a curse on you", one person, beside "those who bless you"; the Samaritan copy and the old Greek have "those who" both times. The singular is kept.
- 12:7 the old Greek adds "to him" after "said"; 12:20 it adds "and Lot with him" (the words of 13:1).

Brackets: 12:1 "your relatives" [alt: your birthplace] (clear); 12:2 "and be a blessing" [alt: so that you will be a blessing] (close); 12:3 "will be blessed in you" [alt: will bless themselves by you] (clear). No "ask" in this chapter.

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 12:1 "said", not "had said": the Hebrew verb is the plain form of the story, and the English is as open as the Hebrew about when Yahweh spoke (Acts 7:2-3 puts it in Mesopotamia, before Haran; 15:7 has Yahweh bring Abram out of Ur; 12:4 ties the going to Haran).
- 12:1 "Go": the Hebrew adds "for yourself" to the command (also 22:2), a turn of speech that English does not have (Deuteronomy 5:30).
- 12:2 the three "I will" verbs could be "so that I may"; one meaning.
- 12:3 two verbs for cursing: "calls down a curse on" is the verb of 8:21 ("bring a curse on"), and "curse" is the verb of 3:14, 17 and 9:25. The first is from the word "be slight", but "treat with contempt" was not written: it is what Balaam was hired to do (Deuteronomy 23:4), and 27:29 gives this promise with "curse" both times.
- 12:3 "in you" and "through you": one Hebrew word; "in" is kept (Galatians 3:8).
- 12:3 "families of the ground": the word is "ground", not "earth", and it is heard with "Cursed is the ground" (3:17) and 8:21.
- 12:5 "the people they had gained": the Hebrew is "the life they had made", the slaves and household gathered in Haran (the verb is "gain" of wealth at 31:1).
- 12:6 "the oak of Moreh": Scripture does not say what tree; "Moreh" is kept as a name.
- 12:6 "were then in the land": as open as the Hebrew (already there, or still there).
- 12:8 "called on the name of Yahweh", as at 4:26; "proclaimed the name" is not noted.
- 12:9 "toward the Negev": the word is also "the south"; the region's name is used (13:1, 3), and 13:14 "southward" is the direction.
- 12:10 "severe" is the word "heavy"; 13:2 has it of Abram's wealth, and the echo is not shown.
- 12:13 "say that you are my sister": the Hebrew quotes the words, "Say, 'You are my sister.'"
- 12:13 "I may live": the Hebrew is "my life will live".
- 12:15 "officials": the word is "princes" at Job 3:15.
- 12:16 "he came to have": the Hebrew does not name who, and the English does not either. "Slaves": they are given as property, in a list with animals; 16:1 has the second word of Hagar.
- 12:17 "struck ... with great plagues": the verb and the noun are one root (the verb is "touch" at 3:3; the noun returns at Exodus 11:1). "Sent out" (12:20) and "take ... and go" (12:19) also return in Exodus (11:1; 12:32), and "sent out" is the verb of 3:23.
- 12:19 "so that I took her as my wife": the Hebrew states it as done, and it is not softened to "might have taken".
- 12:17, 20 "his household", "all that he had": 12:15 "Pharaoh's house" is the same word as "household".

For Genesis 13: keep "the Negev" (13:1, 3), "his wife and all that he had" (13:1, the words of 12:20), "pitched his tent" and "Bethel ... Ai" (13:3), "called on the name of Yahweh" (13:4), "flocks and cattle" (13:5), "possessions" (13:6), "the Canaanites ... were then living in the land" (13:7, the words of 12:6 with "living"), "please" (13:8); 13:2 "very rich" is the word "heavy" of 12:10; "And Lot traveled eastward" (13:11) and "northward and southward and eastward and westward" (13:14) are already quoted in the Genesis 11 decision log; "To your offspring I will give this land" (12:7) returns at 13:15; "the oaks of Mamre" and "built an altar there to Yahweh" (13:18).

# Notes on Genesis 29, for the translator of Genesis 30

Text followed: the Masoretic Text; the chapter has no Qere and no paragraph break. The vowels are followed at 29:6 ("is coming") and 29:9 ("came"): the same letters, two forms.

Places where the old copies differ, none bracketed:
- 29:3, 8 the Samaritan copy has "the shepherds" for "the flocks" (the old Greek too at 29:8). The Hebrew's "they" who roll the stone are the shepherds either way.
- 29:27 the Samaritan copy and the old Greek have "I will give" for "we will give".
- 29:34 the Samaritan copy has "she named him" for the Hebrew "he named him", which is put as "he was named" (25:26), as the old Greek has it.

Brackets: one. 29:17 "tender" [alt: weak] (ask). Four notes, on the names Reuben, Simeon, Levi and Judah (29:32-35).

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 29:1 "lifted his feet": the Hebrew phrase stands only here and is kept as a picture; it means that he set out. "The people of the east" follows Job 1:3; the notes on Genesis 28 had planned "the sons of the east".
- 29:2-3, 8 "flocks" and "sheep" are two Hebrew words; "sheep" is the word that is "flock" elsewhere (it covers goats too, and 30:32 will need "flock"). "They" who roll the stone and water are not named in the Hebrew.
- 29:5 "Laban the son of Nahor": the Hebrew says "son"; he is Bethuel's son and Nahor's grandson (24:15, 29; 28:5). 29:12, 15 "brother" is likewise the Hebrew word, of a kinsman (13:8).
- 29:7 "the day is still long": the Hebrew is "the day is still great".
- 29:13 "all these things": the Hebrew does not say which.
- 29:14 "Surely": the word can also be "only"; "a full month" is "a month of days".
- 29:21 "let me sleep with her" can also be "so that I may sleep with her".
- 29:23, 28 "he" changes from Laban to Jacob and back without a name, as in the Hebrew.
- 29:25, 27, 30 "serve with": the Hebrew says "with", as in "stay with me" (29:19).
- 29:27-28 "this one's week": the Hebrew cannot be "this week"; it is the week of Leah's wedding feast (Judges 14:12). "That one too" is the same Hebrew word as "this one".
- 29:30 "he loved Rachel too, more than Leah": the Hebrew has "too" twice, and the second can go with "loved" (he slept with her and also loved her) or with "Rachel".
- 29:31, 33 "hated": the Hebrew word, not softened; 29:30 shows it is said beside "loved more" (Deuteronomy 21:15).
- 29:32 "has seen": the phrase is "look on" at 21:16; "seen" keeps it with "saw" (29:31) and the name. The last clause begins with "for" (or "surely").
- 29:35 "praise": the word is also "thank"; 49:8 plays on it again.

For Genesis 30: "female slave" for Bilhah and Zilpah (30:3-18; 30:3 has another word, "female servant", 20:17); "sleep with" (30:3, 4, 16); "be built up from" (30:3), as at 16:2; "conceived and gave birth to a son", "named him", "this time" (30:20) and "stopped bearing children" (30:9) as here; "hated", "affliction", "opened her womb" (30:22) and "barren" as here; "wages" (29:15) is one word and the "wages" or "reward" of 30:18, 28-33 another (15:1 has "reward"); "serve", "service" (30:26, 29); "flock" in 30:40 is the "flocks" of 29:2, and the "sheep" of this chapter is the usual word for "flock"; "trough" (24:20) and "in front of" (25:21) return at 30:38; "the younger", "the firstborn" as here. Names: Rachel, Leah, Zilpah, Bilhah, Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah.

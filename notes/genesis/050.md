# Notes on Genesis 50, for the translator of Exodus 1

Text followed: the Masoretic Text; the chapter has no Ketiv and Qere, and no Hebrew paragraph break inside it. 50:26 "he was put": the Hebrew verb is read as a passive with no one named.

Brackets: one, with an entry in the decision log: 50:5 "that I dug out for myself" [alt: that I bought for myself] (clear). One note on sound: 50:11 (the name "Abel-Mizraim" and "mourning of Egypt").

Places where the old copies differ, not bracketed:
- 50:16 the old Greek and Syriac have "they came to Joseph" for "they sent word to Joseph"; 50:18 ("his brothers also went") fits the Hebrew text, where the first approach is by message.
- 50:19 the old Greek has "for I belong to God" for the question "am I in the place of God?"
- 50:20 the Samaritan copy and old versions have "but God".
- 50:23 the Samaritan copy has "in the days of Joseph" for "on Joseph's knees".
- 50:25 the Samaritan copy and the old Greek add "with you" at the end, as Exodus 13:19 has it when it quotes the verse.

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 50:2 "physicians": the word is "healers" (Job 13:4); here it is a class of Joseph's servants.
- 50:3 the Hebrew does not say whether the seventy days of weeping take in the forty days of embalming; the English is as open.
- 50:4 "Pharaoh's house": the people of it, as at 45:2. Joseph speaks through them and not to Pharaoh; the text does not say why.
- 50:5 "there you are to bury me": 47:29-31 tells the oath in other words; nothing is harmonized.
- 50:9 "very large", 50:10 "very heavy", 50:11 "heavy": one Hebrew word three times; English does not call a camp heavy.
- 50:10-11 "the threshing floor of Atad": taken as a name; the Hebrew has the article, and the word is "bramble" at Judges 9:14-15. "Across the Jordan": the Hebrew does not say from which side, and neither does the English.
- 50:11 "a heavy mourning for Egypt": the Hebrew is as open (Egypt's own mourning, or mourning that weighs on Egypt). "Abel" in the place name is spelled in Hebrew with other letters than the name of Cain's brother.
- 50:15 "What if": the Hebrew word is usually "if only" (17:18); here the sentence is a fear and breaks off with no ending. "Really": the doubled verb, as in a question (37:8).
- 50:16 "sent word": the Hebrew verb is "commanded", the same as "Your father commanded" in the same verse: they gave someone a charge for Joseph.
- 50:17 where Jacob's words stop: taken to end at "for they dealt you evil", so that "And now" is the brothers' own plea ("the servants of the God of your father"). The Hebrew could also run Jacob's words to the end. "Forgive the offense" twice: the Hebrew builds the verb two ways, with one meaning. "For they dealt you evil": or "that they dealt you evil".
- 50:17 "when they spoke to him": the "they" is not named (the ones who brought the message).
- 50:18 "Look, we are your slaves": the words of 44:16, and the bowing of 37:7-10.
- 50:20 "planned it for good": the same verb with this small word is "count as" at 15:6; here the next words give the purpose. "As it is this day": the Hebrew is "as this day". "Many people": the Hebrew is "a numerous people", which can be one people or a great many persons (Numbers 21:6).
- 50:21 "spoke to their hearts": "heart" is singular in the Hebrew, as at 34:3.
- 50:22 "lived" twice: two Hebrew verbs (dwelt, was alive), as at 47:27-28.
- 50:23 "sons of the third generation": the Hebrew does not say from whom the three are counted (Ephraim's grandsons or his great-grandsons). "Born on Joseph's knees": the picture of 30:3, kept.
- 50:24 "and God": the Hebrew has "and", as at 48:21. "The land that he swore to Abraham": no "to give" in the Hebrew.
- 50:26 "a coffin": the Hebrew word is a chest, the word used later of the ark of the covenant (Exodus 25:10); it is not the word for Noah's ark.

For Exodus 1: "And these are the names of the sons of Israel who came to Egypt" is the wording of 46:8, and "who came out of Jacob's loins" and "seventy people" that of 46:26-27; "And Joseph died" (50:26); "were fruitful", "swarmed", "multiplied" (1:28; 9:7; 47:27), "very greatly" (17:2) and "powerful" (18:18); "Come," (11:3-4); "afflict" and "serve" (15:13-14, which Exodus 1 begins to fulfill); "forced labor" (49:15); "bricks" and "mortar" (11:3); "Rameses" (47:11; Exodus 1:11 spells the name with other vowels); "the Nile" (41:1); "midwife" (35:17); "the Hebrews" (43:32); "little ones" (50:8, 21); "keep alive" (50:20; 6:19); "attend to" and "bring up" (50:24-25) return at Exodus 3:16-17, 4:31 and 13:19, which quotes 50:25.

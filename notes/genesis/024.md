# Notes on Genesis 24, for the translator of Genesis 25

Text followed: the Masoretic Text, read by its Ketiv. 24:33: the Ketiv and the Qere are two passive forms of one verb, "was set"; "food" is supplied, and nothing is noted. The word for "young woman" (24:14, 16, 28, 55, 57) is written in the short spelling this book always has; it is the same word.

Places where the old copies differ, none bracketed:
- 24:7 the old Greek adds "and the God of the earth", from 24:3.
- 24:22 the Samaritan copy adds "and put it on her nose" after the ring; the Hebrew text says so only at 24:47.
- 24:27 "my master's brothers" follows the Masoretic vowels; the same letters are "brother" in the old Greek, as at 24:48.
- 24:33 "And he said, 'Speak'": the Samaritan copy and the old Greek have "they said".
- 24:36 "after she had grown old": the Samaritan copy and the old Greek have "after he had grown old".
- 24:55 the Samaritan copy has "days or a month"; the old Greek "about ten days".
- 24:62 the Samaritan copy and the old Greek have "through the wilderness" where the Hebrew has "from going to".

Brackets: three, all clear. 24:21 "was gazing at her" [alt: was astonished at her]; 24:55 "a few days, or ten" [alt: a year, or ten months]; 24:63 "to meditate" [alt: to walk about].

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 24:2 "the elder of his household": also "the oldest of his house". He is never named here (15:2 names an Eliezer).
- 24:8, 41 "oath" and "curse" are two Hebrew words. The second is the curse an oath lays on the one who swears; "be clear of" is the same root both times in 24:41 and another verb at 24:8. Obay may prefer "oath" at 24:41.
- 24:10 "with all the good things of his master in his hand": he took them with him, or all his master's goods were in his charge (24:2); the English is as open as the Hebrew.
- 24:10 "the city of Nahor": a city of that name, or the city where Nahor lived.
- 24:12 "make it happen before me": the Hebrew has no object; "it" is supplied.
- 24:14, 44 "decided on": the verb is "rebuke" at 21:25 and "be cleared" at 20:16. 24:14 "by her": also "by this".
- 24:14 and 24:43 have two Hebrew words, "young woman" and "girl"; the second returns at Exodus 2:8 and Isaiah 7:14.
- 24:16 "very beautiful in appearance": the Hebrew is "very good in appearance" (6:2).
- 24:22 "ten shekels of gold": the Hebrew is "ten of gold". A beka is half a shekel (Exodus 38:26). "Arms" is the word "hands" (24:22, 30, 47).
- 24:32 "he unloaded ... and gave": the Hebrew does not say who; "his feet" shows that Laban, or his household, gave the water.
- 24:38 "Rather": the Hebrew is the form of an oath, "if you do not go".
- 24:41 "give one to you": the Hebrew has no object.
- 24:46 "from her shoulder": the Hebrew is "from on her"; "shoulder" is supplied from 24:45.
- 24:48 "the daughter of my master's brother": Rebekah is the daughter of Nahor's son (24:24); kept as the Hebrew has it.
- 24:55 "she may go": the same form is "you may go".
- 24:57 "ask her herself": the Hebrew is "ask her mouth".
- 24:60 "those who hate them": the Hebrew is "him", of the offspring, as at 22:17 (bracketed there, not repeated).
- 24:62 "had come from going to Beer-Lahai-Roi": also "had come from the approach to".
- 24:64 "dropped down": the verb is "fell"; it is said of getting down quickly at 2 Kings 5:21.
- 24:67 "into the tent of Sarah his mother": the Hebrew is "into the tent, Sarah his mother". "After his mother": after her death; nothing is supplied.

For Genesis 25: "gave all that he had to Isaac" (25:5) as at 24:36; "send away" or "send out" for the sons of the concubines (25:6), not "send on one's way"; "the life of" (25:7) and "a good old age" (25:8) as at 23:1 and 15:15; the cave of Machpelah, "facing Mamre" and "from the sons of Heth" (25:9-10) as in chapter 23; Beer-Lahai-Roi (25:11) as at 24:62; "in the presence of all his brothers" and "as you go toward" (25:18) as at 16:12 and 10:19; Rebekah, Bethuel and Laban (25:20) as here, and Paddan-Aram is another name than Aram-Naharaim (24:10); "barren" (25:21) as at 11:30; "separate" (25:23) as at 13:9; "loved" (25:28) as at 24:67.

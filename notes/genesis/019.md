# Notes on Genesis 19, for the translator of Genesis 20

Text followed: the Masoretic Text; the chapter has no Ketiv and Qere. The dot that the Hebrew text sets over "when she rose" (19:33) changes nothing. The main text keeps to the Masoretic vowels: 19:2 the word is written as "my lords"; 19:18 it is written as at 18:3, "Lord" (bracketed, see below).

Places where the old copies differ, none bracketed:
- 19:17 the old Greek has "they said" for "he said"; the Hebrew turns to one speaker here and keeps him to 19:22.
- 19:12 the old Greek has "sons-in-law" for "a son-in-law". 19:37-38 the old Greek adds the reasons for the names ("saying, From my father"; "saying, Son of my kin"); the notes in the text say as much.

Brackets: 19:14 "who were to marry his daughters" [alt: who had married his daughters] (close); 19:18 "Lord" [alt: my lords] (ask); 19:31 "in the land" [alt: on the earth] (close). Three notes on names: 19:22 Zoar, 19:37 Moab, 19:38 Ben-Ammi and Ammon.

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 19:5, 8 "be intimate with": the Hebrew is "know", the verb of 4:1, and Obay's wording for it is kept in the mob's mouth and in Lot's, so that his answer is heard taking up their word. "Get to know them" was weighed and is not a bracket: Lot calls it evil and offers his daughters in their place (19:7-8); Judges 19:22-25 has the same demand and what followed it; Jude 7. Plainer still would be "have sex with"; Obay may want it. The same verb is "he did not know" at 19:33, 35.
- 19:4 "to the last one": the Hebrew is "from the end"; it can also be heard as "from every part of the city". Either way it is all the people.
- 19:9 "This one came to stay as a foreigner": one verb in Hebrew (12:10 "to stay"). "He is actually judging": the verb is doubled; "he keeps on judging" says nearly the same.
- 19:9 "do worse evil to you": the mob takes up Lot's "do not do evil" (19:7); 13:13 called them "evil".
- 19:12 "A son-in-law", one and with no "your", as the Hebrew has it. "All that you have": as open as the Hebrew (people or goods).
- 19:13 "their outcry": the outcry of the place's people, as open as at 18:20-21.
- 19:14 "like one who was joking": the verb is "laugh" (17:17; 18:12) in another form; not noted.
- 19:15 "in the punishment of the city": the word is also "guilt" (15:16); he would be swept away either way.
- 19:16 "because Yahweh was sparing him": a noun in Hebrew, "in Yahweh's sparing of him"; "pity" or "compassion" would also do.
- 19:17 "he said", 19:21 "he said to him": one of the two speaks, and speaks as the one who overturns ("I will not overturn", "I cannot do anything"); 19:13 has "we". Kept as the Hebrew has it.
- 19:19 "faithful love": the glossary's wording for God's; if Obay switches 19:18 to "my lords", "kindness" or "loyalty" would fit better here.
- 19:20 "a small thing" (twice): the city is small, and so it is a small thing to ask; the English leaves both open.
- 19:21 "I grant you this thing also": the Hebrew is "I have lifted your face".
- 19:23-24 three clauses that each begin with their subject (the sun, Lot, Yahweh): all at one time.
- 19:24 "from Yahweh, from the sky": Yahweh is named twice in the sentence, as the Hebrew has it.
- 19:26 "looked back from behind him": "back" is supplied from the command of 19:17; the Hebrew is "looked, from behind him" (she was behind Lot).
- 19:27 "and went" is supplied: the Hebrew is "rose early in the morning to the place".
- 19:28 "smoke": not the word of 15:17; this one is used of thick smoke and vapor (Psalm 119:83; 148:8).
- 19:29 "God" here, "Yahweh" in the rest of the chapter; kept as written.
- 19:30 "in a cave": the Hebrew has "the cave".
- 19:31 "sleep with us": the Hebrew is "come upon us" (Deuteronomy 25:5), put by its meaning as at 6:4 and 16:2; "lie with" is another verb.
- 19:37-38 "to this day": the Moab and Ammon of the writer's day. "The sons of Ammon" keeps the Hebrew (10:21 "the sons of Eber").

For Genesis 20: Abraham sets out "from there", the oaks of Mamre (18:1; 19:27); "stayed as a foreigner" is the verb of 19:9 and 12:10; 20:4 has the word of 18:3 and 19:18 again, said to God in a dream, with "righteous" and "kill" as in Abraham's plea (18:23-25); 20:2, 5, 12-13 "she is my sister" takes up 12:13, 19; 20:7 "all that you have" (19:12); 20:8 "rose early in the morning" (19:27); 20:9, 13 "done to me", "do for me" is the verb of 19:19 ("shown me"), and 20:13 has the word behind "faithful love" between husband and wife, where the glossary has "loyalty"; 20:11 "this place" (19:13) and "over the matter of" (12:17); 20:15 "where it is good in your eyes" (19:8; 16:6); 20:18 "held back" (16:2); 20:14 "flocks and cattle, and male slaves and female slaves" (12:16).

# Notes on Genesis 36, for the translator of Genesis 37

Text followed: the Masoretic Text, read by its written text. Two places have a marginal reading, both the same: the name written "Jeish" (36:5, 14) is marked to be read "Jeush", as 36:18 writes it. The chapter follows the written forms, with one bracketed note at 36:5. The Hebrew has paragraph breaks after 36:19, 36:30 and 36:43. Numbering is the same in Hebrew and English.

Brackets: four, each in the decision log.
- 36:2 "the daughter of Zibeon the Hivite" [alt: the son of Zibeon the Hivite, as the Samaritan copy and the old Greek have it] (clear). Not repeated at 36:14, where the same copies differ in the same way.
- 36:24 "the hot springs" [alt: the mules, or the Emim, as the Samaritan copy has it] (ask). The word stands only here.
- 36:26 "the sons of Dishan" [alt: Dishon, as 1 Chronicles 1:41 and other old copies read] (clear).
- 36:40 "chief Timna" [alt: the chief of Timna, and so with each name in this list] (clear).

Places where the old copies differ, not bracketed:
- 36:6 "to a land": the Samaritan copy and the old Greek have "from the land of Canaan", and the old Syriac "to the land of Seir".
- 36:16 "chief Korah" is not in the Samaritan copy; Korah is a son of Oholibamah (36:5, 14, 18) and is not among the sons of Eliphaz at 36:11-12.
- 36:39 "the daughter of Me-Zahab": the old Greek has "the son of". "Hadar": 1 Chronicles 1:50 and some old copies have "Hadad".
- Names that 1 Chronicles 1:35-54 or another copy gives in another form, where nothing else changes, are spelled as Genesis has them: Zepho, Hemam, Alvan, Shepho, Hemdan, Akan, Pau, Alvah.

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 36:2-3 Esau's wives are named differently at 26:34 and 28:9 (there Judith, Basemath the daughter of Elon, Mahalath the daughter of Ishmael; here Adah the daughter of Elon, Oholibamah, Basemath the daughter of Ishmael). The text says what it says; nothing was changed in either place.
- 36:2 "the Hivite": Zibeon is among the sons of Seir "the Horite" at 36:20; no old copy has "Horite" here. "The daughter of Anah, the daughter of Zibeon" is as open as the Hebrew (the second phrase can go with Oholibamah or with Anah).
- 36:6 "away from Jacob" and 36:7 "because of their herds" are one Hebrew phrase ("from the face of").
- 36:6-7 worded beside 12:5, 13:6 and 31:18.
- 36:1, 8, 19, 43: the same two Hebrew words ("he is Edom") are "(that is, Edom)", "Esau is Edom" and "That is Edom"; "That is" leaves open, as the Hebrew does, whether the "he" is Esau or the whole list.
- 36:12, 16 "the sons of Adah" (and 36:13, 17 "of Basemath") are her grandsons; the Hebrew says "sons".
- The word for the heads of Edom is "chief", lowercase before a name; "chieftain" (17:20; 34:2) is another word. Read as "clans" it would need other vowels, which no old copy has.
- 36:20 "those who lived in the land" as at 34:30. "The Horites" is singular with the article, as at 14:6; "Hori" (36:22) is the same word as a man's name.
- 36:24 "Aiah and Anah": the Hebrew has "and" before both names. 36:25 "sons" stands though one son is named.
- 36:25 the Anah of this verse stands in the place of Seir's son (36:20), not Zibeon's (36:24); the Hebrew does not say which is Oholibamah's father.
- 36:30 "chief by chief" is "according to their chiefs".
- 36:31 "before any king of the sons of Israel reigned": the Hebrew can be heard of a king over Israel or of a king of Israel reigning over Edom; the English leaves both open.
- 36:35 "Hadad": at 25:15 the English name is the same but the Hebrew name begins with another letter. "The country of Moab" as at 32:3.
- 36:37 "Shaul" is the Hebrew name of Saul, king of Israel, in its usual English spelling for this man; "the River" as at 31:21.
- 36:39 "Me-Zahab" means "waters of gold"; kept as a name.

For Genesis 37: 37:1 "the land where his father had stayed" must match "the land where they were staying" (36:7; 17:8; 28:4), with "the land of Canaan"; 37:2 "These are the generations of Jacob" as at 36:1, 9 and 25:19; "the sons of Bilhah and the sons of Zilpah, his father's wives" beside "concubine" (35:22) and "female slave" (35:25-26); "old age" (37:3) as at 21:2, 7; "tunic" for the garment of 37:3 (3:21); "envy" (37:11) as at 26:14 and 30:1; "wander" (37:15) as at 21:14; "throw" (37:22) as at 21:15; Midian (37:28, 36) and the Ishmaelites as at 25:2, 12; "clothes" (37:34) as at 35:2; "be comforted" (37:35) as at 24:67.

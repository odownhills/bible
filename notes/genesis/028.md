# Notes on Genesis 28, for the translator of Genesis 29

Text followed: the Masoretic Text; the chapter has no Qere. The Hebrew text has one paragraph break, after 28:9.

Places where the old copies differ, none bracketed:
- 28:4 the old Greek has "the blessing of Abraham my father".
- 28:13 the old Greek adds "do not be afraid" after "the God of Isaac". (Its "on it" in the same verse is named in the decision log.)
- 28:14 the old Greek has "like the sand of the earth".
- 28:19 the old Greek runs "but" and "Luz" together as one name.

Brackets: three. 28:12 "a stairway" [alt: a ladder] (close). 28:13 "standing beside him" [alt: above it] (ask). 28:22 "then Yahweh will be God to me, and this stone ... will be the house of God" [alt: and Yahweh will be God to me, then this stone ...] (ask): the bracket stands in 28:22, after the two clauses it covers, though the question is whether "then" belongs at 28:21 or at 28:22. Two notes: 28:18 ("pillar" is from the root of "set up" and "standing"), 28:19 (the name "Bethel").

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 28:3 "and you will become an assembly of peoples": the Hebrew changes its form after the three wishes; "and may you become" and "so that you become" are the same thing.
- 28:5 "Laban the son of Bethuel the Aramean": as open as the Hebrew about which of the two is called the Aramean (25:20 says it of both).
- 28:6-7 one sentence: all of it is what Esau saw. 28:8-9 one sentence.
- 28:9 "went to Ishmael": the Hebrew names the man; he may already have died by then (25:17), and then it is his household. Nothing is added. 36:3 gives Ishmael's daughter, the sister of Nebaioth, another name, Basemath (and 26:34 gives that name to Elon's daughter): chapter 36 will have to weigh it. The names stand here as the Hebrew has them.
- 28:11 "the place": the Hebrew has the article, three times in the verse, and it is kept. "One of the stones": the Hebrew is "took from the stones of the place" and does not say how many; 28:18 says "the stone".
- 28:11, 18 "at his head", not "under his head" (1 Samuel 26:7).
- 28:12 "the heavens" (and 28:17), where 11:4 has "a tower with its top in the sky" for the same word; the echo of 11:4 is real and is not shown.
- 28:14 "you will spread out": "you", singular, as the Hebrew has it. "Be blessed in": the alt of 12:3 is not repeated.
- 28:15 "this ground": the word of 12:3 ("the families of the ground"), not "land" (28:13). "What I have spoken to you" can also be "about you".
- 28:16 "and I did not know it": "it" is supplied; the Hebrew stresses "I".
- 28:17 "fearsome": the word is made from "was afraid" in the same verse; "awesome" would say the same.
- 28:18 "set it up as a pillar": the verb is the plain "put", "set" (28:11); 35:14, 20 have the verb of 28:12.
- 28:20 "made a vow": the Hebrew is "vowed a vow". "God" in 28:20 and "Yahweh" in 28:21, as the Hebrew has them.
- 28:22 "the house of God": the same two words as 28:17.

For Genesis 29: Jacob goes on to "the land of the sons of the east" (29:1; 25:6 has "the land of the east"); "Haran", "Laban", "Bethuel", "Paddan-Aram" as here; "Laban, your mother's brother" (28:2) returns at 29:10; "a few days" (27:44) returns at 29:20, and "Is it because you are my brother that" (29:15) has the shape of 27:36; "well" for the well of 29:2-10 (never "spring"), "flock", "shepherd", "water" (the verb) as listed; "kiss" (27:26-27), "lifted his voice and wept" (27:38); "stone" as here (28:11, 18); "the older" and "the younger" of Leah and Rachel beside 27:1, 15 and 19:31; "sleep with" (6:4); "be completed" of days (25:24). Later chapters come back to this one: 31:13 ("the God of Bethel, where you anointed a pillar, where you made a vow to me"), 32:12, 35:1-15 (35:13 is quoted in the decision log as "God went up from him at the place where he had spoken with him"), 48:3-4 ("an assembly of peoples").

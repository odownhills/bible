# Notes on Genesis 41, for the translator of Genesis 42

Text followed: the Masoretic Text; the chapter has no marginal reading and no paragraph break inside it. Numbering is the same in Hebrew and English.

Brackets: four alternates, all in the decision log, and two notes.
- 41:34 "take a fifth from the land of Egypt" [alt: make the land of Egypt ready], `close`. 47:24, 26 are quoted there ahead of time ("you will give a fifth to Pharaoh"; "that the fifth is Pharaoh's").
- 41:38 "the spirit of God" [alt: a spirit of gods], `clear`.
- 41:40 "will submit to your word" [alt: will arm themselves at your word], `close`. The Hebrew is "on your mouth all my people will kiss"; 45:21 is quoted as "at the command of Pharaoh".
- 41:43 "Kneel!" [alt: an Egyptian word whose meaning is not given], `close`. 42:23 is quoted there as "the interpreter was between them".
- 41:51, 52 notes on the names "Manasseh" and "Ephraim".

Places where the old copies differ, not bracketed (the sense is the same):
- 41:8 "his dream ... interpret them", as the Hebrew has it; the Samaritan copy has "his dreams".
- 41:16 the Samaritan copy and the old Greek have "Without God, no answer for Pharaoh's welfare will be given".
- 41:48 "the seven years that were in the land of Egypt"; the Samaritan copy and the old Greek have "the seven years in which there was plenty".
- 41:56 "all that had grain in them": the Hebrew is "all in which there was", with nothing named, and "grain" is supplied; the Samaritan copy and the old Greek have "all the storehouses of grain".

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 41:1 "two full years": the Hebrew is "two years of days" (29:14). "Standing by the Nile" (41:1) and "on the bank of the Nile" (41:17) differ in the Hebrew too.
- The two tellings of the dreams (41:1-7 and 41:17-24) differ in the Hebrew, and each word keeps its own English: "fat", "thin", "lean", "poor", "full", "empty", "shriveled", "beautiful in appearance", "beautiful in form". "Bad" is kept for the cows all through, beside "good" (41:26, 35), though "ugly" is what English would say.
- 41:8 "was disturbed": the verb of Psalm 77:4 and Daniel 2:1, 3, not the "troubled" of 40:6.
- 41:9 "My sins I am mentioning": the verb can also be heard as "I am reminded of"; "mention" keeps 40:14.
- 41:13 "me he restored ..., and him he hanged": the Hebrew names no subject, and "he" is as open as it is there (Pharaoh, or Joseph by what he interpreted).
- 41:15 "you can hear a dream and interpret it": the Hebrew is "you hear a dream to interpret it"; the verb is also "understand" (11:7; 42:23).
- 41:16 "Not I": one word, "apart from me" (14:24); 41:44 "without you" is the same word. "An answer for Pharaoh's welfare": the Hebrew is "God will answer the welfare of Pharaoh" (compare Deuteronomy 20:11); "welfare" is the word for "peace" (37:14).
- 41:21 "went inside them": the Hebrew is "came into their inner parts".
- 41:25, 28 "is going to do": a participle; it can also be "is doing".
- 41:33 "look for": the Hebrew is "see" (22:8).
- 41:35 "under Pharaoh's hand": the picture is kept. "As food in the cities": "as" is supplied.
- 41:38 "Can we find": the same letters can be "Can there be found"; the sense is the same.
- 41:43 "his second chariot": the words of 2 Chronicles 35:24; they can also be put together as "the chariot of his second-in-command". "And so he set him": the Hebrew has a verb form with no subject.
- 41:45 "Potiphera" is not spelled as the "Potiphar" of 37:36 and 39:1; nothing says they are one man. "On" is spelled two ways (41:45, 50) with one English.
- 41:46 "stood before Pharaoh": also said of entering a king's service (1 Samuel 16:21); the picture is kept.
- 41:51 "my misery": the word of Job 3:10; "hardship" would also fit.
- 41:55, 56 "all Egypt", "sold grain to Egypt": the people, one word with the land. 41:56 "sold grain": the verb is "buy grain" elsewhere, here with "to".
- 41:56, 57 "all the face of the earth", "the whole earth": the word is also "land"; 41:54 has "all the lands".

For Genesis 42: "buy grain" (42:2, 3, 5, 7, 10) is the verb of 41:57, and "sold grain" (42:6) its causative, as at 41:56; 42:1, 2, 19, 26 have a third word for grain, the noun of that verb, beside the "grain" of 41:35, 49 (42:3, 25), and it needs its own wording where both stand close; "the famine" was "strong" (41:56, 57) and will be "severe" (43:1; 41:31); "the land" over which Joseph is set (41:33, 41, 43); "Joseph's brothers bowed down" (42:6) answers 37:7-10, and "the dreams that he had dreamed" (42:9) is the wording of 37:5-10 and 41:15; "in custody" (42:17) as at 40:3 and 41:10; "the interpreter was between them" (42:23) and "understood" (42:23) as quoted in this chapter's decision log and at 11:7; "distress" (42:21), "is gone" (42:13, 36) and "demand from the hand" (42:22) as the glossary has them; "the man, the lord of the land" (42:30, 33) and "trade in the land" (42:34) as at 34:10.

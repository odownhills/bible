# Notes on Genesis 16, for the translator of Genesis 17

Text followed: the Masoretic Text; the chapter has no Ketiv and Qere. The dot that the Hebrew text sets over one letter of "and you" (16:5) changes nothing. The main text keeps to the Masoretic vowels: 16:13 has two forms from "see", first a noun ("a God of seeing") and then "the one who sees me"; the old Greek and the old Latin have "the God who sees me" (Latin: "who has seen me") for the first as well.

Places where the old copies differ, none bracketed on that ground:
- 16:13 the old Greek has "for I have seen face to face the one who appeared to me", and at 16:14 "the well of the one whom I saw face to face"; the old Latin has "the back of the one who sees me", which is named in the decision log.

Brackets: 16:9 "let yourself be afflicted under her hands" [alt: humble yourself under her hands] (close); 16:12 "in the presence of all his brothers" [alt: in defiance of all his brothers] (close); 16:13 "Have I even here seen the back of the one who sees me?" [alt: Have I even here gone on seeing, after the one who sees me?] (ask). Three notes: 16:2 ("be built up" and "son"), 16:11 ("Ishmael"), 16:14 (the name of the well).

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 16:2 "be built up from her": the verb is "be built"; taken as a verb made from "son" ("get sons from her") it says the same, and the note gives the echo. Rachel says the same words at 30:3.
- 16:3 "as his wife": the Hebrew is "to him as a wife", the word used of Sarai in the same verse; the text does not use the word for a concubine here (25:6 does).
- 16:4-5 "counted for little": the verb is "be light", from the root of "call down a curse on" (12:3), so Hagar's slight of Sarai is heard with that promise; not shown.
- 16:5 "The violence done to me is on you": the Hebrew is "my violence on you", with no verb. It can be a statement (the blame is yours) or a wish (may it come on you; Jeremiah 51:35 has the same turn of phrase as a wish). "Violence" is the word of 6:11, not softened. "Into your arms": the word is the lap or bosom (2 Samuel 12:8).
- 16:5 "May Yahweh judge": the form can also be "Yahweh will judge" (1 Samuel 24:12 has the same words).
- 16:6, 8 "fled from her", "From Sarai my mistress": the Hebrew is "from the face of".
- 16:7 "spring" is the word for "eye"; the chapter is full of eyes and seeing (16:4, 5, 6, 13, 14). Not shown.
- 16:7-11 "the angel of Yahweh": the word is "messenger". He says "I will greatly multiply" (16:10) in his own person, and 16:13 says that Yahweh was speaking to her; the English adds nothing. "And the angel of Yahweh said to her" stands three times in a row and is kept three times.
- 16:10 "I will greatly multiply": the words said to the woman at 3:16. "Too many to count": the Hebrew is "it will not be counted for its multitude".
- 16:11 "you are pregnant": she has already conceived (16:4). "Heard your affliction": the Hebrew is "heard toward"; the name has "God" and the reason has "Yahweh".
- 16:12 "a wild donkey of a man": the second word is "human being" (Job 11:12 has the two words together). "Against": the same small word could be "with" or "in"; a hand "against" is its common use (Deuteronomy 13:9).
- 16:13 "named Yahweh": the Hebrew is "called the name of Yahweh, the one speaking to her". "A God of seeing" is as open as the Hebrew: a God who sees, or a God who is seen. "Even here": the word is "to here", "this far" (2 Samuel 7:18).
- 16:14 "was named": no subject is named, as at 11:9. In the note, "the living one" is left in small letters: the Hebrew does not say who it is.
- 16:16 "eighty-six years": Abram was seventy-five at 12:4, so the "ten years" of 16:3 are counted from there.

For Genesis 17: Abram is ninety-nine at 17:1 (eighty-six at 16:16) and Ishmael thirteen at 17:25; "Ishmael" and its note are not repeated, but 17:20 ("as for Ishmael, I have heard you") plays on the name again and "heard" must stay; "greatly multiply" (16:10) and "multiply" (17:2, 20) as listed; "offspring"; "female slave" for Hagar; "bore ... to Abram" (16:15-16) for the same words at 17:19, 21; "establish a covenant" (6:18) and "an everlasting covenant", "a sign of a covenant" (9:12-16) as the Genesis 6 and 9 lines planned; "say in his heart" (17:17) as the Genesis 8 line planned; "born in the house" (14:14); 17:18 ("live before you") has another Hebrew phrase than the "in the presence of" of 16:12.

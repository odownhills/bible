# Notes on Genesis 46, for the translator of Genesis 47

Text followed: the Masoretic Text; the chapter has no Qere. The Hebrew has paragraph breaks after 46:7 and 46:27, and the sections follow them. Numbering is the same in Hebrew and English.

Brackets: three, all places where the old copies differ, each with an entry in the decision log (all call: clear): 46:13 "Iob" [alt: Jashub]; 46:27 "seventy" [alt: seventy-five]; 46:28 "to show the way ahead of him to Goshen" [alt: to appear before him in Goshen].

Places where the old copies or other lists differ, not bracketed:
- 46:20 the old Greek adds five descendants of Manasseh and Ephraim; that is the same alternate as 46:27 and is told in its log entry.
- Names that Numbers 26 and 1 Chronicles give in another form, the same man each time: 46:10 Jemuel (Nemuel), Zohar (Zerah), and Ohad is not there; 46:13 Puvah (Puah); 46:16 Ziphion (Zephon, as the Samaritan copy and the old Greek have it here too), Ezbon (Ozni), Arodi (Arod); 46:21 Ehi (Ahiram), Muppim (Shephupham), Huppim (Hupham); 46:23 Hushim (Shuham); 46:24 Shillem (Shallum in Chronicles).
- 46:21 Numbers 26:40 makes Ard and Naaman sons of Bela, and the old Greek here makes several of the ten grandsons. Translated as it stands: ten sons.
- 46:2 the old Greek has "a vision", singular.

Weighed and left unbracketed:
- 46:1 "with all that he had": the Hebrew is "and all that he had", a second subject.
- 46:2 "And God said ..., and he said": both verbs kept, as at 22:7. "Israel" and "Jacob" each as the Hebrew has it, also in 46:5, 8, 29, 30.
- 46:4 "bring you up": the English "you" is as open as the Hebrew (Jacob's body, 50:13, or the people who come from him, 50:24).
- 46:9 "Enoch": the Hebrew name is the one of 4:17, 5:18 and 25:4, so it is spelled as there; most English Bibles write "Hanoch" for Reuben's son. Obay can switch it.
- 46:12 and 46:9 "Hezron": two men, one name in the Hebrew.
- 46:15 the names from Leah come to thirty-three with Dinah and without Er and Onan, who "died in the land of Canaan"; nothing is added to explain the count.
- 46:23 "the sons of Dan: Hushim": plural with one name, as the Hebrew has it (36:25).
- 46:26 "who came out of his loins": the Hebrew word is "thigh" (24:2; 32:25); "loins" (35:11) says in English what it means here. "Belonging to Jacob": it could also be put "who came with Jacob".
- 46:26-27 sixty-six and seventy: the text does not say who makes up the difference (Jacob, Joseph and his two sons do).
- 46:28 "he sent": Jacob, not named. "To show the way": the Hebrew does not say who is to show it, Judah or Joseph.
- 46:29 "he appeared to him and fell on his neck": the Hebrew names no one; Joseph is the subject of the verbs before. "Appeared" is the verb used of God at 12:7. "For a long time": the Hebrew is one word, "still", "more".
- 46:30 "for you are still alive": it could also be put "that you are still alive", as what he has seen.
- 46:31 "I will go up": or "Let me go up", as at 45:28.
- 46:32 is still what Joseph will say to Pharaoh. "Herdsmen" is "men of herds" in the Hebrew, the "herds" of 46:6; "shepherds of flocks" as at 4:2.
- 46:34 the last clause, "for every shepherd of flocks is a detestable thing to Egypt", is set inside Joseph's words; it could be the narrator's, as at 43:32. Joseph tells them to say "herdsmen", not "shepherds".

For Genesis 47: "What is your work?" (47:3; 46:33); at 47:3 the brothers answer "shepherds of flocks", the very word Joseph avoided, and it must not be "herdsmen" (46:32, 34); "both we and our fathers" (47:3; 46:34); "the land of Goshen" (47:1, 4, 6, 27); "their flocks and their cattle and all that they have" (47:1; 46:32); "My brothers and my father's house ... have come" (47:1; 46:31); "herds" for the same word in "men of herds" (47:6, 16-18); "provide for" (47:12; 45:11); "little ones" (47:12, 24; 46:5); "put your hand under my thigh" (47:29; 24:2), where "thigh" stays, though 46:26 has "loins"; "lie with my fathers" and "carry me out of Egypt" (47:30) answer "I myself will certainly bring you up as well" (46:4); "take holdings in" (47:27; 34:10) and "be fruitful and multiply" (47:27; 35:11).

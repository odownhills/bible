---
book: Job
chapter: 14
---
## Short of days and full of turmoil
1 "A human being, born of a woman, is short of days and full of turmoil.
2 Like a flower he comes out and withers; he flees like a shadow and does not stay.

3 "Do you open your eye even on such a one, and is it me you bring into judgment with you?
4 Who can bring what is clean out of what is unclean? Not one.
5 If his days are determined, if the number of his months is with you, if you have set his limit and he cannot pass it,
6 look away from him so that he may rest, until, like a hired worker, he completes his day [alt: until, like a hired worker, he enjoys his day].

## There is hope for a tree
7 "For there is hope for a tree: if it is cut down, it will sprout again, and its shoots will not cease.
8 Though its root grows old in the earth and its stump dies in the dust,
9 at the scent of water it will bud and put out branches like a sapling.

10 "But a man dies and is laid low; a human being breathes his last, and where is he?
11 As water is gone from a sea, and a river is drained and dries up,
12 so a man lies down and does not rise; until the heavens are no more, they will not awake, and they will not be roused from their sleep.

## If only you would hide me in Sheol
13 "If only you would hide me away in Sheol, hide me until your anger turns back, set a limit for me and remember me!
14 If a man dies, will he live? All the days of my soldier's service I would wait, until my relief came. [In Hebrew "relief" is from the same verb as "sprout again" in verse 7.]
15 You would call, and I myself would answer you; you would yearn for the work of your hands.
16 For then you would count my steps; you would not keep watch on my sin.
17 My rebellion would be sealed in a bag, and you would plaster over my guilt [alt: For now you count my steps; do you not keep watch on my sin? My rebellion is sealed in a bag, and you plaster over my guilt].

## You destroy the hope of a mortal
18 "But as a mountain falls and crumbles away, and a rock is moved from its place,
19 as water wears away stones and its floods wash away the dust of the earth, so you destroy the hope of a mortal.
20 You overpower him forever, and he goes; you change his face and send him away.
21 His sons are honored, and he does not know it; they are brought low, and he does not discern it.
22 Only his flesh on him is in pain, and his soul within him mourns."

## Job answers Zophar again
1 And Job answered and said:

2 "Hear my word carefully, and let this be your comforts.
3 Bear with me, and I myself will speak; and after I have spoken, you may mock.
4 As for me, is my complaint to a human being? And why should I not be impatient?
5 Turn to me and be appalled, and put your hand on your mouth.
6 And when I remember, I am dismayed, and shuddering seizes my flesh.

## Why do the wicked live?
7 "Why do the wicked live, grow old, and even grow mighty in wealth?
8 Their offspring are established before them, with them, and their descendants before their eyes.
9 Their houses are at peace, without dread, and the rod of God is not on them.

10 "His bull breeds and does not fail; his cow calves and does not miscarry.
11 They send out their young children like a flock, and their children dance.
12 They lift their voice to the drum and lyre, and rejoice at the sound of the pipe.
13 They wear out their days in good, and in a moment [alt: in peace] they go down to Sheol.

14 "And they say to God, 'Depart from us; we do not wish for the knowledge of your ways.
15 What is the Almighty, that we should serve him? And what do we gain if we appeal to him?'
16 Look, their good is not in their hand [alt: Look, is their good not in their hand?]; the counsel of the wicked is far from me.

## How often is their lamp snuffed out?
17 "How often is the lamp of the wicked snuffed out, and their calamity comes upon them? How often does he portion out pangs [alt: lots] in his anger?
18 How often are they like straw before the wind, and like chaff that a whirlwind steals away?

19 "'God hides away his harm for his sons.' Let him repay it to him, so that he may know it.
20 Let his eyes see his downfall, and let him drink of the wrath of the Almighty.
21 For what does he care about his house after him, when the number of his months is cut off?

## Together they lie down on the dust
22 "Is it God who is to be taught knowledge, when he himself judges those who are high?
23 One dies in his full strength, wholly untroubled and at ease.
24 His pails are full of milk [alt: His body is full of fat], and the marrow of his bones is moist.
25 And another dies with a bitter soul, and has not eaten of the good.
26 Together they lie down on the dust, and maggots cover them.

## Ask those who pass along the way
27 "Look, I know your plans, and the schemes with which you do violence to me.
28 For you say, 'Where is the house of the nobleman, and where is the tent where the wicked had their dwellings?'

29 "Have you not asked those who pass along the way, and do you not recognize their signs:
30 that the evil man is spared in the day of calamity, that in the day of fury they are led away [alt: that the evil man is kept for the day of calamity, that they are led to the day of fury]?
31 Who tells him his way to his face? And what he has done—who repays him for it?

32 "And he is carried to the graves, and watch is kept over the mound. [In Hebrew "carried" is the same verb as "led" in verse 30, and "mound" is the word for "a stack of grain" in 5:26.]
33 The clods of the torrent bed are sweet to him, and every human being follows after him, and those before him are without number.

34 "And how can you comfort me in vain? As for your replies, what is left is unfaithfulness."

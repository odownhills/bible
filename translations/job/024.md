book: Job

## Why are his days not seen?
1 "Why, when times are not hidden away from the Almighty, do those who know him not gaze on his days [alt: Why are times not stored up by the Almighty, and why do those who know him not gaze on his days]?

2 "Some move boundaries; they seize a flock and pasture it.
3 They drive off the donkey of orphans; they take a widow's ox as a pledge.
4 They turn the needy aside from the way; together the afflicted of the land are made to hide.

## The afflicted of the land
5 "Look, wild donkeys in the wilderness, they go out about their work, searching for food; the desert is each one's bread for the young.
6 In the field they harvest his fodder, and they glean the vineyard of the wicked man.
7 Naked they spend the night, for lack of clothing, and there is no covering in the cold.
8 They are wet from the downpour of the mountains, and for lack of shelter they embrace the rock.

9 "They seize the orphan from the breast, and they take pledges from the afflicted [alt: and they take the infant of the afflicted as a pledge].

10 "Naked they go about, without clothing, and hungry, they carry the sheaves.
11 Between their walls they press oil; they tread the winepresses and are thirsty.
12 From the city men groan, and the soul of the wounded cries for help, and God makes no charge of wrongdoing [alt: and God does not consider the prayer, as the old Syriac version reads].

## Those who rebel against the light
13 "These are among those who rebel against the light; they do not recognize its ways, and they do not stay in its pathways.
14 At first light the murderer rises; he kills the afflicted and the needy, and in the night he is like a thief.
15 And the eye of the adulterer keeps watch for the twilight, saying, 'No eye will catch sight of me,' and he puts his face in hiding.
16 In the darkness he digs into houses; by day they seal themselves in; they do not know the light.
17 For to all of them together morning is the shadow of death, for each one recognizes the terrors of the shadow of death.

## A little while, and he is no more
18 "He is swift on the face of the water; their plot is cursed in the land; he does not turn onto the way to the vineyards. [In Hebrew "swift" and "is cursed" come from the same word.]
19 Drought, and heat as well, seize the snow waters; Sheol, those who have sinned.
20 The womb forgets him; the maggot is sweet to him; he is no longer remembered, and injustice is broken like a tree.

21 "He feeds on the barren woman who does not give birth, and he does no good to the widow.
22 And he drags away the mighty [alt: he prolongs the life of the mighty] by his strength; he rises, and is not sure of life.
23 He gives him security, and he leans on it; and his eyes are on their ways.
24 They are raised high for a little while, and he is no more; they are brought low; like all others they are shut away, and like the head of an ear of grain they wither.

25 "And if not, then who will prove me a liar and turn my word into nothing?"

## Bildad answers Job again
1 And Bildad the Shuhite answered and said:

2 "How long before you put an end to words [alt: How long will you set snares for words]? Understand, and afterward we will speak.
3 Why are we counted as beasts, and why are we unclean in your eyes [alt: stupid in your eyes]?
4 One who tears himself in his anger—is the earth to be abandoned for your sake, and a rock to be moved from its place?

## The light of the wicked is snuffed out
5 "Yes, the light of the wicked is snuffed out, and the flame of his fire does not shine.
6 The light goes dark in his tent, and his lamp above him is snuffed out.
7 His vigorous steps are cramped, and his counsel throws him down.

## A net for his feet
8 "For by his feet he is sent into a net, and he walks about on a mesh.
9 A trap seizes him by the heel; a snare holds him fast.
10 The rope for him is hidden in the ground, and what will catch him is on the pathway.

## The king of terrors
11 "On every side terrors terrify him, and they drive him on at his heels.
12 His vigor goes hungry [alt: His harm is hungry], and calamity is ready for his stumbling.
13 It eats his skin, limb by limb; the firstborn of death eats his limbs.
14 What he trusts in is torn from his tent [alt: He is torn from the tent he trusts in], and he is marched to the king of terrors.

## His memory perishes from the earth
15 "What is not his lives in his tent; sulfur is scattered over his home.
16 His roots dry up below, and his branches wither above.
17 His memory perishes from the earth, and he has no name on the face of the open country.
18 He is pushed from light into darkness and chased out of the world.
19 He has no progeny and no posterity among his people, and there is no survivor in the places where he stayed.

20 "Those in the west are appalled at his day, and those in the east are seized with horror [alt: Those who come after are appalled at his day, and those who came before were seized with horror].
21 Surely these are the dwellings of the unjust man, and this is the place of one who does not know God."

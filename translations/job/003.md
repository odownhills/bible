## Job curses his day
1 After this Job opened his mouth and cursed his day.
2 And Job answered and said:

3 "Let the day perish on which I was born, and the night that said, 'A man has been conceived.'
4 That day—let it be darkness. Let God not seek it from above, and let no daylight shine on it.
5 Let darkness and the shadow of death reclaim it [alt: defile it]; let a cloud settle on it; let them terrify it like the bitter things of a day [alt: let all that blackens the day terrify it].

6 "That night—let deep darkness take it. Let it not rejoice among the days of the year [alt: be joined to the days of the year]; let it not come into the number of the months.
7 Look, that night—let it be barren; let no cry of joy come into it.
8 Let those who curse a day put a curse on it, those who are ready to rouse Leviathan. [In Hebrew "those who curse" and "rouse" sound alike.]
9 Let the stars of its twilight go dark; let it hope for light, and there be none; and let it not look on the eyelids of dawn.
10 For it did not shut the doors of my mother's womb and hide misery from my eyes.

## Why did I not die at birth?
11 "Why did I not die at birth, come out of the womb and breathe my last?
12 Why did knees receive me, and why breasts, that I should nurse?

13 "For by now I would be lying down and quiet; I would be asleep; then there would be rest for me,
14 with kings and counselors of the earth, who rebuilt ruins for themselves [alt: who built for themselves what are now ruins],
15 or with princes who had gold, who filled their houses with silver.
16 Or, like a hidden stillborn child, I would not be, like infants who did not see light.

17 "There the wicked have ceased from turmoil, and there those whose strength is worn out are at rest.
18 Prisoners are untroubled together; they do not hear the voice of a taskmaster.
19 Small and great are there, and the slave is free from his master.

## Light for one in misery
20 "Why does he give light to one in misery, and life to those bitter in soul,
21 who wait for death, and there is none, and dig for it more than for hidden treasures,
22 who rejoice to the point of exultation, who are glad when they find a grave—
23 to a man whose way is hidden, around whom God has put a hedge?

24 "For my sighing comes before my bread [alt: comes like my bread], and my roars pour out like water.
25 For the dread that I dreaded has come upon me, and what I shrank from comes to me.
26 I have not been at ease, and I have not been quiet, and I have not rested, and turmoil has come."

## Job answers Bildad a third time
1 And Job answered and said:

2 "How you have helped the one without strength! How you have saved the arm without power!
3 How you have counseled the one without wisdom, and made sound wisdom known in abundance!
4 To whom have you told words [alt: With whose help have you told words], and whose breath has come out of you?

## He hangs the earth on nothing
5 "The dead [alt: The Rephaim, the giant people of old] writhe beneath the waters and those who live in them.
6 Sheol is naked before him, and there is no covering for Abaddon.
7 He stretches out the north over the waste; he hangs the earth on nothing.
8 He wraps up water in his thick clouds, and the cloud does not burst under it.
9 He closes off the face of the throne [alt: the face of the full moon], spreading his cloud over it.
10 He has drawn a circle as a boundary on the face of the waters, as far as the limit of light with darkness.

## The thunder of his might
11 "The pillars of the heavens quake and are astonished at his rebuke.
12 By his strength he stirred up the sea [alt: he stilled the sea], and by his understanding he wounded Rahab.
13 By his wind [alt: By his spirit] the heavens are fair; his hand pierced [alt: brought forth] the fleeing snake.
14 Look, these are the edges of his way, and what a whisper of a word is heard of him! But the thunder of his might—who can understand it?"

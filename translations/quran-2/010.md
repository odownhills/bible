## Whom to marry
221 And do not marry women who give God partners until they believe, and a believing slave woman is better than a woman who gives God partners, even if she impresses you. And do not give women in marriage to men who give God partners until they believe, and a believing slave is better than a man who gives God partners, even if he impresses you. Those call to the Fire, and God calls to the Garden and forgiveness, by his permission, and he makes his signs clear to the people, so that they may remember.

## Menstruation
222 And they ask you about menstruation. Say, "It is a discomfort [alt: It is something unclean], so keep away from women during menstruation, and do not go near them until they are pure. Then when they have purified themselves, come to them from where God has commanded you." God loves those who repent often, and he loves those who purify themselves.

## A field for you
223 Your wives are a field for you, so come to your field however you wish [alt: whenever you wish], and send ahead for yourselves. And be mindful of God, and know that you will meet him. And give good news to the believers.

## Oaths
224 And do not make God, by your oaths, an obstacle to being virtuous, being mindful of him and setting things right between people [alt: And do not make God a target for your oaths, so that you may be virtuous, be mindful of him and set things right between people]. And God is hearing, knowing.
225 God does not take you to task for idle talk in your oaths, but he takes you to task for what your hearts have earned. And God is forgiving, forbearing.

## Those who swear off their wives
226 For those who swear off their wives there is a waiting of four months. Then if they come back, God is forgiving, merciful.
227 And if they resolve on divorce, God is hearing, knowing.

## Divorced women
228 And divorced women keep themselves waiting for three periods [alt: three clean times between periods]. And it is not permitted to them to conceal what God has created in their wombs, if they believe in God and the Last Day. And their husbands have more right to take them back in that time, if they want to set things right. And what is due to them is like what is due from them, as is right, and men have a degree over them. And God is mighty, wise.

## Divorce is twice
229 Divorce is twice. Then there is to be keeping as is right, or letting go in a good way. And it is not permitted to you to take anything of what you have given them, unless the two fear that they will not uphold God's limits. Then if you fear that the two will not uphold God's limits, there is no blame on the two of them for what she ransoms herself with. Those are God's limits, so do not overstep them. And whoever oversteps God's limits, those are the wrongdoers.
230 Then if he divorces her, she is not permitted to him after that until she marries a husband other than him. Then if he divorces her, there is no blame on the two of them if they return to each other, if they think that they will uphold God's limits. And those are God's limits; he makes them clear for a people who know.

## When they reach their term
231 And when you divorce women and they reach their term, keep them as is right or let them go as is right. And do not keep them to harm them, so as to overstep. And whoever does that has wronged himself. And do not make a mockery of God's signs. And remember God's favor to you, and what he has sent down to you of the Book and wisdom, counseling you with it. And be mindful of God, and know that God has knowledge of all things.
232 And when you divorce women and they reach their term, do not prevent them from marrying their husbands, when they agree between themselves as is right. That is the counsel given to whoever among you believes in God and the Last Day. That is cleaner for you, and purer. And God knows, and you do not know.

## Nursing
233 And mothers nurse their children for two full years, for whoever wants to complete the nursing. And their provision and their clothing are on the one to whom the child is born, as is right. No soul is burdened beyond its capacity. Let no mother be harmed through her child, nor one to whom a child is born through his child. And the like of that is on the heir. Then if the two want to wean, by agreement between them and consultation, there is no blame on the two of them. And if you want to seek a nurse for your children, there is no blame on you, when you hand over what you give, as is right. And be mindful of God, and know that God sees what you do.

## Widows
234 And those of you who are taken in death and leave wives behind, these keep themselves waiting for four months and ten. Then when they reach their term, there is no blame on you for what they do with themselves, as is right. And God is aware of what you do.
235 And there is no blame on you for what you hint of a proposal to women, or keep within yourselves. God knows that you will think of them. But do not make promises with them in secret, unless you say what is right. And do not resolve on the knot of marriage until what is prescribed reaches its term. And know that God knows what is within yourselves, so beware of him. And know that God is forgiving, forbearing.

## Divorce before touching
236 There is no blame on you if you divorce women when you have not touched them or set an amount for them. And give them support, the one who has plenty according to his means and the one who has little according to his means, support as is right: a duty on those who do good.
237 And if you divorce them before you touch them, when you have already set an amount for them, then half of what you set, unless they pardon it, or the one in whose hand is the knot of marriage pardons it. And for you to pardon is nearer to mindfulness of God. And do not forget bounty between yourselves. God sees what you do.

## Watch over the prayers
238 Watch over the prayers, and the middle prayer, and stand obedient to God.
239 But if you are in fear, then on foot or riding. Then when you are safe, remember God, as he has taught you what you did not know.

## Support, as is right
240 And those of you who are taken in death and leave wives behind: a bequest for their wives of support for the year, without being driven out. But if they go out, there is no blame on you for what they do with themselves that is right. And God is mighty, wise.
241 And for divorced women there is support, as is right: a duty on those who are mindful of God.
242 That is how God makes his signs clear to you, so that you may use your reason.

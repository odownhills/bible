title: In the name of God, the Most Merciful, the Merciful.
## That is the Book
1 A. L. M. [These are three letters of the Arabic alphabet, standing on their own.]
2 That is the Book. There is no doubt in it; it is guidance [alt: There is no doubt. In it is guidance] for those who are mindful of God,
3 those who believe in the unseen [alt: believe while unseen], keep up the prayer, and spend from what we have provided for them,
4 and those who believe in what has been sent down to you and what was sent down before you, and of the hereafter they are certain.
5 Those are on guidance from their Lord, and those are the ones who succeed.

## Those who disbelieve
6 As for those who disbelieve, it is the same for them whether you warn them or do not warn them: they do not believe.
7 God has set a seal on their hearts and on their hearing, and over their sight is a covering, and for them there is a great punishment.

## Those who say they believe
8 And among people there are some who say, "We believe in God and in the Last Day," but they are not believers.
9 They try to deceive God and those who believe, but they deceive only themselves, and they do not realize it.
10 In their hearts is a sickness, so God has increased their sickness, and for them there is a painful punishment because they used to lie [alt: because they used to call it a lie].

11 And when they are told, "Do not cause corruption on the earth," they say, "We are only setting things right."
12 Truly, they are the ones who cause corruption, but they do not realize it.
13 And when they are told, "Believe as the people have believed," they say, "Should we believe as the fools have believed?" Truly, they are the fools, but they do not know.

14 And when they meet those who believe, they say, "We believe," but when they are alone with their devils, they say, "We are with you; we are only mocking."
15 God mocks them and keeps them going in their excess, wandering blindly.
16 Those are the ones who have bought error in exchange for guidance, so their trade has made no profit, and they were not guided.

## A fire and a rainstorm
17 Their example is like that of one who kindled a fire, and when it lit up what was around him, God took away their light and left them in darkness, unable to see.
18 Deaf, mute, blind, so they do not return.

19 Or like a rainstorm from the sky, with darkness, thunder and lightning in it. They put their fingers in their ears because of the thunderbolts, for fear of death. And God surrounds the disbelievers.
20 The lightning almost snatches away their sight. Whenever it lights up for them, they walk in it, and when it grows dark over them, they stand still. And if God had willed, he would have taken away their hearing and their sight. God has power over all things.

## Worship your Lord
21 People, worship your Lord, who created you and those before you, so that you may be mindful of him,
22 the one who made the earth a bed for you and the sky a building, and sent down water from the sky and with it brought out fruits as provision for you. So do not set up rivals to God while you know.

23 And if you are in doubt about what we have sent down to our servant, then bring a surah like it [alt: a surah from someone like him], and call your witnesses other than God, if you are telling the truth.
24 But if you do not, and you never will, then guard yourselves against the Fire whose fuel is people and stones, prepared for the disbelievers. [In Arabic "guard yourselves against" is the same word as "mindful of" in verses 2 and 21.]

25 And give good news to those who believe and do righteous deeds, that for them there are gardens with rivers flowing beneath them. Whenever they are provided with a provision of fruit from them, they say, "This is what we were provided with before," and what they are brought looks alike. There they have purified spouses, and there they will remain forever.

## The example of a gnat
26 God does not shy away from giving any example, a gnat or anything above it. As for those who believe, they know that it is the truth from their Lord. But as for those who disbelieve, they say, "What did God mean by this example?" By it he leads many astray, and by it he guides many, but he leads astray by it only the defiant,
27 those who break God's covenant after it has been made binding, cut what God has commanded to be joined, and cause corruption on the earth. Those are the losers.

## How can you disbelieve in God?
28 How can you disbelieve in God, when you were dead and he gave you life, then he will cause you to die, then he will give you life, then to him you will be returned?
29 He is the one who created for you all that is on the earth, then turned to the sky and formed them into seven heavens, and he has knowledge of all things.

## Whoever believes in God and the Last Day
62 Those who believe, and those who are Jews, and the Christians, and the Sabians, whoever believes in God and the Last Day and does what is righteous, they have their reward with their Lord, and there is no fear for them, nor will they grieve.

## The Mount and the Sabbath
63 And when we took your pledge and raised the Mount above you: "Take with strength what we have given you, and remember what is in it, so that you may be mindful of God."
64 Then you turned away after that, and had it not been for God's bounty to you and his mercy, you would have been among the losers.

65 And you know well those of you who overstepped on the Sabbath, so we said to them, "Be apes, driven away."
66 So we made it a deterrent for what was before it and what was behind it, and a lesson for those who are mindful of God.

## The cow
67 And when Moses said to his people, "God commands you to slaughter a cow," they said, "Are you making a mockery of us?" He said, "I take refuge in God from being one of the ignorant."
68 They said, "Call on your Lord for us, to make clear to us what it is." He said, "He says it is a cow neither old nor young, of middle age, between the two. So do what you are commanded."
69 They said, "Call on your Lord for us, to make clear to us what its color is." He said, "He says it is a yellow cow, bright in color, that delights those who look at it."
70 They said, "Call on your Lord for us, to make clear to us what it is. Cows look alike to us, and if God wills, we will certainly be guided."
71 He said, "He says it is a cow not broken in to turn the earth or water the field, sound, with no mark on it." They said, "Now you have brought the truth." So they slaughtered it, and they almost did not do it.

72 And when you killed a soul, you blamed one another for it, and God would bring out what you were concealing.
73 So we said, "Strike him with part of it." That is how God gives life to the dead, and he shows you his signs so that you may use your reason.

## Hearts like stones
74 Then your hearts hardened after that, so they are like stones, or harder. And among stones there are some from which rivers gush out, and among them there are some that split open and water comes out of them, and among them there are some that fall down out of fear of God. And God is not unaware of what you do.

## Do you hope that they will believe you?
75 So do you hope that they will believe you, when a group of them used to hear God's speech and then twist it after they had understood it, while they knew?
76 And when they meet those who believe, they say, "We believe," but when they are alone with one another, they say, "Do you talk to them about what God has opened to you, so that they may argue against you with it before your Lord? Will you not use your reason?" [alt: their speech ends at "before your Lord?" and the last question is God's]
77 Do they not know that God knows what they keep secret and what they make public?

## Those who write the Book with their hands
78 And among them are unlettered people who do not know the Book, only wishful thinking [alt: who know the Book only as recitation], and they are only guessing.
79 So woe to those who write the Book with their hands and then say, "This is from God," to sell it for a small price. So woe to them for what their hands have written, and woe to them for what they earn. [In Arabic "the Book" is the word for a thing written, from the same word as "write".]

## A limited number of days
80 And they said, "The Fire will not touch us except for a limited number of days." Say, "Have you taken a covenant with God? Then God will not go back on his covenant. Or do you say about God what you do not know?"
81 Not so. Whoever earns evil and is surrounded by his fault, those are the companions of the Fire; they will remain in it forever.
82 And those who believe and do righteous deeds, those are the companions of the Garden; they will remain in it forever.

## Abraham and the House
124 And when his Lord tested Abraham with words, he fulfilled them. He said, "I am making you a leader for the people." He said, "And of my descendants?" He said, "My covenant does not reach the wrongdoers."
125 And when we made the House a place of return for the people and a place of safety. And take from the place where Abraham stood a place of prayer [alt: and they took from the place where Abraham stood a place of prayer]. And we made a covenant with Abraham and Ishmael: "Purify my House for those who go around it, those who stay there, and those who bow and bow down."
126 And when Abraham said, "My Lord, make this a safe land, and provide its people with fruits, those of them who believe in God and the Last Day," he said, "And whoever disbelieves, I will let him enjoy himself a little, then I will force him to the punishment of the Fire. And miserable is the destination."

## Raising the foundations
127 And when Abraham was raising the foundations of the House, and Ishmael: "Our Lord, accept this from us. You are the Hearing, the Knowing.
128 Our Lord, and make us both people who submit to you, and from our descendants a community that submits to you. And show us our rites, and relent toward us. You are the Ever-Relenting, the Merciful.
129 Our Lord, and raise up among them a messenger from among them, who will recite your signs to them and teach them the Book and wisdom and cleanse them. You are the Mighty, the Wise."

## The creed of Abraham
130 And who wants no part of the creed of Abraham except one who makes a fool of himself? And we certainly chose him in this world, and in the hereafter he is among the righteous.
131 When his Lord said to him, "Submit," he said, "I have submitted to the Lord of the worlds."
132 And Abraham charged his sons with it, and so did Jacob: "My sons, God has chosen the religion for you, so do not die except as people who submit."

## Jacob and his sons
133 Or were you witnesses when death came to Jacob, when he said to his sons, "What will you worship after me?" They said, "We will worship your god and the god of your fathers, Abraham, Ishmael and Isaac, one god, and to him we submit."
134 That is a community that has passed away. It has what it earned, and you have what you earned, and you will not be asked about what they used to do.

## No distinction between any of them
135 And they said, "Be Jews or Christians, and you will be guided." Say, "Rather, the creed of Abraham, who was devoted, and he was not one of those who give God partners."
136 Say, "We believe in God and what has been sent down to us, and what was sent down to Abraham, Ishmael, Isaac, Jacob and the tribes, and what Moses and Jesus were given, and what the prophets were given from their Lord. We make no distinction between any of them, and to him we submit."
137 So if they believe in the like of what you believe in, they are guided. But if they turn away, they are only in opposition. So God will be enough for you against them, and he is the Hearing, the Knowing.
138 "The dye of God, and whose dye is better than God's? And it is him we worship."

## Do you know better, or does God?
139 Say, "Do you argue with us about God, when he is our Lord and your Lord, and we have our deeds and you have your deeds, and to him we are sincere?"
140 Or do you say that Abraham, Ishmael, Isaac, Jacob and the tribes were Jews or Christians? Say, "Do you know better, or does God?" And who does greater wrong than one who conceals a testimony he has from God? And God is not unaware of what you do.
141 That is a community that has passed away. It has what it earned, and you have what you earned, and you will not be asked about what they used to do.

## A middle community
142 The fools among the people will say, "What has turned them from the direction they used to face?" Say, "To God belong the east and the west. He guides whomever he wills to a straight path."
143 And that is how we have made you a middle community [alt: a just community], so that you may be witnesses over the people and the messenger may be a witness over you. And we set the direction you used to face only so that we might know who follows the messenger from who turns back on his heels. And it was hard indeed, except for those God has guided. And God would not let your belief go to waste. God is kind to people, merciful.

## Turn your face
144 We do see your face turning about in the sky, so we will certainly turn you to a direction you will be pleased with. So turn your face toward the Sacred Place of Worship, and wherever you are, turn your faces toward it. And those who were given the Book know well that it is the truth from their Lord. And God is not unaware of what they do.
145 And if you were to bring every sign to those who were given the Book, they would not follow your direction, nor do you follow their direction, nor do they follow one another's direction. And if you were to follow their desires after the knowledge that has come to you, you would then be among the wrongdoers.

146 Those to whom we gave the Book recognize it [alt: recognize him] as they recognize their sons, but a group of them conceal the truth while they know.
147 The truth is from your Lord, so do not be among the doubters.

## Race to what is good
148 And each has a direction he turns toward [alt: a direction he is turned toward], so race to what is good. Wherever you are, God will bring you all together. God has power over all things.

149 And from wherever you go out, turn your face toward the Sacred Place of Worship. And it is certainly the truth from your Lord, and God is not unaware of what you do.
150 And from wherever you go out, turn your face toward the Sacred Place of Worship, and wherever you are, turn your faces toward it, so that people may have no argument against you, except those of them who do wrong. So do not fear them, but fear me. And so that I may complete my favor to you, and so that you may be guided.
151 Just as we have sent among you a messenger from among you, who recites our signs to you and cleanses you and teaches you the Book and wisdom, and teaches you what you did not know.
152 So remember me, and I will remember you. And give thanks to me, and do not be ungrateful to me. [In Arabic "be ungrateful to" is the same word as "disbelieve in".]

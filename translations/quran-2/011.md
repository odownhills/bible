## For fear of death
243 Have you not seen those who went out of their homes in their thousands, for fear of death? So God said to them, "Die." Then he gave them life. God is bountiful to people, but most people do not give thanks.
244 And fight in the way of God, and know that God is hearing, knowing.

## A good loan to God
245 Who is it that will lend God a good loan, so that he may multiply it for him many times over? And God withholds and gives amply, and to him you will be returned.

## Raise up a king for us
246 Have you not seen the chiefs of the Children of Israel after Moses, when they said to a prophet of theirs, "Raise up a king for us, and we will fight in the way of God"? He said, "Might it be that, if fighting is prescribed for you, you will not fight?" They said, "And why should we not fight in the way of God, when we have been driven out of our homes and from our sons?" But when fighting was prescribed for them, they turned away, except a few of them. And God has knowledge of the wrongdoers.
247 And their prophet said to them, "God has raised up Saul for you as king." They said, "How can he have the kingdom over us, when we have more right to the kingdom than he does, and he has not been given plenty of wealth?" He said, "God has chosen him over you and has increased him amply in knowledge and body. And God gives his kingdom to whomever he wills, and God is vast, knowing."
248 And their prophet said to them, "The sign of his kingdom is that the Chest will come to you, in which there is calm from your Lord and a remnant of what the family of Moses and the family of Aaron left, carried by the angels. In that there is a sign for you, if you are believers."

## The river and Goliath
249 So when Saul set out with the forces, he said, "God will test you with a river. Whoever drinks from it is not one of mine, and whoever does not taste it is one of mine, except one who scoops up a handful with his hand." But they drank from it, except a few of them. Then when he had crossed it, he and those who believed with him, they said, "We have no strength today against Goliath and his forces." Those who thought that they would meet God said, "How many a small company has overcome a large company by God's permission! And God is with those who are patient."
250 And when they came out against Goliath and his forces, they said, "Our Lord, pour patience over us, make our feet firm, and aid us against the disbelieving people."
251 So they defeated them by God's permission, and David killed Goliath, and God gave him the kingdom and wisdom and taught him some of what he wills. And had it not been for God repelling some people by means of others, the earth would have been corrupted. But God is bountiful to all peoples.

252 Those are God's signs. We recite them to you with the truth, and you are certainly one of those sent.

## Some messengers above others
253 Those messengers, we set some of them above others. Among them are some to whom God spoke, and he raised some of them in degrees. And we gave Jesus son of Mary the clear proofs and strengthened him with the Holy Spirit. And if God had willed, those after them would not have fought one another after the clear proofs had come to them. But they differed, and some of them believed and some of them disbelieved. And if God had willed, they would not have fought one another. But God does what he wants.

## Before a day comes
254 You who believe, spend from what we have provided for you before a day comes in which there is no selling, no friendship and no intercession. And the disbelievers are the wrongdoers.

## The Living, the Ever-Standing
255 God, there is no god but him, the Living, the Ever-Standing [alt: the Upholder of all]. [In Arabic the one word means both.] Neither drowsiness nor sleep takes hold of him. To him belongs what is in the heavens and what is on the earth. Who is it that will intercede with him except by his permission? He knows what is before them and what is behind them, and they do not encompass anything of his knowledge except what he wills. His seat takes in the heavens and the earth, and watching over them does not tire him. And he is the High, the Great.

## No compulsion in religion
256 There is no compulsion in religion. The right course has become clear from the wrong course. So whoever disbelieves in false gods [alt: in the one who goes beyond all bounds] and believes in God has grasped the firmest handhold, which does not break. And God is hearing, knowing.
257 God is the ally of those who believe; he brings them out of the darkness into the light. And those who disbelieve, their allies are false gods; they bring them out of the light into the darkness. Those are the companions of the Fire; they will remain in it forever.

## The one who argued with Abraham
258 Have you not seen the one who argued with Abraham about his Lord, because God had given him the kingdom? When Abraham said, "My Lord is the one who gives life and causes death," he said, "I give life and cause death." Abraham said, "Then God brings the sun from the east, so bring it from the west." So the one who disbelieved was dumbfounded. And God does not guide the wrongdoing people.

## A hundred years
259 Or like the one who passed by a town that had fallen in on its roofs. He said, "How will God give life to this after its death?" So God caused him to die for a hundred years, then raised him up. He said, "How long have you stayed?" He said, "I have stayed a day or part of a day." He said, "Rather, you have stayed a hundred years. So look at your food and your drink; they have not spoiled. And look at your donkey. And so that we may make you a sign for the people. And look at the bones, how we lift them up [alt: how we bring them to life] and then clothe them with flesh." Then when it became clear to him, he said, "I know that God has power over all things." [alt: he said, "Know that God has power over all things."]

## Four birds
260 And when Abraham said, "My Lord, show me how you give life to the dead," he said, "Do you not believe?" He said, "Yes, but so that my heart may be at rest." He said, "Then take four birds and draw them to you [alt: take four birds to you and cut them up], then place a part of them on each mountain, then call them; they will come to you in haste. And know that God is mighty, wise."

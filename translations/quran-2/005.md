## The word not to say
104 You who believe, do not say, "Attend to us," but say, "Wait for us [alt: Look at us]," and hear. And for the disbelievers there is a painful punishment. [In Arabic the first of these requests could be twisted with the tongue into an insult, and 4:46 tells of people who said it that way.]
105 Those who disbelieve, among the People of the Book or among those who give God partners, would not love any good to be sent down to you from your Lord. But God singles out for his mercy whomever he wills, and God is the possessor of great bounty.

## Whatever sign we cancel
106 Whatever sign we cancel or cause to be forgotten [alt: or postpone], we bring something better than it or like it. Do you not know that God has power over all things?
107 Do you not know that the kingdom of the heavens and the earth belongs to God? And apart from God you have no ally and no one to aid you.
108 Or do you want to ask your messenger as Moses was asked before? And whoever takes disbelief in exchange for belief has gone astray from the even way.

## Pardon and overlook
109 Many of the People of the Book would love to turn you back into disbelievers after your belief, out of envy from their own selves, after the truth has become clear to them. So pardon and overlook, until God brings his command. God has power over all things.
110 And keep up the prayer and give the alms. And whatever good you send ahead for yourselves, you will find it with God. God sees what you do.

## Bring your proof
111 And they said, "No one will enter the Garden except those who are Jews or Christians." That is their wishful thinking. Say, "Bring your proof, if you are telling the truth."
112 Not so. Whoever submits his face to God and does good, he has his reward with his Lord, and there is no fear for them, nor will they grieve.

113 And the Jews said, "The Christians have nothing to stand on," and the Christians said, "The Jews have nothing to stand on," while they recite the Book. In the same way, those who do not know said something like what they said. So God will judge between them on the Day of Resurrection about what they used to differ over.

## Wherever you turn
114 And who does greater wrong than one who keeps God's places of worship from having his name remembered in them, and strives for their ruin? It is not for those to enter them except in fear. For them there is disgrace in this world, and for them in the hereafter there is a great punishment.
115 And to God belong the east and the west, so wherever you turn, there is the face of God. God is vast, knowing.

## Be, and it is
116 And they said, "God has taken a child for himself." Glory to him! Rather, what is in the heavens and the earth belongs to him; all are obedient to him.
117 He is the Originator of the heavens and the earth, and when he decides a matter, he only says to it, "Be," and it is.

## Sent with the truth
118 And those who do not know said, "Why does God not speak to us, or a sign come to us?" In the same way, those before them said something like what they said. Their hearts are alike. We have made the signs clear for a people who are certain.
119 We have sent you with the truth, as a bringer of good news and a warner, and you will not be asked about the companions of the Blaze [alt: and do not ask about the companions of the Blaze].

## God's guidance is the guidance
120 And the Jews will not be pleased with you, nor the Christians, until you follow their creed. Say, "God's guidance is the guidance." And if you were to follow their desires after the knowledge that has come to you, you would have no ally and no one to aid you against God.
121 Those to whom we gave the Book, reciting it as it should be recited, those believe in it. And whoever disbelieves in it, those are the losers.

## A day when no soul pays for another
122 Children of Israel, remember my favor that I showed you, and that I set you above all peoples.
123 And guard yourselves against a day when no soul will pay anything on behalf of another soul, and no compensation will be accepted from it, and no intercession will benefit it, nor will they be aided.

## A grain that grew seven ears
261 The example of those who spend their wealth in the way of God is like that of a grain that grew seven ears, with a hundred grains in every ear. And God multiplies for whomever he wills, and God is vast, knowing.
262 Those who spend their wealth in the way of God, and then do not follow what they have spent with reminders of it or with hurt, they have their reward with their Lord, and there is no fear for them, nor will they grieve.

## Charity followed by hurt
263 Saying what is right, and forgiveness, are better than charity followed by hurt. And God is rich, forbearing.
264 You who believe, do not make your charity worthless with reminders of it and with hurt, like the one who spends his wealth to be seen by people and does not believe in God and the Last Day. So his example is like that of a smooth rock with soil on it: a downpour struck it and left it bare. They have no power over anything of what they have earned. And God does not guide the disbelieving people.

## A garden on high ground
265 And the example of those who spend their wealth seeking to please God and to make themselves firm [alt: and out of a firmness within themselves] is like that of a garden on high ground: a downpour struck it, so it gave its produce twice over, and if no downpour strikes it, then light rain. And God sees what you do.
266 Would any of you love to have a garden of date palms and grapevines, with rivers flowing beneath it, where he has fruits of every kind, and old age has struck him, and he has weak children, and then a whirlwind with fire in it strikes it and it burns? That is how God makes the signs clear to you, so that you may reflect.

## Spend from the good things
267 You who believe, spend from the good things you have earned and from what we have brought out of the earth for you. And do not aim for the bad of it to spend, when you would not take it yourselves unless you shut your eyes to it. And know that God is rich, praiseworthy.
268 Satan promises you poverty and commands you to indecency, and God promises you forgiveness from him and bounty. And God is vast, knowing.
269 He gives wisdom to whomever he wills, and whoever is given wisdom has been given much good. And no one remembers except those who have understanding.

## In secret and in public
270 And whatever you spend, or whatever vow you make, God knows it. And the wrongdoers have no one to aid them.
271 If you disclose charity, it is a fine thing. But if you hide it and give it to the poor, that is better for you, and he will remove some of your evil deeds from you. And God is aware of what you do.

272 Guiding them is not on you, but God guides whomever he wills. And whatever good you spend is for yourselves, and you do not spend except seeking the face of God. And whatever good you spend will be paid to you in full, and you will not be wronged.
273 It is for the poor who are held back in the way of God and cannot travel in the land. An ignorant person supposes them rich because of their restraint. You recognize them by their mark. They do not ask people insistently. And whatever good you spend, God has knowledge of it.
274 Those who spend their wealth by night and by day, in secret and in public, they have their reward with their Lord, and there is no fear for them, nor will they grieve.

## Selling and interest
275 Those who eat up interest [alt: usury] do not stand except as one stands whom Satan beats down by his touch. That is because they said, "Selling is only like interest." But God has permitted selling and forbidden interest. So whoever has counsel come to him from his Lord and stops, what is past is his, and his matter rests with God. But whoever goes back, those are the companions of the Fire; they will remain in it forever.
276 God wipes out interest and makes charity grow. And God does not love any persistent, sinful disbeliever. [In Arabic the word for "interest" means growth, and "makes grow" is from the same word.]
277 Those who believe and do righteous deeds, and keep up the prayer and give the alms, they have their reward with their Lord, and there is no fear for them, nor will they grieve.

278 You who believe, be mindful of God and give up what remains of interest, if you are believers.
279 But if you do not, then take notice of war from God and his messenger. And if you repent, you have your capital. You do not wrong, and you are not wronged.
280 And if there is someone in difficulty, then time is to be given until ease. And to give charity is better for you, if you know.
281 And guard yourselves against a day when you will be returned to God. Then every soul will be paid in full what it earned, and they will not be wronged.

## Write it down
282 You who believe, when you contract a debt with one another for a named term, write it down. And let a scribe write it down between you justly. And let no scribe refuse to write as God has taught him. So let him write, and let the one who owes dictate, and let him be mindful of God, his Lord, and not lessen it by anything. But if the one who owes is foolish or weak, or cannot dictate it himself, then let the one who acts for him dictate justly. And call two witnesses from among your men. And if there are not two men, then a man and two women, from those you are pleased with as witnesses, so that if one of the two errs, one can remind the other. And let the witnesses not refuse when they are called. And do not grow weary of writing it down, small or large, with its term. That is fairer in the sight of God, straighter for testimony, and nearer to your having no doubt. Except when it is trade on the spot that you pass around among yourselves: then there is no blame on you if you do not write it down. And call witnesses when you sell to one another. And let no scribe or witness be harmed [alt: And let no scribe or witness do harm]. And if you do, it is defiance on your part. And be mindful of God. And God teaches you, and God has knowledge of all things.
283 And if you are on a journey and do not find a scribe, then security taken in hand. But if one of you trusts another, let the one who is trusted hand back what he was trusted with, and let him be mindful of God, his Lord. And do not conceal testimony. And whoever conceals it, his heart is sinful. And God has knowledge of what you do.

## What is within yourselves
284 To God belongs what is in the heavens and what is on the earth. And if you disclose what is within yourselves or hide it, God will reckon with you for it. Then he forgives whomever he wills and punishes whomever he wills. And God has power over all things.

## We hear and we obey
285 The messenger believes in what has been sent down to him from his Lord, and so do the believers. Each believes in God, his angels, his Books and his messengers: "We make no distinction between any of his messengers." And they say, "We hear and we obey. Your forgiveness, our Lord! And to you is the destination."
286 God does not burden a soul beyond its capacity. It has what it earned, and against it is what it earned. "Our Lord, do not take us to task if we forget or make a mistake. Our Lord, and do not load on us a weight as you loaded it on those before us. Our Lord, and do not load us with what we have no strength to bear. And pardon us, and forgive us, and have mercy on us. You are our ally, so aid us against the disbelieving people."

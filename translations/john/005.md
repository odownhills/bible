## The man at the pool
1 After these things there was a festival [alt: the festival, as other manuscripts read] of the Jews [alt: the Judeans], and Jesus went up to Jerusalem.

2 Now there is in Jerusalem, by the Sheep Gate, a pool [alt: by the Sheep Pool, a place], which is called in Hebrew Bethzatha [alt: Bethesda, or Bethsaida, as other manuscripts read], having five colonnades.
3 In these lay a multitude of those who were sick—blind, lame, withered [alt: withered, waiting for the movement of the water, as other manuscripts read].
4 [alt: For an angel would go down from time to time into the pool and stir the water; so the first one who stepped in after the stirring of the water would become healthy, whatever disease he was held by, as other manuscripts read.]

5 Now there was a certain man there who had been in his sickness for thirty-eight years.
6 Jesus, seeing this one lying down and knowing that he has already been that way a long time, says to him, "Do you want to become healthy?"
7 The one who was sick answered him, "Sir [alt: Lord], I do not have a man to throw me [alt: put me] into the pool when the water is stirred; but while I myself am coming, another goes down before me."
8 Jesus says to him, "Rise, take up your mat, and walk."
9 And immediately the man became healthy, and he took up his mat and was walking. Now it was a Sabbath on that day.

## Healed on a Sabbath
10 So the Jews were saying to the one who had been cured, "It is a Sabbath, and it is not permitted for you to take up your mat."
11 But he answered them, "The one who made me healthy, that one said to me, 'Take up your mat and walk.'"
12 They questioned him, "Who is the man who said to you, 'Take it up and walk'?"
13 But the one who was healed did not know who it was, for Jesus had slipped away, as there was a crowd in the place.

14 After these things Jesus finds him in the temple and said to him, "Look, you have become healthy; sin no longer, so that something worse may not happen to you."
15 The man went away and declared to the Jews that Jesus was the one who had made him healthy.

16 And for this reason the Jews were persecuting Jesus, because he was doing these things on a Sabbath.
17 But Jesus answered them, "My Father is working until now, and I myself am working."
18 So for this reason the Jews were seeking all the more to kill him, because he was not only undoing the Sabbath [alt: breaking the Sabbath], but was also calling God his own Father, making himself equal to God.

## The Father and the Son
19 So Jesus answered and was saying to them, "Amen, amen [alt: Truly, truly], I say to you, the Son can do nothing from himself [alt: on his own], unless it is something he sees the Father doing; for whatever that one does, these things the Son also does likewise.
20 For the Father holds the Son dear [alt: loves the Son] and shows him all the things that he himself does, and he will show him greater works than these, so that you yourselves may be surprised.
21 For just as the Father raises the dead and makes them alive, in this way the Son also makes alive those whom he wants.
22 For neither does the Father judge anyone, but he has given all judgment to the Son,
23 so that everyone may honor the Son just as they honor the Father. The one who does not honor the Son does not honor the Father who sent him.

## Life and judgment
24 "Amen, amen, I say to you, the one who hears my word and believes the one who sent me has eternal life and does not come into judgment, but has crossed over from death into life.
25 Amen, amen, I say to you, an hour is coming, and it is now, when the dead will hear the voice of the Son of God, and those who have heard will live.
26 For just as the Father has life in himself, in this way he gave to the Son also to have life in himself.
27 And he gave him authority to do judgment, because he is Son of Man [alt: a son of man].

28 "Do not be surprised at this, because [alt: that] an hour is coming in which all who are in the tombs will hear his voice
29 and will come out, those who have done good things into a resurrection of life, but those who have practiced worthless things into a resurrection of judgment.
30 I myself can do nothing from myself; just as I hear, I judge, and my judgment is righteous [alt: just], because I do not seek my will but the will of the one who sent me.

## Testimony concerning Jesus
31 "If I myself testify concerning myself, my testimony is not true;
32 another is the one who testifies concerning me, and I know that the testimony that he testifies concerning me is true.
33 You yourselves have sent to John, and he has testified to the truth.
34 But I myself do not receive the testimony from a human being, but I say these things so that you yourselves may be saved.
35 That one was the lamp that was burning and shining, and you yourselves wanted to exult for an hour in his light.

36 "But the testimony that I myself have is greater than John's; for the works that the Father has given me so that I may make them complete, the works themselves that I am doing, testify concerning me that the Father has sent me.
37 And the Father who sent me, that one has testified concerning me. Neither have you ever heard his voice, nor have you seen his form,
38 and you do not have his word remaining in you, because the one whom that one sent, this one you yourselves do not believe.

39 "You search the scriptures [alt: Search the scriptures], because you yourselves think that in them you have eternal life; and those are the ones that testify concerning me;
40 and you do not want to come to me so that you may have life.

## Glory from one another
41 "I do not receive glory from human beings,
42 but I have come to know you, that you do not have the love of God in yourselves.
43 I myself have come in the name of my Father, and you do not receive me; if another comes in his own name, that one you will receive.
44 How can you yourselves believe, receiving glory from one another, and you do not seek the glory that is from the only God [alt: from the only one, as other manuscripts read]?

## Moses the accuser
45 "Do not think that I myself will accuse you to the Father; there is one who accuses you: Moses, in whom you yourselves have hoped.
46 For if you believed Moses, you would believe me; for that one wrote concerning me.
47 But if you do not believe that one's writings, how will you believe my words?"

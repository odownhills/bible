## A hidden screen
45 And when you read the Quran, we place between you and those who do not believe in the hereafter a hidden screen.
46 And we place veils over their hearts, so that they do not understand it, and in their ears a heaviness. And when you mention your Lord alone in the Quran, they turn their backs in aversion.
47 We know best what they listen with when they listen to you, and when they talk in private, when the wrongdoers say, "You are following only a bewitched man."
48 Look how they have given examples for you, so they have gone astray and cannot find a way.

## Raised up as a new creation
49 And they said, "When we are bones and crumbled remains, will we really be raised up as a new creation?"
50 Say, "Be stones or iron,
51 or some creation of the kind that is great in your chests." Then they will say, "Who will bring us back?" Say, "The one who brought you into being the first time." Then they will shake their heads at you and say, "When is it?" Say, "It may be that it is near.
52 On the day he calls you, you will answer with praise of him, and you will think that you stayed only a little while."

## Say what is best
53 And tell my servants to say what is best. Satan stirs up trouble among them. Satan is a clear enemy to man.
54 Your Lord knows you best. If he wills, he will have mercy on you, or if he wills, he will punish you. And we have not sent you as one in charge of them.
55 And your Lord knows best whoever is in the heavens and the earth. And we have certainly set some of the prophets above others, and we gave David a Scripture.

## Those they call on
56 Say, "Call on those you have claimed apart from him. They have no power to lift harm from you, or to divert it."
57 Those whom they call on are seeking the means of approach to their Lord, to see which of them is nearest [alt: even the nearest of them], and they hope for his mercy and fear his punishment. The punishment of your Lord is a thing to beware of.

## The signs are sent only to frighten
58 And there is no town that we will not destroy before the Day of Resurrection, or punish with a severe punishment. That is inscribed in the Book.
59 And nothing kept us from sending the signs except that the earlier peoples called them lies. And we gave Thamud the she-camel, giving sight, but they did wrong by her. And we send the signs only to frighten.
60 And when we said to you, "Your Lord has surrounded the people." And we made the vision that we showed you, and the cursed tree in the Quran, only a trial for the people. And we frighten them, but it only increases them in great excess.

## Iblis and the descendants of Adam
61 And when we said to the angels, "Bow down to Adam," they bowed down, except Iblis. He said, "Shall I bow down to one you created from clay?"
62 He said, "Do you see this one whom you have honored above me? If you delay me until the Day of Resurrection, I will certainly put a bridle on his descendants [alt: devour his descendants], all but a few."
63 He said, "Go. Whoever of them follows you, Hell is the repayment of you all, an ample repayment.
64 And unsettle whomever of them you can with your voice, and rally your horses and your men on foot against them, and be their partner in wealth and children, and make them promises." And Satan promises them nothing but delusion.
65 "As for my servants, you have no authority over them." And your Lord is enough as one to rely on.

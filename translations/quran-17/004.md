surah: 17

## On land and sea
66 Your Lord is the one who drives the ships for you on the sea, so that you may seek some of his bounty. He is merciful to you.
67 And when harm touches you at sea, those you call on are lost, except him. But when he brings you safely to land, you turn aside. And man is ungrateful.
68 Do you feel safe, then, that he will not make the side of the land sink with you, or send a storm of stones against you? Then you would find no one to rely on.
69 Or do you feel safe that he will not take you back into it another time, and send a shattering blast of wind against you, and drown you because you were ungrateful? Then you would find no one to follow it up for you against us.

70 And we have certainly honored the children of Adam, and carried them on land and sea, and provided them with good things, and set them far above many of those we have created.

## The book in the right hand
71 On the day we call every group of people with their leader [alt: with their record], whoever is given his book in his right hand, those will read their book, and they will not be wronged by so much as a thread.
72 And whoever is blind in this life will be blind in the hereafter, and further astray from the way.

## They almost tempted you away
73 And indeed they almost tempted you away from what we have revealed to you, so that you would invent against us something other than it. And then they would have taken you as a friend.
74 And had we not made you firm, you would almost have leaned toward them a little.
75 Then we would have made you taste double in life and double in death, and then you would have found no one to aid you against us.

76 And indeed they almost unsettled you from the land, to drive you out of it. And then they would have stayed after you only a little while.
77 That was the practice with those of our messengers we sent before you, and you will find no diverting of our practice.

## The prayer and the reading at dawn
78 Keep up the prayer from the declining of the sun until the dark of the night, and the reading at dawn. [In Arabic the word for "reading" here is "Quran".] The reading at dawn is witnessed.
79 And for part of the night, stay awake with it, as something extra for you. It may be that your Lord will raise you up to a praised standing.

80 And say, "My Lord, bring me in by a true entrance, and bring me out by a true exit, and give me from yourself an authority to aid me."
81 And say, "The truth has come, and falsehood has vanished. Falsehood is bound to vanish."

## A healing and a mercy
82 And we send down from the Quran what is a healing and a mercy for the believers, but it only increases the wrongdoers in loss.
83 And when we show favor to man, he turns aside and distances himself, and when evil touches him, he is in despair.
84 Say, "Each one acts according to his own manner, so your Lord knows best who is best guided on the way."

## They ask you about the spirit
85 And they ask you about the spirit. Say, "The spirit is from the command of my Lord [alt: is part of my Lord's matter], and you have been given only a little knowledge."
86 And if we willed, we would certainly take away what we have revealed to you, and then you would find no one to rely on for it against us,
87 except for a mercy from your Lord. His bounty to you is great.

## The like of this Quran
88 Say, "If humans and jinn came together to bring the like of this Quran, they would not bring the like of it, even if they backed one another."
89 And we have certainly put every kind of example in many ways for people in this Quran, but most people have refused anything but disbelief.

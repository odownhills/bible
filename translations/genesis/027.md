## Isaac sends Esau to hunt
1 And when Isaac was old and his eyes had grown too dim to see, he called Esau his older son and said to him, "My son!" And he said to him, "Here I am."
2 And he said, "Look now, I have grown old; I do not know the day of my death.
3 And now, please take up your gear, your quiver and your bow, and go out to the field and hunt game for me.
4 And make me tasty food, the kind I love, and bring it to me, and let me eat, so that my soul may bless you before I die."

## Rebekah's plan
5 Now Rebekah was listening when Isaac spoke to Esau his son. And Esau went to the field to hunt game and bring it.
6 And Rebekah said to Jacob her son, saying, "Look, I heard your father speaking to Esau your brother, saying,
7 'Bring me game and make me tasty food, and let me eat, and let me bless you before Yahweh before my death.'
8 And now, my son, listen to my voice, to what I am commanding you.
9 Please go to the flock and get me from there two good goat kids, and I will make them into tasty food for your father, the kind he loves.
10 And you will bring it to your father, and he will eat, so that he may bless you before his death."
11 And Jacob said to Rebekah his mother, "Look, Esau my brother is a hairy man, and I am a smooth man.
12 Perhaps my father will feel me, and I will be in his eyes like a mocker, and I will bring on myself a curse and not a blessing."
13 And his mother said to him, "On me be your curse, my son. Only listen to my voice, and go, get them for me."
14 And he went and got them and brought them to his mother, and his mother made tasty food, the kind his father loved.
15 And Rebekah took the best garments of Esau her older son, which were with her in the house, and she put them on Jacob her younger son.
16 And the skins of the goat kids she put on his hands and on the smooth part of his neck.
17 And she gave the tasty food and the bread that she had made into the hand of Jacob her son.

## Jacob takes the blessing
18 And he went in to his father and said, "My father!" And he said, "Here I am. Who are you, my son?"
19 And Jacob said to his father, "I am Esau your firstborn. I have done as you told me. Please get up, sit and eat some of my game, so that your soul may bless me."
20 And Isaac said to his son, "How is it that you were so quick to find it, my son?" And he said, "Because Yahweh your God made it happen before me."
21 And Isaac said to Jacob, "Please come close and let me feel you, my son, whether you are really my son Esau or not."
22 And Jacob came close to Isaac his father, and he felt him and said, "The voice is the voice of Jacob, but the hands are the hands of Esau."
23 And he did not recognize him, because his hands were hairy like the hands of Esau his brother, and he blessed him.
24 And he said, "You are really my son Esau?" And he said, "I am."
25 And he said, "Bring it close to me, and let me eat some of my son's game, so that my soul may bless you." And he brought it close to him, and he ate, and he brought him wine, and he drank.
26 And Isaac his father said to him, "Please come close and kiss me, my son."
27 And he came close and kissed him, and he smelled the scent of his garments and blessed him and said, "See, the scent of my son is like the scent of a field that Yahweh has blessed.
28 And may God give you of the dew of the sky and of the richness of the earth, and plenty of grain and new wine.
29 May peoples serve you, and peoples bow down to you. Be master over your brothers, and may your mother's sons bow down to you. Cursed are those who curse you, and blessed are those who bless you."

## Esau comes in from the hunt
30 And when Isaac had finished blessing Jacob, and Jacob had only just gone out from the presence of Isaac his father, Esau his brother came in from his hunting.
31 And he too made tasty food and brought it to his father, and he said to his father, "Let my father get up and eat some of his son's game, so that your soul may bless me."
32 And Isaac his father said to him, "Who are you?" And he said, "I am your son, your firstborn, Esau."
33 And Isaac trembled with an exceedingly great trembling, and he said, "Who then was it who hunted game and brought it to me, and I ate of it all before you came, and I blessed him? Yes, and blessed he will be."
34 When Esau heard the words of his father, he cried out with an exceedingly great and bitter cry, and he said to his father, "Bless me, me too, my father!"
35 And he said, "Your brother came with deceit and took your blessing."
36 And he said, "Is it because he was named Jacob that he has cheated me these two times? My birthright he took, and look, now he has taken my blessing." And he said, "Have you not set aside a blessing for me?" [In Hebrew "cheated" sounds like the name "Jacob," and "my birthright" sounds like "my blessing."]
37 And Isaac answered and said to Esau, "Look, I have made him master over you, and all his brothers I have given to him as servants, and with grain and new wine I have sustained him. And for you, then, what can I do, my son?"
38 And Esau said to his father, "Do you have only one blessing, my father? Bless me, me too, my father!" And Esau lifted his voice and wept.
39 And Isaac his father answered and said to him, "Look, away from the richness of the earth will be the place where you live, and away from the dew of the sky above. [alt: Look, of the richness of the earth will be the place where you live, and of the dew of the sky above.]
40 And by your sword you will live, and you will serve your brother. And when you grow restless [alt: when you roam free], you will break his yoke off your neck."

## Esau's grudge
41 And Esau bore a grudge against Jacob over the blessing with which his father had blessed him, and Esau said in his heart, "The days of mourning for my father are coming near, and I will kill Jacob my brother."
42 And the words of Esau her older son were told to Rebekah, and she sent and called Jacob her younger son and said to him, "Look, Esau your brother is comforting himself about you by planning to kill you.
43 And now, my son, listen to my voice: get up, flee to Laban my brother, to Haran,
44 and stay with him a few days, until your brother's wrath turns away,
45 until your brother's anger turns away from you and he forgets what you have done to him. Then I will send and take you from there. Why should I be bereaved of you both in one day?"
46 And Rebekah said to Isaac, "I am sick of my life because of the daughters of Heth. If Jacob takes a wife from the daughters of Heth like these, from the daughters of the land, why do I have life?"

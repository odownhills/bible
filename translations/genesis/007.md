## Yahweh sends Noah into the ark
1 And Yahweh said to Noah, "Go into the ark, you and all your household, for it is you I have seen to be righteous before me in this generation.
2 Of all the clean beasts you are to take for yourself seven of each [alt: seven pairs], a male and its mate, and of the beasts that are not clean, two, a male and its mate.
3 Also of the birds of the sky, seven of each, male and female, to keep offspring alive on the face of the whole earth.
4 For in seven more days I am going to make it rain on the earth for forty days and forty nights, and I will wipe every being that I have made off the face of the ground."
5 And Noah did according to all that Yahweh had commanded him.

## The flood comes
6 Now Noah was six hundred years old when the flood of water came on the earth.
7 And Noah went into the ark, and his sons and his wife and his sons' wives with him, because of the waters of the flood.
8 Of the clean beasts and of the beasts that are not clean, and of the birds and everything that creeps on the ground,
9 two by two they came to Noah into the ark, male and female, as God had commanded Noah.
10 And after the seven days the waters of the flood came on the earth.

11 In the six hundredth year of Noah's life, in the second month, on the seventeenth day of the month, on that day all the springs of the great deep burst open, and the windows of the sky were opened.
12 And the rain was on the earth for forty days and forty nights.

13 On that very day Noah went into the ark, and Shem and Ham and Japheth, the sons of Noah, and Noah's wife and the three wives of his sons with them,
14 they and all the wild animals according to their kinds, and all the livestock according to their kinds, and every creeping thing that creeps on the earth according to its kind, and all the birds according to their kinds, every bird of every wing.
15 And they came to Noah into the ark, two by two, of all flesh in which there was the spirit of life.
16 And those that came, came male and female of all flesh, as God had commanded him. And Yahweh shut him in.

## The waters prevail
17 And the flood was on the earth for forty days, and the waters increased and lifted the ark, and it rose up from the earth. [In Hebrew "increased" is the verb "multiply" of 1:22 and 1:28.]
18 And the waters prevailed and increased greatly on the earth, and the ark went over the face of the waters.
19 And the waters prevailed very greatly on the earth, and all the high mountains that were under the whole sky were covered.
20 Fifteen cubits above, the waters prevailed, and the mountains were covered.

21 And all flesh that moved on the earth breathed its last—the birds, the livestock, the wild animals, and every swarming thing that swarms on the earth—and all humankind.
22 Everything that had the breath of the spirit of life in its nostrils, of all that was on the dry ground, died.
23 And he wiped away every being that was on the face of the ground, from humankind to beasts, to creeping things, and to the birds of the sky, and they were wiped from the earth. And only Noah was left, and those with him in the ark.
24 And the waters prevailed on the earth for a hundred and fifty days.

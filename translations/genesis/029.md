## Jacob at the well
1 And Jacob lifted his feet and went to the land of the people of the east.
2 And he saw, and look, a well in the field, and look, three flocks of sheep were lying there beside it, for from that well the flocks were watered, and the stone on the mouth of the well was large.
3 And all the flocks would be gathered there, and they would roll the stone off the mouth of the well and water the sheep, and they would put the stone back on the mouth of the well, in its place.
4 And Jacob said to them, "My brothers, where are you from?" And they said, "We are from Haran."
5 And he said to them, "Do you know Laban the son of Nahor?" And they said, "We know him."
6 And he said to them, "Is it well with him?" And they said, "It is well, and look, Rachel his daughter is coming with the sheep."
7 And he said, "Look, the day is still long; it is not time for the herds to be gathered. Water the sheep and go, graze them."
8 And they said, "We cannot, until all the flocks are gathered and they roll the stone off the mouth of the well; then we water the sheep."
9 While he was still speaking with them, Rachel came with the sheep that were her father's, for she was a shepherd.
10 And when Jacob saw Rachel the daughter of Laban, his mother's brother, and the sheep of Laban, his mother's brother, Jacob came close and rolled the stone off the mouth of the well and watered the sheep of Laban, his mother's brother.
11 And Jacob kissed Rachel, and he lifted his voice and wept.
12 And Jacob told Rachel that he was her father's brother and that he was Rebekah's son, and she ran and told her father.
13 And when Laban heard the news of Jacob, his sister's son, he ran to meet him and embraced him and kissed him and brought him into his house. And he recounted all these things to Laban.
14 And Laban said to him, "Surely you are my bone and my flesh." And he stayed with him a full month.

## Jacob serves for Rachel
15 And Laban said to Jacob, "Is it because you are my brother that you should serve me for nothing? Tell me what your wages will be."
16 Now Laban had two daughters; the name of the older was Leah, and the name of the younger was Rachel.
17 And Leah's eyes were tender [alt: weak], but Rachel was beautiful in form and beautiful in appearance.
18 And Jacob loved Rachel, and he said, "I will serve you seven years for Rachel, your younger daughter."
19 And Laban said, "It is better that I give her to you than that I give her to another man. Stay with me."
20 And Jacob served seven years for Rachel, and they were in his eyes like a few days, because of his love for her.

## Leah in place of Rachel
21 And Jacob said to Laban, "Give me my wife, for my days are completed, and let me sleep with her."
22 And Laban gathered all the men of the place and held a feast.
23 And in the evening he took Leah his daughter and brought her to him, and he slept with her.
24 And Laban gave Zilpah his female slave to her, to Leah his daughter, as a female slave.
25 And in the morning, look, it was Leah. And he said to Laban, "What is this you have done to me? Was it not for Rachel that I served with you? And why have you deceived me?"
26 And Laban said, "It is not done so in our place, to give the younger before the firstborn.
27 Complete this one's week, and we will give you that one too, for the service that you will do with me for another seven years."
28 And Jacob did so and completed this one's week, and he gave him Rachel his daughter as his wife.
29 And Laban gave Bilhah his female slave to Rachel his daughter, as her female slave.
30 And he slept with Rachel too, and he loved Rachel too, more than Leah, and he served with him another seven years.

## Leah's sons
31 And Yahweh saw that Leah was hated, and he opened her womb, but Rachel was barren.
32 And Leah conceived and gave birth to a son, and she named him Reuben, for she said, "Yahweh has seen my affliction, for my husband will now love me." [In Hebrew the name "Reuben" sounds like "See, a son," and like the words for "has seen my affliction."]
33 And she conceived again and gave birth to a son, and she said, "Because Yahweh heard that I am hated, he has given me this one too." And she named him Simeon. [In Hebrew the name "Simeon" sounds like the word for "heard."]
34 And she conceived again and gave birth to a son, and she said, "Now, this time, my husband will be joined to me, because I have borne him three sons." That is why he was named Levi. [In Hebrew the name "Levi" sounds like the word for "be joined."]
35 And she conceived again and gave birth to a son, and she said, "This time I will praise Yahweh." That is why she named him Judah. [In Hebrew the name "Judah" sounds like the word for "praise."] And she stopped bearing children.

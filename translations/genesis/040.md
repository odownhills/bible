## Pharaoh's two officials in custody
1 And after these things the cupbearer of the king of Egypt and the baker sinned against their master, the king of Egypt.
2 And Pharaoh was furious with his two court officials, with the chief cupbearer and with the chief baker.
3 And he put them in custody in the house of the commander of the guard, into the prison, the place where Joseph was confined.
4 And the commander of the guard assigned Joseph to them, and he attended them. And they were in custody for some time.

## Two dreams in one night
5 And the two of them dreamed a dream, each his own dream, in one night, each dream with its own interpretation—the cupbearer and the baker of the king of Egypt, who were confined in the prison.
6 And Joseph came to them in the morning and saw them, and look, they were troubled.
7 And he asked Pharaoh's court officials who were with him in custody in his master's house, saying, "Why are your faces sad today?"
8 And they said to him, "We have dreamed a dream, and there is no one to interpret it." And Joseph said to them, "Do interpretations not belong to God? Please recount it to me."

## The cupbearer's dream
9 And the chief cupbearer recounted his dream to Joseph and said to him, "In my dream, look, there was a vine before me,
10 and on the vine were three branches, and as it budded, its blossom came up; its clusters ripened into grapes.
11 And Pharaoh's cup was in my hand, and I took the grapes and squeezed them into Pharaoh's cup, and I put the cup on Pharaoh's palm."
12 And Joseph said to him, "This is its interpretation: the three branches are three days.
13 In three more days Pharaoh will lift your head and restore you to your post, and you will give Pharaoh's cup into his hand according to the former practice, when you were his cupbearer.
14 Only keep me in your memory when it goes well with you, and please show loyalty to me, and mention me to Pharaoh, and bring me out of this house. [In Hebrew "keep me in your memory" and "mention me" are two forms of the verb "remember."]
15 For I was certainly stolen from the land of the Hebrews, and here too I have not done anything that they should have put me in the pit."

## The baker's dream
16 And the chief baker saw that the interpretation was good, and he said to Joseph, "I too—in my dream, look, there were three baskets of white bread [alt: three open-weave baskets] on my head,
17 and in the top basket was some of every food of Pharaoh, the work of a baker, and the birds were eating them out of the basket, from off my head."
18 And Joseph answered and said, "This is its interpretation: the three baskets are three days.
19 In three more days Pharaoh will lift your head from off you and hang you on a tree, and the birds will eat your flesh from off you."

## The third day
20 And on the third day, Pharaoh's birthday, he held a feast for all his servants, and he lifted the head of the chief cupbearer and the head of the chief baker among his servants.
21 And he restored the chief cupbearer to his cupbearing, and he put the cup on Pharaoh's palm.
22 But the chief baker he hanged, as Joseph had interpreted to them.
23 But the chief cupbearer did not remember Joseph, and he forgot him.

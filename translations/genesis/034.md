## Shechem and Dinah
1 And Dinah the daughter of Leah, whom she had borne to Jacob, went out to see the daughters of the land.
2 And Shechem the son of Hamor the Hivite, the chieftain of the land, saw her, and he took her and lay with her and violated her.
3 And his soul clung to Dinah the daughter of Jacob, and he loved the young woman, and he spoke to the heart of the young woman.
4 And Shechem said to Hamor his father, saying, "Take this girl for me as a wife."

## Hamor and Shechem ask for Dinah
5 Now Jacob heard that he had defiled Dinah his daughter, and his sons were with his herds in the field, and Jacob kept silent until they came.
6 And Hamor the father of Shechem went out to Jacob to speak with him.
7 And the sons of Jacob came in from the field when they heard, and the men were pained [alt: came in from the field. When they heard, the men were pained], and they were very angry, because he had done an outrage in Israel [alt: against Israel] by lying with the daughter of Jacob, and such a thing is not done.
8 And Hamor spoke with them, saying, "Shechem my son, his soul is set on your daughter. Please give her to him as a wife.
9 And intermarry with us: your daughters you will give to us, and our daughters you will take for yourselves.
10 And you will live with us, and the land will be before you. Live and trade in it [alt: move about in it], and take holdings in it."
11 And Shechem said to her father and to her brothers, "Let me find favor in your eyes, and whatever you say to me I will give.
12 Put on me a very great bride price and gift, and I will give just as you say to me; and give me the young woman as a wife."

## The sons of Jacob answer with deceit
13 And the sons of Jacob answered Shechem and Hamor his father with deceit and spoke, because he had defiled Dinah their sister.
14 And they said to them, "We cannot do this thing, to give our sister to a man who has a foreskin, for that is a disgrace to us.
15 Only on this condition will we consent to you: if you will be like us, in that every male among you is circumcised.
16 Then we will give our daughters to you, and your daughters we will take for ourselves, and we will live with you, and we will become one people.
17 But if you will not listen to us and be circumcised, then we will take our daughter and go."

## The men of the city are circumcised
18 And their words were good in the eyes of Hamor and in the eyes of Shechem the son of Hamor.
19 And the young man did not delay to do the thing, because he delighted in the daughter of Jacob. Now he was the most honored of all his father's house.
20 And Hamor and Shechem his son came to the gate of their city, and they spoke to the men of their city, saying,
21 "These men are at peace with us. So let them live in the land and trade in it, and the land, look, is broad on every side before them. Their daughters we will take for ourselves as wives, and our daughters we will give to them. [In Hebrew "at peace" is the word for "safely" in 33:18.]
22 Only on this condition will the men consent to us, to live with us, to become one people: that every male among us be circumcised, as they are circumcised.
23 Their herds and what they have acquired and all their livestock—will they not be ours? Only let us consent to them, and they will live with us."
24 And all who went out of the gate of his city listened to Hamor and to Shechem his son, and every male was circumcised, all who went out of the gate of his city.

## Simeon and Levi take the city
25 And on the third day, when they were in pain, two of the sons of Jacob, Simeon and Levi, the brothers of Dinah, each took his sword, and they came against the city in security [alt: came against the city while it felt secure], and they killed every male.
26 And Hamor and Shechem his son they killed with the edge of the sword, and they took Dinah from the house of Shechem and went out.
27 The sons of Jacob came upon the slain, and they plundered the city, because they had defiled their sister.
28 Their flocks and their cattle and their donkeys, and what was in the city and what was in the field, they took.
29 And all their wealth and all their little ones and their wives they took captive and plundered, and everything that was in the houses.
30 And Jacob said to Simeon and to Levi, "You have brought trouble on me by making me stink among those who live in the land, among the Canaanites and the Perizzites. And as for me, I am few in number, and they will gather against me and strike me down, and I will be destroyed, I and my household."
31 And they said, "Should he treat our sister like a prostitute?"

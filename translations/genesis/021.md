## Isaac is born
1 Now Yahweh attended to Sarah as he had said, and Yahweh did for Sarah as he had spoken.
2 And Sarah conceived and bore a son to Abraham in his old age, at the set time that God had spoken of.
3 And Abraham named his son who had been born to him, whom Sarah had borne to him, Isaac.
4 And Abraham circumcised Isaac his son at eight days old, as God had commanded him.
5 Now Abraham was a hundred years old when Isaac his son was born to him.
6 And Sarah said, "Laughter God has made for me; everyone who hears will laugh with me [alt: will laugh at me]." [In Hebrew "laughter" and "will laugh" sound like the name "Isaac."]
7 And she said, "Who would have said to Abraham, 'Sarah has nursed children'? For I have borne a son in his old age."

## Sarah has Hagar and her son sent out
8 And the child grew and was weaned, and Abraham held a great feast on the day Isaac was weaned.
9 And Sarah saw the son of Hagar the Egyptian, whom she had borne to Abraham, laughing [alt: mocking].
10 And she said to Abraham, "Drive out this female servant and her son, for the son of this female servant will not be heir with my son, with Isaac."
11 And the thing was very bad in Abraham's eyes, on account of his son.
12 And God said to Abraham, "Do not let it be bad in your eyes over the boy and over your female servant. In all that Sarah says to you, listen to her voice, for in Isaac offspring will be called yours.
13 And the son of the female servant too I will make into a nation, for he is your offspring."
14 And Abraham rose early in the morning and took bread and a skin of water and gave them to Hagar, putting them on her shoulder, and he gave her the child [alt: putting them on her shoulder, and the child too] and sent her out. And she went and wandered in the wilderness of Beersheba.

## God hears the boy in the wilderness
15 And the water in the skin ran out, and she threw the child under one of the shrubs.
16 And she went and sat down opposite him, far off, about a bowshot away, for she said, "Let me not look on the death of the child." And she sat opposite him and lifted her voice and wept [alt: and the child cried out and wept, as the old Greek has it].
17 And God heard the voice of the boy, and the angel of God called to Hagar from the heavens and said to her, "What is the matter, Hagar? Do not be afraid, for God has heard the voice of the boy where he is. [In Hebrew "God has heard" echoes the boy's name, "Ishmael" (16:11).]
18 Rise, lift up the boy and hold him fast with your hand, for I will make him into a great nation."
19 And God opened her eyes, and she saw a well of water, and she went and filled the skin with water and gave the boy a drink.
20 And God was with the boy, and he grew, and he lived in the wilderness and became an archer, a bowman.
21 And he lived in the wilderness of Paran, and his mother took a wife for him from the land of Egypt.

## The oath at Beersheba
22 And at that time Abimelech and Phicol the commander of his army said to Abraham, saying, "God is with you in all that you do.
23 And now, swear to me here by God that you will not deal falsely with me or with my progeny or with my posterity. The same loyalty that I have shown you, you are to show me and the land in which you have stayed."
24 And Abraham said, "I myself will swear."
25 And Abraham rebuked Abimelech over the well of water that Abimelech's servants had seized.
26 And Abimelech said, "I do not know who did this thing. Neither did you tell me, nor have I heard of it, except today."
27 And Abraham took flocks and cattle and gave them to Abimelech, and the two of them made a covenant.
28 And Abraham set seven ewe lambs of the flock by themselves.
29 And Abimelech said to Abraham, "What are these seven ewe lambs that you have set by themselves?"
30 And he said, "The seven ewe lambs you are to take from my hand, so that it may be a witness for me that I dug this well."
31 That is why that place was named Beersheba, for there the two of them swore. [In Hebrew the name "Beersheba" can be heard as "well of seven" and as "well of the oath"; "seven" and "swore" sound alike.]
32 And they made a covenant at Beersheba, and Abimelech rose, and Phicol the commander of his army, and they returned to the land of the Philistines.
33 And he planted a tamarisk at Beersheba, and there he called on the name of Yahweh, the Everlasting God.
34 And Abraham stayed in the land of the Philistines many days.

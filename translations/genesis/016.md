## Sarai gives Hagar to Abram
1 Now Sarai, Abram's wife, had borne him no children, and she had an Egyptian female slave, and her name was Hagar.
2 And Sarai said to Abram, "Look now, Yahweh has held me back from bearing children. Please sleep with my female slave; perhaps I will be built up from her." [In Hebrew "be built up" sounds like the word for "son."] And Abram listened to the voice of Sarai.
3 And Sarai, Abram's wife, took Hagar the Egyptian, her female slave, at the end of ten years of Abram's living in the land of Canaan, and she gave her to Abram her husband as his wife.
4 And he slept with Hagar, and she conceived. And she saw that she had conceived, and her mistress counted for little in her eyes.
5 And Sarai said to Abram, "The violence done to me is on you. I myself gave my female slave into your arms, and she saw that she had conceived, and I counted for little in her eyes. May Yahweh judge between me and you."
6 And Abram said to Sarai, "Look, your female slave is in your hand. Do to her what is good in your eyes." And Sarai afflicted her, and she fled from her.

## The angel of Yahweh finds Hagar
7 And the angel of Yahweh found her by the spring of water in the wilderness, by the spring on the way to Shur.
8 And he said, "Hagar, female slave of Sarai, from where have you come, and where are you going?" And she said, "From Sarai my mistress I am fleeing."
9 And the angel of Yahweh said to her, "Return to your mistress, and let yourself be afflicted under her hands [alt: and humble yourself under her hands]."
10 And the angel of Yahweh said to her, "I will greatly multiply your offspring, and they will be too many to count."
11 And the angel of Yahweh said to her, "Look, you are pregnant, and you will give birth to a son, and you will name him Ishmael, for Yahweh has heard your affliction. [In Hebrew the name "Ishmael" means "God hears."]
12 And he will be a wild donkey of a man, his hand against everyone and everyone's hand against him, and he will live in the presence of all his brothers [alt: in defiance of all his brothers]."
13 And she named Yahweh, who was speaking to her, "You are a God of seeing," for she said, "Have I even here seen the back of the one who sees me?" [alt: Have I even here gone on seeing, after the one who sees me?]
14 That is why the well was named Beer-Lahai-Roi; look, it is between Kadesh and Bered. [In Hebrew the name means "the well of the living one who sees me."]

## Ishmael is born
15 And Hagar bore a son to Abram, and Abram named his son, whom Hagar had borne, Ishmael.
16 Now Abram was eighty-six years old when Hagar bore Ishmael to Abram.

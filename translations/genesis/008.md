book: Genesis

## God remembers Noah
1 And God remembered Noah and all the wild animals and all the livestock that were with him in the ark, and God made a wind pass over the earth, and the waters subsided. [In Hebrew "wind" is the same word as "spirit" in 1:2.]
2 And the springs of the deep and the windows of the sky were stopped up, and the rain from the sky was withheld.
3 And the waters returned steadily from the earth, and the waters decreased at the end of a hundred and fifty days.

4 And the ark came to rest in the seventh month, on the seventeenth day of the month, on the mountains of Ararat. [In Hebrew "came to rest" sounds like the name "Noah," and so do "resting place" in verse 9 and "soothing" in verse 21.]
5 And the waters decreased steadily until the tenth month. In the tenth month, on the first day of the month, the tops of the mountains appeared.

## The raven and the dove
6 And at the end of forty days Noah opened the window of the ark that he had made.
7 And he sent out the raven, and it went out, going out and returning, until the waters had dried up from the earth.

8 And he sent out the dove from him, to see whether the waters had gone down from the face of the ground.
9 But the dove found no resting place for the sole of its foot, and it returned to him, to the ark, for there was water on the face of the whole earth. And he reached out his hand and took it and brought it to him into the ark.

10 And he waited another seven days, and again he sent out the dove from the ark.
11 And the dove came to him at evening time, and look, there was a freshly plucked olive leaf in its mouth. And Noah knew that the waters had gone down from the earth.
12 And he waited another seven days and sent out the dove, and it did not return to him again.

## The earth is dry
13 And in the six hundred and first year, in the first month, on the first day of the month, the waters were drained from the earth. And Noah removed the covering of the ark, and he saw, and look, the face of the ground was drained.
14 And in the second month, on the twenty-seventh day of the month, the earth was dry.

## God sends Noah out of the ark
15 And God spoke to Noah, saying,
16 "Go out of the ark, you and your wife and your sons and your sons' wives with you.
17 Every living thing that is with you, of all flesh—the birds, the beasts, and every creeping thing that creeps on the earth—bring out with you, and let them swarm on the earth and be fruitful and multiply on the earth."

18 And Noah went out, and his sons and his wife and his sons' wives with him.
19 Every living thing, every creeping thing and every bird, everything that moves on the earth, went out of the ark according to their families.

## Noah's offering
20 And Noah built an altar to Yahweh, and he took some of all the clean beasts and some of all the clean birds, and offered up burnt offerings on the altar.
21 And Yahweh smelled the soothing aroma, and Yahweh said to his heart, "I will never again bring a curse on the ground because of humankind, for [alt: though] what is formed in the heart of humankind is evil from their youth, and I will never again strike down every living thing as I have done. [In Hebrew "bring a curse on" is a different verb from "cursed" in 3:17, and it is from the same root as "gone down" in verses 8 and 11.]
22 While all the days of the earth last, seedtime and harvest, and cold and heat, and summer and winter, and day and night will not cease."

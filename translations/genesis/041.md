## Pharaoh's two dreams
1 And at the end of two full years Pharaoh was dreaming, and look, he was standing by the Nile.
2 And look, coming up from the Nile were seven cows, beautiful in appearance and fat in flesh, and they grazed in the reed grass.
3 And look, seven other cows were coming up after them from the Nile, bad in appearance and thin in flesh, and they stood beside the cows on the bank of the Nile.
4 And the cows that were bad in appearance and thin in flesh ate the seven cows that were beautiful in appearance and fat. And Pharaoh awoke.
5 And he slept and dreamed a second time, and look, seven ears of grain were coming up on one stalk, fat and good.
6 And look, seven ears, thin and scorched by the east wind, were sprouting after them.
7 And the thin ears swallowed the seven fat and full ears. And Pharaoh awoke, and look, it was a dream.
8 And in the morning his spirit was disturbed, and he sent and called all the magicians of Egypt and all its wise men, and Pharaoh recounted his dream to them, and there was no one to interpret them for Pharaoh.

## The chief cupbearer speaks
9 And the chief cupbearer spoke with Pharaoh, saying, "My sins I am mentioning today.
10 Pharaoh was furious with his servants, and he put me in custody in the house of the commander of the guard, me and the chief baker.
11 And we dreamed a dream in one night, I and he; we dreamed, each dream with its own interpretation.
12 And there with us was a young Hebrew man, a slave of the commander of the guard, and we recounted them to him, and he interpreted our dreams for us; for each according to his dream he interpreted.
13 And as he interpreted for us, so it was: me he restored to my post, and him he hanged."

## Joseph before Pharaoh
14 And Pharaoh sent and called Joseph, and they rushed him out of the pit, and he shaved and changed his clothes and came to Pharaoh.
15 And Pharaoh said to Joseph, "I have dreamed a dream, and there is no one to interpret it. And I have heard it said of you that you can hear a dream and interpret it."
16 And Joseph answered Pharaoh, saying, "Not I; God will give an answer for Pharaoh's welfare."
17 And Pharaoh spoke to Joseph, "In my dream, look, I was standing on the bank of the Nile.
18 And look, coming up from the Nile were seven cows, fat in flesh and beautiful in form, and they grazed in the reed grass.
19 And look, seven other cows were coming up after them, poor and very bad in form and lean in flesh; I have not seen any like them in all the land of Egypt for badness.
20 And the lean and bad cows ate the first seven cows, the fat ones.
21 And they went inside them, but it could not be known that they had gone inside them, and their appearance was bad, as at the beginning. And I awoke.
22 And I saw in my dream, and look, seven ears were coming up on one stalk, full and good.
23 And look, seven ears, shriveled, thin, scorched by the east wind, were sprouting after them.
24 And the thin ears swallowed the seven good ears. And I said it to the magicians, and there was no one to tell me."

## Joseph interprets the dreams
25 And Joseph said to Pharaoh, "Pharaoh's dream is one. What God is going to do he has told Pharaoh.
26 The seven good cows are seven years, and the seven good ears are seven years; it is one dream.
27 And the seven lean and bad cows that came up after them are seven years, and the seven empty ears, scorched by the east wind, will be seven years of famine.
28 That is the word that I have spoken to Pharaoh: what God is going to do he has shown Pharaoh.
29 Look, seven years are coming of great plenty in all the land of Egypt.
30 And seven years of famine will arise after them, and all the plenty will be forgotten in the land of Egypt, and the famine will make an end of the land.
31 And the plenty will not be known in the land because of that famine afterward, for it will be very severe.
32 And as for the dream being repeated to Pharaoh twice, it is because the matter is established by God, and God is hurrying to do it.
33 And now let Pharaoh look for a discerning and wise man and set him over the land of Egypt.
34 Let Pharaoh act and put overseers in charge of the land, and let him take a fifth from the land of Egypt [alt: and let him make the land of Egypt ready] in the seven years of plenty.
35 And let them gather all the food of these good years that are coming, and let them heap up grain under Pharaoh's hand as food in the cities and keep it.
36 And the food will be a reserve for the land for the seven years of famine that will be in the land of Egypt, and the land will not be cut off by the famine."

## Joseph set over Egypt
37 And the matter was good in the eyes of Pharaoh and in the eyes of all his servants.
38 And Pharaoh said to his servants, "Can we find anyone like this, a man in whom is the spirit of God [alt: a spirit of gods]?"
39 And Pharaoh said to Joseph, "Since God has made all this known to you, there is no one as discerning and wise as you.
40 You will be over my house, and all my people will submit to your word [alt: will arm themselves at your word]; only as to the throne will I be greater than you."
41 And Pharaoh said to Joseph, "See, I have set you over all the land of Egypt."
42 And Pharaoh removed his signet ring from his hand and put it on Joseph's hand, and he clothed him in garments of fine linen and put the gold chain on his neck.
43 And he had him ride in his second chariot, and they called out before him, "Kneel!" [alt: an Egyptian word whose meaning is not given] And so he set him over all the land of Egypt.
44 And Pharaoh said to Joseph, "I am Pharaoh, and without you no man will raise his hand or his foot in all the land of Egypt."
45 And Pharaoh named Joseph Zaphenath-Paneah, and he gave him Asenath the daughter of Potiphera, priest of On, as his wife. And Joseph went out over the land of Egypt.
46 Now Joseph was thirty years old when he stood before Pharaoh king of Egypt. And Joseph went out from the presence of Pharaoh and passed through all the land of Egypt.

## The seven years of plenty
47 And in the seven years of plenty the land produced by the handful.
48 And he gathered all the food of the seven years that were in the land of Egypt and put food in the cities; the food of the fields around each city he put inside it.
49 And Joseph heaped up grain like the sand of the sea, very much, until he stopped counting, for it was beyond number.
50 And to Joseph two sons were born before the year of famine came, whom Asenath the daughter of Potiphera, priest of On, bore to him.
51 And Joseph named the firstborn Manasseh, "because God has made me forget all my misery and all my father's house." [In Hebrew the name "Manasseh" sounds like the word for "made me forget."]
52 And the second he named Ephraim, "because God has made me fruitful in the land of my affliction." [In Hebrew the name "Ephraim" sounds like the word for "made me fruitful."]

## The famine begins
53 And the seven years of the plenty that was in the land of Egypt came to an end.
54 And the seven years of famine began to come, as Joseph had said. And there was famine in all the lands, but in all the land of Egypt there was bread.
55 And all the land of Egypt was hungry, and the people cried out to Pharaoh for bread. And Pharaoh said to all Egypt, "Go to Joseph; what he says to you, you are to do."
56 Now the famine was over all the face of the earth. And Joseph opened all that had grain in them and sold grain to Egypt, and the famine grew strong in the land of Egypt.
57 And the people of the whole earth came to Egypt, to Joseph, to buy grain, for the famine was strong in the whole earth.

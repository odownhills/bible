## Jacob is embalmed and mourned
1 And Joseph fell on his father's face and wept on him and kissed him.
2 And Joseph commanded his servants the physicians to embalm his father, and the physicians embalmed Israel.
3 And forty days were completed for him, for so the days of embalming are completed, and Egypt wept for him seventy days.

## Joseph buries his father in Canaan
4 And the days of weeping for him passed, and Joseph spoke to Pharaoh's house, saying, "If I have found favor in your eyes, please speak in the ears of Pharaoh, saying,
5 'My father made me swear, saying, "Look, I am about to die. In my grave that I dug out for myself [alt: that I bought for myself] in the land of Canaan, there you are to bury me." And now, please let me go up and bury my father, and I will return.'"
6 And Pharaoh said, "Go up and bury your father, as he made you swear."
7 And Joseph went up to bury his father, and with him went up all the servants of Pharaoh, the elders of his household, and all the elders of the land of Egypt,
8 and all Joseph's household, and his brothers, and his father's house. Only their little ones and their flocks and their cattle they left in the land of Goshen.
9 And both chariots and horsemen went up with him, and the camp was very large.
10 And they came as far as the threshing floor of Atad, which is across the Jordan, and there they lamented with a great and very heavy lament, and he held a mourning of seven days for his father.
11 And those who lived in the land, the Canaanites, saw the mourning at the threshing floor of Atad, and they said, "This is a heavy mourning for Egypt." That is why it was named Abel-Mizraim, which is across the Jordan. [In Hebrew the name "Abel-Mizraim" sounds like the words for "mourning of Egypt."]
12 And his sons did for him just as he had commanded them.
13 And his sons carried him to the land of Canaan and buried him in the cave of the field of Machpelah, the field that Abraham had bought as a holding for a grave from Ephron the Hittite, facing Mamre.
14 And Joseph returned to Egypt, he and his brothers and all who had gone up with him to bury his father, after he had buried his father.

## Joseph reassures his brothers
15 And Joseph's brothers saw that their father was dead, and they said, "What if Joseph bears a grudge against us and really pays back to us all the evil that we dealt him?"
16 And they sent word to Joseph, saying, "Your father commanded before his death, saying,
17 'This is what you are to say to Joseph: "Oh, please forgive the offense of your brothers and their sin, for they dealt you evil."' And now, please forgive the offense of the servants of the God of your father." And Joseph wept when they spoke to him.
18 And his brothers also went and fell before him, and they said, "Look, we are your slaves."
19 And Joseph said to them, "Do not be afraid, for am I in the place of God?
20 And as for you, you planned evil against me; God planned it for good, in order to do as it is this day, to keep many people alive.
21 And now, do not be afraid. I myself will provide for you and your little ones." And he comforted them and spoke to their hearts.

## The death of Joseph
22 And Joseph lived in Egypt, he and his father's house, and Joseph lived a hundred and ten years.
23 And Joseph saw Ephraim's sons of the third generation. Also the sons of Machir the son of Manasseh were born on Joseph's knees.
24 And Joseph said to his brothers, "I am going to die, and God will certainly attend to you and bring you up from this land to the land that he swore to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob."
25 And Joseph made the sons of Israel swear, saying, "God will certainly attend to you, and you are to bring up my bones from here."
26 And Joseph died, a hundred and ten years old, and they embalmed him, and he was put in a coffin in Egypt.

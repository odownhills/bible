## Joseph and his brothers
1 And Jacob lived in the land where his father had stayed, in the land of Canaan.
2 These are the generations of Jacob. Joseph, who was seventeen years old, was shepherding the flock with his brothers, and he was a boy with the sons of Bilhah and the sons of Zilpah, his father's wives. And Joseph brought a bad report about them to their father.
3 Now Israel loved Joseph more than all his sons, because he was a son of his old age, and he made him a long tunic [alt: a tunic of many colors, as the old Greek has it].
4 And his brothers saw that he was the one their father loved more than all his brothers, and they hated him, and they could not speak peaceably to him.

## Joseph's dreams
5 And Joseph dreamed a dream, and he told his brothers, and they hated him still more.
6 And he said to them, "Please listen to this dream that I have dreamed.
7 Look, we were binding sheaves in the middle of the field, and look, my sheaf rose and also stood upright, and look, your sheaves gathered around and bowed down to my sheaf."
8 And his brothers said to him, "Will you really reign over us? Or will you really govern us?" And they hated him still more, because of his dreams and because of his words.
9 And he dreamed yet another dream, and he recounted it to his brothers and said, "Look, I have dreamed a dream again, and look, the sun and the moon and eleven stars were bowing down to me."
10 And he recounted it to his father and to his brothers, and his father rebuked him and said to him, "What is this dream that you have dreamed? Are we really to come, I and your mother and your brothers, to bow down to the ground to you?"
11 And his brothers envied him, but his father kept the matter in mind.

## Joseph is sent to his brothers
12 And his brothers went to graze their father's flock at Shechem.
13 And Israel said to Joseph, "Are your brothers not shepherding at Shechem? Come, and I will send you to them." And he said to him, "Here I am."
14 And he said to him, "Please go and see whether it is well with your brothers and whether it is well with the flock, and bring me back word." And he sent him from the valley of Hebron, and he came to Shechem.
15 And a man found him, and look, he was wandering in the field, and the man asked him, saying, "What are you looking for?"
16 And he said, "It is my brothers I am looking for. Please tell me where they are shepherding."
17 And the man said, "They have set out from here, for I heard them saying, 'Let us go to Dothan.'" And Joseph went after his brothers and found them at Dothan.

## The brothers plot against Joseph
18 And they saw him from far off, and before he came near to them they plotted against him, to put him to death.
19 And they said to one another, "Look, here comes that dreamer.
20 And now, come, let us kill him and throw him into one of the pits, and we will say, 'A savage animal has eaten him.' And we will see what becomes of his dreams."
21 And Reuben heard, and he rescued him from their hand and said, "We must not take his life."
22 And Reuben said to them, "Do not shed blood. Throw him into this pit that is in the wilderness, but do not reach out a hand against him"—so that he might rescue him from their hand, to bring him back to his father.
23 And when Joseph came to his brothers, they stripped Joseph of his tunic, the long tunic that was on him.
24 And they took him and threw him into the pit. Now the pit was empty; there was no water in it.

## Joseph is sold
25 And they sat down to eat bread, and they lifted their eyes and saw, and look, a caravan of Ishmaelites was coming from Gilead, and their camels were carrying gum and balm and resin, on their way to take them down to Egypt.
26 And Judah said to his brothers, "What profit is there if we kill our brother and cover his blood?
27 Come, let us sell him to the Ishmaelites, and let our hand not be against him, for he is our brother, our flesh." And his brothers listened.
28 And Midianite men, merchants, passed by, and they pulled Joseph up and lifted him out of the pit, and they sold Joseph to the Ishmaelites for twenty pieces of silver, and they brought Joseph to Egypt.
29 And Reuben returned to the pit, and look, Joseph was not in the pit, and he tore his garments.
30 And he returned to his brothers and said, "The child is gone! And I, where am I to go?"

## Jacob mourns for his son
31 And they took Joseph's tunic and slaughtered a male goat and dipped the tunic in the blood.
32 And they sent the long tunic and brought it to their father and said, "We found this. Recognize it, please: is it your son's tunic or not?"
33 And he recognized it and said, "My son's tunic! A savage animal has eaten him. Joseph has certainly been torn to pieces."
34 And Jacob tore his clothes and put sackcloth on his waist, and he mourned for his son many days.
35 And all his sons and all his daughters rose to comfort him, but he refused to be comforted, and he said, "I will go down to my son mourning, to Sheol." And his father wept for him.
36 Now the Medanites [alt: the Midianites, as the old Greek and other ancient versions have it] sold him into Egypt, to Potiphar, a court official of Pharaoh [alt: a eunuch of Pharaoh], the commander of the guard.

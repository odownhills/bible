# Glossary of settled renderings

Recurring Hebrew, Aramaic and Greek terms and the default English chosen for them, so every
chapter stays consistent. Read this before translating. Use the listed rendering wherever it fits;
where another wording fits a sentence better, use it, with no bracket (TRANSLATION_RULES.md,
"Alternate readings and wording choices").
When a chapter settles a new recurring term, add one line here in the same commit.
(Transliteration is fine in this file. It is never allowed in the chapter files.)

Since 2026-11-21 a bracket is written only for a real alternate, a place where the original can be
read in two ways that say different things. Many lines below still list an `[alt: ...]` that is
only a second English wording (for example "righteous" [alt: just], "stay" [alt: remain]): do not
write those brackets in new chapters. Chapters published before 2026-11-21 keep their wording and
brackets: Obay decided not to redo them. Do not add "For Obay to decide" lists; the ones below are
closed. Close calls go in the chapter's decision log instead.

## Decided by Obay
- nephesh chayyah: "living creature" everywhere (Gen 1:20, 21, 24; 2:7, 19). Where the phrase is
  not a countable creature (Gen 1:30, "in which there is nephesh chayyah"), write
  "the life of a living creature [alt: the breath of life]". Decided 2026-11-19.
- nishmat chayyim (Gen 2:7): "the breath of life". This is a different phrase from nephesh chayyah;
  keep the two distinct.
- 2026-11-21: the rules for alternates and wording in TRANSLATION_RULES.md ("Alternate readings and
  wording choices") are Obay's. His picks that day, which override any older line below for every
  chapter translated from then on (published chapters are not redone):
  - yada, of a man and his wife (Gen 4:1, 17, 25): "was intimate with", no bracket.
  - skenoo (John 1:14): "lived", no bracket.
  - thaumazein prosopa (Jude 16): "show favoritism to people", no bracket.
  - baptizo: "baptize", no bracket.
  - paradidomi, of the one who gave Jesus up (John 6:64, 71): "betray", no bracket ("both work").
- Luke 17:21 entos hymon: "within you" [alt: in your midst]. Obay's reasons (2026-11-21): the words
  around it, "Look, here" and "There", and the many places in the New Testament that speak of
  Jesus being within his people.
- Left to the translator by Obay on 2026-11-21: "Jesus" or "the Lord" at Jude 5; "the Jews" or
  "the Judeans"; "Amen, amen" or "Truly, truly"; the opening of Genesis 1:1.

## Settled in Genesis 1-2
- Elohim "God"; YHWH "Yahweh"; YHWH Elohim "Yahweh God"; Adonai "Lord".
- bara "create"; asah "make". Never interchange them.
- mashal (the lights) "govern"; radah (humankind) "rule over".
- raqia "vault" [alt: expanse]. shamayim "heavens" in 1:1 and 2:1, 4; "sky" for the visible sky.
- hinneh "look" (never "behold").
- tohu wabohu "desolate and empty" [alt: formless and empty].
- ruach elohim "the spirit of God" [alt: the wind of God, or the breath of God], lowercase.
- adam: "humankind" for the collective (1:26-27); "the human" with the article in chapter 2;
  "Adam" only where it stands without the article as a name.
- ish / ishshah "man" / "woman".
- min "kind" ("according to their kinds").
- remes / romes "creeping things" / "creeps". chayyat ha'arets / hassadeh "wild animals of the earth / field".
- behemah "livestock".
- tov wara "good and bad" [alt: good and evil].
- shavat "cease" [alt: rest].
- toledot "generations" [alt: account].
- Sentence-opening "and" is kept where the Hebrew narrative chain has it; "Now" marks a break in the chain.
- Day formula: "And there was evening and there was morning" followed by a dash and the day.

## Settled in Genesis 5
- sefer (5:1) "book". demut "likeness"; tselem "image" (1:26-27; 5:1, 3), keeping the prepositions as they stand: be- "in", ke- "according to".
- adam in 5:1b-2 (the collective, as in 1:26-27): "humankind" [alt: Adam]; in 5:1a and 5:3-5 it is the name "Adam".
- holid (hiphil of yalad; 5:3-32) "fathered", the same English as qal yalad of a father (4:18). Where it has no object (5:3): "fathered one", so that it stays distinct from "fathered a son" (5:28).
- Genealogy formula: "And X lived N years, and he fathered Y. And X lived N years after he fathered Y, and he fathered sons and daughters. And all the days of X were N years, and he died."
- Numbers are written out in words in normal English order ("a hundred and thirty years", "nine hundred and sixty-nine years"). ben N shanah "N years old".
- hithallekh et-ha'elohim (5:22, 24) "walked with God"; hithallekh on its own stays "walk about" (3:8). ha'elohim (with the article) "God", the same as Elohim.
- einennu (5:24) "he was no more". laqach "take". mut "die".
- nacham (piel; 5:29) "comfort"; with min, "bring comfort from".
- ma'aseh (5:29) "work" (it shares its English with melakhah "work" in 2:2-3).
- Names: Seth, Enosh, Kenan, Mahalalel, Jared, Enoch, Methuselah, Lamech, Noah, Shem, Ham, Japheth.

## Settled in 1 John 1
- Greek text: the main text follows the earliest manuscripts, as in the standard critical editions. Where many manuscripts read differently in a way that changes the meaning (1:4), the other reading goes in an [alt: ..., as other manuscripts read].
- arche "beginning" (ap' arches "from the beginning"). logos "word"; ho logos tes zoes (1:1) "the word of life" [alt: the Word of life].
- zoe "life"; he zoe he aionios "the eternal life" (aionios "eternal").
- akouo "hear"; horao "see"; theaomai "gaze at", kept distinct from horao. pselaphao "feel" (with the hands), kept distinct from "touch" (Gen 3:3).
- phaneroo "reveal"; passive "was revealed".
- martyreo "testify". apangello "announce"; anangello "declare"; angelia "message". Keep the two verbs distinct.
- koinonia "fellowship"; koinonian echein meta "have fellowship with".
- pater of God "the Father"; huios of Jesus "Son" ("his Son"), both capitalized as titles. Pronouns for God and for Jesus stay lowercase, as in Genesis. Iesous Christos "Jesus Christ".
- chara "joy"; pleroo of joy (1:4) "make full", so that "complete" stays free for another verb.
- phos "light". skotia (1:5) and skotos (1:6) both "darkness": two forms of one word, like hen / hinneh.
- peripateo "walk". pseudomai "lie"; pseustes "liar". aletheia "truth"; poiein ten aletheian (1:6) "do the truth".
- haima "blood". katharizo "cleanse". hamartia "sin" (the same English as chatta't); hamartano "sin" (verb).
- planao "lead astray", kept distinct from "deceive" (Gen 3:13).
- homologeo "confess". pistos "faithful". dikaios "righteous" [alt: just]; adikia "unrighteousness", so that the echo between the two survives. aphiemi of sins "forgive".
- hina "so that" (purpose in 1:3-4; result in 1:9).
- An explicit subject pronoun that carries emphasis: "we ourselves" (hemeis, 1:4), "he himself" (autos, 1:7).
- Sentence-opening kai is kept as "And", as with the Hebrew narrative chain. kai ... de (1:3) "And indeed".
- The opening sentence (1:1-3) breaks off and resumes in the Greek; the breaks are marked with dashes and the sentence is not smoothed out.

## Settled in 1 John 2
- Verse numbers: the sentence to the paidia ("I have written to you, young children") opens 2:14, as in the Greek editions and many English versions; the KJV counts it as the end of 2:13. The chapter's `numbering:` line says so once.
- Greek text, applying the 1 John 1 line: another reading is noted only where it changes what the sentence says (2:20, pantes "all of you know" / panta "you know all things"). A different form of address, a different tense, or an added or missing phrase that leaves the sense intact is not noted (2:7, 14, 23, 27).
- teknia "little children"; paidia "young children" (leaving "children" for tekna). pateres "fathers"; neaniskoi "young men". adelphos "brother". agapetoi (address) "Beloved".
- grapho "I am writing"; egrapsa (of this letter: 2:14, 21, 26) "I have written".
- parakletos "advocate". hilasmos "atonement" [alt: appeasement]. kosmos "world".
- ginosko and oida both "know" (English has no second plain verb for it); perfect egnoka "have come to know". en touto ginoskomen "by this we know".
- entole "commandment"; tereo "keep". opheilo "ought".
- ho with a participle "the one who"; hos an "whoever"; pas ho "everyone who"; ean tis "if anyone". houtos of a person (2:4-5) "this person". ekeinos of Jesus (2:6) "that one", kept distinct from autos "he".
- agape "love" (noun); agapao "love" (verb); miseo "hate". he agape tou theou / tou patros "the love of God / of the Father", leaving the genitive as open in English as it is in Greek.
- teleioo "make complete" (2:5 "has been made complete"); this is the verb that "complete" was kept free for.
- meno "remain" (never "abide"). kathos "just as"; hos "as".
- kainos "new"; palaios "old". palin where it marks a contrast (2:8) "then again".
- alethes and alethinos both "true" (two adjectives from one root, like skotia / skotos); alethos "truly". pseudos "a lie".
- paragomai "pass away". phaino "shine". heos arti "until now". skandalon "cause of stumbling". typhloo "blind" (verb). hypago "go".
- nikao "conquer". poneros "evil"; ho poneros "the evil one". ischyros "strong".
- epithymia "desire" (kept apart from "delight", "desirable" and "longing" in Genesis 3). sarx "flesh". alazoneia "arrogance". bios "possessions" [alt: life], kept distinct from zoe "life". thelema "will". eis ton aiona "forever".
- einai ek "be from" (2:16, 19, 21) and gennao (passive) ek "be born from" (2:29): the same English preposition for both, so that the link between them survives. exerchomai ek "go out from".
- eschate hora "the last hour". antichristos "antichrist", lowercase: without the article (2:18) "antichrist", with it (2:22) "the antichrist". ginomai of the antichrists (2:18) "arise". hothen "from which".
- chrisma "anointing". ho Christos as a title (2:22) "the Christ". The echo between anointing, Christ and antichrist is explained in one bracketed note (2:20). ho hagios (2:20) "the Holy One".
- arneomai "deny". echein ton patera "have the Father". epangelia "promise"; epangellomai "promise" (verb). didasko "teach". chreian echein hina (2:27) "have need for (anyone) to".
- hymeis standing first, outside its clause (2:24, 27): "As for you"; as an emphatic subject (2:20): "you yourselves".
- ean of an expected event (2:28) "when" [alt: if]; elsewhere "if". parresia "boldness". aischynomai apo (2:28) "be ashamed before". parousia "coming".
- dikaiosyne "righteousness"; poiein ten dikaiosynen "do righteousness", like "do the truth". The [alt: just] for dikaios is given at its first occurrence in a chapter (2:1), not at every one.
- hoti in 2:12-14 "because" [alt: that], the alt given once, at 2:12. An aorist is put in the English perfect where a past act explains a present state (2:11 "has blinded").

## Settled in 1 John 3
- Greek text, applying the 1 John 1 line: where the earliest manuscripts divide over a sentence-opening kai (3:13, 19) it is left out, and 3:7 reads teknia "Little children" (not paidia); none of these is noted, since the sense is intact.
- idete (3:1; the imperative of horao) "See". potapos "what kind of". homoios "like".
- dia touto "for this reason"; eis touto (3:8) "for this purpose"; charin tinos (3:12) "for what reason".
- hina after a noun whose content it gives (3:11 the message, 3:23 the commandment): "that we should"; elsewhere still "so that" (3:1, 5, 8).
- The aorist egno (3:1) "did not know", a simple past, kept apart from the perfect "have come to know".
- ean phanerothe (3:2) "when he is revealed" [alt: when it is revealed, or if he is revealed], as in 2:28.
- elpis "hope"; elpida echein epi (3:3) "have hope set on". hagnizo "purify"; hagnos "pure", kept distinct from katharizo "cleanse".
- poiein ten hamartian "do sin", like "do righteousness". anomia "lawlessness". airo (3:5) "take away".
- diabolos "the devil", lowercase. lyo (3:8) "undo" [alt: destroy]. ho huios tou theou "the Son of God".
- ergon "work" (ta erga "the works": 3:8, 12); in the pair with logos (3:18) "deed".
- sperma (3:9) "seed" [alt: offspring], the mirror image of zera in Gen 3:15. ou dynatai "cannot".
- phaneros (3:10) "revealed" (phanera estin "are revealed"), so that the echo with phaneroo survives.
- pas ho with a participle and a negated verb stays "everyone who ... does not" (3:6, 9, 10, as in 2:23); pas with a noun and a negative (3:15, as in 2:21) "no ...".
- ou kathos (3:12) "not as", with no alt: "not just as" would read in English as "not merely as". Kain "Cain". sphazo "slaughter", kept distinct from "kill" and "strike down" (Gen 4).
- thaumazo (3:13) "be surprised". metabaino ek ... eis "cross over from ... into". thanatos "death". anthropoktonos "murderer". zoe aionios without the article "eternal life".
- psyche (3:16) "life" [alt: soul], a different word from zoe "life"; the alt is given once in a chapter. tithenai ten psychen hyper "lay down one's life for".
- bios (3:17) "possessions", with no alt where "life" is not a possible reading. theoreo (3:17) "see", the same English as horao (no second plain verb fits here; "gaze at" stays for theaomai). chreian echon "in need".
- splanchna (3:17) "compassion" (kleio ta splanchna apo "shut off one's compassion from"), kept distinct from kardia "heart". glossa "tongue".
- kardia "heart", singular as in the Greek ("our heart"). emprosthen "before"; enopion "in the sight of". peitho (3:19) "reassure" [alt: persuade]. meizon "greater".
- kataginosko "condemn"; its echo with ginosko "know" is explained in one bracketed note (3:20).
- 3:19-20: the first hoti ean is read as ho ti ean "in whatever" and the second hoti as "because"; the other reading ("persuade our heart that, if ...") is the alt.
- parresian echein pros "have boldness toward". aiteo "ask"; lambano "receive". arestos "pleasing". allelous "one another".
- pisteuo with the dative (3:23) "believe" [alt: believe in], leaving "believe in" for pisteuo eis. onoma "name". didonai entolen "give a commandment".
- pneuma (3:24) "spirit", lowercase, as with ruach elohim in Genesis 1:2.
- Where a hoti clause depends on the verse before (3:11), the sentence runs on across the verse break as "because", rather than opening a new sentence with "For" (kept for gar).

## Settled in 1 John 4
- Greek text, applying the 1 John 1 line: the other reading is noted at 4:19 (agapomen with no object; many manuscripts add "him", some "God"). Not noted, since the sense is intact: 4:3, where many manuscripts repeat "Christ as having come in the flesh" from 4:2, and 4:20, where many read a question ("how can he love ...?").
- pneuma stays "spirit", lowercase, all through the chapter: to pneuma tou theou "the spirit of God" (as with ruach elohim), "the spirit of truth", "his spirit". to tou antichristou (4:3) "the spirit of the antichrist": the neuter article stands for pneuma, so the noun is repeated in English.
- dokimazo "test". pseudoprophetes "false prophet". laleo "speak". exerchomai eis "go out into".
- homologein Iesoun Christon en sarki eleluthota (4:2) "confess Jesus Christ as having come in the flesh" [alt: confess Jesus as the Christ who has come in the flesh]; a perfect participle "having come", "has come".
- An emphatic subject pronoun, continued: hymeis / autoi / hemeis (4:4-6) "You yourselves / They themselves / We ourselves"; kai hemeis in a main clause after "if" (4:11) "we ourselves also".
- ho en with a noun (4:4) "the one in". hos with the indicative (4:6) "whoever", the same English as hos an.
- akouo with the genitive of a person (4:5-6) stays "hear" [alt: listen to], the alt given once in a chapter.
- ek toutou (4:6) "from this", kept distinct from en touto "by this". en touto estin (4:10) "in this is"; en touto with any other verb stays "by this" (4:9, 13, 17), with a comma where "this" could be misread with the next noun (4:17).
- plane "error" [alt: leading astray]; the alt carries the echo with planao "lead astray". With plane, he aletheia loses its article in English ("the spirit of truth and the spirit of error").
- ouk egno (4:8) "did not know", as in 3:1, even with a present-tense subject; the perfect stays "have come to know" (4:16).
- en hemin "in us" all through the chapter (4:12, 13), with the other reading as an alt where it is likely: 4:9 [alt: among us]; 4:16 agapen echein en "have love in" [alt: for].
- monogenes "only" ("his only Son") [alt: only-born]. apostello "send": perfect "has sent" (4:9, 14), aorist "sent" (4:10). zao "live"; dia with the genitive "through".
- A second accusative naming a role (4:10, 14): "as" ("as the atonement", "as savior of the world"). soter "savior", lowercase.
- houtos (the adverb; 4:11) "in this way" [alt: so much]. popote "ever". protos "first".
- pisteuo with the accusative (4:16) "believe"; with the dative of a thing or spirit (4:1) "believe", with no alt.
- teleios "complete" (he teleia agape "complete love"), so that the echo with teleioo "make complete" survives; teteleiomene estin (4:12) "has been made complete", the same English as teteleiotai.
- meta with the genitive (4:17) "with". hemera tes kriseos "the day of judgment"; krisis "judgment". kathos ... kai (4:17) "just as ..., so", as in 2:18.
- phobos "fear"; phobeomai "fear" (verb; ho phoboumenos "the one who fears"). exo ballein "throw out", kept distinct from "drive out" (Gen 3:24). kolasis "punishment"; kolasin echei "has punishment", left as compressed as the Greek.
- agapomen (4:19), which can be a statement or an appeal: "we love" [alt: let us love]; in 4:7, without a pronoun and after "Beloved", "let us love".
- hina after entole (4:21) "that ... should", as in 3:23.

## Settled in 1 John 5
- Greek text, applying the 1 John 1 line: 5:7-8 follows the earliest manuscripts, and almost all the others with them: "there are three who testify, the spirit and the water and the blood". The longer reading (three who testify "in heaven, the Father, the Word and the Holy Spirit", and three "on earth"), found in a few late manuscripts and in the KJV, is not in the main text and has no note; whether to note it is for Obay to decide. The verse break falls where the Greek editions and most modern English versions put it.
- 5:18: the earliest manuscripts divide between auton ("the one who was born from God keeps him") and heauton ("keeps himself"), and the sense changes, so both are given: auton in the main text, as in the editions whose readings were followed at 1:7 and 3:7, and heauton as the alt.
- Not noted, since the sense is intact: 5:2 (many read "keep" for "do"), 5:5 (de, which the earliest have, is kept as "And"), 5:10 (en heauto / en auto, both "in himself"), 5:13 (many add "and so that you may believe in the name of the Son of God"), 5:21 (many add "Amen"). Also not noted: 5:6, where some add "and spirit" after "water and blood", and 5:10, where a few read "the Son" for "God".
- gennao active (5:1) "father", as with holid in Genesis 5: ho gennesas "the one who fathered", left without an object as in the Greek; its echo with the passive "be born" is explained in one bracketed note. Perfect passive "has been born" (5:1, 4, 18); the aorist ho gennetheis (5:18) "the one who was born", kept apart from the perfect.
- hotan (5:2) "when". poiein tas entolas (5:2) "do his commandments", kept distinct from tereo "keep" (5:3). barys (5:3) "burdensome".
- hina after "the love of God" (5:3) and after lego (5:16) "that ... should", as in 3:11, 23.
- pan to with a neuter participle (5:4) "everything that". nike "victory"; pistis "faith". Their echoes with nikao "conquer" and pisteuo "believe" are explained in one bracketed note (5:4). The aorist participle he nikesasa (5:4) "that conquered", a simple past, kept apart from the perfect "have conquered" (2:13-14; 4:4).
- de where it carries the thought forward (5:5, 20) "And"; where it contrasts it stays "but".
- hydor "water". erchomai dia (5:6) "come through"; en to hydati / en to haimati "in the water / in the blood" [alt: with], keeping en distinct from dia.
- to martyroun (5:6, neuter) "the one that testifies"; hoi martyrountes (5:7, masculine) "who testify".
- A hoti that opens a new sentence in the Greek editions (5:7) still runs on as "because", after a semicolon, as in 3:11.
- eis to hen einai (5:8) "be at one" [alt: be one].
- martyria "testimony"; he martyria hen memartyreken (5:10) "the testimony that God has testified", keeping the cognate object as in 2:25. anthropoi (5:9) "human beings".
- pisteuo eis "believe in" (5:10, 13); pisteuo with the dative of God (5:10) "believe", with no alt: the point is that what God has said is not believed.
- he zoe with the article (5:12) "the life", pointing back to "this life" (5:11).
- A dative participle set after its clause, in apposition to "you" or "him" (5:13, 16), stays at the end of the English sentence.
- kata with the accusative (5:14) "according to". ho ean (5:15) "whatever", as in 3:22. aitema "request" (noun; it shares its English with erotao). erotao (5:16) "make a request", kept distinct from aiteo "ask".

- heko (5:20) "has come", the same English as the perfect of erchomai (English has no second plain verb for it). dianoia "understanding". ho alethinos "the true one", lowercase like "the righteous one" (2:1) and "the evil one".

## Settled in John 1

Not noted, since the sense is intact: 1:16 (many read kai for the first hoti), 1:19 (pros auton "to him", over which the earliest divide, is kept), 1:27 (the doubtful ego is not marked; many add "who has come to be ahead of me"), 1:39 (many read "Come and see"), 1:41 (proton / protos, both "first"), 1:51 (many add "from now on").
- 1:3-4: ho gegonen is read with what follows, as the Greek editions punctuate it ("What has come to be in him was life"), and the verse break stays where they put it, in mid-sentence; the other division ("not even one thing came to be that has come to be. In him was life") is the alt.

- panta "all things". oude hen "not even one thing". choris "apart from". para with the genitive "from".
- anthropos: in the plural and of people in general (1:4, 9) "human being(s)", as in 1 Jn 5:9; of one particular man (1:6) "man", sharing its English with aner "man" (1:13 [alt: husband]; 1:30). ho huios tou anthropou (1:51) "the Son of Man", capitalized like "the Son of God" and kept as a fixed title.

- apostello and pempo both "send" (English has no second plain verb for it).
- eis martyrian (1:7) "for testimony". all' hina with no verb before it (1:8) "but came so that", the verb taken from 1:7, as "this was" is supplied in 1 Jn 2:19.
- 1:9: erchomenon is read with "light" ("was coming into the world"); the reading with "every human being" is the alt.
- ta idia (1:11) "what was his own"; hoi idioi "those who were his own"; ho idios (1:41) "his own". paralambano "take in", kept distinct from lambano "receive". hosoi "as many as".
- exousia "authority" [alt: the right]. haimata (1:13, a plural) "blood", as with the Hebrew plural in Gen 4:10. Nouns without the article in 1:13 stay without it ("the will of flesh", "the will of a man").
- skenoo (1:14) "pitch one's tent" [alt: live]. en hemin (1:14) "among us", which the tent requires. doxa "glory". charis "grace". pleres "full"; pleroma "fullness". With charis, he aletheia loses its article in English (1:17 "grace and truth"), as with plane in 1 Jn 4:6.
- monogenes with no noun (1:14) "the only Son" [alt: the only-born Son]: on its own the word means an only child. hos monogenous para patros "as of the only Son from the Father" [alt: as of an only son from a father]. monogenes theos (1:18) "the only Son, God" [alt: the only-born God]; "the only God" is left for monos theos (5:44; 17:3).
- krazo "cry out"; boao "shout"; phoneo and kaleo both "call". opiso mou "after me". emprosthen mou with ginomai (1:15, 30) "ahead of me"; protos mou "before me" (protos elsewhere stays "first").
- 1:14-17: verse 15 interrupts the sentence, and 1:16 picks up "full of grace and truth" with hoti. The interruption is marked with dashes, as in 1 Jn 1:1-3, and hoti (1:16, 17) runs on as "because".
- anti (1:16) "in place of" [alt: upon]. nomos "law", lowercase. Mouses "Moses"; Elias "Elijah"; Esaias "Isaiah": people of the Hebrew Bible keep their usual English names.
- kolpos (1:18) "lap" [alt: side], leaving "side" for pleura and "chest" for stethos. exegeomai "make known"; its object ("him") is supplied in English. The aorist exegesato "made known", a simple past beside the perfect "has seen".
- hoi Ioudaioi "the Jews" [alt: the Judeans], the alt given at 1:19. hiereus "priest"; Leuites "Levite"; Pharisaioi "Pharisees".
- erotao of a question (1:19, 21, 25) "question" (verb), kept distinct from aiteo "ask"; of a request it stays "make a request" (1 Jn 5:16). apokrinomai "answer"; apokrisis "answer" (noun). apekrithe kai eipen "answered and said", both verbs kept.
- The historical present in narrative (legei, heuriskei, blepei) is kept as an English present ("he says", "he finds", "he sees"), also where it stands beside a past tense (1:21, 43, 47).
- An emphatic subject pronoun, continued: ego / kago "I myself" / "And I myself" (1:20, 26, 30, 31, 33, 34); hymeis "you yourselves" (1:26). With einai in a question or a statement of who someone is (sy tis ei, sy ei: 1:19, 21, 25, 42, 49) the pronoun is ordinary Greek and is not marked.
- oun in narrative "So"; ti oun "What then?" / "Why then ...?". de that turns aside to give background (1:44) "Now", as in Genesis; otherwise "And" or "But", as in 1 Jn 5.
- kyrios "Lord"; where it stands for the divine name in a quotation from the Hebrew Bible (1:23) "the Lord", translated as the Greek has it. eremos "wilderness". euthyno "make straight". hodos "way".
- baptizo "baptize" [alt: immerse], the alt given once in a chapter. en hydati "in water" [alt: with water], as in 1 Jn 5:6. en pneumati hagio (1:33) "in holy spirit" [alt: in the holy spirit]: lowercase and without the article, as in the Greek, parallel to "in water". hagios "holy".
- mesos hymon "among you". axios "worthy". lyo of a sandal strap (1:27) "undo", the same English as in 1 Jn 3:8. peran "across".
- te epaurion "the next day". blepo "see" (it shares its English with horao and theoreo); emblepo "look at"; theaomai stays "gaze at". ide as an exclamation "Look", the same as hinneh; erchou kai ide (1:46) "Come and see".
- amnos "lamb": ho amnos tou theou "the lamb of God", lowercase like "savior". hyper with a verb of saying (1:30) "about", kept distinct from peri "concerning".
- ouranos "heaven". katabaino "come down"; anabaino "go up". peristera "dove". epi with the accusative (1:32, 33, 51) "on". A relative clause that repeats its pronoun (1:27, 33) gives it once in English.
- mathetes "disciple". akoloutheo "follow". zeteo "seek". heurisko "find". ago "bring". thelo "want". polis "city". apo and ek both "from". agathos "good". dolos "deceit". syke "fig tree"; hypo "under"; hypokato "underneath". basileus "king". angelos "angel". anoigo "open".
- rhabbi "Rabbi", Messias "Messiah" and Kephas "Cephas" are kept as the Greek keeps them, since the text itself goes on to translate them; didaskalos "Teacher". methermeneuo "translate"; hermeneuo "interpret". These asides by the writer stand in parentheses (1:38, 41, 42), and a short bracketed note gives the shared meaning of Messiah / Christ (1:41) and of Cephas / Peter (1:42).
- meno of lodging (1:38-39) "stay" [alt: remain], the alt given once; elsewhere it stays "remain" (1:32, 33). hora "hour", counted as the Greek counts it ("the tenth hour").
- pothen (1:48) "where ... from". pisteueis (1:50) "do you believe?" [alt: you believe]. meizo touton "greater things than these".
- amen amen "Amen, amen" [alt: Truly, truly]: the Greek keeps the Hebrew word untranslated, and "truly" is alethos (1:47).
- A plural "you" spoken to one person (1:51 hymin, opsesthe) "you all", so that the change of number is not lost.
- For Obay to decide (each is easy to reverse): "the Word" capitalized; "the only Son, God" at 1:18; "Amen, amen" or "Truly, truly"; the historical present kept as a present; "you all" for a plural "you" at 1:51; "baptize" or "immerse"; "grace" or "favor" for charis; "the Son of Man" or a rendering with "human being"; whether "[alt: the Judeans]" should be repeated in later chapters; "in holy spirit" with no article and no capital.

## Settled in John 2
- Greek text, applying the 1 John 1 line: the other reading is noted at 2:15 (phragellion "a whip"; the two earliest papyri and many others read hos phragellion "something like a whip"). Not noted, since the sense is intact: 2:4 (the opening kai, which the earliest papyri have, is kept), 2:10 (many add tote "then" before "the lesser"), 2:12 (the doubtful autou after "brothers" is not marked), 2:17 (kataphagetai "will devour"; a few late manuscripts and the KJV read katephagen "has devoured"), 2:22 (many add "to them"), 2:24 (auton / heauton, both "himself"). - hoi Ioudaioi: until Obay decides, "[alt: the Judeans]" is given once in a chapter, at the first occurrence (2:6), like the other standing alts.
- gemizo "fill" (leaving "make full" for pleroo); heos ano "up to the top". antleo "draw (out)", its object ("some") supplied in English. phero "carry", kept distinct from ago "bring".

- An aside by the writer inside a sentence (2:9) stands in parentheses, as in 1:38.
- pas anthropos (2:10) "every human being", as in 1:9; ho anthropos for people as a class (2:25) "a human being". An indefinite third-person plural (2:10 methysthosin) "people".
- kalos (of wine) "fine", kept distinct from agathos "good". elasson "lesser", the pair of meizon "greater". methysko (passive) "become drunk", not softened. tithemi of wine "set out". sy (2:10, 20) "you yourself", as an emphatic subject.
- semeion "sign" (the same English as ot in Genesis); poiein semeion "do a sign". arche ton semeion "the beginning of the signs", arche as always; the second accusative is "as", as in 1 Jn 4:10.
- katabaino of a journey (2:12) "go down", the pair of anabaino "go up"; of what descends from heaven it stays "come down". meta touto "after this". meno of lodging stays "stay" [alt: remain], the alt given once in a chapter. ou pollas hemeras "not many days", left as the Greek puts it.
- pascha "Passover"; heorte "festival". engys "near". Kana "Cana"; Kapharnaoum "Capernaum"; Hierosolyma "Jerusalem".

- poleo "sell". bous "cattle"; probaton "sheep". kermatistes "coin changer" and kerma "coins" (2:14-15), keeping their echo; kollybistes "money changer". trapeza "table". phragellion "whip"; schoinion "cord".
- ekballo "drive out", the same English as garash (Gen 3:24); exo ballein stays "throw out". ekcheo "pour out". anatrepo "overturn". enteuthen "from here".
- 2:15 pantas ... ta te probata kai tous boas: "them all ..., both the sheep and the cattle" [alt: along with the sheep and the cattle]: "all" may be the animals alone, or the people with the animals.
- oikos "house"; ho oikos tou patros mou "my Father's house"; oikos emporiou "a house of trade".

## Settled in John 3
- Greek text, applying the 1 John 1 line: the other reading is noted at 3:13 (the earliest manuscripts end at "the Son of Man"; many add ho on en to ourano "who is in heaven") and 3:25 (meta Ioudaiou "with a Jew"; one of the earliest papyri and many others read meta Ioudaion "with Jews"). 3:15 reads en auto, as in the editions; the earliest manuscripts divide between en auto, ep' auto and eis auton, and the alt there covers them. Not noted, since the sense is intact: 3:2 (many read "to Jesus" for "to him"), 3:15 (many add "should not perish but" from 3:16), 3:16 and 3:17 (many read "his only Son", "his Son"), 3:31 (the second "is above all", over which the earliest divide, is kept, as in the editions), 3:32 (many open with kai), 3:34 (many add ho theos as the subject of "gives"; "he" is left as open as in the Greek, as at 1 Jn 5:16).
- anthropos stays as in John 1 and 2: of one particular man (3:1) "man"; for people as a class (3:4, 27) "a human being"; hoi anthropoi (3:19) "human beings". So the link between 2:25 and 3:1 is still not visible (see the John 2 line for Obay).
- archon "ruler". Nikodemos onoma auto "his name was Nicodemus", as in 1:6. nyktos "at night".
- ean me "unless"; ean me tis "unless someone ..., he". dynamai "can"; ou dynatai "cannot".
- anothen "from above" [alt: again], the alt given once in a chapter (3:3). The word has both senses; Nicodemus answers with deuteron "a second time", and at 3:31 it can only be "from above".
- basileia "kingdom"; he basileia tou theou "the kingdom of God". eiserchomai eis "enter" (the English verb needs no "into"). koilia "womb". geron on "when he is old".
- me opening a question that expects the answer no (3:4): a statement with a tag, "He cannot ..., can he?"
- gennao passive stays "be born" all through 3:3-8; to gegennemenon "what has been born"; ex hydatos kai pneumatos (3:5) "from water and spirit", without articles as in the Greek; ek tou pneumatos (3:6, 8) "from the spirit". dei "must".
- pneuma at its first occurrence in 3:8 "wind" [alt: spirit], with pneo "blow" and phone "sound" [alt: voice]; one bracketed note says that wind and spirit are the same word. phone elsewhere stays "voice" (3:29).
- houtos (the adverb) "in this way", as in 1 Jn 4:11: houtos estin (3:8) "it is this way with"; kathos ... houtos (3:14) "just as ..., in this way" (kathos ... kai stays "just as ..., so"); houtos ... hoste (3:16) "in this way [alt: so much], that".
- A plural "you" spoken to one person, continued (3:7, 11, 12): "you all", once in a sentence, and placed where it cannot be misread (3:12 "said ... to you all", not "told you all the ...").
- lambanein ten martyrian "receive the testimony". martyreo with an accusative (3:11, 32) and with a dative of the person (3:26) "testify to"; moi martyreite (3:28) "testify for me", since "testify to me" would say something else in English.
- ta epigeia / ta epourania "the earthly things / the heavenly things". An aorist beside a present (3:12 eipon ... ou pisteuete) is put in the English perfect, as in 1 Jn 2:11. ei me (3:13) "except". eis ton ouranon "into heaven".
- hypsoo "lift up" (its other sense, "exalt", is not noted). ophis "snake", the same English as nachash.
- 3:15 ho pisteuon en auto: en auto is read with "have" ("may have eternal life in him"), since "believe in" in John is always pisteuo eis; the reading with "believes" is the alt.
- hina after a past tense "so that ... might" (3:16, 17); after a present "so that ... may" (3:15, 20, 21), as in John 1 and 1 John.
- ton huion ton monogene (3:16) "the only Son" [alt: the only-born Son], the alt given once in a chapter; 3:18 "the only Son of God".
- apollymi (middle) "perish". sozo "save". krino "judge", never "condemn" (which is kept for kataginosko), so that the echo with krisis "judgment" survives: ou krinetai "is not judged", kekritai "has been judged". ede "already".
- mallon ... e (3:19) "rather than" [alt: more than].
- phaulos "worthless", kept distinct from poneros "evil" in the verse before, and leaving "bad" for kakos. prasso "practice", kept distinct from poieo "do". elencho (3:20) "expose".
- ergazomai "work" (verb): estin eirgasmena (3:21) "have been worked", keeping the echo with erga "works". hoti in 3:21 "that" [alt: because], on the pattern of 1 Jn 2:19.
- meta tauta "after these things". he Ioudaia ge "the Judean land"; ge "land" (3:22) or "earth" (3:31), by context. diatribo "spend time". Ainon "Aenon"; Saleim "Salim". hydata polla (a plural) "much water", as with haimata. paraginomai "arrive", kept distinct from erchomai "come". ballo "throw"; phylake "prison".
- zetesis "debate"; ginomai of a debate (3:25) "arise"; ek ton matheton "from (John's) disciples". pantes of people (3:26) "everyone", as in 2:24.
- A relative clause left hanging and picked up by houtos (3:26) is marked with a dash.
- autoi hymeis (3:28) "You yourselves". emprosthen ekeinou (3:28) "ahead of that one", as in 1:15.
- nymphe "bride". philos "friend". histemi (perfect) "stand". chairo "rejoice"; chara chairei "rejoices with joy", keeping the cognate dative. dia with the accusative "because of". he chara he eme "this joy of mine".
- auxano "grow"; elattoo (passive) "become less", keeping the echo with elasson "lesser" (2:10).
- epano panton (3:31) "above all", left as open as the Greek (all people or all things).
- A perfect and an aorist under one relative (3:32 ho heoraken kai ekousen) "what he has seen and heard". touto picking up the relative clause: "to this he testifies".
- sphragizo "set one's seal" (3:33 "set his seal to this: that"). rhema "word" (it shares its English with logos; in John it is always plural). ek metrou "by measure". cheir "hand"; didonai en te cheiri "give into his hand".
- apeitheo "disobey" [alt: refuse to believe], kept distinct from me pisteuo "not believe". orge "anger" (plain English for what is often put as "wrath"). menei ep' auton "remains on him", as in 1:32.
- Quotation marks: the Greek does not mark where a speech ends. Where nothing signals a change of speaker, the speech runs on: Jesus' words close at 3:21 and John's at 3:36. A new paragraph inside a speech opens with a quotation mark.
- For Obay to decide (each is easy to reverse): where the two speeches end (3:21 and 3:36 here; many readers end them at 3:15 and 3:30 and take what follows as the writer's own words); "born from above" with "born again" as the alt, or the other way round; "wind" as the main text at 3:8; "in this way [alt: so much]" at 3:16; "judge" rather than "condemn" in 3:17-18; "worthless" for phaulos; "anger" or "wrath" for orge; "disobeys" or "does not believe" at 3:36; whether "who is in heaven" at 3:13 should be noted.

- dos moi pein "Give me a drink"; pino "drink"; phago "eat". trophai (4:8, a plural) "provisions"; brosis and broma both "food" (two forms of one word, like skotia / skotos). agorazo "buy".
- Ioudaios in the singular "a Jew" [alt: a Judean]: the standing alt is given at the first occurrence in the chapter, whatever the number (4:9). Ioudaioi without the article "Jews".
- sy carrying a participle (4:9 "you, being a Jew") is not marked further; the other emphatic subject pronouns are marked as before (4:10, 14, 20, 22, 32, 35, 38, 42, 44, 45, 53).
- synchraomai "have dealings with" [alt: use things in common with]. A sentence with gar that explains the story (4:8, 9) is the writer's: it stands outside the quotation marks, without parentheses, as at 3:24. An aside inside a sentence (4:2, 25) stands in parentheses.
- dorea "gift". hydor zon "living water" [alt: flowing water], the alt given once in a chapter: the phrase is also the ordinary one for spring water.

## Settled in John 5

## Settled in Jude 1
- Greek text, applying the 1 John 1 line: the main text is that of the standard critical editions in their current form, the same that 1 John follows. The other reading is noted at 1:1 (egapemenois "loved"; many read hegiasmenois "made holy"), 1:4 (many add theon "God" after "the only Master"), 1:5 (Iesous "Jesus", as in the editions; the earliest manuscripts divide, and most manuscripts read ho kyrios "the Lord"), 1:15 (pasan psychen "every soul", as in the editions; the earliest divide, and most read "all the godless" or "all the godless among them"), 1:22-23 (three clauses, as in the editions; most manuscripts have two, with diakrinomenoi "making a distinction" and with "in fear" set beside "save") and 1:24 (hymas "you"; many read autous "them"). Not noted, since the sense is intact: 1:3 (many leave out "our"), 1:5 (hymas hapax panta, as in the editions; many read touto "this" for panta, and a few set hapax after "the Lord"), 1:12 (many leave out hoi), 1:16 (heauton / auton, both "their own"), 1:18 (many add hoti before the quotation and read en eschato chrono), 1:25 (many add sopho "wise" after "only" and leave out "through Jesus Christ our Lord" and "before all time"). Also not noted: 1:5, where the earliest papyrus reads "God Christ" for "Jesus"; and 1:22-23, where the earliest papyrus has a shorter form ("seize some from the fire, and have mercy in fear on those who are doubting"), one early manuscript runs the first two clauses together, and some read elenchete "expose" for the first "have mercy on".

## Settled on 2026-11-21 under the rules for alternates (the translator's calls; Obay can switch any of them)
- amen amen at the head of a saying stays "Amen, amen", as John 1 to 6 already have it, so that the refrain is the same all through the book. No bracket in new chapters: "Truly, truly" is the same meaning in other words.
- hoi Ioudaioi: "the Jews" by default; "the Judeans" where the sentence is about the people of the region of Judea, set beside Galileans or Samaritans. No standing bracket: a bracket only at a verse where both are live and the sense changes.
- Jude 5 ("Jesus" [alt: the Lord, as other manuscripts read]) and Genesis 1:1 stay as published.

## Settled in Job 2

## Settled in Job 3
- Hebrew text: the Masoretic Text; the chapter has no Qere. Not noted: changes that no old copy has (3:8 "sea" for "day"; 3:22 "a heap" for "exultation"), and what the old Greek puts differently (3:3 "Look, a male"), as in Job 2.
- Poetry (3:3 on): the file format has no separate poetic lines, so each verse stays on one line and the halves of a line are divided by punctuation. A whole speech stands in quotation marks, each paragraph of it opening with one (the John 3 line); words quoted inside it take single marks (3:3).
- acharei-ken "After this". wayya'an X wayyomar, the formula that opens every speech in Job (3:2; 4:1; 6:1 ...): "And X answered and said", both verbs kept as at 1:7, also where no one has spoken; a colon follows before a speech.

- melekh "king"; yo'ets "counselor"; sar "prince" ("ruler" stays for archon); zahav "gold"; kesef "silver"; mille "fill" (the same English as gemizo); bayit "house". banah with choravot (3:14) "rebuild ruins" [alt: build what are now ruins]; banah elsewhere "build".
- 3:16 lo ehyeh is read as a statement, "I would not be", carrying on from 3:13-15 (compare 10:19); no "why" is brought down from 3:11-12, and the other construction ("Or why was I not like ...") is not noted, since both say that he would be as the stillborn are. nefel "stillborn child"; olel "infant".
- sham "there", as shammah (1:21). rasha "wicked". chadal "cease" (it shares its English with shavat). rogez "turmoil" (3:17, 26), left as open as the Hebrew at 3:17 (the turmoil they make, or the turmoil they are in). yegi'e khoach "those whose strength is worn out".
- yachad "together". asir "prisoner". sha'anan (3:18) "be untroubled" and shalah (3:26) "be at ease": keep the two distinct (they stand side by side at 21:23). noges "taskmaster". qaton wegadol "small and great".
- eved beside chofshi "free" and adon "master" (3:19) "slave": a man who is owned; elsewhere it stays "servant". adon of a human master "master".
- yitten (3:20) with no subject named: "he gives", left as open as the Hebrew, as at John 3:34. chayyim "life". mar nefesh "bitter in soul": nefesh as the seat of feeling "soul"; of a person's life it stays "life" (2:4, 6).
- mawet "death". chafar "dig" (with a suffix, "dig for"). samach "rejoice" (the same English as chairo); gil "exultation" (ele-gil "to the point of exultation"); sus "be glad". Keep the three distinct. matsa "find". qever "grave".
- 3:20-23 is one sentence: legever (3:23) picks up "to one in misery" (3:20) and is set after a dash. derekh "way". wayyasekh ... be'ado (3:23) "put a hedge around", the same English as at 1:10, so that the echo is heard.
- lifne (3:24) "before" [alt: like]. lechem "bread". anachah "sighing". she'agah "roar" (the lion's word, not softened); natakh "pour out".
- pachad "dread", noun and verb, with the cognate object kept ("the dread that I dreaded"); yagor "shrink from". Both are kept distinct from yare "fear" / "be afraid". atah "come upon"; bo li "come to me".
- The perfects of 3:26 are put in the English perfect ("I have not been at ease ... and turmoil has come"), which leaves open, as the Hebrew does, whether Job means the days before the disaster or now.

## Settled in Job 4
- Hebrew text: the Masoretic Text; the chapter has no Qere. The main text keeps to the Masoretic vowels and word division. Not noted: changes that no old copy has (4:20 "with no one to save" for "without anyone considering it"), and what the old Greek puts differently (4:10-11, 21), as in Job 2 and 3.
- A speech that runs over a chapter break: Eliphaz's first speech is 4:2-5:27, so chapter 4 ends with no closing quotation mark and chapter 5 opens with one, as a new paragraph inside a speech does. The words of the voice (4:17-21) stand in single marks and run to the end of the chapter: nothing signals a change of speaker before 5:1, where Eliphaz turns to Job again (the John 3 line).
- nissah davar el (4:2) "venture a word with"; the verb with no subject named "someone". la'ah (4:2, 5) "be weary", the same English both times (it shares its English with kopiao). atsar be- "hold back". millah (plural millin, millim) "word", the same English as davar.
- yissar (4:3) "instruct" (musar, 5:17, is to stand beside it). yadayim rafot "weak hands"; chizzeq "strengthen" (hecheziq be- stays "hold fast to"); immets "make firm". kashal "stumble" (as in skandalon "cause of stumbling"); heqim "raise up" (the same English as anistemi). birkayim kor'ot "buckling knees". The perfect and the imperfects of 4:3-4 (what Job did again and again) are all put in the English perfect.
- ki attah (4:5) "But now" (3:13 "For by now"). The subject of "comes" and "touches" (4:5) is not named, and "it" is left as open as the Hebrew. bo el "come to", as at 3:25. naga ad "touch", the same English as naga be- and naga el (1:11; 2:5), so that the echo is heard. nivhal "be dismayed", kept distinct from bi'et "terrify".
- yir'ah with no object (4:6; again at 15:4; 22:4) "fear of God": on its own the Hebrew word means reverence for God and the English word does not, so the object is supplied; it keeps the echo with "feared God" (1:1). kislah "confidence" (kesel, 8:14; 31:24, is to stand beside it). tiqwah "hope", as listed. tom "blamelessness", the same English as tummah (two forms of one word). In 4:6b the "and" is moved to the head of the clause ("and your hope the blamelessness of your ways"), and the Hebrew order of the two nouns is kept.
- zakar "remember" (the same English as mimneskomai). naqi "innocent". nikhchad "be wiped out" (it returns at 22:20).
- awen "harm" (never "iniquity"), kept distinct from ra "evil" and rasha "wicked". amal stays "misery" at 4:8, so that Eliphaz is heard taking up Job's word (3:10, 20); the pair returns at 5:6-7. charash "plow", as listed; zara "sow" (the same English as speiro); qatsar "harvest" (verb; the same English as therizo, and qatsir "harvest", 5:5, is to stand beside it).
- neshamah "breath", as in "the breath of life". ruach appo (4:9) "the wind of his anger": ruach is "wind", as at 1:19, and af is "anger" (the same English as orge). The word's other sense ("nose") is not noted: the breath of the nose is the Hebrew picture for anger, so it is one meaning in two wordings. kalah "come to an end".
- Five words for a lion in 4:10-11, kept distinct: aryeh "lion"; shachal "fierce lion"; kefir "young lion"; layish "strong lion"; lavi "lioness" (bene lavi "the cubs of the lioness"). she'agah "roar", as listed, so that the echo with 3:24 is heard; qol (4:10) "voice"; shen "tooth". nitta'u (4:10; the verb stands only here) "are broken", as is said of the teeth of young lions in Ps 58:6; the one verb serves all three subjects, as in the Hebrew. teref "prey"; mibbeli "for lack of". hitpared "be scattered".
- we- with a pronoun set first (4:12 we'elay) "Now to me". gunnav "be brought by stealth". shemets (4:12; 26:14) "whisper". laqach of the ear "take in".
- se'ippim (4:13; 20:2) "anxious thoughts". chizzayon "vision": chezyonot laylah "visions of the night". tardemah "deep sleep" and nafal al "fall on", as in Gen 2:21 (the line returns at 33:15). anashim "people". 4:13-14 is one sentence and is kept whole across the verse break.
- pachad stays "dread" (4:14), and hifchid is "fill with dread", so that Eliphaz's word is the one Job used (3:25). qara of what befalls a person "come over" (atah stays "come upon"). re'adah "trembling". rov atsmotai "all my bones".
- ruach (4:15) "a spirit" [alt: a wind] (decision log, call: close); chalaf al panay "pass before my face", as Yahweh passes before the face of Moses (Exod 34:6). simmer "bristle"; sa'arat besari "the hair of my flesh".
- amad (4:16), of what has been passing by, "stand still". hikkir "recognize", as listed; mar'eh "appearance"; temunah "form"; leneged enay "before my eyes". demamah "hush": demamah waqol eshma "I heard a hush, and a voice", both nouns left as objects of the verb, so that the English is as open as the Hebrew (silence and then a voice, or a hushed voice, as in 1 Kgs 19:12).
- enosh "a mortal", kept distinct from adam, ish and gever (gever stays "man"). tsadaq "be righteous", keeping the echo with "righteous". taher "be clean" (tahor "clean"; "pure" is left for zakah and zakh, 15:14-15; 25:4-5). osehu "his maker", lowercase like "savior". min with the two verbs of 4:17 "before" [alt: more ... than] (decision log, call: ask). ha- ... im, a double question, "Can ..., or ...?"
- he'emin be- "put trust in" (4:18; it returns at 15:15). mal'akh of a messenger from heaven "angel", as listed. sim tohalah be- "charge with error" (tohalah stands only here), beside natan tiflah le- "charge with wrongdoing" (1:22).
- af (4:19) "how much more". shakan of people "live" (shakan al stays "settle on"). bayit "house", chomer "clay", afar "dust", yesod "foundation". dikka "crush"; katat (4:20) "shatter": keep the two distinct. A verb with no subject named, "they crush them" (4:19), is put in the passive. lifne (4:19) "sooner than" [alt: like], as "before" [alt: like] at 3:24 (decision log, call: close). ash "moth".
- mibboqer la'erev "from morning to evening". mibbeli mesim (4:20) "without anyone considering it": mesim is short for the idiom "set the heart", which is "consider" at 1:8 and 2:3 (the short form also at 23:6; Isa 41:20). lanetsach "forever" (it shares its English with eis ton aiona).
- nissa yitram bam (4:21) "their tent cord is pulled up within them" [alt: the abundance that is in them is taken away] (decision log, call: clear). yeter "cord"; "tent" is supplied from the verb, which is the one for pulling up tent pegs (Isa 33:20; 38:12). chokhmah "wisdom"; welo vechokhmah "and without wisdom".

## Settled in Job 5
- Hebrew text: the Masoretic Text, read by its Ketiv. The one Qere (5:18, "his hands" for a spelling that can also be read "his hand") changes only the spelling, since the verb is plural, so nothing is noted. The main text keeps to the Masoretic vowels; where the same letters read another way say something different and fit the sentence, that reading is the alt (5:5 tsammim, 5:7 yullad), each with an entry in the decision log. Not noted: 5:15, where old versions have "from the sword of their mouth" for "from the sword, from their mouth" (the sense is the same); changes that no old copy has (5:23 "lords of the field" for "stones of the field"); and what the old Greek puts differently (5:3 "their dwelling was consumed"; 5:27 "this is what we have heard"), as in Job 2 to 4.
- Eliphaz's first speech ends at 5:27: the chapter opens with a quotation mark, as the Job 4 line says, and closes the speech.
- qara with no object (5:1) "call"; anah "answer", as in the speech formula; qedoshim "the holy ones" (again at 15:15), the same English as hoi hagioi; panah el "turn to".
- ewil "fool" (it shares its English with naval "foolish", 2:10: English has no second plain word); poteh "simpleton" (the root of peti). ka'as "resentment", kept distinct from af "anger" (Job takes the word up at 6:2; it returns at 10:17; 17:7, where grief may fit better). qin'ah "jealousy" (of zeal for something it stays "zeal", as with zelos). harag "kill", as listed; hemit "put to death".
- ani with a finite verb (5:3, 8) "I myself", as listed for an emphatic subject pronoun; hu (5:18) "he himself". hishrish "take root". qavav stays "put a curse on" (3:8). naweh "home" (5:3, 24; it returns at 8:6; 18:15), kept distinct from bayit "house" and ohel "tent". pit'om "suddenly".
- yesha "safety" (5:4, 11) and hoshia "save" (5:15), so that the echo is heard. bashsha'ar "in the gate", the place left as the Hebrew has it. hitstsil "rescue" (5:4 en matstsil "there is no one to rescue them", the object supplied; 5:19), kept distinct from nimlat "escape", hoshia "save" and padah "ransom".
- qatsir "harvest", beside qatsar (4:8). asher qetsiro (5:5) "his harvest": the relative clause is made a sentence of its own. Three words kept distinct: ra'ev "the hungry" and ra'av (5:20) "famine"; kafan (5:22; 30:3) "starvation". el-mi- "even from among"; tsinnim "thorns" (it shares its English with qots). sha'af "pant for" (again at 7:2). tsammim (5:5; 18:9) "snare" [alt at 5:5: the thirsty] (decision log, call: close). chayil of what a man owns "wealth".
- awen "harm" and amal "misery" (5:6-7), as at 4:8. yatsa "come out", tsamach "sprout", afar "dust", adamah "ground", as in Genesis. ki after a negative sentence (5:7) "but", with the sentence kept whole across the verse break.
- adam with no article, of people in general (5:7; 14:1) "a human being", as with anthropos, kept distinct from enosh "a mortal" and gever "man"; one bracketed note at 5:7 says that the word sounds like "ground" (5:6). yullad le- "is born to" [alt: begets] (decision log, call: ask). bene reshef "sparks": "sons of" is translated by its meaning, as with bene qedem, and reshef is flame in Song 8:6; the old versions have birds, which is not noted, since the comparison says the same thing. The "and" that sets a picture beside a saying (5:7b) is "as". higbiah uf "fly upward".
- El (5:8, and often in Job) "God", the same English as Elohim and Eloah, with nothing noted. darash el "seek", as listed. sim divrah el "put one's cause to": divrah "cause", leaving "case" for mishpat (13:18; 23:4). The imperfects of 5:8 are "would": Eliphaz says what he would do in Job's place.
- 5:8-11 is one sentence: the participles of 5:9-10 are "who does ..., who gives ..."; from 5:12 they are made sentences ("He frustrates", "He catches"). 5:9 returns almost word for word at 9:10. asah gedolot "do great things" (asah is "do" where "make" does not fit); en cheqer "cannot be searched out" and chaqar (5:27) "search out", so that the echo is heard; nifla'ot "wonders"; ad en mispar "beyond number".
- matar "rain"; al pene "on the face of"; shalach (qal) "send"; mayim "water"; chutsot outside a town "the open country" (sadeh stays "field").
- shafal "lowly"; marom "on high"; qoder (5:11) "one who mourns" (the root is "be dark", and of streams at 6:16 it needs another wording); sagav "be raised". lasum (5:11) "to set", the infinitive kept.
- hefer "frustrate"; machshavah "plan"; arum "shrewd", as listed, and orem (5:13) "shrewdness", keeping the echo; tushiyyah (5:12) "success" (elsewhere "sound wisdom" may fit: 11:6; 12:16; 26:3). lakad "catch"; chakham "wise", beside chokhmah; etsah "counsel", beside yo'ets "counselor"; niftal "devious"; nimhar "be rushed headlong".
- yomam "by day"; piggesh "meet"; mishshesh "grope" (pselaphao stays "feel"); tsohorayim "noon".
- cherev "sword"; mecherev mippihem (5:15) "from the sword, from their mouth", both phrases left as they stand. yad chazaq "the hand of the strong". evyon "the needy"; dal "the poor". A wayyiqtol that carries on the description of what God always does (5:15-16) is put in the English present. awlah "injustice", kept distinct from tiflah "wrongdoing" and adikia "unrighteousness". qafats peh "shut one's mouth" (it shares its English with sagar).
- ashre "happy is", kept distinct from barukh "blessed". hokhiach of God (5:17; 22:4) "correct"; where it is arguing a case (13:3, 15) another wording will fit. ma'as "reject".
- musar (5:17) "discipline": of God's correcting through pain, "instruction" would say too little. So the echo with yissar "instruct" (4:3) is given in one bracketed note at 5:17, and is not kept in the wording as the Job 4 line planned.
- Shadday (5:17, the first of thirty-one times in Job) "the Almighty", with [alt: Shaddai] once, at 5:17 (decision log, call: ask). Under the rule against standing brackets the bracket is not repeated; until Obay decides, later chapters write "the Almighty".
- hikh'iv "cause pain", beside ke'ev "pain" (2:13); chavash "bind up"; machats "wound", kept distinct from hikkah "strike"; rafa "heal".
- tsarah in the plural (5:19) "troubles". ra (5:19) "evil"; naga be- "touch", as listed, so that the echo with 1:11, 2:5 and 4:5 is heard. padah "ransom" (ga'al stays "reclaim", with "redeem" left open); the perfect padekha (5:20), set among futures, is "he will ransom you". milchamah "war". mide cherev "from the power of the sword": "the hands of" a thing is translated by its meaning; yad of a person stays "hand" (5:15).
- be- at the head of 5:19, 20 and 21 is "in" each time. shot lashon "the lashing of the tongue". nechba "be hidden" (a third root that shares "hide"; Gen 3:8, 10). shod "destruction" (5:21, 22; it is the word that Isa 13:6 and Joel 1:15 set beside Shadday). yare min "be afraid of": lo tira (5:21) "you will not be afraid", al tira (5:22) "do not be afraid", keeping the two negatives distinct. sachaq le- "laugh at".
- berit "covenant". avne hassadeh "the stones of the field", as the Hebrew has it. hoshlam le- "be brought to peace with" and shalom (5:24) "at peace", keeping the echo.
- yada ki "know that". ohel "tent". paqad of going over what one owns (5:24) "inspect". lo techeta (5:24) "find nothing missing" [alt: you will not sin] (decision log, call: close); chata elsewhere stays "sin".
- zera "offspring", as listed; tse'etsa'im "descendants"; rav "many"; esev "plants", as in Genesis 1-3.
- bekhelach (5:26; kelach returns at 30:2) "in full vigor" [alt: in ripe old age] (decision log, call: close). qever "grave", as listed. gadish "a stack of grain"; alah "go up"; be'itto "in its season".
- A pronoun set first and picked up again (5:27 zot chaqarnuha) takes a dash, as at 3:4. ken hi "so it is". we'attah da lakh "and you, know it for yourself".

## Settled in Job 6
- Hebrew text: the Masoretic Text, read by its Ketiv. 6:21: the Ketiv lo "not" is followed ("you have now become nothing"), and the Qere lo "to it" is the alt ("you have become like it"), with an entry in the decision log. Two other Qere give the same English, so nothing is noted: 6:2 (hayyati / hawwati, two spellings of "my ruin", as at 30:13) and 6:29 (weshuvi / weshuvu, both the imperative "turn back"). The main text keeps to the Masoretic vowels: 6:18 orchot "the paths of" [alt: Caravans]. Not noted: 6:14, where several ancient versions have "whoever withholds loyalty from his friend" (its sense is the alt there); 6:20, where the Hebrew has a singular "he trusted" among plurals (put as "they had trusted"); 6:25, the reading "how pleasant", with the verb of Ps 119:103; and what the old Greek puts differently (6:6 "empty words"; 6:7; 6:10), as in Job 2 to 5.
- Job's second speech is 6:2-7:21: chapter 6 ends with no closing quotation mark, as the Job 4 line says. Words quoted inside it take single marks (6:22-23).
- lu (6:2) and mi yitten as a wish (6:8; it returns at 11:5; 13:5; 14:13; 19:23; 23:3) both "If only": the idiom "who will give" is translated by its meaning. An infinitive absolute inside a wish (6:2 shaqol yishshaqel) "actually", since "certainly" does not fit a wish.
- ka'as stays "resentment" (6:2): Job takes up Eliphaz's word (5:2). shaqal "weigh"; mo'znayim "scales"; nasa bemo'znayim "lift in the scales"; yachad (6:2) "together with it". An unnamed plural subject (6:2 yis'u) is put in the passive, as at 4:19.
- hawwah "ruin" (6:2; 30:13); the plural hawwot (6:30) "what is ruinous", left as open as the Hebrew (the ruin that has come on him, or ruinous words, as in Ps 5:9; 52:2). Kept distinct from ra'ah "disaster", shod "destruction" and amal "misery".
- ki attah after a wish (6:3) "For then" (3:13 "For by now"; 4:5 "But now"); 6:21 "For you have now", since "For now" would be heard as "for the time being". chol yammim "the sand of the seas". kaved min "be heavier than". al-ken "that is why", as in Gen 2:24 (lakhen stays "therefore"). la'a (6:3) "be rash" (the verb of Prov 20:25); the other verb with these letters, "swallow", is not noted, since "my words swallow" is not a possible sentence.
- chets "arrow". immadi (6:4) "in me". chemah of arrows "poison" (its other sense, "wrath", is not noted; compare 21:20). A relative clause is made a sentence of its own (6:4), as at 5:5. bi'utim "terrors", beside bi'et "terrify". arakh with a suffix (6:4) "be lined up against".
- nahaq "bray"; pere "wild donkey"; deshe of what an animal eats "grass" ("vegetation" in Gen 1:11); ga'ah "low"; shor "ox" (it shares its English with baqar "oxen"); belil "fodder". ha- ... im "Does ..., or ...?", as at 4:17.
- tafel "what is tasteless"; melach "salt"; ta'am "taste". rir challamut (6:6; the second word stands only here) "the white of an egg": rir is the spittle of 1 Sam 21:13. The other understanding, the slimy juice of a plant, is not noted, since the comparison says the same thing (as with "sparks", 5:7).
- me'en "refuse". 6:7 is read as two sentences ("My soul refuses to touch them; they are like ..."), as the same words stand in Ps 77:2; the other construction ("what my soul refused to touch is like ...") is not noted, as at 3:16. The object of "touch" ("them") is supplied. devay "sickness": kidve lachmi "like sickness in my bread"; lechem stays "bread", as at 3:24.
- she'elah "request" (6:8 "what I ask for"); tiqwah stays "hope" (6:8 "what I hope for"). ho'il "be willing" (6:9, 28), so that the echo is heard. dikka "crush", as listed (4:19; 5:4). hittir yad "let loose one's hand". bitstsa "cut off" (the weaver's word of Isa 38:12; it returns at 27:8).
- nechamah "comfort", beside nicham. silled (6:10; only here) "leap for joy" [alt: recoil, or steel myself] (decision log, call: close). chilah "anguish", kept distinct from ke'ev and etsev "pain". chamal "spare": lo yachmol (6:10; 16:13; 27:22) "he does not spare", with God as the subject, the verb being masculine and "anguish" feminine.
- kichad (piel) "conceal", kept distinct from the roots that share "hide" (satar, taman, nechba; and now hit'allem, 6:16 "hide itself"); nikhchad stays "be wiped out" (4:7). emer (imre-) "words" (6:10, 25), sharing its English with davar and millah; where it stands beside millim (6:26) "sayings". qadosh of God (6:10) "the Holy One", as at 1 Jn 2:20.
- yichel (6:11) "wait" (it shares its English with chikkah "wait for"; qiwwah stays "hope for"). qets "end". he'erikh nefesh (6:11) "be patient": the idiom ("lengthen the soul", the opposite of the "short" soul of Num 21:4 and Judg 16:16) is translated by its meaning. even "stone"; nachush "bronze" (adjective), beside nechoshet.
- ha'im (6:13; Num 17:13) opens an anxious question: "Is there no ...?". ezrah "help". tushiyyah stays "success" (5:12). niddach "be driven away" (garash stays "drive out").
- mas (6:14; only here) "one who is losing heart": the word is from the verb "melt", which is said of the heart (Josh 2:11; Ps 22:14). chesed (6:14, its first occurrence) "loyalty": what is owed to those one is bound to, set here against treachery (6:15); of God's chesed "faithful love" may fit better (10:12; 37:13). azav "abandon" (the same English as apoleipo), not "forsake". yir'at Shadday "the fear of the Almighty". The "he" of 6:14b is read as the one losing heart ("even if he abandons") [alt: or that friend abandons] (decision log, call: ask).
- ach "brother". bagad "be treacherous" ("betray" stays for paradidomi). nachal "torrent": a streambed that runs in the rains and is dry in summer; afiq "channel". avar of water (6:15; 11:16) "pass away". qadar of water (6:16) "be dark"; qerach "ice"; sheleg "snow".
- zorav (6:17; only here) "be scorched", as its pair "when it is hot" suggests. nitsmat "be brought to an end" (kalah stays "come to an end"). nid'akh "be snuffed out": the verb of a lamp going out (18:5-6; 21:17). maqom "place".
- orach "path" (derekh stays "way"); nilpat (6:18) "wind about". tohu as a place (6:18; 12:24) "the waste". alah "go up", as listed. 6:18 "The paths of their way wind about" [alt: Caravans turn aside from their way] (decision log, call: close).
- 6:19 orchot Tema "the caravans of Tema": the Masoretic vowels say "paths", standing for those who travel them. halikhot "travelers". hibbit "look". Names: Tema "Tema"; Sheva "Sheba", as at 1:15. The perfects of 6:19-20 are a past scene.
- bosh "be ashamed" (the same English as aischynomai) and chafer "be humiliated", kept distinct; neither is softened to "be disappointed" (compare Jer 14:3). batach "trust" (he'emin be- stays "put trust in"). adeha (6:20) "to it", the singular left as the Hebrew has it.
- chatat (6:21; only here) "a horror", kept distinct from pachad "dread" and bi'utim "terrors". One bracketed note at 6:21 says that "you see" and "you are afraid" sound alike.
- haki (6:22) "Have I ...?". yahav "give" (havu li "Give me something", the object supplied). koach of what a person owns (6:22) "means"; elsewhere "strength" (6:11, 12). shachad "pay a bribe"; be'ad (6:22) "for", as at 2:4. Under a question that expects "no", the Hebrew "and" between the things Job did not say is "or".
- millet (piel) "deliver", beside nimlat "escape"; hitstsil stays "rescue", hoshia "save", padah "ransom". tsar "foe" (leaving "enemy" for oyev and "adversary" for satan). arits "ruthless".

## Settled in Job 7
- Hebrew text: the Masoretic Text, read by its Ketiv. The two Qere change only the spelling, so nothing is noted: 7:1 (al / ale "on") and 7:5 (gish / gush, two spellings of "clod"). 7:20: the Masoretic alay "to myself" is the main text; "to you" (the old Greek, a few Hebrew copies, and the scribes' own note that the wording was changed) is the alt, with an entry in the decision log (call: clear). The main text keeps to the Masoretic vowels. Not noted: 7:4, where middad can also be taken with Job's question ("When will I rise, and the evening be gone?"), since both say that the night will not end, as at 3:16 and 6:7; 7:15, the change of "my bones" to "my pains", which no old copy has; and what the old Greek puts differently (7:4, 15), as in Job 2 to 6.
- Job's second speech ends at 7:21: the chapter opens with a quotation mark, as the Job 4 line says, and closes the speech. From 7:7 Job speaks to God ("Remember" is singular); the friends were "you" in the plural (6:21-29). English does not show the change, and nothing is added.
- tsava (7:1; 14:14; compare 10:17) "a soldier's service": the word is an army and the term a man serves in it (Num 1:3; Isa 40:2), and "soldier's" keeps that; "service" alone would be heard of any work. sakir "hired worker" (7:1, 2; 14:6). halo governs both halves of 7:1 ("Is there not ..., and are his days not ...?").
- eved beside sakir (7:2) "slave", as at 3:19: the man who is owned beside the man who is hired. sha'af "pant for", as listed (5:5). tsel "shadow" (7:2; 8:9; 14:2; 17:7): the shade a worker longs for and the evening shadow that ends his day (Jer 6:4) are one word, and "shadow" leaves both open. po'al (7:2) "pay": the work stands for what is paid for it (Jer 22:13); elsewhere "work" or "deed". qiwwah stays "hope for", so that it is heard beside tiqwah "hope" (7:6).
- 7:2-3 is one sentence ("Like ..., so ..."). honchal (the passive causative of nachal) "be made to inherit": the image is kept, and the li that goes with it is carried by "inherit". shaw "emptiness" (7:3; it returns at 11:11; 15:31; 31:5; 35:13, where "falsehood" may fit better), kept distinct from hevel. minnah "appoint" (the verb of Jonah 1:17); an unnamed plural subject is put in the passive, as at 4:19 and 6:2.
- im with a perfect of what happens again and again (7:4) "When"; ki amarti (7:13) "When I say", with its main clause opened by "then" (7:14). middad erev (7:4) "the evening stretches on": the verb is "measure", used of stretching out in 1 Kgs 17:21. sava "have one's fill of" (it shares its English with empiplemi; seva, 14:1, "full of"). nedudim (only here) "tossing". neshef (7:4) "the morning twilight": the word is "twilight", as at 3:9, and "morning" is supplied, since in English "twilight" alone is heard as dusk.
- lavash "be clothed with". rimmah "maggots" (a collective; tole'ah, 25:6, is to be "worm"). gush (only here) "clod": gush afar "clods of dust", afar staying "dust" so that 7:5 is heard with 7:21. or "skin". raga of skin (7:5) "break out": the verb of the sea stirred up (26:12; Isa 51:15); the sense "harden", which it has nowhere else in Scripture, is not noted. nim'as (7:5) "run" [alt: be loathsome] (decision log, call: close); ma'as stays "reject" (7:16).
- qalal "be swift" (7:6; 9:25). ereg "a weaver's shuttle". kalah stays "come to an end" (7:6, 9). be'efes "without". tiqwah (7:6) "hope" [alt: thread], with a bracketed note that the one word means both (decision log, kind: both meant, call: clear).
- ruach (7:7) "wind", of a life that passes and does not return (Ps 78:39); in 7:11 "spirit". shuv with le- and an infinitive (7:7) "again"; lo ... od (7:10) "no longer". shur (7:8) "catch sight of", kept distinct from ra'ah "see". we'enenni (7:8, 21) "and I will be no more", the same English both times, so that the end of the speech is heard taking up 7:8.
- anan "cloud"; halakh of a cloud "be gone". yarad "go down"; alah of coming back from the dead (7:9) "come up". She'ol (7:9, its first occurrence) "Sheol": a name, as English has it, like Leviathan; it never takes the article in Hebrew, and "grave" stays for qever. No bracket: "the grave" or "the world of the dead" would be the same place in other words.
- gam ani (7:11) "I, for my part". chasakh "restrain" (atsar be- stays "hold back", chamal "spare"). tsar (noun; 7:11) "distress" (tsar "foe" stays at 6:23). The cohortatives of 7:11 are "I will". siach "complain" and siach (noun; 7:13) "complaint", keeping the echo. mar nafshi "the bitterness of my soul" (it returns at 10:1).
- yam "the sea"; tannin "sea monster", as in Gen 1:21, without the article as in the Hebrew. mishmar "a guard", beside shamar "guard".
- eres "couch"; mishkav "bed" (beside shakhav "lie down"). nasa be- (7:13) "help carry" (Num 11:17). chittat (piel) "frighten" (bi'et stays "terrify", nivhal "be dismayed"); chalom "dream". 7:13-15 is one sentence.
- bachar "choose"; machanaq (only here) "strangling". mawet me'atsmotay (7:15) "death rather than my bones": bachar with min, as in Jer 8:3.
- ma'asti with no object (7:16) "I reject my life": the object is supplied from 9:21, where the same verb has it; "it" would be heard as "death". lo le'olam echyeh "I will not live forever", a statement, as the reason that follows shows ("for my days are a breath"). le'olam "forever" (it shares its English with lanetsach). chadal min (7:16) "leave alone": the idiom ("cease from") is translated by its meaning. hevel (7:16) "a breath": the puff of air that is gone at once (Ps 144:4); it shares its English with neshamah, and ruach stays "wind".
- giddel (7:17) "make so much of". shit lev el (7:17) "consider", the same English as sim lev al / el (1:8; 2:3), so that Job is heard complaining of the very thing Yahweh said of him. paqad stays "inspect" (5:24). livqarim "every morning"; lirga'im "every moment". bachan "test" (the same English as dokimazo).
- kammah (7:19) "How long". sha'ah min "look away from" (14:6), beside sha'ah el "look at". hirpah "let go of" (27:6). ad bil'i ruqqi "long enough for me to swallow my spit": ad is "for as long as", as in 20:5. bala "swallow"; roq "spit".
- chata'ti (7:20), with no "if" in the Hebrew, "If I have sinned" [alt: I have sinned] (decision log, call: ask). pa'al "do" (it shares its English with asah). notser ha'adam "watcher of humankind": natsar "watch", left as open as the Hebrew (one who keeps, or one who keeps watch on); ha'adam of the race "humankind". mifga (only here) "target". massa "burden".
- nasa pesha (7:21) "carry away rebellion": the picture of sin lifted and carried off (Lev 16:22) is kept, so that it is heard with "burden" (7:20) and "help carry" (7:13); "forgive" is the same meaning in other words. pesha "rebellion" (the noun of the verb in 1 Kgs 12:19; Isa 1:2); between people "offense" may fit better. he'evir avon "take away guilt" (2 Sam 24:10; Zech 3:4). ki attah with an imperfect (7:21) "For I will now", as at 6:21. shichar "search for" (it returns at 8:5; 24:5), kept distinct from darash "seek" and chaqar "search out".

## Settled in Job 8
- Hebrew text: the Masoretic Text; the chapter has no Qere. The main text keeps to the Masoretic vowels: 8:21 ad with an imperfect is "until" (as at 14:6), running on from 8:20; the same letters read as od ("He will yet fill") are not noted, since both promise Job the same thing. 8:7 yisgeh is masculine though its subject is feminine, and stays "will grow". Not noted: changes that no old copy has (8:14 a noun "gossamer" for the verb; 8:17 "takes hold of" for "gazes on"), and what the old Greek puts differently (8:6 "he will hear your plea"; 8:13 "the end" for "the paths", against Prov 1:19; 8:17 "lives among the stones"; 8:19), as in Job 2 to 7.
- Bildad's first speech is 8:2-22, all in this chapter. Words quoted inside it take single marks (8:18).
- ad-an (8:2; ad-anah 18:2; 19:2) "How long", the same English as kammah (7:19). millel "utter" (the verb of millah "word"; it returns at 33:3). emer stays "sayings" in imre-fikha "the sayings of your mouth", as at 6:26, so that Bildad is heard taking up Job's "sayings ... as wind". kabbir "mighty" (ruach kabbir "a mighty wind"), kept distinct from gadol "great" (1:19).
- iwwet "pervert", the same English both times in 8:3 (it returns at 34:12; at 19:6, with a person as its object, another wording will be needed). mishpat "justice" (8:3); "case" stays for a lawsuit (13:18; 23:4). tsedeq stays "righteousness" (8:3), so that it is heard with 6:29 and 8:6.
- banekha (8:4) "your sons", as at 1:5, so that "Perhaps my sons have sinned" is heard behind it. chata le- "sin against". shillach (piel) of people sent to their end (8:4; 14:20) "send away". beyad pish'am "into the power of their rebellion": "the hand of" a thing is translated by its meaning, as at 5:20; pesha stays "rebellion" (7:21). 8:4 is read as a sentence of its own ("If ..., he sent them away"); taking the whole verse as one more condition for 8:5-6, or beyad as "by means of" (Lev 16:21), is not noted, since each says that their own rebellion carried them off.
- shichar el (8:5) "search for", as listed, so that Bildad is heard turning Job's last words around (7:21). hitchannen el "plead with" (it returns at 9:15; 19:16).
- 8:5-6 is one sentence: two "if" clauses, and then ki attah opens the main clause, "surely now" (3:13 "For by now"; 6:3 "For then"). zakh "pure", as the Job 4 line planned. he'ir with no object (8:6) "rouse himself", as in Ps 35:23; alekha "for you". shillem (8:6) "restore" (Joel 2:25); of paying back it will be "repay" (21:19, 31; 34:11). newat tsidqekha "your righteous home": a noun of quality with a suffix, set after another noun, is an adjective in English, as in "my holy mountain"; naweh stays "home" (5:3, 24).
- reshit "beginning" (the same English as arche); acharit "latter days" (8:7; the pair returns at 42:12), kept distinct from qets "end". mits'ar "a small thing". sagah "grow" (8:7, 11), the same English both times; yisgeh me'od "grow very great".
- sha'al le- (8:8) "ask of". dor "generation"; dor rishon "an earlier generation". konen le- (8:8) "set one's mind on": short for the idiom with "heart" (11:13), like mesim at 4:20. cheqer avotam "what their fathers searched out", cheqer staying beside chaqar "search out" (5:9, 27). av "father".
- temol anachnu (8:9) "we are of yesterday". lo neda with no object "do not know". tsel "shadow" and ale-arets "on earth", as at 7:1-2.
- hem with a finite verb (8:10) "they themselves", as listed. yomeru lakh with no object "tell you". hotsi "bring out". millibbam "from their heart", singular as in the Hebrew.
- gome "papyrus"; bitstsah "marsh" (40:21); achu "reed grass" (Gen 41:2, 18), leaving "reed" for qaneh. ga'ah of a plant "grow tall". belo "where there is no"; beli "without". ha- governs both halves of 8:11, and each is put as a question.
- ev (8:12; Song 6:11) "greenness": odennu be'ibbo "while it is still green". qataf "pick". yavesh of a plant "wither" (of roots or water "dry up"). lifne (8:12) "sooner than", as at 4:19, with no alt: here it can only be about time. chatsir "grass" (it shares its English with deshe).
- ken (8:13) "So". shakhach "forget". chanef (8:13; 13:16; 15:34; 17:8; 20:5; 27:8; 34:30; 36:13) "godless", the same English as asebes; alone, of one person, "the godless man". Not "hypocrite": in Scripture the word stands beside evildoers and sinners (Isa 9:17; 33:14), not beside pretenders. 8:13-14 is one sentence.
- yaqot (8:14; only here) "break": the sentence has to decide, and the next verse says that what he leans on does not stand. Read as a noun for a spider's thread, the line says the same thing, so nothing is noted (as at 6:6). kesel "confidence", beside kislah (4:6); mivtach "trust" (noun), beside batach (6:20). bet akkavish "a spider's house": "house" is kept (not "web"), since the speech goes on with "his house" (8:15) and "a house of stones" (8:17).
- nish'an al "lean on". amad (8:15) "stand"; qum of what should hold (8:15; 15:29) "stay up". hecheziq be- stays "hold fast to" (8:15), as at 2:3, 9; hecheziq beyad (8:20) "hold the hand of". The subject of "stand" and "stay up" is read as the house; the Hebrew can also mean the man, and nothing is noted, since either way the house gives no support.
- ratov (8:16; only here) "full of sap"; the plant of 8:16-19 is "he", as the man of 8:13-15 is. lifne shemesh "before the sun", left as open as the Hebrew (Ps 72:17). yoneqet (a collective; 14:7; 15:30) "shoots". gannah "garden" (the same English as gan). yatsa of shoots "go out".

- An unnamed singular subject (8:18 yevalle'ennu) is put in the passive, like the plurals at 4:19 and 6:2: "If he is swallowed up"; billa stays "swallow up" (2:3). kichesh be- "deny" (31:28), the same English as arneomai. maqom "place", as listed: his place speaks, as at 7:10.
- masos (8:19) "gladness", beside sus "be glad" (3:22), kept distinct from simchah (20:5). The same letters as a form of the verb "melt" (Isa 10:18) are not noted: the gladness is one that ends (20:5), so both say that his way comes to nothing. hen stays "Look" (8:19, 20).
- 8:19 ume'afar acher yitsmachu "and from the dust others sprout" [alt: and from other dust they sprout] (decision log, call: ask). afar "dust" and tsamach "sprout", as at 5:6, so that Bildad is heard using Eliphaz's words.
- tam alone (8:20) "a blameless man". ma'as stays "reject" (5:17; 7:16). mere'im "evildoers". The imperfects of 8:20 are "will", because 8:21 runs on from them. sechoq "laughter", beside sachaq "laugh" (5:22); safah "lip"; teru'ah "shouting" ("of joy" is not added; renanah stays "cry of joy").
- sane "hate" (son'ekha "those who hate you"; the same English as miseo). lavash stays "be clothed with" (7:5). boshet "shame", beside bosh "be ashamed" (6:20). enennu (8:22) "will be no more", so that Bildad's last word is heard answering Job's (7:21).

## Settled in Job 9
- Hebrew text: the Masoretic Text, read by its Ketiv. 9:30: the Ketiv bemo sheleg "with snow" is followed; the Qere beme sheleg "with snow water" is not noted, since the washing is the same. The other Qere (9:13 tachto / tachtav "under him") changes only the spelling. The main text keeps to the Masoretic vowels and letters, and three readings that differ from them are alts, each with an entry in the decision log: 9:17 bis'arah "with a storm" [alt: for a hair]; 9:19 yo'ideni "summon me" [alt: summon him]; 9:33 lo yesh "There is no" [alt: If only there were]. Not noted: 9:15, where the old Greek has "he will not listen to me" for "I could not answer" (the Masoretic lo e'eneh returns word for word at 40:5); 9:19, the reading of hinneh as "look, it is he" (the sense is the same); 9:20, the change of "my mouth" to "his mouth", which no old copy has; and what the old Greek puts differently (9:21-23; 9:26-27; 9:29; 9:31 "in filth" for "in the pit"), as in Job 2 to 8.
- Job's third speech is 9:2-10:22: chapter 9 ends with no closing quotation mark, as the Job 4 line says. Words quoted inside it take single marks (9:12, 22, 27). At 9:28 and 9:31 Job speaks to God ("you" is singular), and at 9:32 he goes back to "he"; English does not show who "you" is, and nothing is added (the Job 7 line).
- omnam "Truly" (it shares its English with alethos). ki-khen "that it is so", beside ken hi "so it is" (5:27). tsadaq im (9:2; 25:4) "be righteous with": tsadaq stays "be righteous" (4:17), so that Job is heard taking up the voice of Eliphaz's vision, and im is "with", as in "contend with him" (9:3) and "my words with him" (9:14); "before" stays for the min of 4:17. mah with a verb (9:2) "how".
- chafets (9:3; 13:3; 21:14; 33:32) "wish" (it shares its English with boulomai). riv im "contend with" (13:19; 23:6; 40:2; riv with other constructions at 10:2; 13:8; 33:13), kept distinct from hokhiach. achat minni-alef "one time in a thousand" (achat is "once" at 40:5). im with the imperfects of 9:3 is put as a supposed case ("If he wished ..., he could not"), and so are 9:14-16.
- 9:3 lo ya'anennu "he could not answer him" [alt: God would not answer him] (decision log, call: clear). That the one who wishes to contend might be God is not noted, since on that reading too it is the man who cannot answer.
- 9:4: the description set first is picked up by "him" and takes a dash, as at 3:4. It is not noted that it could be read of the one who resists ("however wise in heart ..."): the English is as open as the Hebrew, and ammits is said of God at 9:19 (ammits koach also in Isa 40:26). chakham levav "wise in heart". ammits (9:4, 19) "mighty": it shares its English with kabbir (8:2). koach stays "strength". hiqshah el "harden oneself against": what is hardened (the neck, the heart) is left unsaid in the Hebrew. shalam (qal; 9:4) "come out whole"; at 22:21 "be at peace" may fit better.
- The participles of 9:5-10 are made sentences ("He moves ...", "He makes ..."), as at 5:12; the perfect and the imperfects among them are put in the English present. he'tiq "move" (14:18; 18:4). har "mountain". welo yada'u (9:5) "and they do not know it": it comes on them unawares, as in Ps 35:8. asher (9:5) is made a sentence of its own, as at 5:5; read as "that" ("they do not know that he has overturned them") it says the same thing, and nothing is noted. hafakh "overturn" (the same English as anatrepo). hirgiz "shake" (rogez stays "turmoil"). ammud "pillar". hitpallets (only here) "shudder" (pallatsut, 21:6, is to stand beside it), kept distinct from re'adah "trembling".
- amar le- with no words given (9:7) "speak to". cheres (9:7) "the sun": it shares its English with shemesh (8:16). zarach "rise" (of the sun). chatam be'ad (9:7) "seal up": be'ad as in "shut the door behind" (2 Kgs 4:4); chatam returns at 14:17; 33:16; 37:7. kokhav "star".
- natah "stretch out". shamayim (9:8) "the heavens", as listed for Gen 1:1. levaddo "by himself" (1:15 "only I alone"). darakh "tread". bamot "heights" (Amos 4:13; Mic 1:3; Isa 14:14): bamote yam "the heights of the sea", yam without the article as at 7:12.
- Ash, Kesil, Kimah (9:9; 38:31-32; Amos 5:8) "the Bear, Orion and the Pleiades": English names are used, since the chapter files allow no transliteration; which stars are meant is not certain, and nothing is noted. cheder "chamber" (37:9); teman "south".
- 9:10 repeats 5:9, and the English repeats it; the changes in the Hebrew are shown: ad-en cheqer "beyond searching out" (5:9 we'en cheqer "that cannot be searched out"), and the "and" before "wonders".
- avar al (9:11) "pass by" (avar of water stays "pass away"). chalaf (9:11; 11:10) "sweep on"; of days (9:26) "sweep past": the verb of a storm or a flood moving through (Isa 8:8; 21:1); at 4:15 it stays "pass". The object of "see" ("him") is supplied in 9:11. bin le- (9:11; 14:21; 23:8) "discern", as at 6:30.
- chataf (9:12; only here) "snatch away". heshiv with a person or with anger as its object (9:12, 13; 11:10; 23:13) "turn back", the same English as shuv at 6:29, so that 9:12 and 9:13 are heard together; heshiv ruach (9:18) "get one's breath back". mi yeshivennu is "who can turn him back?", as at 11:10 and 23:13; "who can make him give it back" is not noted. mah-ta'aseh "What are you doing?"
- shachach "bow low" (hishtachawah stays "bow down"). ozer "helper", beside ezrah "help" (6:13). Rahav (9:13; 26:12) "Rahab": a name, as English has it, like Leviathan [alt at 9:13: the helpers of the proud] (decision log, call: clear). Under the rule against standing brackets the bracket is not repeated at 26:12.
- af ki after a negative statement (9:14) "How much less" (4:19 af "how much more"). anokhi with a finite verb (9:14, 29) "I myself", as listed for ani. bachar devarim im "choose one's words with".
- asher at the head of 9:15 and of 9:17 points back to "him": in 9:15 it is made a sentence of its own ("I could not answer him"); in 9:17 it is "he who", running on from 9:16. im of what is granted (9:15, 20) "Though". meshofet (9:15; only here in this form) "judge", the same English as shofet (9:24; 23:7). hitchannen le- "plead with", as at 8:5: Job takes up Bildad's word.
- qara "call" and anah "answer" (9:16), as at 5:1. he'emin ki "believe that" (he'emin be- stays "put trust in"). he'ezin "listen to" (shama stays "hear").
- shuf (9:17) "strike", as listed for Gen 3:15. se'arah (9:17, spelled with sin as in Nah 1:3; with samekh at 38:1; 40:6) "storm" [alt: for a hair] (decision log, call: close). hirbah "multiply". petsa "wound" (noun; it shares its English with machats). chinnam "for nothing", as at 1:9 and 2:3: Job says of God what Yahweh said to the adversary.
- hisbia (9:18) "give one's fill of", beside sava (7:4). mamrorim (only here) "bitter things", like kimrire yom (3:5). ki after a negative (9:18) "but", as at 5:7.
- 9:19: im le- "If it is a matter of"; hinneh "look", with "he is" supplied. mishpat (9:19) stays "justice" (8:3); bammishpat (9:32) "into court" (bo bemishpat im, 14:3; 22:4, may need "into judgment"). ho'id (9:19; Jer 49:19; 50:44) "summon": mi yo'ideni "who will summon me" [alt: who will summon him] (decision log, call: clear). The Hebrew can also be heard as God's own words quoted, as they are his in Jeremiah; the English is the same, and that is said in the decision log, not in the chapter.
- hirshia with a person as its object "condemn" (9:20; 10:2; 15:6; 40:8); qal rasha of the verdict (9:29) "be condemned", elsewhere "be wicked" (10:7, 15). Their echo with rasha "wicked" (9:22, 24) is not shown. he'qish (only here) "declare crooked"; iqqesh "crooked", kept distinct from niftal "devious". The subject of "declare me crooked" is read as "my mouth" [alt: he] (decision log, call: close). pi (9:20) "my mouth", with no "own" added.
- lo eda nafshi (9:21) "I do not care about myself": yada with nefesh as its object is to have regard for a life (Prov 12:10; compare Gen 39:6; Ps 31:7), so the idiom is translated by its meaning, and nafshi is "myself". em'as chayyay "I reject my life", as the Job 7 line said.
- achat hi (9:22) "It is all one". killah (piel) "make an end of", so that it is heard with kalah "come to an end" (4:9; 7:6, 9); nitsmat stays "be brought to an end". tam werasha "the blameless and the wicked".
- shot (9:23) "scourge" (5:21 "lashing"). hemit with no object (9:23) "bring death" (5:2 "put to death"). massah (9:23) "testing" [alt: despair] (decision log, call: ask). la'ag le- "mock at", kept distinct from sachaq le- "laugh at".
- erets (9:24) "the earth". nittan beyad "be given into the hand of". kissah "cover". shofet "judge". The "he" of "he covers" is left as open as the Hebrew (God, as the question that follows shows, or the wicked). im-lo efo (9:24; 24:25) "If not, then"; mi-hu "who is it?"
- rats "runner". barach "flee". tovah (9:25) "good", the same English as tov (7:7). The perfects of 9:25-26 are put in the English present, as at 7:6; the "And" that opens 9:25 is kept, so that it is told apart from 7:6.
- im of things compared (9:26) "like". oniyyot eveh "reed boats": oniyyah is "ship" elsewhere; eveh (only here) is near ev "greenness", said of papyrus and reed grass at 8:12, and Isa 18:2 has papyrus vessels on the water. nesher "eagle"; tus (only here) "swoop"; okhel "food".
- im omri (9:27) "If I say". azav panim (9:27) "abandon one's sad face": panim is the look on a face, as in 1 Sam 1:18, and "sad" is supplied; azav stays "abandon". hivlig (9:27; 10:20; Ps 39:13) "be cheerful". The cohortatives are "I will", as at 7:11. 9:27-28 is one sentence, and so is 9:30-31.
- yagor stays "shrink from" (3:25). atsevet "pain" (it shares its English with etsev and ke'ev). niqqah "hold innocent" (9:28; 10:14), beside naqi "innocent" (9:23).
- lammah-zeh "why then". hevel as an adverb (9:29; 21:34; 27:12; 35:16) "in vain"; as a noun it stays "a breath" (7:16). yaga "toil" (the root of "worn out", 3:17).
- hitrachets "wash oneself"; sheleg "snow"; hezakh "make pure", beside zakh "pure" (8:6); kaf "palm", kept distinct from yad "hand" (kaf regel stays "the sole of the foot"). bor (9:30) "lye", as in Isa 1:25 and beside "with snow"; the same letters are "cleanness" at 22:30 ("the cleanness of your palms"), which is not noted, since both say that he makes his hands clean.
- az (9:31) "then". shachat "the pit" (17:14; 33:18-30). taval "dip". ti'ev "detest" (19:19; 30:10); salmah "clothes".
- ish kamoni "a man like me". bo yachdaw bammishpat (9:32) "come together into court".
- mokhiach (9:33) "arbiter": the one who decides between two parties (Gen 31:37, with the same "the two of us"); hokhiach of God stays "correct" (5:17). shit yad al "lay one's hand on"; shenenu "us both". The jussives of 9:33-34 are "who might" and "Let him"; the cohortative that follows (9:35) is "Then I would". lo yesh "There is no" [alt: If only there were] (decision log, call: clear).
- hesir "remove" (he'evir stays "take away"). shevet "rod". emah (9:34; 13:21; 33:7) "terror": it shares its English with bi'utim "terrors" (6:4), and with bi'et "terrify" beside it the two English words are closer than the two Hebrew ones. The "him" of 9:34 is left as open as the Hebrew (God, as 13:21 shows, or the arbiter).
- lo-khen anokhi immadi (9:35) "I am not so in myself": immadi "in myself", as at 6:4, of what a person has within (10:13; 23:14; 27:11), and ken "so", as at 9:2. The English is as short as the Hebrew, and nothing is noted.

## Settled in Job 10
- Hebrew text: the Masoretic Text, read by its Ketiv. 10:20: the Ketiv yechdal yashit, about God in the third person, is followed ("Let him cease; let him withdraw from me"), and the Qere wachadal weshit, two commands, is the alt, with an entry in the decision log (call: clear). The main text keeps to the Masoretic vowels and accents: 10:22 kemo ofel is divided from tsalmawet, as the accents have it, and not read as "the deep darkness of the shadow of death". Not noted: 10:8, where the old Greek has "afterward you turned and struck me" for yachad saviv (either way he was made and is now destroyed); 10:15, the change of ur'eh to a word for "drenched", which no old copy has; 10:17, the reading of edekha "your witnesses" as "your hostility", which no old copy has; and what the old Greek puts differently (10:15-17, 20), as in Job 2 to 9.
- Job's third speech ends at 10:22: the chapter opens with a quotation mark, as the Job 4 line says, and closes the speech. "I will say to God" (10:2) opens words inside the speech, in single marks, each paragraph of them opening with both marks, as at 4:17. They close at 10:19, because the written text of 10:20 speaks of God as "he", as 9:32-35 does after 9:28-31; nothing else signals where they end (the John 3 line).
- naqat be- (10:1) "loathe", kept distinct from ma'as "reject" and ti'ev "detest". azav alay siach (10:1) "abandon oneself to one's complaint": the Hebrew is "let my complaint go upon myself", giving it free course; azav stays "abandon", so that 10:1 is heard answering 9:27 ("I will forget my complaint; I will abandon my sad face"), and alay is carried by "myself". mar nafshi "the bitterness of my soul", as at 7:11.
- hodia "let (someone) know". al-mah "why". riv with the person as its object (10:2) "contend against" (riv im stays "contend with").
- hatov lekha ki (10:3) "Is it good for you that": tov le- is what does a person good (Lam 3:27, the same construction; compare 13:9), not what he likes, which is "good in the eyes of". ashaq "oppress", with no object, as in the Hebrew. yegia kappayim (10:3) "the toil of one's hands": the set phrase for what a person has worked to produce (Gen 31:42; Ps 128:2), where "palms" would not be English; kaf elsewhere stays "palm". hofia al "shine on", as at 3:4; the perfect among the imperfects of 10:3 is put in the English present, as at 9:5-10. atsat resha'im "the counsel of the wicked" (21:16; 22:18).
- ene vasar "eyes of flesh". kir'ot enosh "as a mortal sees". ha- ... im (10:4, 5) "Do ..., or ...?", as at 4:17. 10:5-7 is one sentence. In 10:5b the Hebrew has "days" again, not "years": "your years like the days of a man".
- biqqesh le- "look for"; darash le- (10:6) "seek out" (darash stays "seek"; shichar "search for"; chaqar "search out"). avon (10:6, 14) "guilt". al da'tekha ki (10:7) "though you know that": al as "in spite of" (16:17; 34:6). rasha (qal; 10:7, 15) "be wicked", as the Job 9 line planned. we'en miyyadekha matstsil "and there is no one to rescue from your hand": no object is supplied (5:4 has one), since these are the words of Deut 32:39 said back to God.
- itstsev (piel; 10:8) "shape"; asah "make". yachad saviv (10:8) "altogether, on every side", set between dashes so that the English is as open as the Hebrew: the words can go with the making, or, as the Masoretic accents divide the line, with the swallowing up (saviv of God's attack at 19:10). billa "swallow up", as the Job 2 line planned: Job uses the word Yahweh used (2:3). The wayyiqtol is "you have swallowed me up".
- zekhor-na "Remember now", as at 4:7. kachomer "like clay", left as open as the Hebrew (as clay is worked, or as a thing of clay). heshiv el-afar "return (someone) to dust": the causative of shuv "return" (Gen 3:19; Job 1:21). 10:9b is put as a statement, which leaves open, as the Hebrew does, whether it is a second thing to remember or a protest; the Hebrew does not mark a question.
- chalav "milk"; hittikh "pour out" (the same English as natakh, 3:24); gevinah (only here) "cheese"; hiqpi "curdle". The imperfects of 10:10-11 tell of the past. hilbish "clothe", beside lavash "be clothed with" (7:5; 8:22). gid "sinew".
- sokhekh (10:11; the same verb in Ps 139:13) "weave together" [alt: hedge in] (decision log, call: close). The alt is the meaning that these letters have at 3:23 and 38:8, so "hedge" is used for it.
- chayyim wachesed asita immadi (10:12) "You granted me life and faithful love": one verb serves both nouns, as in the Hebrew. chesed of God "faithful love", as the Job 6 line planned; between people it stays "loyalty" (6:14). pequddah (10:12) "care": the noun of paqad "inspect" (7:18); the echo is not shown.
- shamar (10:12, 14) "keep watch over" / "keep watch on": one English verb for both, so that the care of 10:12 is heard turning into the watching of 10:14 (it is "guard" at 2:6; of God's care again at 29:2, of his watching at 13:27; 14:16).
- tsafan (10:13) "hide away": a fourth root that shares "hide", told apart by "away" (it returns at 15:20; 17:4; 21:19; 23:12; 24:1). zot immakh "this is in your mind", as the Job 9 line said of immadi. A colon follows, since "these things" and "this" point forward to 10:14-17; after 10:12 a full stop would point them back to "life and faithful love".
- im with a perfect followed by weqatal or an imperfect (10:14, 15) "If I sin, you ...", "If I am wicked ...": a general case, in the English present. niqqah min "hold innocent of", as at 9:28.
- alelay li (10:15; Mic 7:1) "woe to me". wetsadaqti "And if I am righteous": the "if" is carried over. nasa rosh "lift one's head". seva (the construct) "full of", as planned for 14:1. qalon "disgrace", kept distinct from boshet "shame" and chafer "be humiliated". oni "affliction" (30:16, 27; 36:8, 15, 21), kept distinct from amal "misery". ur'eh onyi "and seeing my affliction" [alt: and see my affliction!] (decision log, call: close).
- ga'ah (10:16) "rise" (of a plant "grow tall", 8:11). The verb has no subject of its own; it is read of "my head" (10:15), and the noun is repeated, as at 1 Jn 4:3, since "it" would be heard of "affliction". tsud "hunt". kashachal "like a fierce lion", shachal as at 4:10: the English is as open as the Hebrew (God as the lion, as in Hos 5:14; 13:7, or Job hunted as a lion is). shuv with a second verb (10:16) "again". hitpalla be- "work wonders against", so that it is heard with nifla'ot "wonders" (5:9; 9:10).
- chiddesh "renew"; ed "witness"; neged (10:17) "against". hirbah (10:17) "increase" (9:17 "multiply"). ka'as of God stays "resentment" (5:2; 6:2), with im "toward", as in Ps 85:4.
- chalifot wetsava immi (10:17) "relays of troops are against me" [alt: turn after turn of a soldier's service is mine] (decision log, call: ask): tsava is "troops" here and "a soldier's service" at 7:1 and 14:14; chalifah returns at 14:14 ("relief").
- hotsi merechem (10:18) "bring out of the womb": rechem "womb" where it is the place, as the Job 3 line planned ("at birth" stays for 3:11). The imperfects of 10:18b-19 are what would have been ("I would have breathed my last"), as at 3:13, 16. ka'asher lo hayiti ehyeh "I would be as though I had not been". huval "be carried" (21:32).
- me'at (10:20) "few" of days and "a little" with a verb. chadal with nothing after it "cease", as at 3:17 (chadal min stays "leave alone", 7:16). shit min (10:20; only here) "withdraw from": what is taken away (the hand of 9:33, the heart of 7:17) is left unsaid in the Hebrew. hivlig "be cheerful", as listed. we- with a cohortative after a wish "so that I may".
- beterem "before". welo ashuv "and do not return". erets choshekh wetsalmawet "a land of darkness and the shadow of death", as listed. 10:20-22 is one sentence.
- efah / efatah (10:22; Amos 4:13) "blackness", kept distinct from choshekh "darkness" and ofel "deep darkness" (the noun at 11:17 is from the same root). sedarim (only here) "order": welo sedarim "and without order", as at 4:21. wattofa kemo-ofel "and it shines like deep darkness": hofia stays "shine" (3:4; 10:3), and the contradiction is left as the Hebrew has it.

## Settled in Job 11
- Hebrew text: the Masoretic Text; the chapter has no Qere. The main text keeps to the Masoretic vowels: 11:8 govhe shamayim, "the heights of the heavens", is put as "as high as the heavens" (an ancient version has "higher than the heavens", like "deeper than Sheol"; not noted, since the sense is the same); 11:13 kappekha is singular, "your palm"; 11:17 ta'ufah is a verb, "though it grows black" (the same letters read as a noun, "its blackness will be like the morning", are not noted, since both say the same thing). Not noted: changes that no old copy has (11:6 "like wonders" for "double"; 11:16 "now" for "you yourself"), and what the old Greek puts differently (11:2-4, 6, 10, 15-18), as in Job 2 to 10.
- Zophar's first speech is 11:2-20, all in this chapter. Words quoted inside it take single marks (11:4).
- rov devarim "a multitude of words". ish sefatayim (11:2) "a man full of talk": the idiom ("a man of lips") is translated by its meaning, so its echo with "open his lips" (11:5) is not shown. yitsdaq stays "be righteous" (4:17; 9:2, 15, 20; 10:15): Zophar takes up Job's word. ha- ... we'im "Is ..., or is ...?", as at 4:17.
- baddim (11:3; 41:12) "idle talk". metim "men" (11:3, 11): it shares its English with ish and gever. hecherish stays "be silent" (6:24), as everywhere in Job: baddekha is what men are silent at, as in lo acharish baddav (41:12); read as a causative ("your idle talk silences men") the line says the same thing, and nothing is noted. 11:3a has no question particle and is put as a question, like 2:9, carrying on from 11:2; the two wayyiqtol verbs that follow (11:3b, 4) tell what Job has done and are put in the English present as statements. la'ag with no object "mock" (9:23 "mock at"): Zophar says of Job what Job said of God. hikhlim "put to shame" (19:3): it shares "shame" with boshet; the object ("you") is supplied, as at 5:4.
- leqach "teaching", beside horah "teach". bar "clean": it shares its English with tahor (bor "cleanness" was planned for 22:30); zakh stays "pure" (8:6). be'enekha (11:4) "in your eyes": the words are quoted as Job's to God.
- mi yitten with an infinitive (11:5) "if only ... would", as listed. patach sefatayim im "open one's lips with": im is "with", as at 9:2-3, 14.
- ta'alumah "secret" (28:11; Ps 44:21): the root of hit'allem "hide itself" (6:16), given a noun of its own, so that no fifth root shares "hide". tushiyyah (11:6) "sound wisdom", as the Job 5 line planned. kiflayim letushiyyah "sound wisdom has two sides": the word is "double" (Isa 40:2; kefel, 41:13). Taken as "twice as much" it says the same thing, that there is more to wisdom than Job has seen, so nothing is noted.
- hishshah (11:6; 39:17) "let be forgotten": the causative of nashah "forget", with God as its subject both times. yashsheh lekha eloah me'awonekha "God lets some of your guilt be forgotten for you": min is "some of". The other verb with these letters, a creditor's ("God exacts from you less than your guilt"), takes be- of the debtor (Deut 15:2) and is not noted, since both say that Job is not held to the whole of his guilt. weda after the wish (11:6) "And know".
- cheqer eloah (11:7) "what is to be searched out in God", cheqer staying beside chaqar "search out" (5:9; 8:8; 9:10); takhlit "limit" (26:10; 28:3, where the two words stand together again). matsa "find", twice, as in the Hebrew; with ad "find one's way to". The other construction ("Can you find God by searching, or find out the Almighty to the limit?") says the same thing and is not noted.
- govah "height"; amoq "deep"; arokh "long"; rachav "broad"; middah "measure". The feminine "it" of 11:8-9 points back to the limit of the Almighty (or to wisdom), and "It is" is supplied twice in 11:8. pa'al stays "do" (7:20). She'ol "Sheol" and shamayim "the heavens", as listed.
- 11:10 answers 9:11-12 in Job's own words, and the English repeats them: chalaf "sweep on", mi yeshivennu "who can turn him back?" hisgir with no object (11:10) "shut someone in", beside sagar "shut" (3:10; 12:14); with a person handed to others (16:11) "hand over" may fit. hiqhil "call an assembly".
- metei shaw (11:11; Ps 26:4) "empty men": shaw stays "emptiness" (7:3), so that it is heard with "a hollow man" (11:12). hu with a finite verb "he himself", as listed. hitbonen "look closely (at)" (23:15; 30:20; 31:1; 37:14), kept distinct from "consider" (sim lev) and "discern" (bin). 11:11b "and will he not look closely at it?" [alt: he sees harm without having to look closely] (decision log, call: close). The wayyiqtol is put in the English present, as at 5:15.
- navuv "hollow" (Exod 27:8). yillavev (11:12; the form stands only here) "get sense": the verb is made from levav "heart", and "understanding" is left for binah; one bracketed note says so, and that it sounds like "hollow". ayir "colt"; pere "wild donkey", as at 6:5; adam "a human being", as at 5:7. 11:12 "a hollow man will get sense when a wild donkey's colt is born a human being" [alt: Yet a hollow man can get sense, though a human being is born a wild donkey's colt] (decision log, call: ask). The "and" that sets a picture beside a saying is "when" here ("as" at 5:7).
- hekhin lev (11:13) "set one's heart right" (Ps 78:8): the full form of the idiom that is "set one's mind on" at 8:8. paras kaf el "spread out one's palm to": kaf stays "palm" (9:30), beside "in your hand" (11:14). attah with a finite verb (11:13, 16) "you yourself", as listed. 11:13-15 is one sentence, with 11:14 between dashes; ki az (11:15) opens the main clause, "surely then" (8:6 "surely now").
- hirchiq "put far away". hishkin "let live", beside shakan "live" (4:19). ohalim "tents", plural as in the Hebrew. awen stays "harm" and awlah "injustice".
- nasa panim (11:15; 22:26) "lift one's face": Zophar answers "I cannot lift my head" (10:15). mum "blemish": mimmum "without blemish", the same English as amomos. mutsaq (11:15) "solid": the word is for metal that has been cast (37:18; compare 41:23-24). lo tira "you will not be afraid", as at 5:21.
- amal stays "misery" (11:16); shakhach "forget" (8:13); avar of water "pass away", as the Job 6 line planned; zakar "remember".
- cheled (11:17) "life": the span of a life (Ps 39:5); it shares its English with chayyim, and no "your" is added. qum "rise"; mitstsohorayim "brighter than noon", "brighter" supplied. ta'ufah (11:17) "though it grows black": the verb of efah "blackness" (10:22), so that Zophar is heard answering Job's last words. boqer "morning".
- batach with no object (11:18) "trust", as at 6:20; betach "security": lavetach "in security" ("safety" stays for yesha). chafar (11:18) "look around": the verb is "dig" (3:21) and so "search" (39:29; Deut 1:22; Josh 2:2-3). The same letters as a form of chafer "be humiliated" (6:20) are not noted: in a row of promises the sentence does not allow it.
- ravats of a person at rest (11:19) "bed down": the word for animals lying in their place (Isa 17:2; Zeph 3:13), kept distinct from shakhav "lie down"; "crouch" stays for Gen 4:7. en macharid "there will be no one to make you tremble" (Lev 26:6; Mic 4:4): hecherid "make tremble", kept distinct from bi'et "terrify" and chittat "frighten". chillah fene "seek the favor of" (Prov 19:6): the idiom is translated by its meaning. rabbim "many".
- kalah of eyes (11:20; 17:5) "fail"; elsewhere it stays "come to an end". manos "refuge"; avad min (11:20; Ps 142:4; Amos 2:14) "be lost to", where "perish" does not fit; the perfect is put in the English present. mappach nefesh (only here) "the breathing out of life" (the verb with nefesh at 31:39; Jer 15:9): left as open as the Hebrew (their only hope is to die, or their hope dies away); gava stays "breathe one's last".

## Settled in Job 12
- Hebrew text: the Masoretic Text; the chapter has no Qere. The main text keeps to the Masoretic vowels: 12:18 musar "the discipline of kings", and the same letters read as the word for bonds are the alt (decision log, call: close). Not noted: 12:9, where a few Hebrew copies have "God" for "Yahweh" (the name stands only here in the speeches of Job and his friends; the sense is the same); 12:23, where the old Greek has "leads nations astray" for "makes nations grow" (the same consonants); and what the old Greek puts differently (12:4-6), as in Job 2 to 11.
- Job's fourth speech is 12:2-14:22: chapter 12 ends with no closing quotation mark, as the Job 4 line says. "You" is plural in 12:2-3 (the friends) and singular in 12:7-8; English does not show the change, and nothing is added (the Job 7 line).
- omnam ki (12:2) "Truly", as at 9:2. am with no article (12:2) "the people". levav and lev of what a person thinks with (12:3, 24) "sense", so that Job is heard answering Zophar's "get sense" (11:12); "heart" stays elsewhere. nafal min (12:3; 13:2) "fall short of", keeping "fall". et-mi en (12:3) "who does not have": et of what a person has in him, like immadi (9:35). kemo-elleh "such things as these".
- sechoq of a person (12:4) "a laughingstock", beside "laughter" (8:21). The third person inside the first-person sentence is kept ("I am one who is a laughingstock to his friend"). qara le- "call to". The participle and its wayyiqtol (qore ... wayya'anehu) are put in the past, "one who called to God, and he answered him": the days of 29:2-5. tsaddiq "righteous"; tamim "blameless", the same English as tam (two forms of one word, like tom / tummah).
- 12:5: the first word is read as la- with pid, "for misfortune" [alt: a despised torch] (decision log, call: close). pid (12:5; 30:24; 31:29) "misfortune", leaving "calamity" for ed (18:12; 21:17, 30; 31:3, 23). buz "contempt" (12:5, 21; 31:34). ashtut (only here) "thought". sha'anan (12:5) "one who is untroubled" and shalah (12:6) "be at ease" stand in neighboring lines and stay distinct, as the Job 3 line says. nakhon le- "ready for" (15:23; 18:12). ma'ad "slip": mo'ade ragel "those whose foot slips".
- shoded (12:6; 15:21) "destroyer", beside shod "destruction" (5:21-22): Job is heard answering "your tent is at peace" (5:24). battuchot (only here) "security", the same English as betach (11:18): he answers Zophar too. hirgiz with God as its object (12:6) "provoke" ("shake" at 9:6).
- 12:6 la'asher hevi eloah beyado "for the one who brings a god in his hand" [alt: for the one into whose hand God brings it] (decision log, call: ask). hevi "bring"; the perfect is put in the English present, as at 9:5-10. eloah is "a god" here, lowercase, as the same word is "his god" in Hab 1:11; "who brings God in his hand" is the same construction and is not noted.
- behemot of the animals as a whole, beside birds and fish (12:7; 18:3; 35:11) "beasts"; "livestock" stays where they are set beside wild animals (Gen 1:24-26). she'al-na ... wetorekka (12:7) "ask ... now, and they will teach you", the English of 8:8-10: Job turns Bildad's words around. A singular verb with a collective subject (12:7) is "they". of hashamayim "the birds of the sky" and dege hayyam "the fish of the sea", as in Gen 1:26. siach le- (12:8) "speak to" ("complain" stays for 7:11): the feminine "it will teach you" goes with "the earth", so the same letters as the noun "shrub" (30:4, 7) are not noted. sipper "recount" (15:17; 28:27), kept distinct from higgid "tell"; its object ("it") is supplied.
- 12:9 bekhol-elleh "among all these": the creatures just named. "By all these", on the pattern of "by this you will know that" (Exod 7:17), is not noted, since either way the creatures are the witnesses. yad YHWH asetah zot "the hand of Yahweh has done this", the words of Isa 41:20. asher beyado (12:10) is made a sentence of its own, as at 5:5.
- nefesh kol chay (12:10) "the life of every living thing". ruach of the life in a person (12:10; 10:12) "spirit", leaving "breath" for neshamah (the two stand together at 27:3; 34:14). kol besar ish "all human flesh".

## Settled in Job 13
- Hebrew text: the Masoretic Text, read by its Ketiv. 13:15: the Ketiv lo "not" is followed ("I will not wait"), and the Qere lo "for him" is the alt ("I will wait for him"), with an entry in the decision log (call: ask), as at 6:21. The main text keeps to the Masoretic vowels and letters. Not noted: 13:14, where the old Greek has no "Why" (with it or without it Job is putting his life in his hand); 13:12, the reading of gabbekhem as "your answers", a sense the word has nowhere in Scripture; 13:28, where the old Greek has "like a wineskin" for "like something rotten"; and what the old Greek puts differently (13:12, 27), as in Job 2 to 12.
- Job's fourth speech goes on (12:2-14:22): the chapter opens with a quotation mark and ends with none, as the Job 4 line says. "You" is plural in 13:2-17 (the friends); from 13:20 Job speaks to God ("you" is singular). English does not show the change, and nothing is added (the Job 7 line). 13:28 turns to "he" without naming anyone, and the English leaves it so (compare 12:4); 14:1 goes on with "a human being".
- kol alone (13:1) "it all". bin le- "discern", as listed. keda'tekhem yada'ti gam-ani (13:2) "What you know, I myself also know". 13:2b repeats 12:3 word for word, and the English repeats it.
- ulam ani with an imperfect (13:3) "But I myself would", the English of 5:8: Job is heard answering Eliphaz's "But I myself would seek God". we'ulam attem (13:4) "But you yourselves".
- hokhiach, continued: where a person argues his case (13:3, 15) "argue", and tokhachat (13:6; 23:4) "argument"; of God reproving (13:10) it stays "correct" (5:17). The echo between the two is not shown. hokhiach derakhay el-panav (13:15) "argue my ways to his face".
- tafal (13:4; 14:17; Ps 119:69) "plaster": tofle-shaqer "you plaster with lies", the participle made a verb, with no object added. sheqer "lies" (shaw stays "emptiness"). rofe "healer", beside rafa "heal" (5:18); elil "worthless": rofe'e elil "worthless healers", a noun of quality made an adjective, as at 8:6.
- mi-yitten with an infinitive absolute (13:5) "If only you would actually", as at 6:2. utehi lakhem lechokhmah "and that would be wisdom for you".
- hiqshiv "pay attention to" (33:31), beside shama "hear" and he'ezin "listen to". rivot (13:6) "contentions", beside riv "contend"; riv le- (13:8; Judg 6:31) "contend for"; riv im (13:19) "contend with", as listed.
- A phrase set first in a question (13:7, 8) "Is it for God that ...". remiyyah (13:7; 27:4) "deceit" (it shares its English with dolos). hetel be- (13:9) "fool" (verb), kept distinct from remiyyah and from "deceive" (Gen 3:13); hatulim (17:2) will need a wording of its own.
- nasa panim with another person's face (13:8, 10; 32:21; 34:19) "show favoritism to": the idiom is translated by its meaning, as with thaumazein prosopa; of one's own face it stays "lift one's face" (11:15; 22:26). se'et of God (13:11; 31:23) "majesty" (Gen 4:7 "a lifting up"); one bracketed note at 13:11 says that it is from the same verb as "lift the face". pachdo (13:11) "the dread of him", like "the terror of him" (9:34). halo governs both halves of 13:11, as at 7:1. seter: basseter "in secret".
- hatov ki (13:9) "Will it be good when"; chaqar "search out", as listed. ha- ... im in 13:9 is put as two questions.
- zikkaron (13:12) "reminder", beside zakar "remember": what the friends have told Job to remember (4:7; 8:8). mashal "proverb". gav (13:12) "defense": the word is the boss of a shield at 15:26 and a mound in Ezek 16:24; the English is left as open, and the repetition is kept ("your defenses are defenses of clay").
- hecherish min (13:13; Jer 38:27) "be silent and let (someone) be", beside chadal min "leave alone" (7:16). avar al of what befalls a person (13:13) "come over" ("pass by" at 9:11): weya'avor alay mah "and let come over me what may".
- al-mah (13:14) "Why", as at 10:2; the imperfects are put in the English present ("Why do I carry"), since Job is doing it. nasa with be- of what holds a thing (13:14) "carry". sim nefesh bekaf (13:14; Judg 12:3; 1 Sam 19:5; 28:21; Ps 119:109) "put one's life in one's hand": the set phrase for risking it, where "palm" would not be English, as at 10:3; kaf stays "palm" in 13:21.
- qatal (13:15; 24:14) "kill": it shares its English with harag. yichel stays "wait" (6:11): lo ayachel "I will not wait", left as open as the Hebrew (he has nothing to wait for, or he will not hold back). akh (13:15) "but"; (13:20) "Only".
- gam-hu (13:16; 31:28) "This too" [alt at 13:16: He too] (decision log, call: close). yeshu'ah "salvation", the same English as soteria, beside hoshia "save"; yesha stays "safety" (5:4, 11). chanef "a godless man", as listed.
- shim'u shamoa (13:17; 21:2) "Hear ... carefully": an infinitive absolute with an imperative, where "certainly" does not fit. achawah (only here) "declaration", so that chiwwah (15:17; 32:6) can be "declare".
- arakh mishpat (13:18; 23:4) "set out one's case": mishpat "case", as planned, with "my" supplied. etsdaq (13:18) "will be found righteous": tsadaq stays "be righteous", and "found" carries the imperfect, the outcome of the case he has set out (the perfect tsadaqti stays "I am righteous").
- mi-hu yariv immadi (13:19) "Who is it that will contend with me?" (compare Isa 50:8). ki-attah with imperfects (13:19) "For then", as at 6:3; "for if I were silent now, I would breathe my last" is not noted.
- asah im (13:20) "do to". nistar mippene "hide from the face of". hirchiq "put far away", as listed (11:14). 13:21b repeats 9:34b, now said to God, and the English repeats it ("let the terror of you not terrify me").

# Glossary of settled renderings

Recurring Hebrew, Aramaic and Greek terms and the English chosen for them, so every chapter
stays consistent. Read this before translating. Use the listed rendering unless the context
truly cannot bear it, and then put the listed rendering in an `[alt: ...]`.
When a chapter settles a new recurring term, add one line here in the same commit.
(Transliteration is fine in this file. It is never allowed in the chapter files.)

## Decided by Obay
- nephesh chayyah: "living creature" everywhere (Gen 1:20, 21, 24; 2:7, 19). Where the phrase is
  not a countable creature (Gen 1:30, "in which there is nephesh chayyah"), write
  "the life of a living creature [alt: the breath of life]". Decided 2026-11-19.
- nishmat chayyim (Gen 2:7): "the breath of life". This is a different phrase from nephesh chayyah;
  keep the two distinct.

## Settled in Genesis 1-2
- Elohim "God"; YHWH "Yahweh"; YHWH Elohim "Yahweh God"; Adonai "Lord".
- bara "create"; asah "make". Never interchange them.
- mashal (the lights) "govern"; radah (humankind) "rule over".
- raqia "vault" [alt: expanse]. shamayim "heavens" in 1:1 and 2:1, 4; "sky" for the visible sky.
- hinneh "look" (never "behold").
- tohu wabohu "desolate and empty" [alt: formless and empty].
- ruach elohim "the spirit of God" [alt: the wind of God, or the breath of God], lowercase.
- adam: "humankind" for the collective (1:26-27); "the human" with the article in chapter 2;
  "Adam" only where it stands without the article as a name.
- ish / ishshah "man" / "woman".
- min "kind" ("according to their kinds").
- remes / romes "creeping things" / "creeps". chayyat ha'arets / hassadeh "wild animals of the earth / field".
- behemah "livestock".
- tov wara "good and bad" [alt: good and evil].
- shavat "cease" [alt: rest].
- toledot "generations" [alt: account].
- Sentence-opening "and" is kept where the Hebrew narrative chain has it; "Now" marks a break in the chain.
- Day formula: "And there was evening and there was morning" followed by a dash and the day.

## Settled in Genesis 3
- nachash "snake". arum (3:1) "shrewd". arur "cursed".
- Infinitive absolute with its own verb: "certainly" (2:16-17; 3:4); harbah arbeh "greatly multiply" (3:16).
- pen "so that ... not" (3:3, 22).
- ta'avah "delight"; nechmad "desirable"; teshuqah "longing" (3:16; 4:7). Keep the three distinct.
- haskil (3:6) "gain insight" [alt: contemplate].
- qol "sound" (3:8, 10) or "voice" (3:17), by context.
- eivah "hostility".
- zera: "seed" for plants (chapter 1); "offspring" [alt: seed] for descendants (3:15).
- shuf (3:15) "strike" [alt: crush, or bruise], the same English both times.
- itsavon "painful toil" (3:16, 17; 5:29); etsev "pain".
- mashal stays "govern" in 3:16 (the husband) and 4:7, so it remains distinct from radah "rule over".
- ish / ishshah with a possessive: "husband" / "wife".
- adam without the article (2:20; 3:17, 21): "Adam [alt: the human]".
- Chavvah "Eve". keruvim "cherubim". kuttonet "tunic".
- shalach (piel) "send out"; garash "drive out".
- shamar "watch over" (2:15) or "guard" (3:24), by context.
- hen "look", the same as hinneh.

In the name of God, the Most Merciful, the Merciful. A. L. M. That is the Book. There is no doubt in it; it is guidance for those who are mindful of God, those who believe in the unseen, keep up the prayer, and spend from what we have provided for them, and those who believe in what has been sent down to you and what was sent down before you, and of the hereafter they are certain. Those are on guidance from their Lord, and those are the ones who succeed. As for those who disbelieve, it is the same for them whether you warn them or do not warn them: they do not believe. God has set a seal on their hearts and on their hearing, and over their sight is a covering, and for them there is a great punishment. And among people there are some who say, "We believe in God and in the Last Day," but they are not believers. They try to deceive God and those who believe, but they deceive only themselves, and they do not realize it. In their hearts is a sickness, so God has increased their sickness, and for them there is a painful punishment because they used to lie. And when they are told, "Do not cause corruption on the earth," they say, "We are only setting things right." Truly, they are the ones who cause corruption, but they do not realize it. And when they are told, "Believe as the people have believed," they say, "Should we believe as the fools have believed?" Truly, they are the fools, but they do not know. And when they meet those who believe, they say, "We believe," but when they are alone with their devils, they say, "We are with you; we are only mocking." God mocks them and keeps them going in their excess, wandering blindly. Those are the ones who have bought error in exchange for guidance, so their trade has made no profit, and they were not guided. Their example is like that of one who kindled a fire, and when it lit up what was around him, God took away their light and left them in darkness, unable to see. Deaf, mute, blind, so they do not return. Or like a rainstorm from the sky, with darkness, thunder and lightning in it. They put their fingers in their ears because of the thunderbolts, for fear of death. And God surrounds the disbelievers. The lightning almost snatches away their sight. Whenever it lights up for them, they walk in it, and when it grows dark over them, they stand still. And if God had willed, he would have taken away their hearing and their sight. God has power over all things. People, worship your Lord, who created you and those before you, so that you may be mindful of him, the one who made the earth a bed for you and the sky a building, and sent down water from the sky and with it brought out fruits as provision for you. So do not set up rivals to God while you know. And if you are in doubt about what we have sent down to our servant, then bring a surah like it, and call your witnesses other than God, if you are telling the truth. But if you do not, and you never will, then guard yourselves against the Fire whose fuel is people and stones, prepared for the disbelievers. And give good news to those who believe and do righteous deeds, that for them there are gardens with rivers flowing beneath them. Whenever they are provided with a provision of fruit from them, they say, "This is what we were provided with before," and what they are brought looks alike. There they have purified spouses, and there they will remain forever. God does not shy away from giving any example, a gnat or anything above it. As for those who believe, they know that it is the truth from their Lord. But as for those who disbelieve, they say, "What did God mean by this example?" By it he leads many astray, and by it he guides many, but he leads astray by it only the defiant, those who break God's covenant after it has been made binding, cut what God has commanded to be joined, and cause corruption on the earth. Those are the losers. How can you disbelieve in God, when you were dead and he gave you life, then he will cause you to die, then he will give you life, then to him you will be returned? He is the one who created for you all that is on the earth, then turned to the sky and formed them into seven heavens, and he has knowledge of all things. And when your Lord said to the angels, "I am placing a successor on the earth," they said, "Will you place on it one who will cause corruption there and shed blood, while we glorify you with praise and declare you holy?" He said, "I know what you do not know." And he taught Adam the names, all of them. Then he presented them to the angels and said, "Tell me the names of these, if you are telling the truth." They said, "Glory to you! We have no knowledge except what you have taught us. You are the Knowing, the Wise." He said, "Adam, tell them their names." And when he had told them their names, he said, "Did I not say to you that I know the unseen of the heavens and the earth, and know what you disclose and what you have been concealing?" And when we said to the angels, "Bow down to Adam," they bowed down, except Iblis. He refused and was arrogant, and he was one of the disbelievers. And we said, "Adam, live in the Garden, you and your spouse, and eat from it freely wherever you wish, but do not go near this tree, or you will be among the wrongdoers." Then Satan made them slip from it and brought them out of what they had been in. And we said, "Go down, enemies to one another. And on the earth you have a place to settle and enjoyment for a time." Then Adam received words from his Lord, and he relented toward him. He is the Ever-Relenting, the Merciful. We said, "Go down from it, all of you. Then if guidance comes to you from me, for those who follow my guidance there is no fear, nor will they grieve. But those who disbelieve and call our signs lies, those are the companions of the Fire; they will remain in it forever." Children of Israel, remember my favor that I showed you, and keep my covenant, and I will keep your covenant, and it is me you should dread. And believe in what I have sent down, confirming what is with you, and do not be the first to disbelieve in it. And do not sell my signs for a small price, and it is me you should be mindful of. And do not mix the truth with falsehood or conceal the truth while you know. And keep up the prayer and give the alms, and bow with those who bow. Do you command people to virtue and forget yourselves, while you recite the Book? Will you not use your reason? And ask for help through patience and prayer. And it is hard indeed, except for the humble, those who think that they will meet their Lord and that to him they will return. Children of Israel, remember my favor that I showed you, and that I set you above all peoples. And guard yourselves against a day when no soul will pay anything on behalf of another soul, and no intercession will be accepted from it, and no compensation will be taken from it, nor will they be aided. And when we saved you from Pharaoh's people, who were inflicting a terrible punishment on you, slaughtering your sons and letting your women live. And in that there was a great test from your Lord. And when we parted the sea for you, and saved you, and drowned Pharaoh's people while you looked on. And when we appointed forty nights for Moses, then you took the calf for yourselves after he had gone, and you were doing wrong. Then we pardoned you after that, so that you might give thanks. And when we gave Moses the Book and the Criterion, so that you might be guided. And when Moses said to his people, "My people, you have wronged yourselves by taking the calf for yourselves, so repent to your Maker and kill yourselves. That is better for you in the sight of your Maker." Then he relented toward you. He is the Ever-Relenting, the Merciful. And when you said, "Moses, we will not believe you until we see God in plain sight," the thunderbolt took you while you looked on. Then we raised you up after your death, so that you might give thanks. And we shaded you with clouds and sent down manna and quails to you: "Eat from the good things we have provided for you." And they did not wrong us, but it was themselves they were wronging. And when we said, "Enter this town and eat from it wherever you wish, freely, and enter the gate bowing down, and say, 'Unburden us,' and we will forgive you your faults. And we will give more to those who do good." But those who did wrong put a different saying in place of the one they had been told, so we sent down on those who did wrong a plague from the sky, because they were defiant. And when Moses asked for water for his people, we said, "Strike the rock with your staff," and twelve springs gushed out of it. Each group of people knew its drinking place. "Eat and drink from God's provision, and do not act wickedly on the earth, causing corruption." And when you said, "Moses, we will not put up with one food, so call on your Lord for us, to bring out for us some of what the earth grows: its greens, its cucumbers, its garlic, its lentils and its onions," he said, "Would you take what is lower in exchange for what is better? Go down to a city, and you will have what you asked for." And humiliation and destitution were stamped on them, and they came away with anger from God. That was because they used to disbelieve in God's signs and kill the prophets without right. That was because they disobeyed and used to overstep. Those who believe, and those who are Jews, and the Christians, and the Sabians, whoever believes in God and the Last Day and does what is righteous, they have their reward with their Lord, and there is no fear for them, nor will they grieve. And when we took your pledge and raised the Mount above you: "Take with strength what we have given you, and remember what is in it, so that you may be mindful of God." Then you turned away after that, and had it not been for God's bounty to you and his mercy, you would have been among the losers. And you know well those of you who overstepped on the Sabbath, so we said to them, "Be apes, driven away." So we made it a deterrent for what was before it and what was behind it, and a lesson for those who are mindful of God. And when Moses said to his people, "God commands you to slaughter a cow," they said, "Are you making a mockery of us?" He said, "I take refuge in God from being one of the ignorant." They said, "Call on your Lord for us, to make clear to us what it is." He said, "He says it is a cow neither old nor young, of middle age, between the two. So do what you are commanded." They said, "Call on your Lord for us, to make clear to us what its color is." He said, "He says it is a yellow cow, bright in color, that delights those who look at it." They said, "Call on your Lord for us, to make clear to us what it is. Cows look alike to us, and if God wills, we will certainly be guided." He said, "He says it is a cow not broken in to turn the earth or water the field, sound, with no mark on it." They said, "Now you have brought the truth." So they slaughtered it, and they almost did not do it. And when you killed a soul, you blamed one another for it, and God would bring out what you were concealing. So we said, "Strike him with part of it." That is how God gives life to the dead, and he shows you his signs so that you may use your reason. Then your hearts hardened after that, so they are like stones, or harder. And among stones there are some from which rivers gush out, and among them there are some that split open and water comes out of them, and among them there are some that fall down out of fear of God. And God is not unaware of what you do. So do you hope that they will believe you, when a group of them used to hear God's speech and then twist it after they had understood it, while they knew? And when they meet those who believe, they say, "We believe," but when they are alone with one another, they say, "Do you talk to them about what God has opened to you, so that they may argue against you with it before your Lord? Will you not use your reason?" Do they not know that God knows what they keep secret and what they make public? And among them are unlettered people who do not know the Book, only wishful thinking, and they are only guessing. So woe to those who write the Book with their hands and then say, "This is from God," to sell it for a small price. So woe to them for what their hands have written, and woe to them for what they earn. And they said, "The Fire will not touch us except for a limited number of days." Say, "Have you taken a covenant with God? Then God will not go back on his covenant. Or do you say about God what you do not know?" Not so. Whoever earns evil and is surrounded by his fault, those are the companions of the Fire; they will remain in it forever. And those who believe and do righteous deeds, those are the companions of the Garden; they will remain in it forever.

In the name of God, the All-Merciful, the Ever-Merciful. Glory to the one who took his servant by night from the Sacred Place of Worship to the Farthest Place of Worship, whose surroundings we have blessed, so that we might show him some of our signs. He is the Hearing, the Seeing. And we gave Moses the Book and made it guidance for the Children of Israel: "Do not take for yourselves anyone to rely on other than me, descendants of those we carried with Noah." He was a thankful servant. And we decreed to the Children of Israel in the Book: You will certainly cause corruption on the earth twice, and you will certainly rise to a great height. So when the promise of the first of the two comes, we will raise up against you servants of ours who are fierce in battle, and they will go back and forth among the homes. And it is a promise that will be carried out. Then we will give you back the turn against them, and we will supply you with wealth and sons and make you more numerous in men. If you do good, you do good for yourselves, and if you do evil, it is for yourselves. So when the promise of the last one comes, it is so that they may bring grief to your faces, and enter the place of worship as they entered it the first time, and bring to utter ruin whatever they overcome. It may be that your Lord will have mercy on you. But if you go back, we will go back. And we have made Hell a prison for the disbelievers. This Quran guides to what is straightest, and it gives good news to the believers who do righteous deeds that for them there is a great reward, and that for those who do not believe in the hereafter we have prepared a painful punishment. And man calls for evil as he calls for good, and man is hasty. And we have made the night and the day two signs. Then we erased the sign of the night and made the sign of the day give sight, so that you may seek bounty from your Lord, and so that you may know the number of the years and the reckoning. And everything we have set out in detail. And every person, we have fastened his fate to his neck. And on the Day of Resurrection we will bring out for him a book that he will meet spread open: "Read your book. Your own soul is enough today as a reckoner against you." Whoever is guided is guided only for himself, and whoever goes astray goes astray only against himself. And no bearer of burdens bears the burden of another. And we would not punish until we had raised up a messenger. And when we want to destroy a town, we command those in it who live in luxury, and they are defiant in it, so the word comes true against it, and we demolish it completely. And how many generations we have destroyed after Noah! And your Lord is enough as one who is aware of the sins of his servants and sees them. Whoever wants the hasty life, we hasten for him in it whatever we will, for whomever we want. Then we make Hell his, to burn in it, condemned and banished. And whoever wants the hereafter and strives for it with the striving due to it, and is a believer, those are the ones whose striving is thanked. Each one we supply, these and those, from the gift of your Lord. And the gift of your Lord is not restricted. Look how we have set some of them above others. And the hereafter is indeed greater in degrees and greater in setting some above others. Do not set up another god with God, or you will sit condemned and abandoned. And your Lord has decreed that you worship none but him, and do good to parents. If one of them or both of them reach old age with you, do not say "Ugh" to them, and do not scold them, but speak to them with gracious words. And lower to them the wing of humility, out of mercy, and say, "My Lord, have mercy on them, as they raised me when I was small." Your Lord knows best what is within yourselves. If you are righteous, he is forgiving to those who keep returning. And give the relative his due, and the destitute, and the traveler, and do not squander wastefully. Those who squander are brothers of the devils, and Satan is ungrateful to his Lord. And if you turn aside from them, seeking a mercy from your Lord that you hope for, then speak to them with gentle words. And do not keep your hand chained to your neck, and do not extend it to its full extent, or you will sit blamed and stripped bare. Your Lord extends provision to whomever he wills, and measures it out. He is aware of his servants and sees them. And do not kill your children for fear of poverty. It is we who provide for them and for you. Killing them is a great wrong. And do not go near unlawful sex. It is an indecency, and an evil way. And do not kill the soul that God has made sacred, except by right. And whoever is killed wrongfully, we have given authority to the one who acts for him, but let him not go too far in killing. He is aided. And do not go near the orphan's wealth, except in the way that is best, until he reaches his full strength. And keep the covenant. The covenant will be asked about. And give full measure when you measure, and weigh with the straight scale. That is better, and best in outcome. And do not follow after what you have no knowledge of. The hearing, the sight and the heart, each of those will be asked about. And do not walk on the earth swaggering. You will not tear through the earth, and you will not reach the mountains in height. In all of that, what is evil is hateful in the sight of your Lord. That is part of the wisdom your Lord has revealed to you. And do not set up another god with God, or you will be thrown into Hell, blamed and banished. Has your Lord, then, chosen sons for you and taken females for himself from among the angels? You are indeed saying a grave thing. And we have certainly put it in many ways in this Quran, so that they may remember, but it only increases them in aversion. Say, "If there were gods with him, as they say, then they would have sought a way to the Possessor of the Throne." Glory to him! And he is high above what they say, by a great height. The seven heavens and the earth and whoever is in them glorify him. And there is nothing that does not glorify him with praise, but you do not understand their glorifying. He is forbearing, forgiving. And when you read the Quran, we place between you and those who do not believe in the hereafter a hidden screen. And we place veils over their hearts, so that they do not understand it, and in their ears a heaviness. And when you mention your Lord alone in the Quran, they turn their backs in aversion. We know best what they listen with when they listen to you, and when they talk in private, when the wrongdoers say, "You are following only a bewitched man." Look how they have given examples for you, so they have gone astray and cannot find a way. And they said, "When we are bones and crumbled remains, will we really be raised up as a new creation?" Say, "Be stones or iron, or some creation of the kind that is great in your chests." Then they will say, "Who will bring us back?" Say, "The one who brought you into being the first time." Then they will shake their heads at you and say, "When is it?" Say, "It may be that it is near. On the day he calls you, you will answer with praise of him, and you will think that you stayed only a little while." And tell my servants to say what is best. Satan stirs up trouble among them. Satan is a clear enemy to man. Your Lord knows you best. If he wills, he will have mercy on you, or if he wills, he will punish you. And we have not sent you as one in charge of them. And your Lord knows best whoever is in the heavens and the earth. And we have certainly set some of the prophets above others, and we gave David a Scripture. Say, "Call on those you have claimed apart from him. They have no power to lift harm from you, or to divert it." Those whom they call on are seeking the means of approach to their Lord, to see which of them is nearest, and they hope for his mercy and fear his punishment. The punishment of your Lord is a thing to beware of. And there is no town that we will not destroy before the Day of Resurrection, or punish with a severe punishment. That is inscribed in the Book. And nothing kept us from sending the signs except that the earlier peoples called them lies. And we gave Thamud the she-camel, giving sight, but they did wrong by her. And we send the signs only to frighten. And when we said to you, "Your Lord has surrounded the people." And we made the vision that we showed you, and the cursed tree in the Quran, only a trial for the people. And we frighten them, but it only increases them in great excess. And when we said to the angels, "Bow down to Adam," they bowed down, except Iblis. He said, "Shall I bow down to one you created from clay?" He said, "Do you see this one whom you have honored above me? If you delay me until the Day of Resurrection, I will certainly put a bridle on his descendants, all but a few." He said, "Go. Whoever of them follows you, Hell is the repayment of you all, an ample repayment. And unsettle whomever of them you can with your voice, and rally your horses and your men on foot against them, and be their partner in wealth and children, and make them promises." And Satan promises them nothing but delusion. "As for my servants, you have no authority over them." And your Lord is enough as one to rely on. Your Lord is the one who drives the ships for you on the sea, so that you may seek some of his bounty. He is merciful to you. And when harm touches you at sea, those you call on are lost, except him. But when he brings you safely to land, you turn aside. And man is ungrateful. Do you feel safe, then, that he will not make the side of the land sink with you, or send a storm of stones against you? Then you would find no one to rely on. Or do you feel safe that he will not take you back into it another time, and send a shattering blast of wind against you, and drown you because you were ungrateful? Then you would find no one to follow it up for you against us. And we have certainly honored the children of Adam, and carried them on land and sea, and provided them with good things, and set them far above many of those we have created. On the day we call every group of people with their leader, whoever is given his book in his right hand, those will read their book, and they will not be wronged by so much as a thread. And whoever is blind in this life will be blind in the hereafter, and further astray from the way. And indeed they almost tempted you away from what we have revealed to you, so that you would invent against us something other than it. And then they would have taken you as a friend. And had we not made you firm, you would almost have leaned toward them a little. Then we would have made you taste double in life and double in death, and then you would have found no one to aid you against us. And indeed they almost unsettled you from the land, to drive you out of it. And then they would have stayed after you only a little while. That was the practice with those of our messengers we sent before you, and you will find no diverting of our practice. Keep up the prayer from the declining of the sun until the dark of the night, and the reading at dawn. The reading at dawn is witnessed. And for part of the night, stay awake with it, as something extra for you. It may be that your Lord will raise you up to a praised standing. And say, "My Lord, bring me in by a true entrance, and bring me out by a true exit, and give me from yourself an authority to aid me." And say, "The truth has come, and falsehood has vanished. Falsehood is bound to vanish." And we send down from the Quran what is a healing and a mercy for the believers, but it only increases the wrongdoers in loss. And when we show favor to man, he turns aside and distances himself, and when evil touches him, he is in despair. Say, "Each one acts according to his own manner, so your Lord knows best who is best guided on the way." And they ask you about the spirit. Say, "The spirit is from the command of my Lord, and you have been given only a little knowledge." And if we willed, we would certainly take away what we have revealed to you, and then you would find no one to rely on for it against us, except for a mercy from your Lord. His bounty to you is great. Say, "If humans and jinn came together to bring the like of this Quran, they would not bring the like of it, even if they backed one another." And we have certainly put every kind of example in many ways for people in this Quran, but most people have refused anything but disbelief. And they said, "We will not believe you until you make a spring gush out of the earth for us, or you have a garden of date palms and grapevines, and you make rivers gush out through the midst of it in abundance, or you make the sky fall on us in pieces, as you have claimed, or you bring God and the angels face to face with us, or you have a house of gold, or you climb up into the sky. And we will not believe in your climb until you send down to us a book that we can read." Say, "Glory to my Lord! Am I anything but a human being, a messenger?" And nothing kept people from believing when the guidance came to them except that they said, "Has God raised up a human being as a messenger?" Say, "If there were angels on the earth, walking about at ease, we would have sent down to them from the sky an angel as a messenger." Say, "God is enough as a witness between me and you. He is aware of his servants and sees them." And whoever God guides, he is the one who is guided. And whoever he leads astray, you will find no allies for them apart from him. And we will gather them on the Day of Resurrection on their faces, blind, mute and deaf. Their shelter is Hell. Whenever it dies down, we increase the flame for them. That is their repayment, because they disbelieved in our signs and said, "When we are bones and crumbled remains, will we really be raised up as a new creation?" Have they not seen that God, who created the heavens and the earth, has the power to create the like of them? And he has set a term for them in which there is no doubt. But the wrongdoers have refused anything but disbelief. Say, "If it were you who owned the storehouses of my Lord's mercy, then you would hold back for fear of spending. And man is tightfisted." And we certainly gave Moses nine clear signs. So ask the Children of Israel. When he came to them, Pharaoh said to him, "I do think, Moses, that you are bewitched." He said, "You know well that no one but the Lord of the heavens and the earth has sent these down as insights. And I do think, Pharaoh, that you are doomed." So he wanted to unsettle them from the land, but we drowned him and those with him, all together. And after him we said to the Children of Israel, "Live in the land. So when the promise of the hereafter comes, we will bring you as a mixed crowd." And with the truth we have sent it down, and with the truth it has come down. And we have sent you only as a bringer of good news and a warner. And it is a Quran that we have divided, so that you may read it to the people slowly, and we have sent it down in stages. Say, "Believe in it or do not believe. Those who were given knowledge before it, when it is recited to them, fall on their chins, bowing down, and they say, 'Glory to our Lord! The promise of our Lord is indeed carried out.' And they fall on their chins weeping, and it increases them in humility." Say, "Call on God, or call on the All-Merciful. Whichever you call on, to him belong the best names." And do not be loud in your prayer, and do not be hushed in it, but seek a way between the two. And say, "Praise belongs to God, who has not taken a child for himself, and has no partner in the kingdom, and has no ally against humiliation." And declare how great he is.

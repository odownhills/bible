Quran, surah 1. In the name of God, the Most Merciful, the Merciful. Praise belongs to God, Lord of the worlds, the Most Merciful, the Merciful, Master of the Day of Judgment. It is you we worship, and it is you we ask for help. Guide us to the straight path, the path of those you have favored, not those who have incurred anger, nor those who have gone astray.

Quran, surah 2, verses 30 to 61. And when your Lord said to the angels, "I am placing a successor on the earth," they said, "Will you place on it one who will cause corruption there and shed blood, while we glorify you with praise and declare you holy?" He said, "I know what you do not know." And he taught Adam the names, all of them. Then he presented them to the angels and said, "Tell me the names of these, if you are telling the truth." They said, "Glory to you! We have no knowledge except what you have taught us. You are the Knowing, the Wise." He said, "Adam, tell them their names." And when he had told them their names, he said, "Did I not say to you that I know the unseen of the heavens and the earth, and know what you disclose and what you have been concealing?" And when we said to the angels, "Bow down to Adam," they bowed down, except Iblis. He refused and was arrogant, and he was one of the disbelievers. And we said, "Adam, live in the Garden, you and your spouse, and eat from it freely wherever you wish, but do not go near this tree, or you will be among the wrongdoers." Then Satan made them slip from it and brought them out of what they had been in. And we said, "Go down, enemies to one another. And on the earth you have a place to settle and enjoyment for a time." Then Adam received words from his Lord, and he relented toward him. He is the Ever-Relenting, the Merciful. We said, "Go down from it, all of you. Then if guidance comes to you from me, for those who follow my guidance there is no fear, nor will they grieve. But those who disbelieve and call our signs lies, those are the companions of the Fire; they will remain in it forever." Children of Israel, remember my favor that I showed you, and keep my covenant, and I will keep your covenant, and it is me you should dread. And believe in what I have sent down, confirming what is with you, and do not be the first to disbelieve in it. And do not sell my signs for a small price, and it is me you should be mindful of. And do not mix the truth with falsehood or conceal the truth while you know. And keep up the prayer and give the alms, and bow with those who bow. Do you command people to virtue and forget yourselves, while you recite the Book? Will you not use your reason? And ask for help through patience and prayer. And it is hard indeed, except for the humble, those who think that they will meet their Lord and that to him they will return. Children of Israel, remember my favor that I showed you, and that I set you above all peoples. And guard yourselves against a day when no soul will pay anything on behalf of another soul, and no intercession will be accepted from it, and no compensation will be taken from it, nor will they be aided. And when we saved you from Pharaoh's people, who were inflicting a terrible punishment on you, slaughtering your sons and letting your women live. And in that there was a great test from your Lord. And when we parted the sea for you, and saved you, and drowned Pharaoh's people while you looked on. And when we appointed forty nights for Moses, then you took the calf for yourselves after he had gone, and you were doing wrong. Then we pardoned you after that, so that you might give thanks. And when we gave Moses the Book and the Criterion, so that you might be guided. And when Moses said to his people, "My people, you have wronged yourselves by taking the calf for yourselves, so repent to your Maker and kill yourselves. That is better for you in the sight of your Maker." Then he relented toward you. He is the Ever-Relenting, the Merciful. And when you said, "Moses, we will not believe you until we see God in plain sight," the thunderbolt took you while you looked on. Then we raised you up after your death, so that you might give thanks. And we shaded you with clouds and sent down manna and quails to you: "Eat from the good things we have provided for you." And they did not wrong us, but it was themselves they were wronging. And when we said, "Enter this town and eat from it wherever you wish, freely, and enter the gate bowing down, and say, 'Unburden us,' and we will forgive you your faults. And we will give more to those who do good." But those who did wrong put a different saying in place of the one they had been told, so we sent down on those who did wrong a plague from the sky, because they were defiant. And when Moses asked for water for his people, we said, "Strike the rock with your staff," and twelve springs gushed out of it. Each group of people knew its drinking place. "Eat and drink from God's provision, and do not act wickedly on the earth, causing corruption." And when you said, "Moses, we will not put up with one food, so call on your Lord for us, to bring out for us some of what the earth grows: its greens, its cucumbers, its garlic, its lentils and its onions," he said, "Would you take what is lower in exchange for what is better? Go down to a city, and you will have what you asked for." And humiliation and destitution were stamped on them, and they came away with anger from God. That was because they used to disbelieve in God's signs and kill the prophets without right. That was because they disobeyed and used to overstep.

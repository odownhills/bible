Quran, surah 2, verses 153 to 177. You who believe, ask for help through patience and prayer. God is with those who are patient. And do not say of those who are killed in the way of God, "They are dead." Rather, they are alive, but you do not realize it. And we will certainly test you with something of fear and hunger, and a loss of wealth, lives and fruits. And give good news to those who are patient, those who, when a misfortune strikes them, say, "We belong to God, and to him we will return." On those are blessings from their Lord, and mercy, and those are the ones who are guided. Safa and Marwa are among the symbols of God. So whoever makes the pilgrimage to the House or visits it, there is no blame on him if he goes around them. And whoever does good of his own accord, God is thankful, knowing. Those who conceal what we have sent down of the clear proofs and the guidance, after we have made it clear to the people in the Book, God curses them, and those who curse curse them, except those who repent, set things right and make it clear. Those I relent toward, and I am the Ever-Relenting, the Merciful. Those who disbelieve and die as disbelievers, on those is the curse of God, the angels and the people, all of them, remaining in it forever. The punishment will not be lightened for them, nor will they be given time. And your god is one god. There is no god but him, the Most Merciful, the Merciful. In the creation of the heavens and the earth, and the alternation of night and day, and the ships that sail on the sea with what benefits people, and the water God has sent down from the sky, giving life with it to the earth after its death and scattering on it animals of every kind, and the shifting of the winds, and the clouds made to serve between the sky and the earth, there are signs for a people who use reason. And among people there are some who take rivals for themselves apart from God, loving them as God is loved. But those who believe are stronger in love for God. And if only those who do wrong could see, when they see the punishment, that all strength belongs to God and that God is severe in punishment, when those who were followed disown those who followed, and they see the punishment, and their ties are cut. And those who followed will say, "If only we could go back, we would disown them as they have disowned us." That is how God will show them their deeds, as regrets for them, and they will not come out of the Fire. People, eat from what is on the earth, what is permitted and good, and do not follow Satan's footsteps. He is a clear enemy to you. He only commands you to evil and indecency, and to say about God what you do not know. And when they are told, "Follow what God has sent down," they say, "Rather, we follow what we found our fathers on." Even if their fathers understood nothing and were not guided? And the example of those who disbelieve is like that of one who shouts at what hears nothing but a call and a cry. Deaf, mute, blind, so they do not use reason. You who believe, eat from the good things we have provided for you, and give thanks to God, if it is him you worship. He has forbidden you only carrion, blood, the flesh of pigs, and what has been dedicated to other than God. But whoever is forced, neither transgressing nor overstepping, there is no sin on him. God is forgiving, merciful. Those who conceal what God has sent down of the Book and sell it for a small price, those eat nothing but the Fire into their bellies. God will not speak to them on the Day of Resurrection, nor will he cleanse them, and for them there is a painful punishment. Those are the ones who have bought error in exchange for guidance, and punishment in exchange for forgiveness. So how patient they are with the Fire! That is because God has sent down the Book with the truth. And those who differ over the Book are far gone in opposition. Virtue is not that you turn your faces in the direction of the east and the west, but virtue is the one who believes in God and the Last Day, the angels, the Book and the prophets, and gives wealth, though he loves it, to relatives, orphans, the destitute, the traveler and those who ask, and to set slaves free, and keeps up the prayer and gives the alms; and those who keep their covenant when they make a covenant, and those who are patient in hardship and suffering and in time of battle. Those are the ones who are truthful, and those are the ones who are mindful of God.

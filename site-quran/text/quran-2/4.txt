Quran, surah 2, verses 83 to 103. And when we took the pledge of the Children of Israel: "You shall worship none but God, and do good to parents, and to relatives, orphans and the destitute, and say what is good to people, and keep up the prayer and give the alms." Then you turned away, except a few of you, and you were turning aside. And when we took your pledge: "You shall not shed your own blood or drive your own out of your homes." Then you affirmed it, and you bore witness. Then here you are, killing your own and driving a group of you out of their homes, backing one another against them in sin and aggression. And if they come to you as captives, you ransom them, though it is forbidden to you to drive them out. So do you believe in part of the Book and disbelieve in part? Then what is the repayment for those of you who do that, except disgrace in the life of this world? And on the Day of Resurrection they will be sent back to the most severe punishment. And God is not unaware of what you do. Those are the ones who have bought the life of this world in exchange for the hereafter, so the punishment will not be lightened for them, nor will they be aided. And we certainly gave Moses the Book and made the messengers follow after him, and we gave Jesus son of Mary the clear proofs and strengthened him with the Holy Spirit. So is it that whenever a messenger came to you with what your souls did not desire, you were arrogant, and some you called liars and some you kill? And they said, "Our hearts are wrapped." Rather, God has cursed them for their disbelief, so it is little that they believe. And when a Book from God came to them, confirming what is with them, and before that they used to ask for victory over those who disbelieve, then when what they recognized came to them, they disbelieved in it. So God's curse is on the disbelievers. Miserable is what they sold themselves for: that they disbelieve in what God has sent down, out of resentment that God should send down from his bounty on whomever he wills among his servants. So they came away with anger upon anger, and for the disbelievers there is a degrading punishment. And when they are told, "Believe in what God has sent down," they say, "We believe in what was sent down to us," and they disbelieve in what is beyond it, though it is the truth, confirming what is with them. Say, "Then why did you kill God's prophets before, if you are believers?" And Moses certainly came to you with the clear proofs, then you took the calf for yourselves after he had gone, and you were doing wrong. And when we took your pledge and raised the Mount above you: "Take with strength what we have given you, and hear." They said, "We hear and we disobey." And they were made to drink the calf into their hearts for their disbelief. Say, "Miserable is what your belief commands you to do, if you are believers." Say, "If the home of the hereafter with God is yours alone, apart from other people, then wish for death, if you are telling the truth." But they will never wish for it, because of what their hands have sent ahead. And God has knowledge of the wrongdoers. And you will certainly find them the most eager of people for life, and more so than those who give God partners. Each one of them would love to be given a life of a thousand years, but to be given a long life would not move him away from the punishment. And God sees what they do. Say, "Whoever is an enemy to Gabriel, it is he who has sent it down on your heart by God's permission, confirming what was before it, and as guidance and good news for the believers. Whoever is an enemy to God and his angels and his messengers, and Gabriel and Michael, then God is an enemy to the disbelievers." And we have certainly sent down to you clear signs, and no one disbelieves in them except the defiant. And is it that whenever they made a covenant, a group of them threw it away? Rather, most of them do not believe. And when a messenger from God came to them, confirming what is with them, a group of those who were given the Book threw the Book of God behind their backs, as if they did not know. And they followed what the devils recited against the kingdom of Solomon. Solomon did not disbelieve, but the devils disbelieved, teaching people magic and what was sent down to the two angels in Babylon, Harut and Marut. And the two do not teach anyone until they say, "We are only a trial, so do not disbelieve." Then they learn from the two what they use to separate a man from his spouse. But they do not harm anyone with it except by God's permission. And they learn what harms them and does not benefit them. And they know well that whoever buys it has no share in the hereafter. And miserable is what they sold themselves for, if only they knew. And if they had believed and been mindful of God, a recompense from God would have been better, if only they knew.

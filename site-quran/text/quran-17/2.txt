Quran, surah 17, verses 22 to 44. Do not set up another god with God, or you will sit condemned and abandoned. And your Lord has decreed that you worship none but him, and do good to parents. If one of them or both of them reach old age with you, do not say "Ugh" to them, and do not scold them, but speak to them with gracious words. And lower to them the wing of humility, out of mercy, and say, "My Lord, have mercy on them, as they raised me when I was small." Your Lord knows best what is within yourselves. If you are righteous, he is forgiving to those who keep returning. And give the relative his due, and the destitute, and the traveler, and do not squander wastefully. Those who squander are brothers of the devils, and Satan is ungrateful to his Lord. And if you turn aside from them, seeking a mercy from your Lord that you hope for, then speak to them with gentle words. And do not keep your hand chained to your neck, and do not extend it to its full extent, or you will sit blamed and stripped bare. Your Lord extends provision to whomever he wills, and measures it out. He is aware of his servants and sees them. And do not kill your children for fear of poverty. It is we who provide for them and for you. Killing them is a great wrong. And do not go near unlawful sex. It is an indecency, and an evil way. And do not kill the soul that God has made sacred, except by right. And whoever is killed wrongfully, we have given authority to the one who acts for him, but let him not go too far in killing. He is aided. And do not go near the orphan's wealth, except in the way that is best, until he reaches his full strength. And keep the covenant. The covenant will be asked about. And give full measure when you measure, and weigh with the straight scale. That is better, and best in outcome. And do not follow after what you have no knowledge of. The hearing, the sight and the heart, each of those will be asked about. And do not walk on the earth swaggering. You will not tear through the earth, and you will not reach the mountains in height. In all of that, what is evil is hateful in the sight of your Lord. That is part of the wisdom your Lord has revealed to you. And do not set up another god with God, or you will be thrown into Hell, blamed and banished. Has your Lord, then, chosen sons for you and taken females for himself from among the angels? You are indeed saying a grave thing. And we have certainly put it in many ways in this Quran, so that they may remember, but it only increases them in aversion. Say, "If there were gods with him, as they say, then they would have sought a way to the Possessor of the Throne." Glory to him! And he is high above what they say, by a great height. The seven heavens and the earth and whoever is in them glorify him. And there is nothing that does not glorify him with praise, but you do not understand their glorifying. He is forbearing, forgiving.

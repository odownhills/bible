Quran, surah 17, verses 90 to 111. And they said, "We will not believe you until you make a spring gush out of the earth for us, or you have a garden of date palms and grapevines, and you make rivers gush out through the midst of it in abundance, or you make the sky fall on us in pieces, as you have claimed, or you bring God and the angels face to face with us, or you have a house of gold, or you climb up into the sky. And we will not believe in your climb until you send down to us a book that we can read." Say, "Glory to my Lord! Am I anything but a human being, a messenger?" And nothing kept people from believing when the guidance came to them except that they said, "Has God raised up a human being as a messenger?" Say, "If there were angels on the earth, walking about at ease, we would have sent down to them from the sky an angel as a messenger." Say, "God is enough as a witness between me and you. He is aware of his servants and sees them." And whoever God guides, he is the one who is guided. And whoever he leads astray, you will find no allies for them apart from him. And we will gather them on the Day of Resurrection on their faces, blind, mute and deaf. Their shelter is Hell. Whenever it dies down, we increase the flame for them. That is their repayment, because they disbelieved in our signs and said, "When we are bones and crumbled remains, will we really be raised up as a new creation?" Have they not seen that God, who created the heavens and the earth, has the power to create the like of them? And he has set a term for them in which there is no doubt. But the wrongdoers have refused anything but disbelief. Say, "If it were you who owned the storehouses of my Lord's mercy, then you would hold back for fear of spending. And man is tightfisted." And we certainly gave Moses nine clear signs. So ask the Children of Israel. When he came to them, Pharaoh said to him, "I do think, Moses, that you are bewitched." He said, "You know well that no one but the Lord of the heavens and the earth has sent these down as insights. And I do think, Pharaoh, that you are doomed." So he wanted to unsettle them from the land, but we drowned him and those with him, all together. And after him we said to the Children of Israel, "Live in the land. So when the promise of the hereafter comes, we will bring you as a mixed crowd." And with the truth we have sent it down, and with the truth it has come down. And we have sent you only as a bringer of good news and a warner. And it is a Quran that we have divided, so that you may read it to the people slowly, and we have sent it down in stages. Say, "Believe in it or do not believe. Those who were given knowledge before it, when it is recited to them, fall on their chins, bowing down, and they say, 'Glory to our Lord! The promise of our Lord is indeed carried out.' And they fall on their chins weeping, and it increases them in humility." Say, "Call on God, or call on the All-Merciful. Whichever you call on, to him belong the best names." And do not be loud in your prayer, and do not be hushed in it, but seek a way between the two. And say, "Praise belongs to God, who has not taken a child for himself, and has no partner in the kingdom, and has no ally against humiliation." And declare how great he is.

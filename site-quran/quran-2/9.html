Surah 2 · The Cow

Quran 2:204-220

▶ Listen from here

One whose speech impresses you

204And among people there is one whose speech impresses you in the life of this world [alt: whose speech about the life of this world impresses you], and he calls God to witness what is in his heart, though he is the fiercest in a quarrel. 205And when he turns away [alt: And when he takes charge], he goes about the earth to cause corruption on it and to destroy crops and offspring. And God does not love corruption. 206And when he is told, "Be mindful of God," pride takes hold of him in sin. So Hell is enough for him. And miserable is the resting place.

One who sells himself

207And among people there is one who sells himself, seeking to please God. And God is kind to his servants.

Enter into peace

208You who believe, enter into peace [alt: into submission], all together, and do not follow Satan's footsteps. He is a clear enemy to you. 209But if you slip after the clear proofs have come to you, then know that God is mighty, wise.

What are they waiting for?

210Are they waiting for anything but that God should come to them in canopies of clouds, and the angels, and the matter is decided? And to God matters are returned.

Ask the Children of Israel

211Ask the Children of Israel how many clear signs we gave them. And whoever changes God's favor after it has come to him, God is severe in retribution.

The life of this world

212The life of this world has been made attractive to those who disbelieve, and they ridicule those who believe. But those who are mindful of God will be above them on the Day of Resurrection. And God provides for whomever he wills without reckoning.

One community

213People were one community, then God raised up the prophets as bringers of good news and warners, and sent down with them the Book with the truth, to judge between people about what they differed over. And no one differed over it except those who were given it, after the clear proofs had come to them, out of resentment among themselves. So God guided those who believe to the truth that they differed over, by his permission. And God guides whomever he wills to a straight path.

When is God's aid?

214Or did you suppose that you would enter the Garden when the example of those who passed away before you has not yet come to you? Hardship and suffering touched them, and they were shaken, until the messenger and those who believed with him said, "When is God's aid?" Truly, God's aid is near.

What they should spend

215They ask you what they should spend. Say, "Whatever good you spend is for parents, nearest relatives, orphans, the destitute and the traveler." And whatever good you do, God has knowledge of it.

Fighting is prescribed for you

216Fighting is prescribed for you, though it is hateful to you. But it may be that you hate a thing while it is good for you, and it may be that you love a thing while it is bad for you. And God knows, and you do not know.

Fighting in the sacred month

217They ask you about the sacred month, about fighting in it. Say, "Fighting in it is grave, but barring people from the way of God, and disbelief in him, and from the Sacred Place of Worship, and driving its people out of it, are graver in the sight of God. And persecution is graver than killing." And they will not stop fighting you until they turn you back from your religion, if they can. And whoever of you turns back from his religion and dies as a disbeliever, the deeds of those have come to nothing in this world and the hereafter. And those are the companions of the Fire; they will remain in it forever.

218Those who believe, and those who have emigrated and striven in the way of God, those hope for God's mercy. And God is forgiving, merciful.

Wine, gambling and orphans

219They ask you about wine and gambling. Say, "In both there is great sin [alt: much sin], and benefits for people, and their sin is greater than their benefit." And they ask you what they should spend. Say, "What you can spare." That is how God makes the signs clear to you, so that you may reflect 220on this world and the hereafter. And they ask you about orphans. Say, "Setting things right for them is good. And if you mingle with them, they are your brothers." And God knows the one who causes corruption from the one who sets things right. And if God had willed, he would have put you in distress. God is mighty, wise.

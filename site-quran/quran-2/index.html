In the name of God, the Most Merciful, the Merciful.

That is the Book

1A. L. M. [These are three letters of the Arabic alphabet, standing on their own.] 2That is the Book. There is no doubt in it; it is guidance [alt: There is no doubt. In it is guidance] for those who are mindful of God, 3those who believe in the unseen [alt: believe while unseen], keep up the prayer, and spend from what we have provided for them, 4and those who believe in what has been sent down to you and what was sent down before you, and of the hereafter they are certain. 5Those are on guidance from their Lord, and those are the ones who succeed.

Those who disbelieve

6As for those who disbelieve, it is the same for them whether you warn them or do not warn them: they do not believe. 7God has set a seal on their hearts and on their hearing, and over their sight is a covering, and for them there is a great punishment.

Those who say they believe

8And among people there are some who say, "We believe in God and in the Last Day," but they are not believers. 9They try to deceive God and those who believe, but they deceive only themselves, and they do not realize it. 10In their hearts is a sickness, so God has increased their sickness, and for them there is a painful punishment because they used to lie [alt: because they used to call it a lie].

11And when they are told, "Do not cause corruption on the earth," they say, "We are only setting things right." 12Truly, they are the ones who cause corruption, but they do not realize it. 13And when they are told, "Believe as the people have believed," they say, "Should we believe as the fools have believed?" Truly, they are the fools, but they do not know.

14And when they meet those who believe, they say, "We believe," but when they are alone with their devils, they say, "We are with you; we are only mocking." 15God mocks them and keeps them going in their excess, wandering blindly. 16Those are the ones who have bought error in exchange for guidance, so their trade has made no profit, and they were not guided.

A fire and a rainstorm

17Their example is like that of one who kindled a fire, and when it lit up what was around him, God took away their light and left them in darkness, unable to see. 18Deaf, mute, blind, so they do not return.

19Or like a rainstorm from the sky, with darkness, thunder and lightning in it. They put their fingers in their ears because of the thunderbolts, for fear of death. And God surrounds the disbelievers. 20The lightning almost snatches away their sight. Whenever it lights up for them, they walk in it, and when it grows dark over them, they stand still. And if God had willed, he would have taken away their hearing and their sight. God has power over all things.

Worship your Lord

21People, worship your Lord, who created you and those before you, so that you may be mindful of him, 22the one who made the earth a bed for you and the sky a building, and sent down water from the sky and with it brought out fruits as provision for you. So do not set up rivals to God while you know.

23And if you are in doubt about what we have sent down to our servant, then bring a surah like it [alt: a surah from someone like him], and call your witnesses other than God, if you are telling the truth. 24But if you do not, and you never will, then guard yourselves against the Fire whose fuel is people and stones, prepared for the disbelievers. [In Arabic "guard yourselves against" is the same word as "mindful of" in verses 2 and 21.]

25And give good news to those who believe and do righteous deeds, that for them there are gardens with rivers flowing beneath them. Whenever they are provided with a provision of fruit from them, they say, "This is what we were provided with before," and what they are brought looks alike. There they have purified spouses, and there they will remain forever.

The example of a gnat

26God does not shy away from giving any example, a gnat or anything above it. As for those who believe, they know that it is the truth from their Lord. But as for those who disbelieve, they say, "What did God mean by this example?" By it he leads many astray, and by it he guides many, but he leads astray by it only the defiant, 27those who break God's covenant after it has been made binding, cut what God has commanded to be joined, and cause corruption on the earth. Those are the losers.

How can you disbelieve in God?

28How can you disbelieve in God, when you were dead and he gave you life, then he will cause you to die, then he will give you life, then to him you will be returned? 29He is the one who created for you all that is on the earth, then turned to the sky and formed them into seven heavens, and he has knowledge of all things.

A successor on the earth

30And when your Lord said to the angels, "I am placing a successor [alt: a deputy] on the earth," they said, "Will you place on it one who will cause corruption there and shed blood, while we glorify you with praise and declare you holy?" He said, "I know what you do not know." 31And he taught Adam the names, all of them. Then he presented them to the angels and said, "Tell me the names of these, if you are telling the truth." 32They said, "Glory to you! We have no knowledge except what you have taught us. You are the Knowing, the Wise." 33He said, "Adam, tell them their names." And when he had told them their names, he said, "Did I not say to you that I know the unseen of the heavens and the earth, and know what you disclose and what you have been concealing?"

Bow down to Adam

34And when we said to the angels, "Bow down to Adam," they bowed down, except Iblis. He refused and was arrogant, and he was one of the disbelievers. 35And we said, "Adam, live in the Garden, you and your spouse, and eat from it freely wherever you wish, but do not go near this tree, or you will be among the wrongdoers." 36Then Satan made them slip from it and brought them out of what they had been in. And we said, "Go down, enemies to one another. And on the earth you have a place to settle and enjoyment for a time." 37Then Adam received words from his Lord, and he relented toward him. He is the Ever-Relenting, the Merciful.

38We said, "Go down from it, all of you. Then if guidance comes to you from me, for those who follow my guidance there is no fear, nor will they grieve. 39But those who disbelieve and call our signs lies, those are the companions of the Fire; they will remain in it forever."

Keep my covenant

40Children of Israel, remember my favor that I showed you, and keep my covenant, and I will keep your covenant, and it is me you should dread. 41And believe in what I have sent down, confirming what is with you, and do not be the first to disbelieve in it. And do not sell my signs for a small price, and it is me you should be mindful of. 42And do not mix the truth with falsehood or conceal the truth while you know. 43And keep up the prayer and give the alms, and bow with those who bow.

44Do you command people to virtue and forget yourselves, while you recite the Book? Will you not use your reason? 45And ask for help through patience and prayer. And it is hard indeed, except for the humble, 46those who think that they will meet their Lord and that to him they will return.

Remember my favor

47Children of Israel, remember my favor that I showed you, and that I set you above all peoples. 48And guard yourselves against a day when no soul will pay anything on behalf of another soul, and no intercession will be accepted from it, and no compensation will be taken from it, nor will they be aided.

49And when we saved you from Pharaoh's people, who were inflicting a terrible punishment on you, slaughtering your sons and letting your women live. And in that there was a great test from your Lord. 50And when we parted the sea for you, and saved you, and drowned Pharaoh's people while you looked on.

The calf

51And when we appointed forty nights for Moses, then you took the calf for yourselves after he had gone, and you were doing wrong. 52Then we pardoned you after that, so that you might give thanks. 53And when we gave Moses the Book and the Criterion [alt: the deliverance], so that you might be guided. 54And when Moses said to his people, "My people, you have wronged yourselves by taking the calf for yourselves, so repent to your Maker and kill yourselves [alt: kill one another]. That is better for you in the sight of your Maker." Then he relented toward you. He is the Ever-Relenting, the Merciful. [In Arabic "repent" and "relent" are one word, to turn back: the people to their Maker, and he to them.]

The thunderbolt and the manna

55And when you said, "Moses, we will not believe you until we see God in plain sight," the thunderbolt took you while you looked on. 56Then we raised you up after your death, so that you might give thanks. 57And we shaded you with clouds and sent down manna and quails to you: "Eat from the good things we have provided for you." And they did not wrong us, but it was themselves they were wronging.

The town, the rock and the food

58And when we said, "Enter this town and eat from it wherever you wish, freely, and enter the gate bowing down, and say, 'Unburden us,' and we will forgive you your faults. And we will give more to those who do good." 59But those who did wrong put a different saying in place of the one they had been told, so we sent down on those who did wrong a plague from the sky, because they were defiant.

60And when Moses asked for water for his people, we said, "Strike the rock with your staff," and twelve springs gushed out of it. Each group of people knew its drinking place. "Eat and drink from God's provision, and do not act wickedly on the earth, causing corruption." 61And when you said, "Moses, we will not put up with one food, so call on your Lord for us, to bring out for us some of what the earth grows: its greens, its cucumbers, its garlic [alt: its grain], its lentils and its onions," he said, "Would you take what is lower in exchange for what is better? Go down to a city [alt: Go down to Egypt], and you will have what you asked for." And humiliation and destitution were stamped on them, and they came away with anger from God. That was because they used to disbelieve in God's signs and kill the prophets without right. That was because they disobeyed and used to overstep.

Whoever believes in God and the Last Day

62Those who believe, and those who are Jews, and the Christians, and the Sabians, whoever believes in God and the Last Day and does what is righteous, they have their reward with their Lord, and there is no fear for them, nor will they grieve.

The Mount and the Sabbath

63And when we took your pledge and raised the Mount above you: "Take with strength what we have given you, and remember what is in it, so that you may be mindful of God." 64Then you turned away after that, and had it not been for God's bounty to you and his mercy, you would have been among the losers.

65And you know well those of you who overstepped on the Sabbath, so we said to them, "Be apes, driven away." 66So we made it a deterrent for what was before it and what was behind it, and a lesson for those who are mindful of God.

The cow

67And when Moses said to his people, "God commands you to slaughter a cow," they said, "Are you making a mockery of us?" He said, "I take refuge in God from being one of the ignorant." 68They said, "Call on your Lord for us, to make clear to us what it is." He said, "He says it is a cow neither old nor young, of middle age, between the two. So do what you are commanded." 69They said, "Call on your Lord for us, to make clear to us what its color is." He said, "He says it is a yellow cow, bright in color, that delights those who look at it." 70They said, "Call on your Lord for us, to make clear to us what it is. Cows look alike to us, and if God wills, we will certainly be guided." 71He said, "He says it is a cow not broken in to turn the earth or water the field, sound, with no mark on it." They said, "Now you have brought the truth." So they slaughtered it, and they almost did not do it.

72And when you killed a soul, you blamed one another for it, and God would bring out what you were concealing. 73So we said, "Strike him with part of it." That is how God gives life to the dead, and he shows you his signs so that you may use your reason.

Hearts like stones

74Then your hearts hardened after that, so they are like stones, or harder. And among stones there are some from which rivers gush out, and among them there are some that split open and water comes out of them, and among them there are some that fall down out of fear of God. And God is not unaware of what you do.

Do you hope that they will believe you?

75So do you hope that they will believe you, when a group of them used to hear God's speech and then twist it after they had understood it, while they knew? 76And when they meet those who believe, they say, "We believe," but when they are alone with one another, they say, "Do you talk to them about what God has opened to you, so that they may argue against you with it before your Lord? Will you not use your reason?" [alt: their speech ends at "before your Lord?" and the last question is God's] 77Do they not know that God knows what they keep secret and what they make public?

Those who write the Book with their hands

78And among them are unlettered people who do not know the Book, only wishful thinking [alt: who know the Book only as recitation], and they are only guessing. 79So woe to those who write the Book with their hands and then say, "This is from God," to sell it for a small price. So woe to them for what their hands have written, and woe to them for what they earn. [In Arabic "the Book" is the word for a thing written, from the same word as "write".]

A limited number of days

80And they said, "The Fire will not touch us except for a limited number of days." Say, "Have you taken a covenant with God? Then God will not go back on his covenant. Or do you say about God what you do not know?" 81Not so. Whoever earns evil and is surrounded by his fault, those are the companions of the Fire; they will remain in it forever. 82And those who believe and do righteous deeds, those are the companions of the Garden; they will remain in it forever.

The pledge of the Children of Israel

83And when we took the pledge of the Children of Israel: "You shall worship none but God, and do good to parents, and to relatives, orphans and the destitute, and say what is good to people, and keep up the prayer and give the alms." Then you turned away, except a few of you, and you were turning aside. 84And when we took your pledge: "You shall not shed your own blood or drive your own out of your homes." Then you affirmed it, and you bore witness. 85Then here you are, killing your own and driving a group of you out of their homes, backing one another against them in sin and aggression. And if they come to you as captives, you ransom them, though it is forbidden to you to drive them out. So do you believe in part of the Book and disbelieve in part? Then what is the repayment for those of you who do that, except disgrace in the life of this world? And on the Day of Resurrection they will be sent back to the most severe punishment. And God is not unaware of what you do. 86Those are the ones who have bought the life of this world in exchange for the hereafter, so the punishment will not be lightened for them, nor will they be aided.

Whenever a messenger came to you

87And we certainly gave Moses the Book and made the messengers follow after him, and we gave Jesus son of Mary the clear proofs and strengthened him with the Holy Spirit. So is it that whenever a messenger came to you with what your souls did not desire, you were arrogant, and some you called liars and some you kill? 88And they said, "Our hearts are wrapped." Rather, God has cursed them for their disbelief, so it is little that they believe.

A Book confirming what is with them

89And when a Book from God came to them, confirming what is with them, and before that they used to ask for victory over those who disbelieve, then when what they recognized came to them, they disbelieved in it. So God's curse is on the disbelievers. 90Miserable is what they sold themselves for: that they disbelieve in what God has sent down, out of resentment that God should send down from his bounty on whomever he wills among his servants. So they came away with anger upon anger, and for the disbelievers there is a degrading punishment. 91And when they are told, "Believe in what God has sent down," they say, "We believe in what was sent down to us," and they disbelieve in what is beyond it, though it is the truth, confirming what is with them. Say, "Then why did you kill God's prophets before, if you are believers?"

We hear and we disobey

92And Moses certainly came to you with the clear proofs, then you took the calf for yourselves after he had gone, and you were doing wrong. 93And when we took your pledge and raised the Mount above you: "Take with strength what we have given you, and hear." They said, "We hear and we disobey." And they were made to drink the calf into their hearts for their disbelief. Say, "Miserable is what your belief commands you to do, if you are believers."

Then wish for death

94Say, "If the home of the hereafter with God is yours alone, apart from other people, then wish for death, if you are telling the truth." 95But they will never wish for it, because of what their hands have sent ahead. And God has knowledge of the wrongdoers. 96And you will certainly find them the most eager of people for life, and more so than those who give God partners. Each one of them would love to be given a life of a thousand years, but to be given a long life would not move him away from the punishment. And God sees what they do.

An enemy to Gabriel

97Say, "Whoever is an enemy to Gabriel, it is he who has sent it down on your heart by God's permission, confirming what was before it, and as guidance and good news for the believers. 98Whoever is an enemy to God and his angels and his messengers, and Gabriel and Michael, then God is an enemy to the disbelievers."

The Book of God behind their backs

99And we have certainly sent down to you clear signs, and no one disbelieves in them except the defiant. 100And is it that whenever they made a covenant, a group of them threw it away? Rather, most of them do not believe. 101And when a messenger from God came to them, confirming what is with them, a group of those who were given the Book threw the Book of God behind their backs, as if they did not know.

What the devils recited

102And they followed what the devils recited against the kingdom of Solomon [alt: recited in the reign of Solomon]. Solomon did not disbelieve, but the devils disbelieved, teaching people magic and what was sent down to the two angels in Babylon, Harut and Marut [alt: and it was not sent down to the two angels in Babylon, Harut and Marut]. And the two do not teach anyone until they say, "We are only a trial, so do not disbelieve." Then they learn from the two what they use to separate a man from his spouse. But they do not harm anyone with it except by God's permission. And they learn what harms them and does not benefit them. And they know well that whoever buys it has no share in the hereafter. And miserable is what they sold themselves for, if only they knew. 103And if they had believed and been mindful of God, a recompense from God would have been better, if only they knew.

The word not to say

104You who believe, do not say, "Attend to us," but say, "Wait for us [alt: Look at us]," and hear. And for the disbelievers there is a painful punishment. [In Arabic the first of these requests could be twisted with the tongue into an insult, and 4:46 tells of people who said it that way.] 105Those who disbelieve, among the People of the Book or among those who give God partners, would not love any good to be sent down to you from your Lord. But God singles out for his mercy whomever he wills, and God is the possessor of great bounty.

Whatever sign we cancel

106Whatever sign we cancel or cause to be forgotten [alt: or postpone], we bring something better than it or like it. Do you not know that God has power over all things? 107Do you not know that the kingdom of the heavens and the earth belongs to God? And apart from God you have no ally and no one to aid you. 108Or do you want to ask your messenger as Moses was asked before? And whoever takes disbelief in exchange for belief has gone astray from the even way.

Pardon and overlook

109Many of the People of the Book would love to turn you back into disbelievers after your belief, out of envy from their own selves, after the truth has become clear to them. So pardon and overlook, until God brings his command. God has power over all things. 110And keep up the prayer and give the alms. And whatever good you send ahead for yourselves, you will find it with God. God sees what you do.

Bring your proof

111And they said, "No one will enter the Garden except those who are Jews or Christians." That is their wishful thinking. Say, "Bring your proof, if you are telling the truth." 112Not so. Whoever submits his face to God and does good, he has his reward with his Lord, and there is no fear for them, nor will they grieve.

113And the Jews said, "The Christians have nothing to stand on," and the Christians said, "The Jews have nothing to stand on," while they recite the Book. In the same way, those who do not know said something like what they said. So God will judge between them on the Day of Resurrection about what they used to differ over.

Wherever you turn

114And who does greater wrong than one who keeps God's places of worship from having his name remembered in them, and strives for their ruin? It is not for those to enter them except in fear. For them there is disgrace in this world, and for them in the hereafter there is a great punishment. 115And to God belong the east and the west, so wherever you turn, there is the face of God. God is vast, knowing.

Be, and it is

116And they said, "God has taken a child for himself." Glory to him! Rather, what is in the heavens and the earth belongs to him; all are obedient to him. 117He is the Originator of the heavens and the earth, and when he decides a matter, he only says to it, "Be," and it is.

Sent with the truth

118And those who do not know said, "Why does God not speak to us, or a sign come to us?" In the same way, those before them said something like what they said. Their hearts are alike. We have made the signs clear for a people who are certain. 119We have sent you with the truth, as a bringer of good news and a warner, and you will not be asked about the companions of the Blaze [alt: and do not ask about the companions of the Blaze].

God's guidance is the guidance

120And the Jews will not be pleased with you, nor the Christians, until you follow their creed. Say, "God's guidance is the guidance." And if you were to follow their desires after the knowledge that has come to you, you would have no ally and no one to aid you against God. 121Those to whom we gave the Book, reciting it as it should be recited, those believe in it. And whoever disbelieves in it, those are the losers.

A day when no soul pays for another

122Children of Israel, remember my favor that I showed you, and that I set you above all peoples. 123And guard yourselves against a day when no soul will pay anything on behalf of another soul, and no compensation will be accepted from it, and no intercession will benefit it, nor will they be aided.

Abraham and the House

124And when his Lord tested Abraham with words, he fulfilled them. He said, "I am making you a leader for the people." He said, "And of my descendants?" He said, "My covenant does not reach the wrongdoers." 125And when we made the House a place of return for the people and a place of safety. And take from the place where Abraham stood a place of prayer [alt: and they took from the place where Abraham stood a place of prayer]. And we made a covenant with Abraham and Ishmael: "Purify my House for those who go around it, those who stay there, and those who bow and bow down." 126And when Abraham said, "My Lord, make this a safe land, and provide its people with fruits, those of them who believe in God and the Last Day," he said, "And whoever disbelieves, I will let him enjoy himself a little, then I will force him to the punishment of the Fire. And miserable is the destination."

Raising the foundations

127And when Abraham was raising the foundations of the House, and Ishmael: "Our Lord, accept this from us. You are the Hearing, the Knowing. 128Our Lord, and make us both people who submit to you, and from our descendants a community that submits to you. And show us our rites, and relent toward us. You are the Ever-Relenting, the Merciful. 129Our Lord, and raise up among them a messenger from among them, who will recite your signs to them and teach them the Book and wisdom and cleanse them. You are the Mighty, the Wise."

The creed of Abraham

130And who wants no part of the creed of Abraham except one who makes a fool of himself? And we certainly chose him in this world, and in the hereafter he is among the righteous. 131When his Lord said to him, "Submit," he said, "I have submitted to the Lord of the worlds." 132And Abraham charged his sons with it, and so did Jacob: "My sons, God has chosen the religion for you, so do not die except as people who submit."

Jacob and his sons

133Or were you witnesses when death came to Jacob, when he said to his sons, "What will you worship after me?" They said, "We will worship your god and the god of your fathers, Abraham, Ishmael and Isaac, one god, and to him we submit." 134That is a community that has passed away. It has what it earned, and you have what you earned, and you will not be asked about what they used to do.

No distinction between any of them

135And they said, "Be Jews or Christians, and you will be guided." Say, "Rather, the creed of Abraham, who was devoted, and he was not one of those who give God partners." 136Say, "We believe in God and what has been sent down to us, and what was sent down to Abraham, Ishmael, Isaac, Jacob and the tribes, and what Moses and Jesus were given, and what the prophets were given from their Lord. We make no distinction between any of them, and to him we submit." 137So if they believe in the like of what you believe in, they are guided. But if they turn away, they are only in opposition. So God will be enough for you against them, and he is the Hearing, the Knowing. 138"The dye of God, and whose dye is better than God's? And it is him we worship."

Do you know better, or does God?

139Say, "Do you argue with us about God, when he is our Lord and your Lord, and we have our deeds and you have your deeds, and to him we are sincere?" 140Or do you say that Abraham, Ishmael, Isaac, Jacob and the tribes were Jews or Christians? Say, "Do you know better, or does God?" And who does greater wrong than one who conceals a testimony he has from God? And God is not unaware of what you do. 141That is a community that has passed away. It has what it earned, and you have what you earned, and you will not be asked about what they used to do.

A middle community

142The fools among the people will say, "What has turned them from the direction they used to face?" Say, "To God belong the east and the west. He guides whomever he wills to a straight path." 143And that is how we have made you a middle community [alt: a just community], so that you may be witnesses over the people and the messenger may be a witness over you. And we set the direction you used to face only so that we might know who follows the messenger from who turns back on his heels. And it was hard indeed, except for those God has guided. And God would not let your belief go to waste. God is kind to people, merciful.

Turn your face

144We do see your face turning about in the sky, so we will certainly turn you to a direction you will be pleased with. So turn your face toward the Sacred Place of Worship, and wherever you are, turn your faces toward it. And those who were given the Book know well that it is the truth from their Lord. And God is not unaware of what they do. 145And if you were to bring every sign to those who were given the Book, they would not follow your direction, nor do you follow their direction, nor do they follow one another's direction. And if you were to follow their desires after the knowledge that has come to you, you would then be among the wrongdoers.

146Those to whom we gave the Book recognize it [alt: recognize him] as they recognize their sons, but a group of them conceal the truth while they know. 147The truth is from your Lord, so do not be among the doubters.

Race to what is good

148And each has a direction he turns toward [alt: a direction he is turned toward], so race to what is good. Wherever you are, God will bring you all together. God has power over all things.

149And from wherever you go out, turn your face toward the Sacred Place of Worship. And it is certainly the truth from your Lord, and God is not unaware of what you do. 150And from wherever you go out, turn your face toward the Sacred Place of Worship, and wherever you are, turn your faces toward it, so that people may have no argument against you, except those of them who do wrong. So do not fear them, but fear me. And so that I may complete my favor to you, and so that you may be guided. 151Just as we have sent among you a messenger from among you, who recites our signs to you and cleanses you and teaches you the Book and wisdom, and teaches you what you did not know. 152So remember me, and I will remember you. And give thanks to me, and do not be ungrateful to me. [In Arabic "be ungrateful to" is the same word as "disbelieve in".]

Those who are patient

153You who believe, ask for help through patience and prayer. God is with those who are patient. 154And do not say of those who are killed in the way of God, "They are dead." Rather, they are alive, but you do not realize it.

155And we will certainly test you with something of fear and hunger, and a loss of wealth, lives and fruits. And give good news to those who are patient, 156those who, when a misfortune strikes them, say, "We belong to God, and to him we will return." 157On those are blessings from their Lord, and mercy, and those are the ones who are guided. [In Arabic "blessings" is the plural of the word for "prayer" in verse 153.]

Safa and Marwa

158Safa and Marwa are among the symbols of God. So whoever makes the pilgrimage to the House or visits it, there is no blame on him if he goes around them. And whoever does good of his own accord, God is thankful, knowing.

Those who conceal what was sent down

159Those who conceal what we have sent down of the clear proofs and the guidance, after we have made it clear to the people in the Book, God curses them, and those who curse curse them, 160except those who repent, set things right and make it clear. Those I relent toward, and I am the Ever-Relenting, the Merciful.

161Those who disbelieve and die as disbelievers, on those is the curse of God, the angels and the people, all of them, 162remaining in it forever. The punishment will not be lightened for them, nor will they be given time.

Your god is one god

163And your god is one god. There is no god but him, the Most Merciful, the Merciful. 164In the creation of the heavens and the earth, and the alternation of night and day, and the ships that sail on the sea with what benefits people, and the water God has sent down from the sky, giving life with it to the earth after its death and scattering on it animals of every kind, and the shifting of the winds, and the clouds made to serve between the sky and the earth, there are signs for a people who use reason.

Those who take rivals

165And among people there are some who take rivals for themselves apart from God, loving them as God is loved. But those who believe are stronger in love for God. And if only those who do wrong could see, when they see the punishment [alt: And if only you could see those who do wrong when they see the punishment], that all strength belongs to God and that God is severe in punishment, 166when those who were followed disown those who followed, and they see the punishment, and their ties are cut. 167And those who followed will say, "If only we could go back, we would disown them as they have disowned us." That is how God will show them their deeds, as regrets for them, and they will not come out of the Fire.

Satan's footsteps

168People, eat from what is on the earth, what is permitted and good, and do not follow Satan's footsteps. He is a clear enemy to you. 169He only commands you to evil and indecency, and to say about God what you do not know.

What we found our fathers on

170And when they are told, "Follow what God has sent down," they say, "Rather, we follow what we found our fathers on." Even if their fathers understood nothing and were not guided? 171And the example of those who disbelieve is like that of one who shouts at what hears nothing but a call and a cry. Deaf, mute, blind, so they do not use reason.

What he has forbidden you

172You who believe, eat from the good things we have provided for you, and give thanks to God, if it is him you worship. 173He has forbidden you only carrion, blood, the flesh of pigs, and what has been dedicated to other than God. But whoever is forced, neither transgressing nor overstepping [alt: neither desiring it nor overstepping], there is no sin on him. God is forgiving, merciful.

Nothing but the Fire

174Those who conceal what God has sent down of the Book and sell it for a small price, those eat nothing but the Fire into their bellies. God will not speak to them on the Day of Resurrection, nor will he cleanse them, and for them there is a painful punishment. 175Those are the ones who have bought error in exchange for guidance, and punishment in exchange for forgiveness. So how patient they are with the Fire! [alt: So what has made them patient with the Fire?] 176That is because God has sent down the Book with the truth. And those who differ over the Book are far gone in opposition.

Virtue

177Virtue is not that you turn your faces in the direction of the east and the west, but virtue is the one who believes in God and the Last Day, the angels, the Book and the prophets, and gives wealth, though he loves it [alt: out of love for him], to relatives, orphans, the destitute, the traveler and those who ask, and to set slaves free, and keeps up the prayer and gives the alms; and those who keep their covenant when they make a covenant, and those who are patient in hardship and suffering and in time of battle. Those are the ones who are truthful, and those are the ones who are mindful of God.

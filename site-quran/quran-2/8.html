Surah 2 · The Cow

Quran 2:178-203

▶ Listen from here

Retaliation

178You who believe, retaliation for those who are killed is prescribed for you: the free for the free, the slave for the slave, and the female for the female. But whoever is pardoned something by his brother, then there is to be a following up as is right, and payment to him in a good way. That is a lightening from your Lord, and a mercy. So whoever oversteps after that, for him there is a painful punishment. 179And there is life for you in retaliation, you who have understanding, so that you may be mindful of God.

The bequest

180It is prescribed for you, when death comes to one of you, if he leaves goods, to make a bequest to parents and nearest relatives, as is right: a duty on those who are mindful of God. 181Then whoever changes it after he has heard it, the sin of it is only on those who change it. God is hearing, knowing. 182But whoever fears partiality or sin from one making a bequest, and sets things right between them, there is no sin on him. God is forgiving, merciful.

Fasting

183You who believe, fasting is prescribed for you as it was prescribed for those before you, so that you may be mindful of God. 184For a limited number of days. But whoever among you is sick or on a journey, then a number of other days. And on those who can bear it there is a ransom: food for a destitute person. And whoever does good of his own accord, it is better for him. And to fast is better for you, if you know.

185The month of Ramadan is the one in which the Quran was sent down, as guidance for the people and as clear proofs of the guidance and the criterion. So whoever of you witnesses the month, let him fast it. And whoever is sick or on a journey, then a number of other days. God wants ease for you and does not want difficulty for you. And so that you may make the number full and declare God great for having guided you, and so that you may give thanks.

I am near

186And when my servants ask you about me, I am near. I answer the call of the one who calls, when he calls on me. So let them answer me and believe in me, so that they may take the right course.

The night of the fast

187It is permitted to you, on the night of the fast, to be intimate with your wives. They are a garment for you, and you are a garment for them. God knew that you were betraying yourselves, so he relented toward you and pardoned you. So now lie with them, and seek what God has written for you. And eat and drink until the white thread of the dawn becomes clear to you from the black thread. Then complete the fast until the night. And do not lie with them while you are staying in the places of worship. Those are God's limits, so do not go near them. That is how God makes his signs clear to the people, so that they may be mindful of him. [In Arabic "written" in this verse is the same word as "prescribed" in verses 178, 180 and 183.]

Your wealth among yourselves

188And do not eat up your wealth among yourselves by falsehood, or put it before the judges so that you may eat up a part of people's wealth in sin, while you know.

The new moons

189They ask you about the new moons. Say, "They are appointed times for the people and for the pilgrimage." And virtue is not that you come to houses from the back, but virtue is the one who is mindful of God. And come to houses by their doors, and be mindful of God, so that you may succeed.

Fight those who fight you

190And fight in the way of God those who fight you, and do not overstep. God does not love those who overstep. 191And kill them wherever you come upon them, and drive them out from where they drove you out. And persecution is more severe than killing. And do not fight them at the Sacred Place of Worship until they fight you there. But if they fight you, then kill them [alt: And do not kill them at the Sacred Place of Worship until they kill some of you there. But if they kill some of you, then kill them]. Such is the repayment of the disbelievers. 192But if they stop, God is forgiving, merciful. 193And fight them until there is no persecution and the religion belongs to God. But if they stop, then there is no aggression except against the wrongdoers.

194The sacred month for the sacred month, and for sacred things there is retaliation. So whoever oversteps against you, overstep against him in the same way he overstepped against you. And be mindful of God, and know that God is with those who are mindful of him. 195And spend in the way of God, and do not throw yourselves into destruction with your own hands, and do good. God loves those who do good.

The pilgrimage and the visit

196And complete the pilgrimage and the visit for God. But if you are held back, then whatever offering comes easily. And do not shave your heads until the offering reaches its place. But whoever among you is sick or has an ailment of the head, then a ransom of fasting or charity or sacrifice. And when you are safe, whoever enjoys the visit until the pilgrimage, then whatever offering comes easily. But whoever finds none, then a fast of three days in the pilgrimage and seven when you return. Those are ten in full. That is for one whose family is not present at the Sacred Place of Worship. And be mindful of God, and know that God is severe in retribution. 197The pilgrimage is in known months. So whoever undertakes the pilgrimage in them, then there is no intimacy, no defiance and no dispute in the pilgrimage [alt: and there is no dispute about the pilgrimage]. And whatever good you do, God knows it. And take supplies, for the best supply is mindfulness of God. And be mindful of me, you who have understanding.

Pouring out from Arafat

198There is no blame on you if you seek bounty from your Lord. Then when you pour out from Arafat, remember God at the Sacred Landmark. And remember him as he has guided you, and before that you were indeed among those who had gone astray. 199Then pour out from where the people pour out, and ask God for forgiveness. God is forgiving, merciful.

When you have finished your rites

200And when you have finished your rites, remember God as you remember your fathers, or with a stronger remembrance. And among people there are some who say, "Our Lord, give to us in this world," and they have no share in the hereafter. 201And among them there are some who say, "Our Lord, give us good in this world and good in the hereafter, and guard us against the punishment of the Fire." 202Those have a portion of what they have earned, and God is swift in reckoning.

203And remember God during a limited number of days. Then whoever hurries away in two days, there is no sin on him, and whoever delays, there is no sin on him, for whoever is mindful of God. And be mindful of God, and know that to him you will be gathered.

Surah 17

The Night Journey

In the name of God, the All-Merciful, the Ever-Merciful.

The journey by night

1Glory to the one who took his servant by night from the Sacred Place of Worship to the Farthest Place of Worship, whose surroundings we have blessed, so that we might show him some of our signs. He is the Hearing, the Seeing.

The decree to the Children of Israel

2And we gave Moses the Book and made it guidance for the Children of Israel: "Do not take for yourselves anyone to rely on other than me, 3descendants of those we carried with Noah." He was a thankful servant.

4And we decreed to the Children of Israel in the Book: You will certainly cause corruption on the earth twice, and you will certainly rise to a great height. 5So when the promise of the first of the two comes, we will raise up against you [alt: So when the promise of the first of the two came, we raised up against you] servants of ours who are fierce in battle, and they will go back and forth among the homes. And it is a promise that will be carried out. 6Then we will give you back the turn against them, and we will supply you with wealth and sons and make you more numerous in men. 7If you do good, you do good for yourselves, and if you do evil, it is for yourselves. So when the promise of the last one comes, it is so that they may bring grief to your faces, and enter the place of worship as they entered it the first time, and bring to utter ruin whatever they overcome.

8It may be that your Lord will have mercy on you. But if you go back, we will go back. And we have made Hell a prison [alt: a mat] for the disbelievers.

This Quran guides

9This Quran guides to what is straightest, and it gives good news to the believers who do righteous deeds that for them there is a great reward, 10and that for those who do not believe in the hereafter we have prepared a painful punishment. 11And man calls for evil as he calls for good, and man is hasty.

Read your book

12And we have made the night and the day two signs. Then we erased the sign of the night and made the sign of the day give sight, so that you may seek bounty from your Lord, and so that you may know the number of the years and the reckoning. And everything we have set out in detail. 13And every person, we have fastened his fate to his neck. [In Arabic the word for "fate" here is "bird", which is also the word for an omen.] And on the Day of Resurrection we will bring out for him a book that he will meet spread open: 14"Read your book. Your own soul is enough today as a reckoner against you." 15Whoever is guided is guided only for himself, and whoever goes astray goes astray only against himself. And no bearer of burdens bears the burden of another. And we would not punish until we had raised up a messenger.

When we want to destroy a town

16And when we want to destroy a town, we command those in it who live in luxury [alt: we multiply those in it who live in luxury], and they are defiant in it, so the word comes true against it, and we demolish it completely. 17And how many generations we have destroyed after Noah! And your Lord is enough as one who is aware of the sins of his servants and sees them.

The hasty life and the hereafter

18Whoever wants the hasty life, we hasten for him in it whatever we will, for whomever we want. Then we make Hell his, to burn in it, condemned and banished. 19And whoever wants the hereafter and strives for it with the striving due to it, and is a believer, those are the ones whose striving is thanked. 20Each one we supply, these and those, from the gift of your Lord. And the gift of your Lord is not restricted. 21Look how we have set some of them above others. And the hereafter is indeed greater in degrees and greater in setting some above others.

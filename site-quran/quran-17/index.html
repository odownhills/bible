In the name of God, the All-Merciful, the Ever-Merciful.

The journey by night

1Glory to the one who took his servant by night from the Sacred Place of Worship to the Farthest Place of Worship, whose surroundings we have blessed, so that we might show him some of our signs. He is the Hearing, the Seeing.

The decree to the Children of Israel

2And we gave Moses the Book and made it guidance for the Children of Israel: "Do not take for yourselves anyone to rely on other than me, 3descendants of those we carried with Noah." He was a thankful servant.

4And we decreed to the Children of Israel in the Book: You will certainly cause corruption on the earth twice, and you will certainly rise to a great height. 5So when the promise of the first of the two comes, we will raise up against you [alt: So when the promise of the first of the two came, we raised up against you] servants of ours who are fierce in battle, and they will go back and forth among the homes. And it is a promise that will be carried out. 6Then we will give you back the turn against them, and we will supply you with wealth and sons and make you more numerous in men. 7If you do good, you do good for yourselves, and if you do evil, it is for yourselves. So when the promise of the last one comes, it is so that they may bring grief to your faces, and enter the place of worship as they entered it the first time, and bring to utter ruin whatever they overcome.

8It may be that your Lord will have mercy on you. But if you go back, we will go back. And we have made Hell a prison [alt: a mat] for the disbelievers.

This Quran guides

9This Quran guides to what is straightest, and it gives good news to the believers who do righteous deeds that for them there is a great reward, 10and that for those who do not believe in the hereafter we have prepared a painful punishment. 11And man calls for evil as he calls for good, and man is hasty.

Read your book

12And we have made the night and the day two signs. Then we erased the sign of the night and made the sign of the day give sight, so that you may seek bounty from your Lord, and so that you may know the number of the years and the reckoning. And everything we have set out in detail. 13And every person, we have fastened his fate to his neck. [In Arabic the word for "fate" here is "bird", which is also the word for an omen.] And on the Day of Resurrection we will bring out for him a book that he will meet spread open: 14"Read your book. Your own soul is enough today as a reckoner against you." 15Whoever is guided is guided only for himself, and whoever goes astray goes astray only against himself. And no bearer of burdens bears the burden of another. And we would not punish until we had raised up a messenger.

When we want to destroy a town

16And when we want to destroy a town, we command those in it who live in luxury [alt: we multiply those in it who live in luxury], and they are defiant in it, so the word comes true against it, and we demolish it completely. 17And how many generations we have destroyed after Noah! And your Lord is enough as one who is aware of the sins of his servants and sees them.

The hasty life and the hereafter

18Whoever wants the hasty life, we hasten for him in it whatever we will, for whomever we want. Then we make Hell his, to burn in it, condemned and banished. 19And whoever wants the hereafter and strives for it with the striving due to it, and is a believer, those are the ones whose striving is thanked. 20Each one we supply, these and those, from the gift of your Lord. And the gift of your Lord is not restricted. 21Look how we have set some of them above others. And the hereafter is indeed greater in degrees and greater in setting some above others.

Worship none but him

22Do not set up another god with God, or you will sit condemned and abandoned.

23And your Lord has decreed that you worship none but him, and do good to parents. If one of them or both of them reach old age with you, do not say "Ugh" to them, and do not scold them, but speak to them with gracious words. 24And lower to them the wing of humility, out of mercy, and say, "My Lord, have mercy on them, as they raised me when I was small." 25Your Lord knows best what is within yourselves. If you are righteous, he is forgiving to those who keep returning.

Give, but do not squander

26And give the relative his due, and the destitute, and the traveler, and do not squander wastefully. 27Those who squander are brothers of the devils, and Satan is ungrateful to his Lord. 28And if you turn aside from them, seeking a mercy from your Lord that you hope for, then speak to them with gentle words.

29And do not keep your hand chained to your neck, and do not extend it to its full extent, or you will sit blamed and stripped bare. 30Your Lord extends provision to whomever he wills, and measures it out. He is aware of his servants and sees them.

Do not kill

31And do not kill your children for fear of poverty. It is we who provide for them and for you. Killing them is a great wrong.

32And do not go near unlawful sex. It is an indecency, and an evil way.

33And do not kill the soul that God has made sacred, except by right. And whoever is killed wrongfully, we have given authority to the one who acts for him, but let him not go too far in killing. He is aided.

Keep the covenant and give full measure

34And do not go near the orphan's wealth, except in the way that is best, until he reaches his full strength. And keep the covenant. The covenant will be asked about. 35And give full measure when you measure, and weigh with the straight scale. That is better, and best in outcome.

Do not follow what you do not know

36And do not follow after what you have no knowledge of. The hearing, the sight and the heart, each of those will be asked about [alt: each of those will itself be asked about it]. 37And do not walk on the earth swaggering. You will not tear through the earth, and you will not reach the mountains in height.

38In all of that, what is evil is hateful in the sight of your Lord. 39That is part of the wisdom your Lord has revealed to you. And do not set up another god with God, or you will be thrown into Hell, blamed and banished.

Everything glorifies him

40Has your Lord, then, chosen sons for you and taken females for himself from among the angels? You are indeed saying a grave thing. 41And we have certainly put it in many ways in this Quran, so that they may remember, but it only increases them in aversion.

42Say, "If there were gods with him, as they say, then they would have sought a way to the Possessor of the Throne." 43Glory to him! And he is high above what they say, by a great height. 44The seven heavens and the earth and whoever is in them glorify him. And there is nothing that does not glorify him with praise, but you do not understand their glorifying. He is forbearing, forgiving.

A hidden screen

45And when you read the Quran, we place between you and those who do not believe in the hereafter a hidden screen. 46And we place veils over their hearts, so that they do not understand it, and in their ears a heaviness. And when you mention your Lord alone in the Quran, they turn their backs in aversion. 47We know best what they listen with when they listen to you, and when they talk in private, when the wrongdoers say, "You are following only a bewitched man." 48Look how they have given examples for you, so they have gone astray and cannot find a way.

Raised up as a new creation

49And they said, "When we are bones and crumbled remains, will we really be raised up as a new creation?" 50Say, "Be stones or iron, 51or some creation of the kind that is great in your chests." Then they will say, "Who will bring us back?" Say, "The one who brought you into being the first time." Then they will shake their heads at you and say, "When is it?" Say, "It may be that it is near. 52On the day he calls you, you will answer with praise of him, and you will think that you stayed only a little while."

Say what is best

53And tell my servants to say what is best. Satan stirs up trouble among them. Satan is a clear enemy to man. 54Your Lord knows you best. If he wills, he will have mercy on you, or if he wills, he will punish you. And we have not sent you as one in charge of them. 55And your Lord knows best whoever is in the heavens and the earth. And we have certainly set some of the prophets above others, and we gave David a Scripture.

Those they call on

56Say, "Call on those you have claimed apart from him. They have no power to lift harm from you, or to divert it." 57Those whom they call on are seeking the means of approach to their Lord, to see which of them is nearest [alt: even the nearest of them], and they hope for his mercy and fear his punishment. The punishment of your Lord is a thing to beware of.

The signs are sent only to frighten

58And there is no town that we will not destroy before the Day of Resurrection, or punish with a severe punishment. That is inscribed in the Book. 59And nothing kept us from sending the signs except that the earlier peoples called them lies. And we gave Thamud the she-camel, giving sight, but they did wrong by her. And we send the signs only to frighten. 60And when we said to you, "Your Lord has surrounded the people." And we made the vision that we showed you, and the cursed tree in the Quran, only a trial for the people. And we frighten them, but it only increases them in great excess.

Iblis and the descendants of Adam

61And when we said to the angels, "Bow down to Adam," they bowed down, except Iblis. He said, "Shall I bow down to one you created from clay?" 62He said, "Do you see this one whom you have honored above me? If you delay me until the Day of Resurrection, I will certainly put a bridle on his descendants [alt: devour his descendants], all but a few." 63He said, "Go. Whoever of them follows you, Hell is the repayment of you all, an ample repayment. 64And unsettle whomever of them you can with your voice, and rally your horses and your men on foot against them, and be their partner in wealth and children, and make them promises." And Satan promises them nothing but delusion. 65"As for my servants, you have no authority over them." And your Lord is enough as one to rely on.

On land and sea

66Your Lord is the one who drives the ships for you on the sea, so that you may seek some of his bounty. He is merciful to you. 67And when harm touches you at sea, those you call on are lost, except him. But when he brings you safely to land, you turn aside. And man is ungrateful. 68Do you feel safe, then, that he will not make the side of the land sink with you, or send a storm of stones against you? Then you would find no one to rely on. 69Or do you feel safe that he will not take you back into it another time, and send a shattering blast of wind against you, and drown you because you were ungrateful? Then you would find no one to follow it up for you against us.

70And we have certainly honored the children of Adam, and carried them on land and sea, and provided them with good things, and set them far above many of those we have created.

The book in the right hand

71On the day we call every group of people with their leader [alt: with their record], whoever is given his book in his right hand, those will read their book, and they will not be wronged by so much as a thread. 72And whoever is blind in this life will be blind in the hereafter, and further astray from the way.

They almost tempted you away

73And indeed they almost tempted you away from what we have revealed to you, so that you would invent against us something other than it. And then they would have taken you as a friend. 74And had we not made you firm, you would almost have leaned toward them a little. 75Then we would have made you taste double in life and double in death, and then you would have found no one to aid you against us.

76And indeed they almost unsettled you from the land, to drive you out of it. And then they would have stayed after you only a little while. 77That was the practice with those of our messengers we sent before you, and you will find no diverting of our practice.

The prayer and the reading at dawn

78Keep up the prayer from the declining of the sun until the dark of the night, and the reading at dawn. [In Arabic the word for "reading" here is "Quran".] The reading at dawn is witnessed. 79And for part of the night, stay awake with it, as something extra for you. It may be that your Lord will raise you up to a praised standing.

80And say, "My Lord, bring me in by a true entrance, and bring me out by a true exit, and give me from yourself an authority to aid me." 81And say, "The truth has come, and falsehood has vanished. Falsehood is bound to vanish."

A healing and a mercy

82And we send down from the Quran what is a healing and a mercy for the believers, but it only increases the wrongdoers in loss. 83And when we show favor to man, he turns aside and distances himself, and when evil touches him, he is in despair. 84Say, "Each one acts according to his own manner, so your Lord knows best who is best guided on the way."

They ask you about the spirit

85And they ask you about the spirit. Say, "The spirit is from the command of my Lord [alt: is part of my Lord's matter], and you have been given only a little knowledge." 86And if we willed, we would certainly take away what we have revealed to you, and then you would find no one to rely on for it against us, 87except for a mercy from your Lord. His bounty to you is great.

The like of this Quran

88Say, "If humans and jinn came together to bring the like of this Quran, they would not bring the like of it, even if they backed one another." 89And we have certainly put every kind of example in many ways for people in this Quran, but most people have refused anything but disbelief.
